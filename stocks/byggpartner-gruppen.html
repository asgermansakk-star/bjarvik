    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −12 of −30
    • Margin
      Low-margin contractor: EBITA margin 4.0% and net margin 3.0% over 12 months, gross margin only 6.8%. The margin is the highest in the company's history after losses in 2022 and 2023 (operating margin −2.4% and −3.6%), and Flodéns earns only 1.7% in Q3 and is still in a transition period.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation and dilution
      The acquisitions of Åhlin & Ekeroth and Flodéns in 2022 were followed by two loss-making years and a share issue of kr 238m in 2023. The share count has risen from 12.1m (2021) to 45.3m, and EPS of kr 3.98 (TTM) is still below kr 5.75 in 2021.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat
      Regional contractor in Central and Western Sweden competing with the large nationwide companies for tenders. ROIC of 41% is due to negative working capital and customer advances rather than pricing power. The Samverkan model and long customer relationships give repeat orders, but no lasting moat.

      −3

Both key posts change in a period with a record-high order book.
