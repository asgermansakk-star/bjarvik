    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisitions have given large write-downs: Vonjo (from the CTI BioPharma purchase in 2023) was written down by SEK 6,612m in Q3 2025, and Zynlonta by SEK 452m in Q2 2026 after LOTIS-5. At the same time Arthrosi was bought for USD 950m in cash plus up to USD 550m in milestones, financed with debt; net debt rose from SEK 10.1bn to 14.8bn.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Moat and business model
      Almost all growth products are licensed in or bought (Altuvoct from Sanofi, Aspaveli from Apellis, Tryngolza from Ionis, lacutamab from Innate, pozdeutinurad from Arthrosi). Growth requires continuous expensive deals and royalty or milestone payments to partners rather than own research.

      −2

      The share count has risen 2.21% in a year and from 296m to 348m since 2022 (new issue for the CTI acquisition in 2023 and C shares for incentive programmes). There are no buybacks and no dividend.

    • Beyfortus royalty
      The royalty from Sanofi's sales of Beyfortus fell 37% to kr 732m in Q3 and 33% year to date. Barclays (Hold, price target kr 308) points to uncertainty about the contribution in Q4. The royalty was kr 2.2bn in 2025, when it gave almost pure profit.
      placera.se
      −2
    • Product concentration
      Doptelet (kr 1,829m) and Altuvoct (kr 1,729m) accounted for 39% of revenue in Q3 and for most of the growth. Elocta fell 24% at the same time, because patients are switching to Altuvoct.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Management change
      CEO Guido Oelkers leaves by the end of January 2027 at the latest to take a job outside Sobi, and there is no successor yet in the middle of six major launches. The chair also changes (Börje Ekholm has been proposed).
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Execution
      FDA rejected NASP for uncontrolled gout (complete response letter, June 2026) due to deficiencies in manufacturing at contract manufacturers. Approval of one of the six launch products (peak sales DKK 4–6bn) is thereby delayed, and inventories for the launch of NASP and pozdeutinurad have been written down.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow fell to kr 4,908m year to date (5,584) despite 18% revenue growth. Working capital tied up kr 2,588m (739), so earnings convert less well to cash.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt is kr 14.8bn (1.2x EBITDA) with only kr 1.0bn in cash and a current ratio of 0.99. On top come contingent milestones of up to USD 550m to Arthrosi and USD 40m to Pint Pharma.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1

      CEO Oelkers exercised options and sold 179,208 shares at kr 464–467 in July 2026 (about kr 84m). Duane Barnes sold 97,515 shares, and Daniel Rankin sold 41,350 shares in October. There are no notable insider purchases, and insiders own 0.33%.
