    • Margin
      Gross margin is high (82% adjusted in Q1), but operating margin is −38% TTM and was −56% in FY 2025/26; the company has made a profit in only one of the last five years. The positive EBITDA of 3.8m in Q1 stems from a one-off customs refund of 6.2m; without it EBITDA was −2.4m.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has stood still for three years: SEK 104m (FY 23/24), 91.1 (FY 24/25, −12.5%) and 91.4 (FY 25/26, +0.4%). Q1 2026/27 showed +27% and 17% more S1 operations (48 vs 41), but that is one quarter after a long period without growth.

    • Moat
      OPRA is one of few bone-anchored prosthesis systems with long-term clinical documentation (incl. high implant survival among war-wounded in Ukraine) and a network of Centers of Excellence. The niche is, however, small, and competing osseointegration systems exist.

The bionics research is being spun out into the subsidiary Afyri, which also has to be financed.
