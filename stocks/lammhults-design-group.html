    In plain words

    The score of 56 shows a business with weak profit and flat sales at a middling price. Most weighing it down: operating margin is only 3.3% against a target of 8-10%, revenue has stood still since 2022, and cash flow is negative. In its favour, order intake rose 20.8% last quarter and customers are spread out. Watch the debt, which is about 3.4 times earnings.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
