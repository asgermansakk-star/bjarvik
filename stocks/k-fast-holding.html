    In plain words

    A score of 66 is middling: the share is cheap against the booked value of its properties, but there are many signs of pressure. The biggest drag is heavy debt: earnings cover interest only 1.7 times, close to the loan terms' minimum of 1.5, and the return on invested money is low (1.65%). 95.2% of apartments are let. Watch the property sales meant to cut debt.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −23 of −35
    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio is 57.3% vs a covenant of at most 65%, and net debt/EBITDA is 19.9x. Interest cover is 1.7x over 12 months, below the company's own target of 1.75x and close to the covenant of 1.5x. The average debt maturity has fallen to 1.4 years (2.1), and SEK 3,977m of the debt is short-term vs only SEK 120.6m in cash and SEK 247m in unused credit facilities. The bond of SEK 500m costs STIBOR + 600 bp.
      mb.cision.com
      −10
    • Cash flow and financing need
      Operating cash flow was DKK 450m over 12 months (of which +DKK 249m in working capital), while investments in properties were DKK 1,353m and sales only DKK 75m. The company has therefore borrowed a further DKK 420m net, and cash flow after investments is negative.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Execution of the restructuring
      The previously announced sale of a larger property portfolio to Viga, primarily as a forward sale to a joint fund structure, has been dropped. The properties are instead to be sold on the open market while long-term interest rates rise. The company is working on ten properties for sale, and the separation of Brinova has no timetable: it will happen only when the balance sheet allows.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Governance and ownership concentration
      Chairman Erik Selin, who is also CEO of Balder, and CEO Jacob Karlsson own 32.4% and 31.4% of the capital and have 35.0% and 34.2% of the votes through A shares with five votes. JV partner Kilenkrysset got 10% of the votes through a share swap in August and has a representative on the board.

      −2

      Brinova accounts for SEK 11.0bn of the group's interest-bearing debt of SEK 14.5bn, but K-Fast owns only 57.6%, and the minorities hold SEK 2.7bn of the equity.     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property and construction company: the main multiple is price vs long-term net asset value (NAV SEK 24.60 per share at 30/9-2026). stockanalysis's P/B of 0.34 uses total equity including minority interests in Brinova (SEK 2.7bn); equity attributable to the parent company's shareholders is SEK 21.15 per share (P/B approx. 0.51). Look-through NAV with the Brinova stake at market price is a rough estimate. The insider share of 34% at stockanalysis understates ownership: Erik Selin and Jacob Karlsson together own approx. 64% of the capital. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so analyst coverage is very thin. The credit ratios for interest and capital tie-up are excluding Brinova. The yield requirements of the property valuations and the share of external valuations are not scored. The distribution of Brinova to shareholders has no timetable and is not included.
