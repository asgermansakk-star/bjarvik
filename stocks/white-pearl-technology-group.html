    In plain words

    White Pearl Technology scores 52 out of 100: an IT group growing fast through acquisitions. The biggest drags are that earnings per share fell despite growth, that the share count rose 40% in a year, and that one hardware order equals about 40% of annual revenue. On the plus side, the shares look cheap relative to profit. Cash flow is weaker than profit.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue grew 32.7% in the first nine months of the year to kr 482.9m, almost entirely through 13 acquisitions in 2026. EPS nevertheless fell to kr 2.09 from kr 2.14, and in Q3 EPS fell 28% to kr 0.56 from kr 0.78 because the average share count rose from 28.9m to 34.0m. The company is getting bigger, but not stronger per share.

    • Margin
      The EBITDA margin in Q3 fell to 17.45% from 21.8%, and the gross margin fell to 35.6% from 37.3%. Management expects the growing share of AI infrastructure to keep the gross margin in the lower half of the 35–37% range.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation and ROIC
      The acquisitions in 2026 cost kr 235.7m, and 73% was paid in new shares. Aixia alone cost kr 162m, of which 114m was goodwill. Intangibles now make up kr 344m of equity of 498m. ROIC fell to 27.8% from 41.5% in 2025, even before Aixia was consolidated.

      Growth in AI & Platforms, which now makes up 27% of revenue, is built mainly on resale and installation of NVIDIA hardware, e.g. a DGX Rubin order. It is a business with a low gross margin and a poor moat. The rest of the group is IT consultants and agencies that compete on price.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis uses 31.3m shares, but after the non-cash issue to Aixia and the set-off issue there are 40.6m shares. Market cap is therefore approx. SEK 510m and not SEK 393m, so stockanalysis' P/E of 4.9 and forward P/E of 4.15 are too low. On the actual share count, P/E is approx. 7. No independent analysts cover the stock; there is only paid research (Carlsquare), and the forecast page on stockanalysis does not exist. stockanalysis' financials were last updated with Q2, so Q3 figures are taken from the report of 5/11-2026. Lease liabilities are not disclosed separately, so FCF is measured after purchases of intangible assets, but not after leasing. Customer concentration is not disclosed in figures. Insider trades on the market after August have not been investigated.
