    In plain words

    Titania Holding scores 49 out of 100 and is a Stockholm property developer with very large debt. Operations do not cover the interest, and interest-bearing debt is 3.8bn Swedish kronor against a market value of 0.59bn. The stock costs only about 0.27 times the value of its assets after debt, and rental income rose 40% in the first half. Watch the roughly 60% of loans that fall due in 2027.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • Margin and returns
      Operations do not cover the interest: in H1 2026 operating profit was kr 49m and profit before value changes kr 33m, while financial items were −kr 114m. That gives a management result of approx. −kr 81m and an interest cover of 0.3 times (0.1 for all of 2025). ROIC is 0.4%, and the ROE of 21.6% comes solely from value adjustments.

    • Quality of growth
      Of H1 profit before tax of kr 236m, kr 339m came from unrealised value gains, of which kr 293m on projects under way. The earnings are not realised in cash: operating cash flow was −kr 149m over 12 months, and EPS fell 47% TTM as the value gains faded.

    • Capital allocation
      Growth is financed with expensive debt (construction loans at 10% fixed and bonds at STIBOR + 7.75–8.75%) and a directed issue in November 2025 of 11.3m B shares at SEK 7. It diluted the share count by 16% to about 0.30 times net asset value (SEK 23.33 at year-end). The company deliberately pays no dividend in the coming years.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Segment and moat
      Titania mainly builds rental housing in low-price areas in the Stockholm region (Rågsved, Hallunda, Gottsunda in Uppsala) and is now focusing on houses for sale. It is a cyclical segment without pricing power, and only 806 homes are in operation vs approx. 6,100 building rights, so the value depends on projects that lie several years out.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property developer and residential landlord in Stockholm: price vs net asset value is the main multiple, and P/E (1.7) is meaningless, because the result consists almost only of unrealised value increases. The net asset value (SEK 26.17 per share at 30/6-2026) adds all the deferred tax to equity and is partly based on the company's own valuations of ongoing projects. The Q3 report (expected 12–13/11-2026) is not out, so the figures are from H1 2026. No analyst coverage, no price target and no forecast page on stockanalysis (PEG and upside are empty). Insider trades beyond the share sale from Einar Janson Invest to the deputy CEO in 2025 have not been investigated, nor have bond terms (covenants) in detail, so they give 0. The VAT case, which the company won in Kammarrätten 18/9-2026, has not been investigated in terms of amount. Very illiquid stock with A and B shares.
