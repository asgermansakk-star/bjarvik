    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin
      EBIT margin before special items was −0.5% in 2025/26 (−kr 13m), and the operating margin has not been above 2.4% in any year since 2021/22; the net result has been negative five years in a row (−kr 107m in 2025/26). Even guidance for 2026/27 is only a 1–3% EBIT margin, and Q1 produced −4.3%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • ROIC
      ROIC has been below WACC (10.6%) in each of five years; the best year was 6.3% (2023/24). The loss is counted under margin; here the deduction is only for the fact that the capital structurally earns no return, even in profitable years.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen four years in a row from kr 2,948m (2021/22) to kr 2,468m (2025/26), −16%. Brand Partnering/licence fell from kr 363m to 270m since 2022/23, and Q1 2026/27 gave only 2.2% growth in local currency. The store network was reduced by a net 31 monobrand stores.
      inderes.dk
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The share count rose approx. 20% in a directed issue in November 2024 (24.6m shares at kr 9.27, kr 228m gross) to finance a strategy plan whose medium-term targets were withdrawn in March 2026. At the same time own shares are being bought for incentive programmes (kr 50m in 2025/26 and a new programme of up to kr 33m from October 2026), even though FCF after capitalised development is negative.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Scale and moat
      With DKK 2.5bn in revenue and about DKK 200m a year in capitalised development, B&O is a small player against Sonos, Apple and Samsung in speakers, headphones and TVs; without scale in component purchasing and software, the moat rests almost solely on brand and design.
