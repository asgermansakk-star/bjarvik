Denmark fell organically by 13.7% after lost contracts, and Norway by 2.4% on lower variable volumes. Only Sweden is growing (+3.4%). Analysts expect −0.5% in 2026.

    • Debt and liquidity
      Net debt of kr 2.14bn incl. leasing (2.3 times adjusted EBITDA), of which a corporate bond of 1.5bn. Interest cover 3.9, current ratio 0.80 and equity ratio only 22%. The high tax rate (27%) is due to interest deductions with limited deductibility.

      −3
    • Ownership concentration and governance
      The WISAG sphere (Michael Wisser, German FM competitor) has bought up to 29.99%, just below the bid obligation, and Shark Invest's 9.96% was transferred to Wisser. According to FI documents, WISAG-affiliated companies entered as early as April 2025, months before the ownership became known. At the April general meeting they postponed the dividend and voted down the board's authorisations for buybacks and new issues. A competitor thus in practice controls capital allocation without bidding.

      −3

      Items affecting comparability are recurring: kr 92m in 2025 and kr 106m LTM, mainly restructuring. In addition came a lost arbitration case in Denmark from 2022, costing 27m in Q3. Reported EBIT (503m) is thus clearly below adjusted EBITA (646m).
