    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The equity certificate fraction is 41.86%, so approx. 58% of equity is self-owned. The security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.44 and book value of NOK 361 per share cover total equity including self-owned capital and perpetual bonds and have not been used. T2 is based on the bank's own book equity per certificate (NOK 151.13 at 30/6-2026) and earnings per certificate. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are H1 2026. The Q3 report is due on 11/11-2026 and is not included. The bank has flagged a significant positive one-off effect in Q3 from a transaction in the Eika Gruppen share on 24/8-2026. The amount has not been disclosed, and the effect is not included. It will lift trailing EPS and book value, but it is a one-off item. Earnings for 2025 also included NOK 63m in fair value adjustments of financial instruments. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 11.54 has an uncertain basis and has not been used. Analyst coverage is effectively non-existent. Customer concentration in single exposures has not been examined (0 points). Insider trades have only been read from the owner lists in the annual report and the half-year report, as no insider trade notifications have been found for 2025–2026.
