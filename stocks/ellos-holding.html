    In plain words

    The score is middling: a recently listed Nordic online retailer with low profit and high debt. The margin is below the company's target, the return on invested money is negative, and the parent company went bankrupt in 2024. The share trades very little, and former bondholders own many shares. In its favour, gross profit is rising, own brands make up 65% of sales and the chairman has bought shares.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
