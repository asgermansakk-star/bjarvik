    In plain words

    The score of 56 shows a small medical firm whose sales doubled, but which loses money and is expensive. Most weighing it down: the operating loss equals the entire revenue, nearly all growth rests on one customer, and new shares have diluted owners. In its favour, the partner's catheter is FDA-approved, and cash exceeds debt. Watch the next report on 16 November.
