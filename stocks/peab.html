    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Peab reports both segment figures and IFRS; stockanalysis uses IFRS, in which Swedish bostadsrättsföreningar (tenant-owner associations) under construction are consolidated (net debt SEK 10.2bn vs SEK 7.2bn in segment figures). The 2025 result is depressed by a one-off charge of SEK 611m in financial items from the Mall of Scandinavia settlement, so trailing P/E for 2025 (17.9) and EPS growth for 2026 (+79%) are distorted; multiples are compared on forward and EV/EBITDA. The 1.7% fall in share count has been checked against buybacks of SEK 739m (TTM) in the cash flow statement. Only 4 analysts cover the stock (price targets SEK 100–110). Insider trades have not been examined (no data found), and customer concentration has only been assessed from the segment breakdown. The CFO change at 4/2-2027 is a planned internal succession and has not been deducted.
