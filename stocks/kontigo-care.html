    In plain words

    The score is 54 out of 100: Kontigo is a very small company with sales flat for four years and still a loss. Management has changed several times, the shares trade very thinly, and over three quarters of equity is development costs booked as assets. In its favour, there is no interest-bearing debt and cash is about 30% of market value. The next report has been postponed to 30 November.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • Margin
      Continued losses: EBIT −SEK 1.1m in Q2 (−14.9%), operating margin −3.5% and net margin −3.3% TTM. The positive EBITDA (0.8m in Q2) does not cover the amortisation of capitalised development (1.7m in the quarter), and it continues even though nothing new is being capitalised any more.

Even so, revenue has barely increased, and equity now consists 77% of capitalised development (38.2 of 49.7m).
