    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro company (market value about SEK 65m, 11 employees) with no analyst coverage and no forward estimates; PEG and upside cannot be calculated. Cash of SEK 9.5m as of 30/6 contains Werfen's prepaid minimum royalties for 2027 and 2028 (about USD 1.3m in total received in 2026), which appear as accruals of SEK 10.4m. Adjusted for them, there is in reality no net cash; cash has therefore not been counted as a plus. The convertible of SEK 15m to Calyrex (paid about 27/10) is not in stockanalysis' balance sheet as of 30/6. The Q3 report comes on 25/11 and is not included. The split of revenue across customers beyond Werfen and the financial strength of Calyrex and Intelmetis have not been examined. Insider trades have only been checked in FI's register (no purchases in the last 12 months). Very low liquidity (about 29,000 shares a day).
