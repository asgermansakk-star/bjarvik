      One small mine (Fäbodtjärn) with approx. 250 kg of gold a year. The reserve was cut in 2026 to 117 kt at 7.06 g/t (827 kg of gold vs 1,299 kg), and the production plan only reaches the start of Q2 2029. The company has no processing plant of its own and only 6 employees; operations are carried out by the contractor Bergteamet.

    • Gold price dependence without hedging
      All earnings follow the gold price, which according to the company has fallen almost 21% from the peak of about USD 5,280/oz in Q1 to about USD 4,194/oz at the end of Q3. The company has no hedging of the gold price, only partial currency hedging of individual deliveries.

      The ore is enriched in campaigns at other companies' plants (Dragon Mining's Svartliden and Björkdal). In Q2 a campaign was cut short due to a breakdown in a mill motor, and in Q3 the settlement of concentrates (quotational periods) gave a negative revenue adjustment of SEK 5.7m.

      A fire at Fäbodtjärn on 10 October destroyed three special vehicles and a storage tent; production ran at reduced capacity for two weeks, and the company expects one-off costs of SEK 5–10m (depending on insurance).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analyst coverage and no forward estimate on stockanalysis (the forecast page gives 404), so PEG is n/a and there is no price target. Botnia Gold (formerly Botnia Exploration) has only produced since the end of 2024, so its own multiple history is short and based on start-up years. Earnings fluctuate greatly from quarter to quarter because the gold is sold in 3 concentration campaigns a year (Q3 2026 had a loss, 68 kg of gold from the campaign was only booked as revenue in October). The remaining reserve after 2026 is roughly estimated (827 kg at 31/12-2025 minus approx. 256 kg produced in 2026, part of which was marginal ore outside the reserve); an updated reserve calculation with the extended production plan only comes in February 2027. stockanalysis shows −2.43% in share count, but the share count is unchanged at 40,805,028 in the accounts, so no points have been given for buybacks. The full owner list (the nomination committee's four owners hold approx. 45.8%) and AISC per ounce have not been examined, as the company does not disclose AISC in the report. Vargbäcken's development budget and financing are unknown.
