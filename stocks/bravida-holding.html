      The EBITA margin is 6.7% (12 months), but only about 6.4% excluding the ABEKA gain and the restructuring in Sweden – still below the company's own target of over 7%, which has not been reached since 2021 (operating margin 6.9%). A thin margin for an installer with fixed-price risk.

      ROIC 11.9% vs 15.6–15.9% in 2021–2022 and 11.1–11.6% in 2024–2025; the capital base is dominated by goodwill of kr 11.7bn from many acquisitions, so the return has not recovered to its earlier level.

      −3

      Revenue in 2025 (kr 28.2bn) was below 2023 (29.4bn), and organic growth was −6% in 9M 2025; growth from 2021 to 2023 came mainly from acquisitions. The current organic growth of 9% is a cyclical recovery driven by a few large data centre orders.

      Three data centre contracts (Stavanger kr 4.3bn, Kouvola kr 2.2bn and Mälardalen/Sweden kr 1.5bn) make up approx. one third of the order backlog of kr 24.2bn. Large fixed-price and turnkey contracts carry write-down risk, which the company itself mentions among material risks.
