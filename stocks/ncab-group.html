    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −11 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Earnings follow the PCB cycle: EPS fell from kr 2.23 (2022) to kr 1.10 (2025), and even after the record quarter EPS (TTM 1.82) is still below the 2022 peak. The current growth is driven especially by price increases across the market, not by a stronger business.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC 15.2% vs 23–28% in 2021–2023; the acquisition strategy has lifted goodwill to kr 1,934m, more than equity of kr 1,723m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Moat
      Asset-light intermediary without own production, dependent on partner factories (mainly in Asia) and their material allocation; gross margin fell to 34.8% (35.2) because higher purchase prices have not been fully passed on.
      mfn.se
      −3

      Operating margin (TTM) is 11.0% vs 13–14% in 2022–2023; Q3 gave a record EBITA margin of 14.4%, but the level over a cycle is not yet higher than before.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −16 of −35
    • Customer behaviour (pre-orders)
      Organic order intake +155% in Q3 (book-to-bill 1.79); management estimates that advance orders account for about 80 percentage points and higher prices for about 50 percentage points. Customers are ordering through to deliveries in 2027 due to material shortages – classic double ordering, which can turn into an order drop when supply normalises (expected late 2027 at the earliest).
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Insider trading
      Chairman Christian Salamon (via a related party) sold 400,000 shares at kr 89.25 (about kr 36m) on 29/10, a week after the record report; the CEO's purchases of 5,000 shares in total in October do not offset it.
      marknadssok.fi.se
      −3
    • Macro and geopolitics
      Production is with partner factories, mainly in China, while North America is the largest growth segment (about 25% of sales); tariff rules and export restrictions can hit directly (the report mentions refunded tariffs).
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Working capital
      Tied-up working capital rose to 9.6% of sales (7.9%), partly via prepayments to factories to secure materials; receivables and inventory are growing faster than revenue.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Debt and acquisition financing
      Net debt rose to DKK 832m (686), and the equity ratio fell to 36.4% (41.5); FCF after leasing (DKK 337m TTM) covers the dividend, but not acquisitions of DKK 278m as well. The Board Shark earnout is booked at the maximum of DKK 107m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1

      A stronger SEK reduced EBITA by SEK 48m in January–September; earnings are predominantly in USD and EUR.
