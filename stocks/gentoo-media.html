    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 report does not come until 25/11-2026, so the latest figures are from Q2 and the trading update on 1/10. Stockanalysis' market cap, EV, forward P/E (2.8) and P/FCF are based on 134.7m shares and have not been updated for the 91.9m new Class Z shares issued on 2/11 (about 226.6m shares in total). The forward estimates are probably from before the Brazil ban and the new share count, so PEG and forward P/E are misleading. ROIC (489%) and ROE are distorted by negative equity. Customer concentration at the operators and traffic dependence on Google have not been quantified (0 points). The Juroszek family's total ownership after the issue is estimated from the announcements (at least about 53%); it has not been examined whether an exemption from the mandatory bid obligation has been granted. Analyst coverage is thin.
