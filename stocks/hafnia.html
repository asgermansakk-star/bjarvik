    • ROIC through the cycle
      ROIC 17.6% (TTM) and 35% annualised in Q2, but 10.9% in 2025 and 0% in 2021; the operating margin swings from 0% (2021) to 41% (2022) and 15.7% (2025). The return follows the freight cycle.

      Transport of oil products without pricing power: rates are set by the market. The advantage is scale (103 owned vessels) and the pools, which provide fee income (USD 16.6m in H1), but they are small against vessel earnings.

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The BW Group ownership stake (approx. 45%) is from an SEC filing from March 2026 and has not been checked against VPS; related-party transactions with BW, customer concentration in the pools and insider trades have not been examined.
