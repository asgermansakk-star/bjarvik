    In plain words

    The score is low because this is a small investment company that, as CombiGene, burned through its capital. About 91% of its value is a single listed holding, and costs exceed income. On the other hand, the company has no interest-bearing debt, and the shares trade about 18% below the stated value. Watch for a need for new capital, which the board itself has mentioned.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
