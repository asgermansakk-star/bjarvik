    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap with 6 employees, market cap about SEK 11m and no analyst coverage (no forecast page on stockanalysis). The Q3 report does not come until 10/11-2026, and nothing has been published about what happened to the owner and board loans totalling SEK 12.4m that fell due on 30/9-2026 – the status is therefore unknown. stockanalysis' EV of 9.9m does not include these loans (booked as other liabilities), and the insider ownership of 0.91% is wrong: principal owner Kattson (Håkan Johansson, board member) owns 29.3% and chairman Ulf Gustavsson 4.9%. The loss in 2025 includes about SEK 12m of write-down of the stake in Andritz Technologies. Royalty income from Andritz (about SEK 230k per FlowScanner sold) is not documented in the accounts and is not included.
