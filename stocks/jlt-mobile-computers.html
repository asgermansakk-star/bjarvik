    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −20 of −35
    • Cash flow and financing need
      Operating cash flow before working capital was −SEK 7.3m in 9M, and cash fell to 9.7m (16.9 at year-end). Excluding prepaid service agreements of 13.7m, cash is negative. FCF in 2024–2025 came from inventory reduction (12.7 and 7.0m), not operations. If the loss continues, an issue may become necessary in 2027.

    • Components and customer behaviour
      Memory prices have in some cases risen up to 15 times, and JLT has raised its prices; customers are postponing investments and sales cycles are longer. Management still sees risk in lead times, purchasing costs and inventory tie-up.

      −4

      Dollar customers accounted for 74% of revenue in 2025 and America for SEK 92m of 131.5m; the weaker dollar squeezed the gross margin, and the uncertainty about US tariffs makes customers wait and see and increases administration.
