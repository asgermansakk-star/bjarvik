    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −16 of −30
    • Margin over the cycle
      Despite an EBITDA margin of 57%, the net margin was only 6.2% TTM and −7.0% (2025), 3.5% (2024) and −10.7% (2023). Norwegian special tax (effective tax 73–82%), high production costs and hedging losses (−USD 20m in Q3) eat into earnings; net income was USD 29m in Q3 on revenue of USD 332m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      The 15% TTM revenue growth is due to prices (realised gas USD 121.3/boe vs 65.7 a year earlier), not volume: production fell from 32.1 kboepd in 2025 to 29.1 in Q3 2026, and 2026 guidance is 31–32. Growth to 39–43 kboepd in 2027 rests on one project (Bestla).
      okea.no
      −3
    • Moat and cost position
      Small price taker in mature fields (Draugen, Brage, Statfjord, Gjøa, Ivar Aasen) without pricing power. Production cost was USD 29.5/boe in Q3 and 34.0 in Q2 vs 23.7 in 2025, which is high for the Norwegian shelf and makes the company vulnerable at lower prices.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Future segment
      100% oil and gas from late-life fields, where the field life must be extended through continuous production drilling and small tie-backs (Springmus East, Talisker West, Kyllinglår); no low-emission business.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisitions of, among others, the Statfjord interests have been followed by write-downs year after year: USD 134m (2023), 66m (2024) and 67m (2025) and again 94m in Q2 and 33m in Q3 2026 on Statfjord after lower volumes and higher capex. Equity has shrunk to USD 140m, and the dividend was suspended in 2024–2025.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4

      Consensus expects revenue down 6% in 2027 and EPS growth of −26% a year over three years. There is no oil hedging after Q4 2026, while gas collars with ceilings of USD 128–315/boe cover 33–47% of gas after tax in 2027 and limit the gain from high prices.

      FCF after leasing was only USD 63m TTM on operating cash flow of 432m, and −19m in 2025. Cash is flattered by tax timing: tax payable rose from 26 to USD 179m in 2026, while only 12m has been paid in tax TTM; 64m falls due in Q4. Capex guidance for 2027 has been raised to USD 260–320m.

      Decommissioning obligations of USD 1,012m vs equity of only USD 140m; they are partly offset by reimbursement claims of 464m against Equinor, Shell and Harbour Energy. Debt/equity is 2.7, and the bonds of USD 296m must be refinanced.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    OKEA reports in USD while the stock trades in NOK; stockanalysis mixes the currencies (EPS NOK 5.71 TTM vs USD 0.60 in the financials table). ROIC of 110% and P/TBV of 12.9 are meaningless because equity is only USD 140m after repeated impairments and large decommissioning obligations; ROIC has therefore not been used. Only 5 analysts have price targets and only 2 have estimates, and the 2025 EPS of USD 1.88 in the forecast table is adjusted and does not match the reported loss. The shareholder list on okea.no loads dynamically; Bangchak's stake of 45.6% is from Bangchak's own site and has not been checked against a 2026 shareholder list. Field concentration (share per field) and reserve life have not been examined in figures. Analysis framework, not investment advice.
