      The parent company's income rose only 6.8% in H1 2026 (SEK 9.58m vs 8.97m), even though costs for shops, fairs, marketing and organisation have been lifted sharply. Management writes that the result is below the 'expansiva plan', and that orders could not be delivered because of delayed subcontractors.

      The company is a small niche brand in solid wood furniture with 12 employees, and 97% of sales come from Sweden. Capacity increasingly depends on external joineries. The brand is known locally, but there is no documented pricing power against larger design brands.
