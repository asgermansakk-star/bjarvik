    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −23 of −30
    • Margin
      No sales revenue; net loss of SEK 16.8m and operating cash flow of −SEK 18.0m over the last 12 months. The company has posted a loss every year as a development company (−SEK 8.8m to −SEK 19.5m a year in 2021–2025).
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Capital allocation and execution
      The restart of Blötberget has been planned for over ten years: a DFS from 2019 was never funded, project financing was put on hold in 2025 for a strategic review, and the project is now back at PFS level. The share count rose from 37.1 to 67.9m (+83%) with the SEK 126m issue in 2024.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Project economics and maturity
      The PFS (19/8-2026) shows a post-tax NPV8 of USD 325m, but only an IRR of 15.6% and a payback of 6.2 years at a capex of USD 447m. The life-of-mine plan rests partly on inferred resources in the Väsmanfältet, which have not been re-drilled, and on a premium price of USD 124/t.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat and future segment
      Ultra-high-quality iron ore concentrate for fossil-free steel production (direct reduction) is a growing segment, but the company is a small price taker with no technological advantage and competes with LKAB and other DR projects.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Development company without operations: P/E, EV/EBIT, ROIC and PEG are not meaningful, and stockanalysis' 'revenue' in the report is capitalised expenses. Whether the existing environmental permit covers the expanded project (4.8 Mt/year incl. Väsmanfältet) has not been examined (0 points). No analyst coverage or estimates found.
