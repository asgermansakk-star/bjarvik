    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo, not Oslo Børs' main list. The company reports only half-yearly under NGAAP, and the accounts are not audited. stockanalysis uses an outdated share count (19.75m); after the Mdco purchase and the directed issue there are 36.58m shares, so market value is about NOK 173m. Mdco is recognised from 1/11-2026, so all pro forma figures for the new group come from the sellers' own business plans, which are neither audited nor adopted as guidance. The history at stockanalysis from 2021–2024 (revenue of about NOK 200m and large write-downs) belongs to a previous business before the transition to a holding company and reverse split 1:50 in 2025, so it has not been used as a basis. There is no analyst coverage and no forward estimates. No listed OHS peers have been compared, so the peer criterion gives 0, and the grade is a ceiling. Solvit (45%) and one more OHS bolt-on are still only non-binding term sheets.
