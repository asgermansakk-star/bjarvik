    In plain words

    The score of 77 shows a company with a low price but weak progress. Sales have fallen since 2022, the return on invested money is below its cost, and the push into charging stations led to large write-downs. It relies on a few big customers such as Amazon. On the plus side, management has bought shares and targets are about 65% above the price. Watch the goal of doubling sales.
