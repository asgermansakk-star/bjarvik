    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    NCC has agreed to sell the Industry business area (stone and asphalt) to Heidelberg Materials and CRH for an EV of kr 8.2bn. The deal awaits regulatory approval and is expected to close in H2 2027, and Industry is reported as a discontinued operation (IFRS 5) from Q3 2026. stockanalysis' figures are therefore mixed: trailing P/E is n/a (reported loss after write-downs of kr 1.4bn in Q4 2025), and forward P/E 20.6 is based on an estimate for 2026 with revenue of kr 46.7bn, which matches neither the group nor the continuing operations. Multiples have therefore been assessed on NCC's own supplementary figures for the whole group before items outside normal operations (EPS kr 13.90 over 12 months) and on a rough estimate for the continuing operations after the sale proceeds. FCF includes Industry. OBOS's holding (10.3% of capital and 28.8% of votes) comes from a secondary source based on the shareholder list at 5/2-2026 and has not been checked against NCC's own shareholder list. Insider trades have not been examined (0 points). Only 4 analysts with consensus Hold.
