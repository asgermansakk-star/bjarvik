    In plain words

    The score is 71 out of 100 for an online gambling operator. The profit margin has fallen from 22% to 13%, earnings per share are expected to drop about 29% in 2026, and Brazil has banned online gambling, so Betsson stopped there. The CEO bought shares in March, and net cash is about 8% of market value. Sports betting made a weak start to the fourth quarter.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −12 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin has fallen from 22.4% to 13.3% in the first nine months of the year and the gross margin from 64.2% to 55.5% in Q3, because gaming duties rise as the share from locally regulated markets grows (77.7% vs 63.8%), and because payment costs are rising. Management also states that unprofitable B2C markets are pulling EBIT down by EUR 10–15m per quarter. ROIC has fallen from 26–29% in 2023–2025 to 17.4%.
      mb.cision.com
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is growing (organic +6% in the first nine months), but EPS has fallen to EUR 0.59 from EUR 1.04, and consensus expects −29% EPS in 2026. Growth is being bought with marketing in Latin America (marketing +21% in Q3), while the Nordics are falling (EUR 125.8m TTM vs 212m in 2022).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat
      Betsson is a B2C operator in a competitive market with no clear structural moat. CEECA fell 16.8% and the Nordics 10% in Q3, and earnings swing with the sportsbook margin (8.1% vs 8.8%).
      mfn.se
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −18 of −35
    • Customer concentration (B2B)
      B2B licence revenue fell to EUR 46.7m from EUR 76.9m in Q3 (14% vs 26% of revenue) and to EUR 51m from 90m in Q1, mainly because a single B2B customer generated lower revenue. A high-margin revenue stream thus still depends on few partners.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Regulation
      Brazil introduced an immediate ban on online gaming on 25/9, and Betsson has stopped deposits and marketing there. Buenos Aires has introduced a tax of 2% on winnings, Chile 19% VAT combined with blocking of sites, Lithuania is considering raising the gaming tax from 22% to 30%, and the EU is discussing a levy of 3%. About 22% of revenue comes from markets without a local licence.
      mb.cision.com
      −6
    • Cash flow and payouts
      FCF after capitalised development and leases (approx. EUR 115m TTM) does not cover dividends (90m) and buybacks (40m) combined. Net cash incl. client balances has fallen from 220 to EUR 98m in a year, also because Rhino cost 51m in cash, and a payment of approx. 13m is outstanding.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Currency
      Latin America accounts for 36% of revenue (incl. Argentina), earnings are reported in EUR, and the share trades in SEK. This gives double currency risk.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Sportsbook margin in Q4
      According to the trading update, the sportsbook margin at the start of Q4 has been markedly below the average of the last eight quarters, even though daily revenue has risen 11%.
      mb.cision.com
      −1
    • Governance
      Betsson has two share classes, and the A shares (about 15m of 135m shares) carry more votes. This gives the founders' circle, which the managing director Pontus Lindwall comes from, disproportionate control.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1
