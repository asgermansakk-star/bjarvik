    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 2 analysts cover the stock (S&P), so forward P/E and price targets are thin. stockanalysis shows −2.38% in share count, but the report shows an unchanged 711.7m shares outstanding; the figure has not been used. The accounts are in EUR, the stock trades in SEK. Half of the Uppsala tram order sits in a 50/50 JV, which is accounted for under the equity method, so the order book overstates consolidated revenue. The customer behind the data centre in Mikkeli and the contract types (fixed price vs collaboration agreements) have not been investigated. Insider trades have only been checked on the first page of FI's register (only purchases found). Peers are limited to Peab, Skanska and NCC.
