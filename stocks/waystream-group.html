    In plain words

    A score of 61 shows a very small network equipment supplier with weak earnings and a high price. The margin has fallen sharply, and the share price assumes a big recovery in 2027. Nearly half of 2025 sales came from a few customers, mainly one German operator. New customers have arrived, though, and management has bought shares. The two analysts see 53% upside, but their estimate looks outdated.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small company (approx. SEK 330m in market cap, 26 employees) with low liquidity in the stock (about 6,000 shares a day). Only 2 analysts at stockanalysis, and their 2026 estimate (EPS 1.40) looks outdated when EPS for 9M 2026 is SEK 0.11; forward P/E 19.95 therefore really rests on a sharp recovery in 2027. Tellus Fiber is consolidated from 4 May 2026, so TTM figures and history mix organic and acquired revenue; the company's own pro forma and organic figures have been used where they exist. EBITDA includes capitalised development (about 11% of revenue) as income, so EBIT and FCF after capitalised development have been used. Net cash at stockanalysis (10.2m) does not take into account the tax deferral of SEK 7.7m (due August 2027) and the additional purchase price of up to SEK 10m in 2027. The principal shareholders' and the board's total ownership have not been examined; management's ownership (about 500,000 shares, about 5.7%) is from December 2025.
