    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin
      Profit before tax −NOK 36m and net loss −NOK 28m TTM (profit margin −20%). Total production cost for 2026 is expected to be approx. NOK 81/kg, while the realised price in Q2 was NOK 62.4/kg; the H2025 generation is expected to cost NOK 86/kg (up to 98). Every harvested kilo thus makes a loss, and the weak operating result is propped up by fair-value adjustment of the biomass.
      ilaks.no
      −8
    • Moat
      The flow-through facility at Lille Indre Rosøy has not yet been proven profitable: only one generation has been harvested, the superior share was 72% and the economic feed conversion ratio was 1.31 in Q2. The salmon is sold at spot price with no documented premium, and the cost is above traditional sea farming.
      ilaks.no
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue (NOK 113m YTD) comes only from harvest volume and is very uneven: nothing was harvested in Q3, so the quarter had no operating revenue, and the result was driven by biological growth and fair value. There are as yet no economies of scale per kg.
      inderes.dk
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The construction budget was raised in 2024 by about NOK 350m to about NOK 995m, and FCF has been negative every year (−397, −505 and −391m NOK in 2023–2025, −NOK 342m TTM). ROIC is −0.2% and ROE −3.2%. In Q2 2026 a further approx. NOK 60m was invested, including in fish barriers, which did not work as intended.

    • Dilution
      The share count rose 31% (171.3 → 224.6m shares) after the issue in February–March 2026 of approx. NOK 382m at NOK 7.50. The price is now NOK 4.74, 37% below the issue price, and with negative FCF and a target of 16,000 t, further capital is likely.

    • Biology and execution
      New fish barriers caused crowding, oxygen deficiency, reduced appetite and mechanical injuries in the H2025 generation, and the company is considering removing them again in whole or in part. The base scenario for H2025 is now 5,800 t HOG (5,000–6,200 t) with lower slaughter weight and superior share, and the delay also hits the H2026 generation.

    • Guidance
      In May 2026 the harvest target for 2027 was lowered from 10,600 t to 8,000–8,500 t, and the EBIT cost price raised from 58 to NOK 60–65/kg, because late-released smolt (about 1m fish in November 2024) gave winter ulcers and high mortality.

      Mattilsynet has issued an order on fish welfare, and the company is in dialogue with both Mattilsynet (survival, welfare, environment in the tanks) and the County Governor (sludge, treatment and discharge to the sea). Tougher requirements may require further investment.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Operating profit at stockanalysis (operating margin −2.3%, EBITDA NOK 13.6m TTM) is inflated by fair value adjustments of the biomass; gross profit exceeded revenue in 2025. The underlying earnings have therefore been assessed on the company's own cost prices per kg. The Q3 report has only been read via the company's announcement (no harvest in the quarter), not the full PDF, so covenants, the exact shareholder list and the extent of the Norwegian Food Safety Authority's orders have not been reviewed. Gigante Havbruk's ownership share is derived from free float (65m of 225m shares) and not checked against the shareholder register. Only 3 analysts cover the stock. Salmon is cyclical, and food production fits best in 'Consumer: food, beverages & restaurants'. MFN gives Euronext Growth Oslo as the listing; here the release's trading venue has been used.
