    In plain words

    Gentian scores 64 out of 100: a small diagnostics company that abandoned an important development project. That cost a NOK 30.2m write-off, and sales in Asia fell 33% because of Chinese restrictions. On the plus side, it holds more cash than debt, and the dividend is covered by cash flow. Watch that 37% of sales go through one partner.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −14 of −30
    • Capital allocation and pipeline (NT-proBNP)
      The highest-priority pipeline project, the NT-proBNP test, was abandoned in May 2026 after several years of development, and the entire capitalised amount of kr 30.2m (approx. 5% of market cap) was written down. In December 2025 the company was still writing about 'solid progress' and a CE launch in Q4 2026; two months later the test failed verification. The next new product is a test for an unnamed partner with launch at the end of 2027 at the earliest.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −6
    • Margin
      Underlying EBIT margin is 14.7% TTM and the EBITDA margin 18% for January–September (kr 26.7m on kr 144.8m), far from the target of a 40% EBITDA margin. The gross margin of 56% YTD (61% in Q3, lifted by good production yields) is low for a reagent producer, and the target is only 55–60%. In 2025, kr 5.5m of R&D was capitalised, so the comparison with last year is only partly improved.
      gentian.wrep.it
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Reported growth is 11% YTD (17% in local currency) and 10.9% TTM vs 16% in 2025 and 23% a year in 2020–2025. Cystatin C, the largest product, is standing still YTD (NOK 50.2m vs 50.6m), and YTD sales include NOK 2.8m in milestone payments. Q3 was strong, however, with 22% reported and 30% currency-adjusted growth.
      gentian.wrep.it
      −2
    • Moat and future segment
      The business model is to convert known biomarkers into PETIA tests on other companies' analysers, so sales go through large IVD partners that own the customer relationship. GCAL was launched years ago, but according to the company commercial uptake is still only slow, and the pipeline (seven assays and one technology) is mainly in early stages.
      gentian.wrep.it
      −3
