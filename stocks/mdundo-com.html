    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −24 of −30
    • Margin
      EBIT was −DKK 3.9m on revenue of DKK 8.3m in 2025/26 (EBIT margin −47%) and net profit −3.8m. EBITDA of −1.4m is flattered by reversal of provisions from earlier years: adjusted, gross margin is 48.9% instead of 59.1%, equal to approx. DKK 0.8m, so the underlying EBITDA is approx. −2.2m. Guidance for 2026/27 is still EBITDA of −1.0 to 0.0m. Costs have been cut 42%, but there is no profit to measure ROIC on.
      mdundoforinvestors.com
      −8
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen three years in a row: kr 12.6 → 11.9 → 11.0 → 8.3m (−24.5% in 2025/26). Subscription revenue fell 18.5% and advertising revenue 51%. Telco payments are falling every quarter (9.9 → 8.1 → 6.9 → 5.7m from Q2 2025/26 to Q1 2026/27), and management calls the fall structural at several of the largest partners. Direct music subscriptions via mobile money and cards grew 25% in Q1, but only to approx. 9,300 transactions, and guidance for 2026/27 is kr 8–10m, i.e. flat at best.
      files.quartr.com
      −8
    • Moat and competition
      A company with 12 employees competing with Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music and regional services for African users with structurally low ARPU. Earnings rest on telco partners' billing, and the company does not control the terms itself. The blended ARPU for direct subscribers was below the target of USD 1.50 a month, because more African than diaspora subscribers paid the low price. Spotify opened an office in Johannesburg in 2026.
      mdundoforinvestors.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation and strategy shift
      The strategy has changed several times: from MAU growth (41.5m users) to paying subscribers, so music games were launched in Q1 2025/26 and expanded to five markets, and already deprioritised in Q1 2026/27 because of low revenue per user and few repeat payments. The issue proceeds are being spent on new apps, where the focus is not yet earnings. A strategic process with Deloitte Corporate Finance did not lead to a sale.
      files.quartr.com
      −3

      Operating cash flow was −kr 2.6m and capitalised development/intangible investments −kr 1.2m in 2025/26, i.e. FCF of −kr 3.8m (−kr 6.5m the year before). Guidance for cash at the end of 2026/27 is kr 6.5–7.5m vs 8.8m, i.e. a further kr 1.3–2.3m of burn.

      Subscription revenue of kr 7.1m of 8.3m (86%) comes mainly through eight telco partners. Only one larger partner grew in Q1 (+26%), while payments fell at the other large partners because unit economics do not support new customer acquisition. The company itself names dependence on the telco partners' strategy and invoicing as the first risk.

      Revenue guidance for 2025/26 of kr 9–11m (set 27/10-2025 and confirmed in February) was not met: 8.3m. At the share issue in April the message was that the company was approaching profitability and that the telco base was stable; in the annual report management wrote that the year deviated from expectations of improvement in telco and ads. EBITDA guidance was only hit because of reversed provisions.
      mdundoforinvestors.com
      −4

      Cash of kr 8.8m (30/6-2026) almost exactly matches trade payables of 9.0m (including royalties to rights holders), and current liabilities are 9.9m. Trade payables are larger than a full year's revenue, so free cash is far smaller than net cash appears.

The company itself cites currency and political risk as key risks.
