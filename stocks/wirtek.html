    In plain words

    The score is 50 out of 100. Wirtek is a small IT consultancy where sales fell 10.5% in 2025, and the small profit comes partly from public grants. Cash flow is negative, net debt has risen to DKK 11.2m, and the shares are very hard to trade. On the plus side, management and the board own 38.6% of the shares. Watch the debt, which rises further after a cash acquisition.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
