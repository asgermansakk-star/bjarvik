    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 28.3% to SEK 233.4m in January–September 2026 and 26.3% in Q3, after a 20% decline in 2025. It is now SEK 331m TTM vs SEK 550m in 2023. Consultant volume in Digitala lösningar (53% of sales) has fallen almost 30% in a year, and the number of employees and subcontracted consultants has fallen from approx. 450 in August 2025 to approx. 260.

    • Future segment
      DevPort sells hourly-based consulting work in product development, production development and IT. It has no IP of its own, and both hours and prices can be squeezed by AI. The company itself describes price pressure and greater cost awareness at several large framework agreement customers and fewer subcontractors because of the car customers' new procurement routines.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −13 of −35
    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow was SEK 13.3m TTM, but 42.3m came from released working capital, because receivables fell from 122 to 65m as revenue shrank. After lease repayments of 6.55m FCF is approx. 6.7m, and that does not cover the dividend of 10.1m. Without the working capital release, cash flow is negative.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Customer concentration and macro
      The automotive industry accounts for about 60% of revenue (the company's target is about 40% outside automotive, and it has just been reached). Heavy vehicles are the largest single market, and invoicing is concentrated in a few large framework agreements. Management itself points to the weak car market as the main reason for the decline.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Execution and management
      The CEO was replaced in August 2025 (Charlotta Dehn first acting), and the company has had repeated adjustments. In September 2026 18 people were given notice, and the restructuring cost SEK 9.1m in one-offs in Q3. The regional structure was abolished after the quarter, and the reported operating result was SEK −10.6m in Q3.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Governance
      The company has A and B shares. OstVast Capital Management became the largest owner in Q3 with 16.0% of the capital and 22.0% of the votes, after buying Carnegie's stake. A new dominant owner with an unclear strategy in a microcap where insiders own approx. 21%.
