    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Safran steers by adjusted figures (currency hedging and purchase price allocation stripped out). Reported net income swings wildly with the market value of currency derivatives (2025: 7.2 bn €, 2024: −0.7 bn €), so stockanalysis' trailing P/E of 35.8 and EPS decline of −46% are misleading; adjusted EPS H1 2026 was 4.63 €, and analysts' 2026E is about 10.4 €. Adjusted and reported figures are not the same. The share count has risen 1% in a year despite buybacks of about 1.5 bn € annually; the cause (convertible bond/employee shares) has not been examined in detail. Insider trading, US tariffs and the Collins Aerospace acquisition (integration) have not been investigated.
