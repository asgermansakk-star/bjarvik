    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small, illiquid First North stock without analyst coverage, P/E or forward estimate. All operations are in China (rooftop solar parks for C&I customers), and cash can only be moved to Sweden to a limited extent. stockanalysis shows 113.1m shares; after the set-off issue to the SOLT4 owners the figure is 130.0m, so market cap is about kr 93m. Balance-sheet figures are from 30/6-2026, i.e. before the issues, which according to the company reduce debt by about kr 80m. The Q3 report comes on 13/11-2026 and is not included. Lease debt of about kr 1.1bn is financing of the plants; repayments on it are part of the financing and are not deducted from FCF, so FCF after leasing is weaker than shown. The second written procedure on extension of SOLT4 starts on 9/11-2026, and the bondholders must waive their rights for the missing payment of principal; it is not a special situation under the rule, but the stock may become a restructuring case if the proposal fails. Insider trades beyond the issue commitments and customer concentration have not been examined (0 points). Flagged non-cyclical because the electricity is sold on contracts at tariff and not on the spot market, but the provinces' tariffs have just hit revenue.
