    The score is 70 out of 100. The retailer earns well and has more cash than debt, but the share is expensive: the price has risen from 72 to 536 kr. since 2023 and costs more relative to profit than at any point in recent years. Its profit on goods sold is historically high and could slip back. Watch the stronger Norwegian krone and the next report on 3 December.
