    Exploration company with no revenue; stockanalysis has only an overview page (no statistics, financials, ratios or forecast pages), so the figures are taken from the annual accounts to 28/2-2026 and the information document for the listing on Euronext Growth Oslo (26/10-2026). Customer, margin and ROIC criteria are not meaningful before production. The company was previously a SPAC, but now has projects and exploration and is therefore scored as an operating company.
