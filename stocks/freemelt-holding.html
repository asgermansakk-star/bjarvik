    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Freemelt makes 3D printers for metal (electron beam, E-PBF) and from 2026 also finished components. The closest on the industry list is 'Machinery & automation'. The stockanalysis figures run to Q2 2026 (TTM). The Q3 figures are the company's preliminary, unaudited figures from 2/11. The full report comes 16/11. Net result (TTM −SEK 97m) is dragged down by amortisation of goodwill from the 2021 reverse merger, about 47.5m a year over 5 years, which is almost finished. Margin has therefore been assessed on EBITDA and cash flow. The rights issue of SEK 151.8m at SEK 1.00 requires approval at an extraordinary general meeting on 20/11. It is not a special situation, but multiples and share count after the issue are estimates (TERP about SEK 1.14, about 379.5m shares). No analysts cover the stock on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist), so there are neither price targets nor forward estimates. Insiders' individual trades and the exact owner list have not been investigated. The same applies to patent strength against Colibrium Additive (GE/Arcam). Criteria not investigated give 0, so the grade is a ceiling.
