    • Margin

    • ROIC
      ROIC is 5.1% TTM vs WACC of 6.5% and 7.6% in 2021; ROE is 4.6%. The company's own return on operating capital before special items is 7.2% (6.7% in 2025), so Arjo does not earn its cost of capital.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has stood still at kr 11.0–11.3bn since 2023, and EPS has fallen from kr 2.72 (2021) to kr 1.33 TTM. Organic growth of 6.0% in Q3 and 4.8% year to date is better, but EPS fell in Q3 to kr 0.21 (0.30).

      −2

      Interest-bearing net debt kr 4.24bn (4.53bn incl. leasing), 2.2x adjusted EBITDA and interest cover 3.1. Altman Z 2.1 and quick ratio 0.81.
