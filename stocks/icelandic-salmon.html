    In plain words

    The score of 48 shows a salmon farmer that loses money but is starting to improve. Operating margin is minus 4.4%, debt has risen from EUR 46m to EUR 134m since 2021, and the expected harvest was cut to 20,000 tonnes. On the plus side, operations turned positive in the third quarter and a licence was renewed for 16 years. Only 7% is contracted, so salmon prices drive results.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company reports in EUR, while the share trades in NOK. stockanalysis' TTM figures only go to June 2026, so Q3 2026 (positive operating EBIT) is not included in their key figures. Only 2 analysts (price targets NOK 65–77) and one EPS estimate, which is negative for 2026. The share is extremely illiquid: approx. 300 shares a day on average, and the 20 largest own 96.7%. stockanalysis shows +3.66% in share count, but the balance sheet and the half-year report show an unchanged 30.96m shares since 2021, so no points were given for dilution. Covenant waivers from September 2025 and new requirements (ICR ≥ 2.5 and NIBD/EBITDA ≤ 5.0 from 30/9-2026) are not commented on in the Q3 presentation; status is unexamined. Insider trades in 2026 have not been examined. The new Icelandic aquaculture law has not been passed, and the licence in Ísafjarðardjúp has not been reissued.
