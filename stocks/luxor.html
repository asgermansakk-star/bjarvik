    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company (self-managed AIF) that mainly owns property credit loans (mortgage deeds) and four rental properties: price to NAV is the main multiple. The score applies to the B share (LUXOR B). stockanalysis calculates market cap (730m) on all 1.0m shares including the unlisted A shares at the B price; NAV of kr 347.72 per share is the company's own figure at 30/6-2026. The core earnings multiple is based on the company's guidance for base earnings (approx. 45m before tax) with 22% tax; there is no forward estimate and no analysts (stockanalysis forecast = 404), so PEG and upside are empty. The 2025/26 annual report (financial year to 30/9) will not be published until 17/12-2026 and is not included. Insider trades in 2025–2026 have not been examined (Nasdaq's announcement archive could not be retrieved, and web search was used up), and Danish media have not been searched. P/NAV for 2016–2020 is calculated from the 2019/20 annual report (https://luxor.blob.core.windows.net/aarsrapporter/16122020Luxor%2030.%20september%202020.pdf). Credit quality has been assessed only from the company's own figures (losses, recoveries, credit adjustment); loan-to-value ratios and ranking of the mortgage deeds have not been examined. How much revenue disappears with the Danish FSA's order to stop administration for third parties is not stated (the 'Other income' item was 1.3m in 2024/25). The share is extremely illiquid.
