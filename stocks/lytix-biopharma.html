    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Clinical biotech without revenue: P/E, PEG, ROIC and FCF multiples are not meaningful; the price is assessed on P/B and EV vs own history and Nordic biotech peers. The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main Oslo Børs list). The latest accounts are H1 2026 (30/6); the Q3 report comes on 13/11-2026, so cash at 30/9 is an estimate based on spending of NOK 28.8m per quarter. The NeoLIPA result (3/11) has only been announced as 'primary endpoint met' (pCR ≥ 40% in the evaluable population to date); the exact pCR rate has not been published. Only 2 analysts with price targets (NOK 14–16), and an upside of approx. 90% should be read with great caution. Insider ownership (4.5%) is from stockanalysis; the largest owners are Norwegian family holding companies, and whether any of them are close to the board has not been examined. The option grant in July 2026 has not been reviewed in detail.
