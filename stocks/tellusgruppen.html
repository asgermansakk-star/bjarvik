    • Quality of growth
      Growth is bought: in Q3 organic growth in total income was 0.1% vs 5.8% from acquisitions. Occupancy in the preschools fell to 73.5% (75.8%), comparable units have fewer children, and management calls the falling birth cohorts the industry's biggest challenge. The preschools' EBITDA margin fell in Q3 to 26.3% from 31.4%, and Omnipro went from +SEK 3.9m to −SEK 0.2m in EBITDA for nine months.

    • Pricing power
      Revenue is mainly childcare and school fees, which the municipalities set. Wages are adjusted from September, while school fees are only adjusted at the turn of the year, so the company cannot itself pass on cost increases.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −17 of −35
    • Regulation and politics
      All revenue depends on Swedish rules for free schools and municipal funding. New rules for free schools (incl. the principle of public access and accounting per school unit) take effect in 2027, the government question after the election in September was unresolved at the report, and a new government may bring more changes. The company has itself structured the additional purchase price in the Sandviken acquisition to take account of regulatory risk in the funding of free schools.
      storage.mfn.se
      −6
    • Governance and related parties
      CEO Bijan Fahimi and Narges Moshiri own 58.9% via Bina Holding. In August Elnaz Madani, who is close to the largest shareholder, was hired as Legal & Regulatory Lead in group management and at the same time remains on the board. The group also owes SEK 4.2m to the main shareholder.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Debt and liquidity
      Interest-bearing net debt incl. leasing is SEK 319m vs a market value of SEK 120m and equity of SEK 62m (equity ratio 13.4%). Current ratio is 0.40, the bank loan costs 9.5% interest, and net debt rises with the Sandviken acquisition (SEK 17.35m plus up to SEK 17.35m in earn-out). Net debt excluding IFRS 16 is, however, only 1.0x adjusted EBITDA.
      mfn.se

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The industry list has no education category; Tellusgruppen is placed under 'Health insurance & services' like AcadeMedia and the other Swedish welfare companies with public payers. The company switched to IFRS in 2026, so stockanalysis' history (P/B 102, ROE and ROIC before 2026, debt of SEK 33m in 2025) is K3 figures without leases and cannot be compared with the current figures; the company's own IFRS figures from the Q3 report (5/11-2026, unaudited) have been used. stockanalysis still shows net income of SEK −3.1m TTM, while the company's own LTM result is +SEK 3.6m. FCF is measured after lease payments (repayments and interest) and investments: about SEK 22m LTM and about SEK 29m in 2025. No analysts cover the stock, there is no forward estimate, and the company gives no forecasts. Trading is very thin (about 2,400 shares per day). Sandvikens Friskola (taken over 1/10) is not included in the figures; its purchase price allocation only comes in the annual report. The new government's line towards free schools after the election in September 2026 has not been verified. Customer concentration per municipality has not been examined (0 points).
