    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Petrolia SE (Cyprus) reports in USD and only half-yearly; the latest figures are H1 2026. No analyst coverage, so there is no forward estimate, price target or PEG. Stockanalysis's TTM operating profit (USD 1.7m) differs from the company's own figures (approx. USD 3.0m TTM: 4.5 in 2025 − 3.4 in H1 2025 + 1.9 in H1 2026); the findings are based on the company's figures. Trailing P/E 7.0 is based on net income in which the associate and currency gains make up more than half of pre-tax profit. The value of the 49.9% stake in Petrolia NOCO is set at the NOTC price (USD 19.1m), but NOTC is a thin marketplace for unlisted shares, and PNO's debt and investment needs (Dugong, Talisker) have not been examined in detail. Stockanalysis shows insider ownership of 0.34%, but chair Berge Gerdt Larsen in reality controls approx. 85% of the shares via the IOR group. Peers have not been compared systematically; insider trades after April 2026 have not been examined beyond the Newsweb list. Extraordinary general meeting on 11/11-2026 on a distribution of USD 0.02 per share.
