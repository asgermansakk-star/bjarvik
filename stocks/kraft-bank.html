    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −10 of −30
    • Profitability
      ROE after tax was 12.7% in Q3 and 12.8% in the first nine months of the year vs 13.5% for 2025, and the bank still does not reach its own target of 15%. The interest margin fell from 3.7% to 3.1% in a year, and cost/income rose from 45% to 49% (54% in Q2) for a bank with only 41 employees.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Net lending grew only 2.3% over 12 months and fell kr 103m in Q3, where new sales fell to kr 373m (456) and repayments rose to kr 477m (414). The earnings lift in 2025 (net result kr 27.6m → 65.0m) came mainly from loans with a three-year rate guarantee being repriced, and that effect is largely used up (12% of lending now has a rate guarantee).
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −3
    • Business model and moat
      Niche bank for homeowners with payment remarks, where the aim is for customers to refinance back to a regular bank (75% do); the business therefore has built-in customer churn and constantly has to refill the book via its own and external loan brokers. The bank itself writes that competition is high despite few players, and deposits are price-sensitive (interest of 4.49–4.70%).
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −12 of −35
    • Credit quality
      Non-performing loans over 90 days amount to kr 579m or about 15% of gross lending (kr 524m a year earlier) with a provision coverage of only 12%. Individual provisions rose from 47 to kr 77m in a year, and losses rose 39% to kr 25.7m in the first nine months of the year, particularly because realised collateral values on recovery were lower than expected – which raises doubt about the collateral values behind the rest of the non-performing book.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −5
    • Capital
      Total capital ratio was 24.7% vs a requirement of 23.7% incl. management buffer – only 1.0 percentage point (27.2% counting the year's profit) – because the Pillar 2 requirement is high (4.7%). The bank's own capital plan mentions issuing a hybrid Tier 1 bond, reduced growth and 'additional equity', which contradicts a dividend policy of up to 100% of profit. The unweighted Tier 1 ratio fell from 10.4% to 8.4% in 2026.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −3
    • Execution and guidance
      Lending growth in Q3 was below the bank's own expectation, and the ROE target of 15% was again not reached because of loss provisions. The target of lending of kr 3.9bn at the turn of the year requires about kr 164m (4.4%) of net growth in Q4 after a decline in Q3.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −2
    • Governance, insiders and liquidity
      Insiders own only 2.7%, and an associate of board member Bjørn Maaseide sold his entire stake of 132,400 shares at kr 12.34 in September 2025; there have been no insider purchases since. The stock trades on Euronext Growth with 757 shareholders and average turnover of about 13,000 shares (about kr 0.16m) a day.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (stockanalysis' FCF of NOK 323m is driven by deposit growth); valuation on P/TBV, P/B and P/E, and dividend is measured against net profit. The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list), which means lighter disclosure requirements and very low liquidity. stockanalysis' financials only go to Q2 2026 and show the wrong next report date (9/11); the Q3 report came out on 27/10-2026 and is used here. The bank has no AT1 capital, so P/B and P/TBV are calculated on shareholders' equity (NOK 502.6m, TBV approx. NOK 11.64 per share). stockanalysis has no forecast page and no price targets, so analyst coverage has not been examined; P/E on guidance is based on the bank's own target of 15% ROE. Insider trades have only been examined via Newsweb since June 2025 (one reportable trade). The change of external auditor at the extraordinary general meeting in June 2026 has not been examined further (0 points). Peers are only Instabank, Lea Bank and Morrow Bank, which are all unsecured consumer loan banks and not directly comparable.
