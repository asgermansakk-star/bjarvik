The main owners did take up their share of the latest issue. Watch the takeover of Aura Light, about four times larger, and a new CEO starting 30 November.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −23 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin has been negative every year since 2023 (−2.6%, −11.1% and −5.7%) and is −2.9% TTM. In Q2 2026, operating result was −SEK 1.1m and EBITDA −SEK 0.3m; in H1, EBITDA margin was only 1.7%. The acquired Aura Light had an operating result of approx. −SEK 17m in 2025, so the new group is also loss-making pro forma on an annual basis.
      stockanalysis.com
      −8
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 48.5% in H1 2026, but the growth was primarily bought (MaSeTek in 2025) or comes from the newly started Priolight. Vadsbo LightTech, the largest subsidiary, had lower revenue in Q2 than a year earlier. No organic growth is disclosed. Aura Light's revenue has fallen for several years because of phased-out products.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has risen from 18.2m (2024) to 45.4m (rights issue at SEK 0.70 in 2025) and on to 77.3m (issue of SEK 22.6m in August 2026), i.e. 4.2 times in approx. 15 months. Equity per share has fallen from SEK 1.26 (2022) to SEK 0.62, and goodwill from acquisitions is SEK 21.5m of equity of SEK 30m (end 2025).
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Scale and moat
      With 27 employees and SEK 76m in revenue (before Aura Light), Lightning Group is a small player in a competitive lighting market with Fagerhult, Zumtobel and Signify. The gross margin of 49.5% is reasonable, but the niches (wireless lighting control, LED profiles, suspension) do not provide a documented moat.

    • Cash flow and funding
      FCF has been negative every year in 2021–2025 and is −SEK 2.1m TTM, partly due to inventory build-up (inventory is SEK 15.4m, or 20% of revenue). Net debt was SEK 10m at 30/6 and debt/EBITDA 32. Of the issue's SEK 22.6m, 20m went directly to Aura Light as a shareholder contribution, so the parent company's balance sheet is only slightly strengthened. Capital was raised in 2022, 2025 and 2026.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Execution and integration
      Aura Light is approx. 4 times the size of Lightning Group and has approx. 120 employees. The company's managing director left three weeks after the takeover, and the group CEO was acting managing director until a new one starts 30/11-2026. The turnaround plan depends on synergies in purchasing and product structure, and the sellers receive 60% of Aura Light's EBITDA for three years as additional purchase consideration.
