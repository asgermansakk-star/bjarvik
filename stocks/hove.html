    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap on First North (market cap about DKK 178m) with no analyst coverage: no forward P/E, PEG or price target, so the formula points rest on missing data. The Q3 report comes on 16/11-2026 and is not included; the scoring is based on H1 2026 (unaudited figures) and the 2025 annual report. stockanalysis shows −2.7% in share count, but the share count rose from 24.65m to 25.28m in 2025 through exercise of warrants; the figure has not been used. stockanalysis' gross margin (26.6%) uses a different definition from the company's (32.6%); the company's own figures are used. Customer concentration (share of revenue from the largest wind turbine manufacturers) is not disclosed and has therefore only been assessed qualitatively. Whether the board's warrant authorisation (expired 31/5-2026) has been renewed has not been examined. Web search was exhausted during this run, so the research is based on the company's own reports, Nasdaq announcements and stockanalysis; Danish media and forums have not been reviewed.
