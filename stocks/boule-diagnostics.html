    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is in the middle of a transition: the instrument business is being wound down (announced 4/11-2026), and a fully underwritten rights issue of approx. SEK 93m (3 new shares per share at SEK 0.80) plus a directed over-allotment option of up to approx. SEK 63m must be approved at an extraordinary general meeting on 7/12-2026. The share count rises from 38.8m to 155m and up to approx. 234m, so stockanalysis' forward P/E (11.8), P/B and P/FCF are based on the old share count and have not been used as a basis. The price of SEK 1.22 trades with subscription rights (last day 7/12) and is above the subscription price. Net debt is SEK 203m according to stockanalysis (incl. owner loans and leasing), while the company itself reports 79m; the difference has not been reconciled. The company's pro forma figures without instruments (adjusted EBIT approx. SEK 86m) are an illustration, not a forecast, and have not been used in the multiples. Customer concentration in OEM CDS has not been examined (0 points). No analyst coverage on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist).
