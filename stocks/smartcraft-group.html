    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    SmartCraft moved from Oslo Børs to Nasdaq Stockholm in March–April 2026 via a cross-border merger in which SmartCraft Group AB took over SmartCraft ASA. It was a redomiciliation within the same group, not a combination of two businesses, so the financial history is used as continuous. stockanalysis has no 52-week price change and no 200-day average for the Swedish listing, and the price history from Oslo is in NOK, so the comparison with its own multiple history is only approximate. stockanalysis' TTM figures run through Q2; FCF after leases and capitalised development (approx. kr 108m) is calculated with Q3 from the company's own report. Forward P/E is based on adjusted EPS (0.81) without amortisation of acquired intangible assets; reported EPS is approx. 0.58 TTM. Only 4 analysts cover the stock. It has not been investigated whether Valedo has sold down since the listing, nor the price of earlier buybacks.
