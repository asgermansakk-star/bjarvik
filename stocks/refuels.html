    • Margin and ROIC
      ReFuels itself is loss-making (operating profit −GBP 0.32m in Q1, net loss −GBP 37.7m in FY2026) and ROIC is −0.8%. In CNG Fuels the adjusted EBITDA margin is only about 10% (4.9m on GBP 47.9m), and profit before tax was GBP 0.3m because interest on shareholder loans eats up the operating earnings.

    • Quality of growth
      The certificate trade (RTFS) accounted for GBP 4.3m of GBP 4.9m adjusted EBITDA in Q1 (about 88%), while the station network contributed only GBP 0.6m and has only now had two quarters of positive EBITDA. That sets a time limit for new CNG lorries, unless the rules become technology-neutral, which is only an opposition proposal.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −25 of −35
    • Capital structure after the Foresight deal
      In the deal with Foresight in April 2025, Foresight received GBP 150.15m and ReFuels GBP 15.95m in shareholder loans at 10% interest, which rank ahead of ReFuels' 40% of the equity. Interest of approx. GBP 16.6m a year corresponds to almost the whole EBITDA guidance, so equity gets nothing distributed for years. The deal also cost an accounting loss of GBP 34.1m in the group and GBP 71.6m in the parent company.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Certificate prices (macro)
      The RTFC spot price fell to 16.9 pence in September from 19.1 in August, because higher diesel prices after the closure of the Strait of Hormuz narrow the spread to biodiesel. Q1's realised price of 26.1 pence is due to forward sales for 2026. The company itself warns that a narrower spread could hit earnings in the coming years.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Financing need in the parent company
      ReFuels N.V. had only GBP 0.15m in cash at 30/6-2026, an operating cash flow of −GBP 0.56m in the quarter and negative working capital of −GBP 1.2m. Operations are financed with loans from the associate CNG Fuels (GBP 1.0m). Without distributions from CNG Fuels there is a risk of more debt to associates or dilution.

      CNG Fuels guides 15–20% growth in volume sold in FY2027, but Q1 gave only +8% and September +8%. The company itself writes that the diesel crisis is delaying hauliers' investments in new trucks, and that registrations have fallen this year.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    ReFuels N.V. has been a holding company since April 2025, owning 40% of CNG Fuels (Foresight Group owns the rest) and itself having only administrative costs, management fees and interest on a preference loan. The operating figures in the findings are CNG Fuels' own pro forma figures from the Q1 FY2027 report (April-June 2026), not ReFuels' group figures. Stockanalysis' P/FCF of 10.2 and FCF of NOK 80.8m are distorted by figures from before the deconsolidation; the parent company's own operating cash flow was −GBP 0.6m in Q1, so no FCF figures from there have been used. The implied EV for CNG Fuels is calculated on the market value (approx. GBP 62m at 13.1 NOK/GBP) and depends on whether the shareholder loans are taken at book value (GBP 103m) or nominal value (GBP 166m plus accrued 10% interest); a range is therefore given. The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo, not the main list. No analysts on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist), so there are no price targets or EPS estimates. Q2 FY2027 is due 27/11-2026. Not examined: customer concentration in detail beyond 180 fleets and 8 of the 10 largest supermarkets, and the terms of the return mechanism of up to 55%.
