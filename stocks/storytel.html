    In plain words

    Storytel scores 62 out of 100. The core business grows only 7.8%, the price per subscriber is flat, and competition from Spotify and others is growing. Profit is lifted by a tax income of about 170m kr., so the share looks cheaper than it is. On the plus side, operations have moved from loss to profit, and the outlook for the year was raised.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −13 of −30
    • Margin
      EBIT margin 11.9% and adjusted EBITDA margin 19.9% (R12M) are a clear improvement from losses in 2021–2023, but the gross margin is only about 46%, because royalties to publishers and authors follow listening. Streaming's EBITDA margin fell in Q3 to 15.1% (16.9%) due to higher marketing.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      The core business Streaming grew only 7.8% at constant exchange rates in Q3 and in 9M; Americas fell 1.5% and APAC grew 3.7% due to lower ARPU. ARPU for 9M was 116 kr (118). Group growth of 16.5% is lifted by the acquisition of Overamstel (Publishing +54%, of which 27% organic).
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat
      The catalogue is largely non-exclusive, and the price per subscriber is standing still (ARPU 119.6 vs 118.1 kr); subscriber growth is bought with campaigns and more marketing. Own publishers (Norstedts, Rabén & Sjögren, Gummerus etc.) give a certain content advantage.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The expansion in 2021–2023 was financed with share issues of kr 1.56bn at far higher prices (kr 160 at end-2021) and ended in write-downs of kr 579m and a loss of 819m in 2023. The new course (profitability, smaller publisher acquisitions) is only about three years old.

Only 5 analysts, and the median target (SEK 125) is below the price after +44% in 52 weeks. In 2026 the stock moved from First North to the Nasdaq Stockholm main list.
