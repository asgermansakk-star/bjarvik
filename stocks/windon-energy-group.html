    In plain words

    The score is 37 out of 100: the model found several serious problems. The company has shifted from installer to solar-park owner, paid for with 151m new shares that diluted old owners by about 89%. Revenue has fallen three years in a row, and operations lose money. The solar parks were bought debt-free. A combined financial statement is still missing, and the promised cash balance is unconfirmed.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −25 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin was −78% (TTM) and −67% in 2025 (EBIT −SEK 11.7m on revenue of 17.4m). Gross margin fell in Q2 2026 to 8% vs 25% the year before, according to management due to one-off costs on earlier projects. ROIC was −38%. In 2023, the company had an operating margin of 9%.
      mfn.se
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen three years in a row, from SEK 76.0m in 2023 to 22.4m in 2024, 17.4m in 2025 and 15.2m TTM (−36%). Battery orders of over SEK 12m in Q1 were less than 30% invoiced. All future growth comes from the share-financed acquired portfolio, not from organic growth.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      The company has switched from EPC contractor to owner of market-priced solar power. The 22.19 MW portfolio was bought from the largest EPC customer (Solid Vind & Sol) and the financing companies from Opportun Capital Partners for 151.1m new shares at SEK 1.00. That dilutes the old shareholders by approx. 89%. The parent company wrote down SEK 17.8m on the shares in the subsidiaries at the same time. The promised net cash of approx. SEK 12m is not documented after the takeover.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat
      The company is a small installer of solar and batteries with 5 employees in a market where 'many companies have disappeared'. The portfolio sells power at spot price with no PPA, and production-weighted prices in SE3/SE4 were only 39–49 øre/kWh in 2023–2025 because of cannibalisation in summer. The company has no pricing power.
      mfn.se
      −4

    • Price vs earnings
      The market cap is about SEK 78m (172.35m shares at SEK 0.45), and EV is about SEK 65m if the net cash of 12m holds. Earnings cannot carry it yet. The portfolio's indicative revenue is about SEK 9.5m a year at 45 øre/kWh (before operation and maintenance), and the EPC part lost SEK 7.6m at EBITA in 2025. That gives a pro forma EBITDA around zero and an EV/revenue for the portfolio of about 7x.

      P/B looks low, about 0.4 pro forma, but equity rests on a contribution-in-kind value that the parties themselves set at SEK 1.00 per share, while the share price was SEK 0.58–0.74 on the takeover day. In addition there is SEK 26m in goodwill from the old Windon. P/TBV was 3.7 before the deal. The discount to book value is therefore not a safe margin.

    • Cash flow and liquidity

    • Dilution and weak investor appetite
      The rights issue in January 2026 at SEK 1 was subscribed only 29%. The underwriters brought it up to 50%, so the company received SEK 6.1m before costs, and the share count rose 50%. On top of that come a convertible loan of SEK 3m and up to 9.12m new shares from TO 2. No warrants were exercised in September because the price was below SEK 1.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Governance and conflicts of interest
      The sellers are Windon's largest EPC customer and its owners. After the deal, Björnås Invest, J. Losell Holding and Ekelöf Group each own approx. 7%, and the old major shareholders have fallen to under 1%. The adviser Opportun Capital Partners was paid SEK 3m in new shares. The 22 SPV companies have no employees and are run via service agreements with related-party suppliers.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Macro: electricity price
      Revenue from the portfolio swings with the spot price in SE3 and SE4. According to the company's own table, it would have been SEK 8.2m in 2024 and 30.8m in 2022. Approx. 80% of production falls in April–September, when prices are lowest. Management itself calls the timing 'near a cyclical bottom', but there is no price hedging.
      mfn.se

After the acquisition these orders become internal, so they no longer give external revenue. According to the annual report, the framework agreement with Eljon for 30 MW a year has given only limited revenue and depends on Eljon's financing.
