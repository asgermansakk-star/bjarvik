    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The equity certificate fraction is 51.0%, the rest is self-owned. It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.44 and book value of NOK 221.81 per share cover total equity including self-owned capital and perpetual bonds and have not been used. T2 is based on the bank's own book equity per certificate (NOK 113.5 at 30/6-2026) and earnings per certificate. Goodwill is only approx. NOK 40m, so P/TBV is close to P/B. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are H1 2026; the Q3 report is due on 10/11-2026 and is not included. The merger with Ørland Sparebank has been approved by both general meetings and cleared by Konkurransetilsynet on 30/10-2026, but still lacks Finanstilsynet's approval; completion is expected around 4/5-2027. Trøndelag Sparebank is the acquiring bank and issues 2,432,948 new certificates (from 4.78m to 7.21m), so the price does not follow the deal terms, and there is no special situation. The exchange ratio (60/40 on value-adjusted equity, new certificates at NOK 110.22) is calculated to be roughly neutral for current certificate holders and has not been scored; the integration costs in 2027 are unknown. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 17.75 has an uncertain basis and has not been used. Analyst coverage is effectively non-existent. Single exposures and the sector breakdown of the corporate book (e.g. fish farming) have not been examined in detail. Insider trades have only been read from the stock exchange announcements since 2025.
