    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company reports only half-yearly, and the H1 2026 accounts are unaudited. Almost all the value lies in Jetcarrier AS, which ABTEC owns 100% of but does not consolidate (no control while the vendor credit runs). Jetcarrier is carried at a level 3 DCF value of NOK 117.4m and accounts under Norwegian GAAP. The look-through multiples are own calculations from the H1 report and the announcement of the 2025 figures (EBIT NOK 7m). The outcome of the claims of NOK 75m (directors' liability insurance) and NOK 64.1m (former auditor) is unknown and not included; any compensation from the insurance is shared 50/50 with the Jetcarrier sellers. No analysts and no estimates. Insider trades after June 2026 have not been examined. Stockanalysis' share count (14.97m) lags the registered one (15.62m as of 25/8-2026).
