    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −14 of −35
    • Acquisitions, debt and possible share issue
      The company's largest acquisition ever (Castellum's 95 properties in Skåne for kr 13.3bn) is financed with bank loans and lifts the loan-to-value ratio from 52.5% to approx. 59%, close to the limit of 60%. The target of 55% is not reached until the end of 2028. Even before the purchase, net debt/EBITDA was 10.6, interest cover 2.9x, the average interest rate rose to 3.45% (3.29), the fixed-rate period is only 2.5 years, and kr 7.5bn falls due in the rest of 2026 and kr 8.8bn in 2027. Management itself mentions a new share issue as an option, which at 0.71x NRV would dilute.
      mfn.se
      −9
    • Letting
      EPRA vacancy rose to 11.2% from 9.3%, and net letting was SEK −57m in 9M (+65) and SEK −27m in Q3 after a late termination in Denmark. New leases fell to SEK 179m (300). The acquired portfolio has only 83–85% occupancy and 3.6 years' average remaining term.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow fell 18% to kr 1,394m in 9M (kr 4.53 per share vs 5.50), driven by working capital. Investments in properties of approx. kr 2bn and a dividend of kr 1,015m are partly loan-financed.
      mb.cision.com
      −1

      Insiders own only 0.09% of the shares. Insider trades are mixed: the CEO bought 10,000 shares at DKK 78.92 in July, but an associate of a management member has sold 61,900 shares since July, most recently at DKK 68–69 in October–November.
