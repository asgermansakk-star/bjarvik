    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The net cash (adjusted net receivable DKK 11.6bn) rests on negative working capital and customer advances (free working capital in Construction DKK 37.0bn) and is therefore not given as a Plus. TTM cash flow is lifted by working capital, so FCF is assessed on the 2021–2025 average. Trailing EPS is pulled down by write-downs in Project Development and up by property gains. Only 9 analysts; the price targets are from July–October 2026, before the Q3 report. Not investigated: pending contract disputes and specific project losses in 2026, credit rating and the voting share of the major shareholders (the A shares); they give 0 points. The insider trades in October are small purchases via a share savings plan (9–516 shares) and are not counted.
