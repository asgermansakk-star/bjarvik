    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −20 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin is −16.7% (TTM), and the net loss is −SEK 81m. The company has posted a net loss every year since 2021. Adjusted EBITDA was −9.1m for the first nine months of the year (+12.1 a year earlier) and only about 9m TTM, equal to about 2% of net revenue. Without the goodwill amortisation, adjusted EBIT was −27.2m (−2.7) in 9M.

    • Quality of growth
      GMV grew 18% in Q3, but gross profit after marketing (GPAM) fell 10% (−4% in 9M), and the GPAM margin fell from 10.8% to 8.3%. Take rate fell 1.2 percentage points to 18.1%, even though the company's own financial target is for it to rise over time. Marketing grew 36%, and net revenue only 3%. Growth costs more than it gives.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      Fyndiq was bought in 2023 for new shares: the share count rose from 6.4 to 10.75m (+67%), and the purchase gave about SEK 620m in goodwill. Three years later the group still has no profit at EBIT level before goodwill. Equity has fallen from 687 to 484m, and tangible equity is negative (−12m).

      −3
    • Moat
      CDON is a Nordic marketplace without clear pricing power against Amazon, Temu, Shein and the big specialist retailers. Organic traffic is falling, so the company becomes more dependent on paid channels such as Google Shopping. Large European retailers are entering on introductory terms and in low-commission categories, which squeezes the take rate.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    CDON is loss-making, so P/E, forward P/E and PEG are n/a. Only 1–2 analysts cover the stock, and the price target (75.67) is based on very few targets. The accounts follow K3, so goodwill from the Fyndiq acquisition is amortised by about SEK 16.7m in the quarter. The amortisation is non-cash and is included in the net loss, while the margin finding is based on EBITDA. Cash of 137.9m is adjusted for 163.5m of debt to retailers, and stockanalysis' FCF (75.6m, P/FCF 7.6) is not used because it rests on working capital. There is no lease debt on the balance sheet (K3), so rent is in operating costs. Total insider ownership and the full ownership list have not been examined; only the nomination committee (Nantahala, Kanen Wealth, Bisslinge/Bonnier) has been used, so that criterion scores 0. Peers such as Vend, Allegro and Zalando have not been compared with figures. The effect of the brand campaign and the product data service from September is too new to measure. Liquidity is very low: about 3,000 shares a day.
