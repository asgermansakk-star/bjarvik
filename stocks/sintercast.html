    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −13 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is kr 113m (TTM) vs kr 107m in 2021, and EPS has fallen from 4.64 to kr 3.83. The company has not grown in five years; 2025 gave −20% in revenue, and Q3 2026 (+46%) is a recovery from a low level.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Future segment
      Revenue comes from CGI engine blocks for diesel trucks and cars, a segment under long-term pressure from electrification. Power generation and new 13-litre truck programmes cushion this, but the core is the combustion engine.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The dividend has for several years been above profit (payout 200% in 2025, 117% TTM) and has now been cut 57% from kr 7 to 3. At the same time the new management is seeking acquisitions outside the core business, which is a new and untested risk for a company with 23 employees.

      −3

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −13 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      Brazil (kr 48.8m) and Mexico (kr 29.0m) accounted for 72% of revenue in 2025, and the fall in Brazil from kr 73.6m to 48.8m drove most of the revenue loss. Revenue depends on a few large foundries and their truck customers.

      −5
    • Execution
      The milestone of 5m Engine Equivalents has been pushed from 2026 to 2027, because MAN extended an older engine generation, FAW postponed a new engine family and EPA 2027 pre-buying lapsed. Volume is governed by the OEMs' decisions.

      FCF after leasing was DKK 33.1m in 2025 vs dividends of DKK 49.3m, and 27.6m TTM vs 31.6m. The distribution is partly financed by cash, which fell from DKK 23.1m to 5.2m in 2025.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small company (23 employees, market cap about kr 790m) with very low liquidity. stockanalysis has no forecast page; forward P/E 19.4 is presumably based on 1–2 analysts (ABG/Analysguiden). Cash at 30/9 (kr 24.6m) must cover the rest of the dividend (kr 21.1m in total, in two instalments), and the tax expense is mainly use of a deferred tax asset, so tax paid is close to zero. The customer behind the Brazil/Mexico geography is not named in the sources read; concentration is measured on revenue by country. Insider trades (the chairman's purchase in March 2026 in aggregator data) have not been verified in FI's register and give 0. Peer comparison is rough, as there are no listed pure CGI licence companies.
