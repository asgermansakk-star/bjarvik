    • Margin

Gross margin fell from 44.7% to 42.5%, because distributed partner games are growing fastest.

      −3
    • ROIC
      ROIC 4.8% TTM (3.7% in FY25/26) and ROE 2.4%; goodwill and publishing rights make up EUR 2.39bn, more than all of equity, so tangible equity is negative.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      The 23% growth in FY25/26 came from partner-published games (+40%, especially distributed trading card games such as Pokémon), while own studios fell 5.8%; in Q1 26/27 own studios grew 1.8% and were roughly flat organically. The company is getting bigger as a distributor, not stronger as a publisher.

      −5

      Acquisition-driven: ATM Gaming at EUR 205.6m, of which 190.6m is goodwill, and the last 45% of Exploding Kittens at USD 173.8m in the same quarter. At the same time a number of IPs in the back catalogue were written down by EUR 23.6m in Q4.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −17 of −35
    • Customer and partner concentration
      Over half of sales come from five product lines, including Magic and Pokémon (the company's own risk description at the listing). In Q1 26/27, collectible card games accounted for 67% and partner-published games for 78% of sales; the partners can choose other distributors.
      boardgamewire.com
      −6
    • The peak of the Pokémon cycle
      Trading card games grew 23% in Q1 on top of +34% organic the year before, and Pokémon's 30th anniversary is pulling demand forward. Placera's estimates show EPS of EUR 0.86 in 26/27, but 0.84 in 27/28, i.e. stagnation after the boom.
      placera.se
      −3
    • Tariff rates and the US consumer
      Asmodee has had to raise prices to compensate for tariffs and faces a weaker US consumer who, according to the CEO, is choosing cheaper products; production for the US is partly imported.
      placera.se
      −2
    • Governance
      Dual share class: chairman Lars Wingefors has approx. 17% of the capital but approx. 38% of the votes via A shares, and his Embracer history of debt-financed acquisitions and large write-downs is relevant to the acquisition strategy. He sold 4.2m shares in August 2025.
      placera.se
      −3
    • Debt
      Net debt EUR 534.6m after M&A liabilities (1.7x EBITDA) and bonds of EUR 631m; cash fell from 437 to EUR 197m after the acquisitions. Rating only BB/Ba3, and Altman Z-score 1.41.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Dilution from earn-outs
      In addition to the 3.03m new B shares for ATM Gaming, earn-out and share-based remuneration may trigger up to 7.07m more B shares (about 3%) through 2031.
      storage.mfn.se
      −1
