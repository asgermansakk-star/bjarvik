    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Trifork Group AG is a Swiss company listed in Copenhagen and reports in EUR; stockanalysis' financial pages are in EUR, while multiples and share price are in DKK. Forward P/E and EPS estimates are based on only 2 analysts (4 price targets), so PEG, upside and revisions are uncertain. The company's net interest-bearing debt (EUR 20.3m) is excluding leasing; stockanalysis' net debt (EUR 83m) includes leasing of approx. EUR 52m. The Labs portfolio (book value EUR 57.2m, approx. 23% of market cap) is valued by management itself and has not been examined company by company. Web search was used up in this run, so the analysis is based on stockanalysis, the Q3 report, the 2025 annual report and company announcements from investor.trifork.com; Danish media and analyst comments have not been read, and the 2024 guidance history has not been examined (scores 0).
