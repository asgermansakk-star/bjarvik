    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Figures up to and including Q3 2026 (report 22/10-2026). The forest and paper industry has no industry of its own on the list; like UPM, Stora Enso, Holmen and SCA, Billerud is set to 'Raw materials & uranium' and marked cyclical (cartonboard, paper and pulp follow price cycles). Billerud does not own forest, so the low P/B is not covered by forest values as at Holmen and SCA. Lease payments are not disclosed separately in the Q3 report; FCF after leases is therefore stated only as lower than SEK 291m (lease debt SEK 569m). Customer concentration has not been examined (liquid packaging board, approx. 21% of sales, goes to a few large packaging customers) and gives 0. Only 7 analysts cover the stock, and the price targets (SEK 88–100) are close to the price of SEK 88.20. The stock has risen from approx. SEK 61 in April (RSI 73). Reported earnings in 2025–2026 contain items affecting comparability (−350m in Q3 2025, −47m in Q1 2026).
