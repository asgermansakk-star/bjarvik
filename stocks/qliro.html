      Qliro is a small Nordic checkout and BNPL player with a transaction volume of SEK 21bn a year in a market dominated by Klarna, where merchants switch provider. Take rate fell to 2.41% (2.52%) in the first nine months of the year, and the Enterprise segment is losing customers.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Qliro is a credit market company under Finansinspektionen with payment solutions (checkout, invoice and BNPL) and deposits. It is therefore assessed as a bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and operating cash flow fluctuates with lending and deposits. stockanalysis shows P/TBV 0.66, but that figure does not deduct intangible assets of SEK 309m and includes AT1 capital; the real P/TBV is about 1.45. Only 2 analysts cover the stock (price targets SEK 19–20, set before the Q3 report and the 14% price fall on the report day), and forward P/E is based on their estimates. It is not stated which Enterprise customers have disappeared and how much volume they accounted for. The deferred tax asset of SEK 79m depends on future profit. Price pressure from customers and competitors (Klarna, Walley, Svea) has not been quantified.
