    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share, and it is listed on Euronext Growth Oslo (since 23/5-2024). The ownership fraction is only 11.6%, so about 88% of equity is self-owned. The security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/E 1.38, P/B 0.11, EPS 83.45 and forward P/E 11.79 are based on the whole bank's earnings and equity and have not been used; their figures are also only updated to Q1 2026. T2 is based on the bank's own figures: earnings per equity certificate (NOK 9.40 in 2024, NOK 7.93 in 2025, NOK 5.31 for Q1–Q3 2026) and book equity per certificate calculated as ownership fraction × equity excluding hybrid capital (approx. NOK 118 at 30/9-2026). The history is short (listed May 2024). Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. No analyst coverage and no forward estimate; P/E is calculated on annualised earnings. The management changes (CEO appointed on an interim basis in spring, newly hired CEO withdrew the day after 29/6, chair left in July) are documented only through headlines, DN's lead paragraph and the signatures in the Q3 report; the reasons are not known. Insider trades have not been examined. Trading is very thin (approx. 565 certificates a day).
