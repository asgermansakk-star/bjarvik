    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    A cyclical staffing company: multiples are measured on trough earnings, so the forward P/E looks high, while the FCF yield (10%) is lifted by released working capital as revenue fell – neither is used uncritically. The trailing P/E of 19.9 is on reported EPS of 1.78; the forward P/E of 13.2 is on adjusted EPS (2.58 for 2026), and the gap is due to recurring integration and one-off costs. Stockanalysis' EPS growth of 320% is a base-effect artefact from 2024 (the Monster write-down and more). The stock has risen about 37% in 90 days from a 52-week low of EUR 21.30; targets range from 24 (Barclays, Sell) to 60 and are not used as findings. Insiders own 6.7% (the founding Goldschmeding family); the voting structure with preferred shares and a foundation has not been examined. Staffing does not fit the industry list; placed under Transport & logistics as the closest (logistics and industry are the largest customer groups in Operational). Next report 21 Oct 2026.
