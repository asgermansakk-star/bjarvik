    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap on First North with no analyst coverage. stockanalysis is based on figures to 30/6-2026 and a share count of 16.94m; after the contribution-in-kind issue and the directed issue in September there are about 27.74m shares (market cap about SEK 107m at SEK 3.86), so P/E 3.8, P/B 1.9 and an EV of 29m on stockanalysis are misleading. The trailing result is almost solely the gain of SEK 22.3m on the sale of Svensk Linjebesiktning and has been stripped out. The pro forma figures (revenue about 69m, EBITDA about 2m, net debt about 17m) are own estimates based on the company's disclosures on dLab (TTM) and Techinova (2025/26), before integration costs and before the Q3 report on 12/11-2026. The terms of the SEK 25m loan and Techinova's accounts have not been examined. The pro forma rule applies for 12 months after the acquisition, so the history on stockanalysis has only been used as background.
