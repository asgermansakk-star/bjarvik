      Revenue growth has fallen from 24% in 2024 to 11% in 2025 and 9% TTM; ARR grew 12.5% and guidance for recurring revenue in 2026 is only 10–16%. Growth must accelerate to 15% ± 2% in 2027–2029 to keep the plan.

      Large gap between adjusted EBITDA (+EUR 1.4m in 2025) and net profit (−12.7m): capitalised development and amortisation on legacy platforms (Sanimalis, VetVision) are kept out of the adjusted figures, while capex is approx. EUR 5m per year.

      −3

      Strong position in Norway (77%) and Finland (70%), but growth must come from markets where the company is small: UK 14%, Germany 12% and USA 1% (EUR 1.8m ARR). The US requires extra investments in sales in 2027–2029.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −15 of −35
    • Cash flow and financing need
      FCF −EUR 7.3m TTM, and cash fell from EUR 20.5m to 12.5m in a year. With guidance of −EUR 4m to −EUR 1m in adjusted EBITDA minus capex for 2026, there is only room for approx. 1.5 years if the turnaround in 2027 fails to materialise.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Execution and guidance
      The Rule of 40 target from CMD 2023 has been dropped, and profit on adjusted EBITDA minus capex has been pushed to 2027. Adjusted EBITDA fell EUR 2.3m in 2025, and the veterinary unit earned less in H1 2026 than the year before after increased investment in R&D.
      investing.com
      −4
    • Governance and liquidity
      Insiders own 44%, and CEO Charles MacBain is the dominant shareholder. There are two share classes (only 44.5m of 78.9m shares are listed A shares), and on average only about 4,900 shares are traded a day (about NOK 120,000).
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Customer concentration
      The three largest customers make up 19% of veterinary ARR, and enterprise chains (incl. Pets at Home/Vets4Pets) have risen from 29% to 44% of veterinary ARR. That gives them bargaining power, and a large part of ARR (EUR 3.7m) is signed but not yet implemented.
      nordhealth.com
      −2
    • Migration risk
      The therapy customers are being moved from Aspit (1,000 users moved at the end of Q2, 700 booked for H2), and Norway's veterinary customers must also be migrated. It is a source of churn and costs until 2027.
      nordhealth.com
      −1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (A shares); the company reports in EUR, and TTM figures are as of June 2026. The Q3 report comes 10 November 2026 and is not included. The H1 report PDF could not be read by machine, so the figures are from the CMD presentation and stockanalysis. stockanalysis has no analyst estimates (the forecast page does not exist), so PEG and upside lack data. The profitability targets for 2027 are adjusted figures (adjusted EBITDA minus capex), not IFRS. The seller in the CEO's off-market purchase is not disclosed. The peer comparison builds on Vitec only.
