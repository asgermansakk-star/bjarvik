      The number of diluted shares rose from 573.3m to 590.2m (+3%) in a year. The cause is forward contracts with partners who buy shares at a 15% discount to the share price, and performance shares for the FIH sellers. The buyback only offsets part of the dilution.

      The company is led by an acting CEO (Kristian Fyksen), and the new CEO, Sverre Tyrhaug, takes over in Q4. The change of management coincides with the integration of FIH Partners and the wind-down of the private banking platform in favour of a joint venture with Nordnet.
