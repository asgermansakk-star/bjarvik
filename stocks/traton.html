      Net cash flow in TRATON Operations was −EUR 269m in H1 (vs +54m a year earlier) and only approx. EUR 35m after 9M, while full-year guidance is EUR 0.9–1.7bn, so almost all of the year's cash flow must come in Q4. The group's FCF is −EUR 107m TTM.

      Volkswagen owns about 87.5%, and the free float is only about 10% (51m shares). Minority shareholders have no real influence, and VW has sold down before (2.2% in March 2025), so further selling is an overhang.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock also trades in Stockholm (8TRA), but stockanalysis only has the Xetra listing (quote/etr/8TRA), so the path and currency (EUR) follow Xetra. Trailing EPS (€6.24) and hence P/E 5.4 are inflated by one-off gains from the sale and remeasurement of the Sinotruk stake in Q1–Q3 2026 (Q3 net profit €1,674m vs operating profit €695m). Part of the gain is probably also included in the 2026 estimate (€5.33), so both trailing and forward P/E understate the underlying multiple; PEG 0.29 is therefore not representative. stockanalysis' net debt (€26.4bn), EV multiples, ROIC and FCF include TRATON Financial Services; Operations itself had net debt of €5.2bn (1.1 × EBITDA) at 31/3. The Q3 figures for net cash flow (9M €35m) come from a Quartr summary and are not verified in the official Q3 report. Volkswagen owns about 87.5%; insider trades have not been examined (0 points).
