    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Hybrid of a small gaming and esports business (FunRock/KovaPlay, Fragbite AB/Config) and a bitcoin treasury (31.25 BTC plus 10,245 shares in Strategy/MSTR). The industry is set to 'Software, cloud & internet' according to operations, even though most of the value is bitcoin. Stockanalysis' TTM figures are unusable: net income of SEK 60.7m (P/E 0.85) is the gain of SEK 90.1m on the sale of Playdigious in Q4 2025, and the operating cash flow of SEK 88.6m does not match the company's own figure (−SEK 9.1m in H1 2026). The findings are therefore based on the company's own and pro forma figures (less than 12 months after the divestment), and own multiple history has not been used. NAV is an estimate: BTC about SEK 750,000 and MSTR about USD 129 as of 6/11-2026, balance sheet as of 30/6-2026 without Q3 losses. It has not been examined what 'Övriga kortfristiga skulder' of SEK 14.4m consists of. The Q3 report comes 11/11-2026, after the scoring. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates. The stock is very illiquid (about 3,100 shares a day on average over 20 days).
