    In plain words

    Prysmian gets 94. The share is up 50% in a year and is expensive against both its own history and its competitors. The company is making a large acquisition of Atkore, mostly funded by debt, and its data-centre growth rests on one big customer. On the plus side, expectations were raised after a record quarter. Watch whether the acquisition, not yet completed, goes ahead without delays.

    In the 70+ zone: a score above 70 with more than 20% upside to the median analyst target.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −15 of −35
    • Debt
      Net debt fell to EUR 2.86bn at 30 Sep (from EUR 4.15bn in June), but only because the EUR 847m share issue and the Molex prepayment sat in the cash pile before closing. Atkore was financed on 19 Oct with EUR 2.65bn in new loans: two bridge loans of EUR 2.15bn with an 18-month term and a EUR 500m term loan, all at floating rates and unhedged. The hybrid bonds from the financing plan have not yet been issued, so the bridge loans must be refinanced. Pro forma net debt is about EUR 6.2bn (about 2x adjusted EBITDA incl. Atkore, before the Q4 cash flow), and the Altman Z-score is 2.6.
      prysmian.com
      −3
    • Cash flow
      The company's LTM FCF rose to EUR 1,434m (EUR 978m in June), but long-term customer prepayments rose from EUR 9m to EUR 591m (the EUR 550m Molex prepayment, to be repaid in 2030–33). Adjusted for this, LTM FCF is about EUR 850m, i.e. lower than in June and only about 30% of adjusted EBITDA. Inventories have risen EUR 697m in a year, and net investments are EUR 887m against EUR 765m. FCF guidance was raised to EUR 1,750–1,850m, but includes the prepayment and two months of Atkore.
      prysmian.com

      A 10-year agreement with Molex (Koch) worth up to EUR 5.5bn and up to EUR 1.1bn a year in 2031 requires investments of EUR 1.25bn up to 2031; data centres are expected to be about 90% of Digital Solutions in 2031. One customer and one theme carry a large part of the growth story.
