    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is Singaporean, reports in SGD and trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (BARRA). It reports only half-yearly, gives no financial guidance and has no analyst coverage (no forward estimate, no price targets). Trailing P/E 2.24 and ROE 706% on stockanalysis are distorted by a one-off gain of SGD 8.76m from the debt forgiveness in the 2025 restructuring (Scheme of Arrangement); without it, operations are loss-making. Currency conversion: approx. NOK 7.9 per SGD (market cap approx. SGD 9.5m). Customer concentration and macro (feed, energy and fish prices) have not been examined and score 0. The stock is very illiquid (approx. 18,500 shares per day).
