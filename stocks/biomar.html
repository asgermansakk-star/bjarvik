    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −9 of −30
    • Margin
      EBIT margin 7.5% and net margin 5.3% (TTM) vs 6.7–6.8% and 4.1–4.3% in 2024–2025 and a 5.2% EBIT margin in 2023. Feed is a thin-margin business where revenue is largely passed-through commodity prices, so small changes in commodities and currency hit hard.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell from kr 17.9bn in 2023 to 16.5bn in 2025, and volume guidance for 2026 has been lowered to 1,600–1,650 kt (from 1,630–1,700) owing to weaker demand. The EBIT jump in 2026 is partly due to timing on expensive marine raw materials; management itself says earnings are above the historical level and not necessarily sustainable.
      inderes.dk
      −3
    • Moat and pricing power
      The feed market is an oligopoly (Skretting, Cargill, Mowi Feed, BioMar) where customers are large farmers with strong bargaining power: in Shrimp EBIT rose only 6% in Q2 despite 18% higher volume because of lower prices on large key account contracts and more standard feed.
      biomar.com
      −2
    • Future segment
      Tech Solutions (AQ1 Systems), the company's technology leg, fell 47% in revenue to kr 41m in H1 and gave EBIT of kr −12m (vs +22m) during the shift to SaaS; the segment is under 1% of revenue.
      biomar.com
      −1

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −14 of −35
    • Debt and hidden financing
      Net debt rose from kr 1,833m to 2,953m (1.93x EBITDA) in H1, because BioMar paid kr 850m in dividend to Schouw before the listing and bought the rest of BioMar Ecuador. On top come supply chain financing of kr 1,333m (964m a year earlier) and non-recourse factoring of kr 894m, which are not included in NIBD and flatter working capital and cash flow. Altman Z 2.92 and current ratio 1.16.
      biomar.com
      −4
    • Commodities, guidance and macro
      Record-high prices of fishmeal and fish oil after Peru's cancelled fishing season and El Niño, and earnings are sensitive to NOK/DKK and AUD/DKK. The raised guidance implies Q4 EBIT of DKK 313–383m vs 347m the year before and a consensus of 429m, and the CEO expects a softer quarter because of raw materials, weather and demand.
      inderes.dk
      −3
    • Customer concentration
      Salmon accounts for kr 11.6bn of kr 17.4bn in revenue, and receivables sit with a limited number of large customers. In Norway sales volume fell 11% in Q2 because BioMar lost a contract in a customer consolidation, and the salmon segment's volume fell 6% in the quarter.
      biomar.com
      −2
    • Customer behaviour and credit risk (Shrimp)
      Shrimp EBIT fell 51% to kr 20m in Q3 due to low farm gate prices in Ecuador, rising fishmeal prices and kr 13m in extra provisions for bad debts, while capacity in Ecuador is being expanded.
      inderes.dk
      −2
    • Control, free float and lock-up
      Schouw & Co. owns approx. 74% (30/6), and the free float is only approx. 26m of 100m shares; minority shareholders have effectively no influence. Schouw's and management's 360-day lock-up expires around May 2027 and creates an overhang if Schouw sells down further.
      inderes.dk
      −3
