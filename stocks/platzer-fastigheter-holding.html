    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −9 of −30
    • ROIC and returns
      ROIC is 3.5% vs WACC of 6.5% and has been 2.9-4.1% since 2021 with no improvement. The company's target of over 10% annual growth in net asset value is not being met: EPRA NRV per share rose only 4% in 9M (kr 135 vs 130), and ROE of 6.2% is lifted by unrealised derivative gains.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Rental income rose only 1.1% in 9M, and in the comparable portfolio rent and net operating income rose 1.5%, primarily through indexation. Förvaltningsresultat per share rose 7% (13% in Q3), but part of it comes from buybacks, lower interest margins and the acquisition of Sörred 8:15. Analysts expect EPS to fall 8% in 2027.
      investors.platzer.se
      −3
    • Future segment
      Office makes up 67% of property value and has an economic occupancy rate of 88.4%, while vacancy in the Gothenburg office market is 14.3% with heavy new construction. The shift towards industry and logistics (21% of value, 97% let) and Arendal 5.0 with 200,000 sqm pull in the right direction, but take years.
      investors.platzer.se
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −12 of −35
    • Debt and interest
      Loan-to-value is 48% of assets and 49% net on the properties vs the policy ceiling of 50%, net debt/EBITDA 10.8 and interest cover 2.6x (target above 2). The rating is BBB- at NCR, the lowest investment grade level. 45% of the interest volume (SEK 7.0bn) is repriced within a year, the fixed-rate period has fallen to 2.6 years, and the company itself writes that the higher long rates will gradually hit.
      investors.platzer.se
      −5
    • Letting and contract expiries
      Net letting was −kr 9m in Q3, and renegotiations gave −8% in rent in the quarter. NCC has given notice that it will leave Kineum (kr 17m), and Ramboll is expected to terminate (kr 19m); terminated, not vacated contracts are kr 77m vs 81m signed, not moved in. Kr 36m of the rent expires in 2027.
      investors.platzer.se
      −2
    • Cash flow and financing need
      Operating cash flow was kr 918m over 12 months, but of that kr 107.5m is a one-off distribution from the Sörred JV. After investments in existing properties (kr 374m), the rest only just covers the dividend (256) and buybacks (260). The acquisition of Sörred 8:15 (kr 539m) and the development of Arendal 5.0 are loan-financed, and interest-bearing debt rose kr 591m in 9M.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Customer concentration and geography
      The ten largest tenants make up 32% of contract value (ESS Hotel Group 5%, Västra Götalandsregionen, Volvo and Migrationsverket 4% each), more than at Wihlborgs (19%) and Dios (21%). The whole portfolio is in the Gothenburg region, and the two Volvo companies (AB Volvo and Volvo Personvagnar) together account for approx. 6%, so the local car industry weighs heavily.
      investors.platzer.se
      −1
    • Valuation of properties
      The properties are valued internally at an average yield requirement of 5.08% (5.09), only approx. 1.8 percentage points above the average interest rate of 3.26%, even though long rates have risen. The internal value was 1.4% above the external at the latest check, and +0.25 percentage points in yield requirement costs SEK 1.4bn or approx. SEK 12 per share.
      investors.platzer.se
      −1
    • Governance
      Two share classes: 20m A shares with ten votes each give Neudi & C:o 39.5% and Länsförsäkringar Göteborg and Bohuslän and Skaraborg together 35% of the votes, so two ownership groups control the company with approx. 35% of the capital. Group management was cut from ten to six roles this summer.

      −1
