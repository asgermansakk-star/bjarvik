    In plain words

    Cloudflare scores 49 out of 100 because the stock is very expensive relative to its revenue and earnings (about 51 times revenue). It has never made a profit under accounting rules, and share-based pay of 508m dollars exceeds its free cash flow. On the plus side, revenue is growing 36%, guidance was raised, and customers spend more over time. A new pricing model and restructuring make revenue harder to predict.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.
