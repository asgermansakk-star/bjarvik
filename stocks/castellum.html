    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −14 of −30
    • ROE and ROIC
      ROE over the last 12 months is 3.6% vs the company's own target of at least 10% over a business cycle, and it has been far below the target since 2022 (−14.9% in 2023 and 1.2% in 2025). ROIC is 5.05% vs a WACC of 7.13%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Rental income in the comparable portfolio fell 1.7% and net operating income 3.6% in 9M. The economic occupancy rate fell to 87.3% from 89.2% at the turn of the year, and EPRA vacancy has risen every year from 6.7% in 2022 to 11.6%. Förvaltningsresultat fell to SEK 3,427m (3,474), and the increase per share (+5%) comes solely from buybacks. Without the Ericsson contract in Hagastaden, net letting was negative, and the CEO himself writes that the rental market has not turned.
      storage.mfn.se
      −6
    • Future segment
      Office makes up 63% of the portfolio, and office demand is squeezed by remote working and new construction. In addition there is a 37.1% stake in Entra, which mainly owns offices in Oslo. After the sale of Skåne and the offices in Stockholm, the property base is shrinking; analysts expect revenue to fall 9% in 2026 and 10% in 2027.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: the main multiple is price vs EPRA NTA (SEK 163 at 30/9-2026). At a price of 135.35, P/NTA is approx. 0.83, and P/NRV (SEK 169) approx. 0.80. Ongoing earnings are measured as förvaltningsresultat per share (SEK 7.39 in 9M, SEK 9.36 in 2025). stockanalysis' P/E 23.1 is affected by value adjustments, derivatives and Entra. The share count has fallen from 492m to 430m via buybacks (SEK 7.6bn at an average of SEK 122.74) and cancellation of 46.5m shares. The buyback was financed with property sales, not with free cash flow (SEK 3.8bn), and therefore gives no plus under the rule. A new programme of up to SEK 8bn runs from 22/10. The sale of Skåne to Wihlborgs (SEK 13.3bn) was completed 1/10, so all 2025-2026 figures include properties that are now sold. Analysts expect revenue to fall 9% in 2026 and 10% in 2027. Akelius' current ownership stake has not been checked against the flagging register; the latest known flagging (May 2025) was 20.1%, and the stake has risen mechanically after the cancellation. stockanalysis' insider ownership of 4.6% hardly includes Akelius. The price targets were last updated 5/10-2026, and 11 analysts cover the stock. Peers are only compared on P/B, forward P/E and EV/EBITDA from stockanalysis (Wihlborgs, Fabege, Wallenstam and Balder). The properties' sensitivity to the required yield and the maturity profile of leases have not been reviewed in detail.
