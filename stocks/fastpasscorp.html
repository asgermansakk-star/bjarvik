    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Stockanalysis' P/B of 17.1, equity of 2.77m and FCF of 1.07m do not match the company's own figures (equity 1.15m at 30/6; FCF after capitalised development about 0) and have not been used. Peers have not been examined systematically (the search budget was used up), so T2 peers score 0 and T2 is a ceiling. Customer concentration (largest customer's share) is not disclosed and has not been examined.
