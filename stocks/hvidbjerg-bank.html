    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful. The valuation is based on P/TBV (= P/B, no goodwill) and P/E on guidance, and distribution is measured against net profit. No analysts cover the stock (stockanalysis forecast = 404), so there is no forward estimate. P/E is calculated on the bank's original guidance (DKK 28-33m before tax) without the gain from the BEC sale, with approx. 20% tax and deduction for hybrid interest. The raised guidance (DKK 38-43m) assumes that the BEC sale to Nykredit is completed in 2026. Figures run through H1 2026. The interim announcement for the first three quarters comes 17/11 2026 (stockanalysis estimates 9/11) and is not included. Insider ownership (approx. 1.3%) is summed from the board's and executive board's holdings in the 2025 annual report. Insider trades in 2026 have not been examined beyond the announcement list on Inderes, where none are published. The industry breakdown of the 20 largest exposures and the rise in large exposures in H1 2026 have not been examined in detail. Stockanalysis shows share count and insider share as n/a.
