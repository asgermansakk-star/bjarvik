    In plain words

    Middling score (63 of 100): a low price, but a pressured market. The book market is squeezed by falling reading, part of the improvement comes from a changed accounting rule, and the company has gone from net cash to DKK 220m of debt. Guidance has been raised twice, and the DKK 20 dividend is covered by cash flow. The B share has no voting rights and trades very little.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Scored on the B share (GYLD.B, price 408). The A share with voting rights trades at approx. 1,200, and stockanalysis' market cap (823m) and historical P/E are based on both share classes, while P/E 7.4 is per B share; the multiples have therefore been recalculated per B share. stockanalysis' FCF (80m) is before capitalised development (approx. 47–52m a year) and has not been used. EBIT 2026 was lifted approx. 10m in H1 by an extended depreciation period. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates (the forecast page does not exist), so PEG and upside are effectively without data. The company only publishes half-year reports; there is no Q3 report, and the latest new figures are the upgrade on 14/10-2026. Insider trades and customer concentration (e.g. purchases by schools and municipalities) have not been examined in detail and give 0.
