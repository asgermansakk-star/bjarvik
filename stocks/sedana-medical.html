    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analyst coverage on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist), so there are no forward multiples, price target or PEG; the formula points are therefore purely negative or zero. The whole investment case is binary around the FDA decision (PDUFA 16/4-2027), which the score cannot price. The size and form of future US financing (issue, partner or loan) are not known. According to the press, the major shareholder Linc bought approx. 1.66m shares in August 2026, but Linc is not an insider in FI's register, and the purchase has not been given a plus. Owner lists and Finansinspektionen's insider register have only been spot-checked; customer concentration at hospital level has not been examined beyond the country split.
