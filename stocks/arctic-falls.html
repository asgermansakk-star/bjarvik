    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −14 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue grew only 4.0% in 2025/26 (SEK 159.3m) vs the company's own target of 10–20% annually; quarters Q2–Q4 were −1.6%, −0.1% and +2.7%, and EPS fell 11.8% to SEK 3.90. Management itself writes that it 'hade hoppats på en högre försäljningstillväxt'.
      storage.mfn.se
      −6
    • Margin and ROIC
      The EBIT margin fell to 31.9% (adjusted 36.1%) from 38.9%, partly due to structurally higher costs as a listed company, more employees and higher electricity prices; ROIC has fallen from 26% (2021/22) and 20% (2022/23) to 14%. The margin target of about 40% has not been reached.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Segment of the future and scale
      Small niche player (49 employees, eight facilities in Norrbotten) whose demand follows the development budgets of European car and tyre makers, which are under pressure; management reports spare capacity ('kapacitet att hantera betydligt större kundvolymer'), and On-demand bookings are hit by customers' shorter planning horizon.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4

      No history before the IPO; at the IPO price of SEK 72, P/E was approx. 16 on 2024/25 EPS of 4.42. The price is SEK 64 (−11%), but EPS has fallen 12%, so trailing P/E of 16.4 (approx. 14 adjusted for IPO costs) is at the same level as at the listing despite weaker growth.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −15 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      The ten largest customers accounted for approx. 88% of revenue according to the IPO analysis, and several plants are dedicated to individual customers in the tyre segment; the loss of one contract customer would hit hard.
      affarsvarlden.se
      −5
    • Customer behavior and macro
      On-demand sales are weak due to uncertainty in the automotive industry; a large customer paid late in Q3 due to problems with its ERP system (caught up in Q4). The share fell more than 30% on the first report as a listed company, as growth was negative.
      affarsvarlden.se
      −3
    • Debt
      Net debt SEK 119.6m and net debt/EBITDA 1.7; the company repays approx. SEK 25m a year. The debt is manageable, but the balance sheet is tied up in plant, and new customer plants must be financed with loans.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Weather and energy
      Outdoor testing depends on winter conditions, and electricity costs rose SEK 2.0m in the cold winter; earnings are highly seasonal (Jan–Mar gave about half of the year's EBIT).
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Liquidity and control
      Only about 2.5m shares in free float (28%) and average trading of under 900 shares a day; four founder companies control about 70% of the votes, so minority shareholders have little influence.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Listed 11/12-2025 (IPO at SEK 72), so there is no multiple history beyond the IPO price; stockanalysis has no forecast page, and forward P/E 11.3 is based on very few (probably 1) estimates that assume about +45% EPS growth. Q1 2026/27 (Jul–Sep) is published on 12/11-2026 and is not included. stockanalysis states insider ownership of 0.55%, but the annual report shows that four founder/management companies own about 70% (of which the CEO's Jalar Finans & Konsult 13.9%). Customer concentration (top 10 about 88%) comes from an IPO analysis from before the listing. Insider trades since the listing have not been investigated (0 points). Not flagged as cyclical: about 77% of revenue is 5–10-year contracts, and demand follows the development budgets of the car and tyre industries rather than a price, commodity or inventory cycle; the On-demand part is cyclical, however. The Volvo agreement is counted in one place (Plus, net of investment and permit risk).
