    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Trailing EPS 18.30 (P/E 30.7) is inflated by non-taxable income from written-down earn-outs for XGX Pharma (SEK 36.4m in Q3, 6.4m in Q2 and 9.3m in Q4 2025); the company's own adjusted EPS is SEK 14.5 over 12 months (P/E approx. 39), and adjusted EPS for 9M is unchanged at 10.6. Forward P/E 25 and the EPS estimate of 24.5 for 2026 come from only 2 analysts (S&P) and appear to be adjusted before amortisation of acquired intangible assets; they cannot be reconciled with the company's own figures. Lease payments are not shown separately in the cash flow statement (they are included in 'Repayments'); FCF after leasing is therefore an estimate, with approx. SEK 45m a year based on the short-term lease liability. Insider trades are taken from the Finansinspektionen register for the last 12 months. The company gives no guidance, so the guidance criterion has not been assessed (0). Customer concentration is not disclosed at group level. stockanalysis shows only 1.13% insider ownership, but chair Karl Tobieson is managing director of Linc AB, which owns 19.7%.
