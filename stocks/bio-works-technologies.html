    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Gross margin is high (77%), but operating margin is −10.7% TTM, and in Q3 2026 operating profit was −SEK 8.1m on revenue of SEK 9.6m. ROIC is −14.6% and ROE −11%. The company has made a loss every year and has an accumulated deficit of about SEK 364m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −9
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 52% in the first nine months to SEK 59.0m, but fell 42% in Q3 (9.6 vs 16.6m), and order intake for nine months fell 5% (65.3 vs 68.9m). Staff and external costs rose 14% to 50.6m. Growth comes from a few large deliveries in Europe (80% of sales).
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Moat and market
      WorkBeads resins are used in about 100 drug projects and 20+ commercial production programmes, but with SEK 83m in revenue Bio-Works is a small player in a resin market of USD 2.5–3.3bn dominated by large equipment suppliers. The company itself estimates a potential contribution of SEK 250–500m over 3–5 years, but it is not contractually secured.
      mfn.se
      −4

      The share count has risen from 34.9m (2021) to 100.4m (Sept 2026) through repeated issues. Approx. SEK 180m has been raised since 2021, while book equity per share has fallen from SEK 3.11 to 0.46.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −19 of −35
    • Customer concentration and lumpy orders
      The company itself writes that revenue fluctuates sharply because there is 'et relativt litet antal större kunder'. Europe accounted for SEK 47.4m of 59.0m in nine months, and the largest orders in Q3 (22.6m and 8.9m) came from returning customers with delivery through Q4 2027.
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Quality of cash flow
      The positive operating cash flow of SEK 13.5m in Q3 is mainly due to customer prepayments (SEK 16.7m at 30/9 vs 10.0m at the turn of the year) and lower receivables. Before changes in working capital, operating cash flow was −SEK 5.1m over nine months, so TTM FCF of 2.5m is not driven by earnings.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Dilution and financing
      The share count has risen 25% in a year, and 16.5m warrants (TO 2) with a subscription price of SEK 1 are outstanding until Q3 2027, i.e. approx. 16% further dilution at a third of the price. In March the company called itself 'fullt finansierad fram till break-even'; the Q3 report says only that operations are financed 'under minst de kommande 12 månaderna'.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Management and execution
      Lone Carlbom, who took over as CEO in a 'critical' period, is replaced on 1/11-2026 by board member Eva Lindskog. It is yet another change of leadership, and it comes as the organisation has been expanded and the weak Q3 has shown higher costs.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Break-even target
      The target is break-even in 2027, but operating profit was −SEK 8.1m in Q3 and about −SEK 7.9m TTM. With costs of about SEK 67m over nine months, the target requires annual revenue of well over 100m vs 83m TTM. The company gives no forecasts.
