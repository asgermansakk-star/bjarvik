    • Quality of growth

      ROE after tax over the last 12 months fell to 10.6% (11.0%), below the larger savings banks at 12–16%. Operating costs rose 10.9% to kr 96.1m owing to more FTEs and new offices. Cost/income rose to 36.1% (34.5%) and has been rising since 32.9% in 2024.
      newsweb.oslobors.no

Book equity per certificate has only risen from NOK 177.33 at the end of 2023 to NOK 183.88 at the end of 2025 and was NOK 173.87 after the dividend at 30/6.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The equity certificate fraction is only 34.23%, so approx. 66% of equity is self-owned. The security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.47 and book value of NOK 508 per share cover total equity including self-owned capital and perpetual bonds and have not been used. T2 is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (book value NOK 173.87 at 30/6-2026). stockanalysis's +2.37% in share count has not been used: the number is 4,622,601 certificates, unchanged since the issue in 2022 according to the Q2 report. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are Q2 2026. The Q3 report is due on 12/11-2026 and is not included, so the effect of the interest rate rise from August is not included. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so analyst coverage is very thin, and forward P/E 14.30 has an uncertain basis. The Stage 2 share is calculated from the bank's IFRS 9 table. Customer concentration in single exposures has not been examined (0 points).
