      A second review of the order book led to the cancellation of orders worth DKK 2,197m in Q2 2025/26 after a stricter interpretation of the criteria. The reported order book has thus been overstated, which weakens the credibility of book-to-bill as a growth signal.

      −3

      Sales in China are falling after several weak years, and APJ and TIMEA also fell in Q1. The strong krone gave −7% in reported revenue in 2025/26 vs +1% at constant exchange rates. The Q1 margin was lifted by a one-off refund of US tariffs of kr 53m.

      The main competitor Varian (Precision Therapy in Siemens Healthineers) grew 9.2% comparable with a 17.5% adjusted EBIT margin in the same period in which Elekta fell 2% at constant exchange rates. Elekta is losing market share to the dominant player.
