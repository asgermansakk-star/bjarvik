    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −10 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has risen 38% (TTM, USD 4.78bn), but almost entirely on the copper price: a realised record price of USD 6.85/lb in Q3, while copper production fell to 62,610 t in the quarter, and the year's production guidance has been cut to 285–310 kt. More price, not a stronger business.
      news.cision.com
      −4
    • ROIC through the cycle
      ROIC is 24% (TTM) and EBITDA margin 49% at record prices, but ROIC was only 4.1–5.5% in 2023–2024 and 8.6% in 2022, and net income was negative in 2024. The return follows the copper price, not a lasting improvement.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Moat and minorities
      Lundin is a price taker in copper. 68% of Candelaria's gold and silver production is sold via streaming at about USD 437/oz gold, so by-product upside is limited, and the minority owners in Caserones and Candelaria took USD 494m of the net income of USD 2,071m (TTM).
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The Filo acquisition together with BHP in 2025 was partly paid for with shares (share count +10.4% in 2025), and the next big capital commitment is Vicuña Stage 1 at USD 7.1bn (Lundin's share approx. 3.55bn) with an IRR of 14.8% in the base case and payback of 8.4 years.
      news.cision.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −14 of −35
    • Execution and guidance
      Copper guidance for 2026 has been cut three times: in January due to lower underground mining at Candelaria, on 18 August and again on 4 November due to winter storms and snowmelt at Caserones (from 130–140 to 105–115 kt). Group guidance is now 285–310 kt, and the stock fell 11.4% the day after Q3.
      news.cision.com
      −6
    • Financing need and country risk (Vicuña)
      Vicuña is to be sanctioned around year-end with a Stage 1 share of about USD 3.55bn vs net cash of about USD 200m; the financing structure is not yet in place. The project is in Argentina, and the economics rest on a PEA with partly assumed resources. That creates a risk of debt or dilution.
      news.cision.com
      −5
    • Macro and copper price
      Beta 2.05 and earnings that rest on record copper prices; Q3 was lifted by USD 51m of positive provisional price adjustments. A fall in the copper price would hit hard.
      news.cision.com
      −2
    • Governance and insiders
      Insiders own only 0.80% of the shares according to stockanalysis (the family's trusts are probably not included), and no insider purchases have been found.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stockanalysis path quote/sto/LUMI gives 404 on statistics, so data and path are from the Toronto listing (TSX:LUN, price in CAD, financials in USD); the stock trades on Nasdaq Stockholm as LUMI. Trailing P/E (10.7) is below forward P/E (13.9), because TTM earnings include discontinued operations (the Neves-Corvo/Zinkgruvan sale to Boliden incl. earn-out) and a deferred tax income; multiples are assessed on forward figures. Insider ownership of 0.80% presumably does not include the Lundin family's trusts; insider trades, customer concentration (sales of concentrate to smelters) and rating have not been investigated and score 0. The Vicuña figures are from a PEA on a 100% basis (Lundin's share 50%) and are partly based on assumed resources. Peers are compared on stockanalysis figures (FCX, Antofagasta, First Quantum, Capstone) and Boliden from the model's own data.
