    • Dilution and funding need
      The refinancing requires an issue of at least EUR 97m (about DKK 1.1bn) in Q4 2026, i.e. about 2.4 times the current market cap, and the main shareholder must also receive EUR 6.5m in fees paid in shares. That comes on top of the EUR 45.4m issue in 2025 (share count 122 → 168m, +38%) and a tripling of the share count since 2021.

    • Debt and covenants
      The EBITDA covenant was waived for 2026 in March, and minimum liquidity was lowered from EUR 10m to 5m. On 30/9 the company nevertheless breached minimum liquidity and the requirement for the revolving facility. The waiver has been extended five times and now runs to 17/12-2026. Interest-bearing net debt is EUR 196m (Q2), and interest cover is −3.0x.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −8
    • Biology
      Jellyfish in Reyðarfjörður caused approx. 11.5 percentage points of extra mortality in the 2025 generation from September (first report was 0.3 and then 6.5 points). The 2024 generation had cumulative mortality of 35.6%. Harvest guidance is held at approx. 17,000 t, but the incident itself brought forward the refinancing.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −6
    • Governance
      Euronext placed the stock on the Penalty Bench in June 2026 because the annual report came too late. There have been three finance directors in a year (interim in January, new in April). Austur (approx. 60%) has replaced a board member with a man from its own ownership circle. The refinancing gives the main shareholder EUR 6.5m in guarantee fees, and the free float is only approx. 25m shares.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −5

      The company warned in January of possible covenant breaches due to accelerated harvesting and lower salmon prices. The price achieved fell to EUR 5.92/kg in Q2, and the contract share in H2 is only approx. 35%, so earnings are largely fully exposed to the spot price.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (and First North Iceland), not Oslo Børs' main list. The company reports in EUR and only with half-year accounts; stockanalysis' TTM figures run to June 2026, so the jellyfish incident in Q3 is not included. The Q3 figures come 13/11-2026. The refinancing requires an issue of at least €97m in Q4 2026, but price and structure are not known. All per-share ratios (P/B, forward P/E 6.6) are therefore calculated on the old share count and say little about the value after the issue. The number of analysts and price targets have not been examined, because stockanalysis' forecast page does not exist for the stock. There is no special situation: there is no bid, and the waiver is not a suspension. Austur Holding owns about 60%, although stockanalysis shows only 5% insider ownership. Insider trades have been examined; the only one in 2026 is an intra-group transfer.
