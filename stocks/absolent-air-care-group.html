    In plain words

    Absolent Air Care scores 68 out of 100: a solid industrial company that is weaker than three years ago. The main drags are lower margins and returns, three CEO changes in a year and a half, and two large owners controlling most shares. In its favour are a broad customer base and a dividend covered three times by cash flow. The share trades very thinly.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
