    • Liquidity and financing need

      Interest-bearing debt is about EUR 33m vs equity of 23.9m. Of this, EUR 27.6m is loans from BASF (convertible loan 5.7m, loan of 4.0m and 19.3m drawn on a 50m facility), secured by transfer of title to almost the whole Dillingen plant. BASF is also a shareholder and the buyer of the oil.

The mill and pelletising plant yields 1,250–1,350 kg/h vs guaranteed 1,600–1,650 kg/h. The shredder had four breakdowns in 2025, equal to eight weeks of stoppage, and Perl-Besch is delayed.
