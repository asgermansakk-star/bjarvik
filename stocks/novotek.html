    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −10 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin has fallen from 13.3% in 2024 (adjusted for the SEK 30.5m gain on the sale of ROB-EX) to 10.4% in 2025 and 7.2% in H1 2026 (9.0%). Q1 2026 produced only 6.6%, and management itself mentions uneven utilisation in several teams and one-off costs for new administrative systems.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue grew only 2.9% (TTM SEK 519.6m) and 2.9% in 2025, even though Venlor and Premier Tech in France and, earlier, Taschek & Gruber in Austria were acquired. France contributed +SEK 9.4m in H1, while Benelux and UK & Ireland fell. Service revenue fell 14% (75.1 to 64.3m), so organic growth is roughly zero or negative.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat
      Novotek is mainly a reseller of others' software (Velotic/Proficy, Kepware, AMDT, HighByte) with its own consulting services on top. 81% of revenue comes from products without own IP, and the gross margin has fallen from 70% in 2023 to 62% TTM, so the pricing power lies with the suppliers.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −14 of −35
    • Supplier concentration
      Largest supplier is GE Vernova, whose Proficy software, together with PTC's Kepware and ThingWorx, has been sold to the private equity fund TPG and combined in Velotic. The software agreement runs only to 2027, and a PE owner can change channel strategy, discounts and prices.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Cash flow and cash
      Operating cash flow fell from SEK 84.0m to 44.8m in 2025, and Q2 2026 gave only SEK 0.7m (10.1). Net cash of SEK 119m is less than the contract liabilities (prepaid customer contracts) of SEK 140m, so the cash is largely the customers' money.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Customer behavior and macro
      Customers are postponing decisions, more decision-makers are involved even in small investments, and projects start on a smaller scale than planned. Deals were pushed from Q4 2025, and management still expects long decision processes.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Execution
      Novotek must at the same time integrate two French acquisitions, buy 70% of Italian Servitecno (with a put/call option on the remaining 30% in 2029–2031) and complete new administrative systems. At the same time the CFO leaves the company in November 2026, and the successor does not start until 1 May 2027 at the latest.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Governance and liquidity
      The Larsson family (Noveko Syd, with board member Fredrik Larsson) has 44.2% of the votes, but only 11.3% of capital via A shares with 10 votes. Together with the CEO's and Ulf Andersson's A shares, insiders control 65% of the votes. The stock is very illiquid.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis shows trailing P/E 47.3 and EPS 1.38, but that is a data error: the group's own quarterly figures give a profit attributable to the parent company's shareholders of SEK 37.4m for the latest four quarters, or SEK 3.53 per share, and thus a P/E of approx. 18.5. T2 is based on the corrected figures. There are no forward estimates and no analyst coverage, so PEG is n/a and there is no upside. The Q3 report is due 10/11-2026 and is not included. The stock is very illiquid (approx. 7,500 shares, approx. SEK 0.5m per day). Novotek sells industrial software and consulting services to process industry and factories and is not flagged as cyclical, but revenue follows industry's appetite for investment. Cash is not fully adjusted for customer prepayments, because the company has also prepaid suppliers (SEK 52.7m). Insider trading has been checked only in FI's register.
