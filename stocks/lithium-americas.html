    In plain words

    Lithium Americas gets 55: the company has no revenue and one project, Thacker Pass, which earns money in 2028 at the earliest using a method not proven at scale. The budget was raised 31%, the company's extra contribution of $650m is larger than its entire market value, and the share count is up 70% in a year. The Department of Energy received free warrants for 5% of the company and 5% of the project company in return for deferred loan repayments, so the government has added no new money but dilutes shareholders. On the plus side, the project is funded by a $2.23bn government loan with no interest or repayments before 2029, and GM will buy output for 20 years. Watch for further budget overruns.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    New in Bjarvik (24 Nov 2026), scored in light of government equity stakes. Pre-revenue developer: Thacker Pass phase 1 (40,000 t/year lithium carbonate) is owned in a JV with GM (LAC 62% of votes; rises after LAC's contributions of $120m + $650m). The trailing P/E of 2.8 and net income of +$175m TTM are non-cash fair-value gains on warrants and conversion derivatives (Q3: $53.6m on the JV warrant, $39.3m on the Orion derivative), because the share price fell – not operations. P/B (0.32) and equity include GM's minority interest. The government stake is neither new capital nor an offtake guarantee: DOE received warrants for 18,268,687 LAC shares (5%) at $0.01 and 5% of the JV at $0.0001/unit (issued 30 Jan 2026, 10 years) in return for amended loan terms (OWCA 7 Oct 2025). The DOE loan ($2.23bn, $1.448bn drawn) and the GM agreement are the real pillars of support. The parent company's cash has not been determined precisely (group cash $961.6m at 30 Sep, part of it in the JV); the amount left after the $770m of contributions is therefore an estimate (at most about $190m). The lithium price in Thacker Pass' latest study has not been checked against today's price. Insider trades have not been examined. Price targets (median $3.99, 12 analysts) are outdated after the fall to about $1.28.
