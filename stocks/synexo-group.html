    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −17 of −30
    • Margin
      The group is still loss-making: pro forma EBITDA −SEK 0.41m in Q2 2026 (adjusted −SEK 0.19m) on revenue of SEK 1.58m, and statutory operating profit −SEK 1.65m in Q2 and −SEK 6.7m TTM (operating margin −155%). The gross margin is good (74% in BaaS, 78% in September), but hosting is at 60%, and cash EBITDA only turned positive in June 2026.
      cdn.bequoted.com
      −7
    • Scale and moat
      Live ARR is only SEK 11.25m, and the group has 26 employees. Backup-as-a-Service and hosting is a fragmented market with large global providers; the moat is Norwegian data storage/data sovereignty and a partner channel, not own technology.
      cdn.bequoted.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Live ARR rose 155% in a year, but a large part was bought: Deploi contributed SEK 2.83m, and hosting grew only 8% pro forma. Organic BaaS growth is strong (+77%), but ARR growth was 4% from August to September, and management itself says the pace must be lifted.
      cdn.bequoted.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation and dilution
      The strategy is acquisition-driven ('mini-Constellation'). The rights issue in June 2026 at SEK 0.15 increased the share count by 13.3% (324.4 → 367.7m), and the next acquisition is to be partly paid for with shares. The acquisitions are cheap, though: Deploi for NOK 3.5m incl. earn-out for approx. SEK 2.8m in ARR.
      cdn.bequoted.com

    • Cash flow and funding
      Cash was SEK 4.5m at 30/6-2026 with no interest-bearing debt, but operating cash flow was −SEK 3.3m in Q2 and FCF −SEK 5.4m TTM. The Deploi earn-out (NOK 1.0m) and the LOI acquisition (NOK 5.9m in cash, earn-out and shares) have to be financed from a small cash balance; the company plans no new issue, but that requires cash EBITDA to stay positive.

    • Governance and accounting quality
      Listed via a reverse takeover of a stock exchange shell on NGM Nordic SME with a small auditor (Göteborgs Revision KB). Management steers by unaudited pro forma figures and 'one-off costs' every quarter (−SEK 186k in Q1, −SEK 222k in Q2); the Q4 report was postponed due to M&A work, and other receivables rose to SEK 3.8m at 30/6 and pulled operating cash flow down, without it being clearly explained.

      −4

      The target is over SEK 15.5m in live ARR at the end of 2026 vs SEK 11.25m in September; that requires about SEK 4.3m in one quarter, i.e. that almost the whole backlog (SEK 4.32m) goes live or that the LOI acquisition closes in time. The integration of Deploi and new acquisitions rests on a very small organisation.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro-cap company (market cap about SEK 43m, price SEK 0.118) listed on NGM Nordic SME after a reverse takeover of the shell Sperrung AB in September 2025. Statutory figures are distorted by the reverse takeover (consolidation from 12/9-2025, goodwill amortisation, listing costs), and Deploi is only included from 1/7-2026, so stockanalysis' TTM figures, P/S and history cannot be used as a basis; pro forma figures and ARR are management's own unaudited APMs. stockanalysis shows +0.04% in share count, but the rights issue on 30/6-2026 increased the share count by 13.3% (confirmed by SEK 6.4m in issue proceeds in the cash flow). No analyst coverage, no forward estimates and no PEG, so PEG, upside and revision points are not based on data. Peers are only illustrated through the company's own acquisition multiples; customer concentration is not disclosed. The Q3 report is due 20/11-2026 and is not included. The LOI for another Norwegian hosting acquisition (20/10-2026) is non-binding.
