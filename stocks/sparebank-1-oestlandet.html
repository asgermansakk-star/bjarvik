    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share; ECC holders have 73.1% of equity, the rest is self-owned. It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. stockanalysis's P/B of 1.02 is calculated on total equity including self-owned capital and hybrid capital and has not been used; T2 is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (1.49) from the Q3 report. stockanalysis's +5.1% in share count is a lagging effect of the Toten merger in 2024; the number of certificates has been unchanged at 135.86m since 2024 and earns no points. Only 3 analysts cover the security (all Hold). The ownership structure among the largest owners (savings bank foundations) has not been examined in detail.
