    In plain words

    Wolfspeed gets 45: the company emerged from bankruptcy in 2025, still loses money on every product sold, and revenue fell 21% in the latest quarter. Debt is expensive, with interest of up to 12.9%, and convertible loans equal an almost doubling of the share count. The US government has only given a conditional commitment for loans of up to $1.5bn; if paid out, it gets warrants for 7.5% of the company and a board observer, but this requires, among other things, Congress's approval and at least $750m from other sources. On the plus side, the company has new supply deals with BorgWarner and Infineon. Watch Chinese competitors, who have pushed substrate prices down by over 30%.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    New analysis 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). The government deal is only a conditional commitment letter (8-K 7 Oct 2026) with the Department of War's Office of Strategic Capital: a secured loan of up to $1.5bn in up to four tranches ($600m to redeem the 12.9% first-lien notes due 2030, the rest $200–400m per tranche for the project), 30-year term, 36-month draw period, interest = Treasury rate + about 1.25–1.75%, interest can be capitalised for 5 years. The government receives warrants pro rata as the tranches are disbursed: 5% + 2.5% (7.5% in total) of fully diluted equity at two VWAP-based prices, 10-year term, automatic cashless exercise. Conditions: Congressional authorisation and appropriations, OMB, OSC due diligence, that a 'substantial majority' of the convertibles convert to shares, at least $750m from other sources (of which at least $150m in new equity before the first tranche), offtake contracts and a secured project commitment (SiC, GaN, GaN-on-SiC RF, radiation hardening); also a non-voting board observer, a US majority on the board and restrictions on change of control. Nothing has been disbursed, so the loan earns no plus. Wolfspeed emerged from Chapter 11 on 29 Sep 2025 with fresh-start accounting: figures before and after are not comparable, the multiple history covers only one year, and TTM net income (+$504m, trailing P/E 1.6) is a one-off gain from the reorganisation – operations lose money. Only 2 analysts, so price targets and estimates are thin. Customer concentration (e.g. Renesas, Infineon) and lawsuits from before the bankruptcy have not been examined in depth. No insider purchases found (only grants and tax withholding in October).
