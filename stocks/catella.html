      Operating cash flow was −DKK 132m over the last 12 months, and FCF after leasing (61m) approx. −199m, while 80m was paid in dividend and own shares were bought for 25.5m in Q3 alone. The distributions are financed by the proceeds from Kaktus, not by operations.

      After the redemption of the kr 600m bond, the Swedish holding company cash has fallen to kr 89m (1,119), while the bond of about kr 534m (Stibor + 450 bp, maturity March 2029) remains. The group has net debt of kr 236m and a covenant requirement of equity above 1,000m (1,710).

      −2

      There is a new CEO (Rikke Lykke), a new CFO, a new CDO, a new CPO and a new chair of the board in a little over a year. The restructuring costs DKK 30m in one-off costs in 2026, which are consultant fees and severance, and from 2027 a new 'federated' operating model is introduced. The gains lie in the future.
