    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −14 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin is 10.7% TTM (approx. 11.3% without the PPP one-off item) vs 16.8% in 2022 and 16.2% in 2023. Management itself points to large costs for IT, warehouses and automation, which squeeze the margin until the end of 2027.

      −3
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen from 17.3% (2022) and 14.3% (2023) to 8.3% TTM, below stockanalysis' WACC of 9.8%. ROE is 11.8% vs 22.4% in 2022. Capital in inventory and warehouse buildings earns a worse return than before.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Organic growth was only 2.2% in Q2 and 2.4% in H1 2026 in local currency; the rest (11.5–12.3 pp) came from the acquisition of Cotton Classics. EPS has fallen three years in a row from kr 8.81 (2022) to kr 5.90 (2025) and kr 6.04 TTM.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation (acquisitions)
      Growth is bought in with companies that have lower margins: Cotton Classics pulls the gross margin down, and the uhlsport group (about EUR 67m in revenue in 2025) gave a small loss. In addition the warehouse properties in Balingen are being bought.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Moat
      The core is profile and promotional clothing, a fragmented market with limited pricing power, where demand according to the CEO has been cautious for several years. Craft, Cutter & Buck and Kosta/Orrefors give some brand value.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −11 of −35
    • Inventory and capital tie-up
      Inventory is kr 5.95bn, 57% of TTM revenue, and inventory turnover is below 1x. The inventory increase of 643m TTM halved operating cash flow to 590m (1,278m in 2024), and in Q2 it fell to 95m (150). That creates a risk of write-downs if demand disappoints.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Debt
      Net debt has risen from kr 1.80bn (2024) to 3.48bn and debt/EBITDA from 1.3–1.6x to 2.7x, because acquisitions, capex and dividend (431m TTM) are financed with loans (net borrowing 1.0bn). The equity ratio is still 51.8%, however.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • One-off effect and tariffs
      Gross margin of 50.0% in Q2 was lifted by refunded US tariffs, so the rise in operating profit (+22%) overstates the underlying progress. The US is an important market (Cutter & Buck, new warehouse in Dallas), and tariff policy is a persistent risk.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Governance and control
      The founder Torsten Jansson is both CEO and board member. Via A shares he owns approx. a third of the capital and approx. 82% of the votes, so minority shareholders have no real influence.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Compliance (PPP loan)
      After an investigation by the US Department of Justice, the US subsidiaries must repay forgiven PPP loans plus fees, interest and damages. It cost USD 7m (kr 66m) in Q3 2025.
      mfn.se
      −1
    • Execution
      The integration of two acquisitions (Cotton Classics and uhlsport), the opening of the warehouse and logistics centre in Dallas in October, the launch of Tenson in the US and the automation of the warehouses through 2027 are all running at once.

      −1
