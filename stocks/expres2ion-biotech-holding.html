    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −25 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating profit was −SEK 44.3m in 2025 and −23.9m in H1 2026, and the operating margin is −181% (TTM). Actual CRO revenue is only 3.0m in H1 (3.7m in 2025). There have been large losses every year since 2021 (−36 to −119m), and ROE is −85%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Total income rose 238% in Q2, but this is due to grants for the Nipah programme VICI-Disease (9.5m), which are matched by corresponding subcontractor costs. CRO sales rose 31% to only 2.0m, and the company itself writes 'limited new project activity'. The main asset ES2B-C001 is in Phase I with 19 patients and has only shown antibody response, not an effect on the cancer.

      −6
    • Moat
      The ExpreS2 platform (Drosophila S2 cells) has been used in partner programmes all the way to phase III, but it competes with established expression systems, and the CRO business is small. HER2 vaccines have a long string of clinical failures behind them, so the advantage for ES2B-C001 has not been shown.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Operations are financed by repeated issues: a 40:1 reverse split in 2024 and a rights issue of SEK 53m that was only 59.9% subscribed (SEK 32.4m), with issue costs of SEK 7.7m (24%). The guarantor in TO 13 received 10% of the guaranteed amount in cash and 30% in new shares.
