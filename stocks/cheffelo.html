    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −14 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Growth in Q3 2026 was only 3.6% in local currency (26.6% a year earlier and 18% in H1); active customers fell 9.0% to 77,500, and deliveries fell 1.8%. Growth rests on price increases (order value +5.5% in local currency), the NOK rate and one-off campaigns such as the SAS EuroBonus partnership and new distribution areas in Norway in 2025, not on a growing customer base.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat
      Meal kits have low switching costs: new customers pause quickly (management's own cohort curve), and a price increase of approx. 3% in August caused higher churn than expected in Denmark, where revenue fell 3.2% in local currency. Pricing power is thus limited.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Future segment
      Meal kits are a mature niche market where the market leader HelloFresh is shrinking (revenue −5% a year expected over 3 years); Cheffelo's own long-term target is only 7–9% annual growth to kr 1.5bn in 2028, and the Finland pilot is not yet material.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin has risen to 8.9% LTM (3.1% in 2023) and ROIC to 24%, but is already at the top of the 7–9% target range. The fulfilment cost per delivery rose 9.9% in Q3 on fuel and regulation, and logistics will be about 0.5 percentage points more expensive from Q4.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small company (market cap about kr 1.06bn, free float about 7.4m shares) with only 2–3 analysts, so price targets and estimates are thin; the median price target (kr 150) is from before the price fall after the trading update and the Q3 report, and the price is about 26% below the 200-day average. The multiples are based on a record-high LTM EBIT margin (8.9%) and a price history from 2021, when the stock was priced close to bankruptcy in 2022–2023; P/B history is therefore of little use. About 54% of revenue is in NOK, and reported growth (8.1%) is lifted by the krone rate (3.6% in local currency). The expected one-off cost of kr 3–4m for 'business development' in Q4 is not explained further; a possible acquisition has not been examined. The acquisition channel and partnerships (e.g. SAS EuroBonus) are only covered through the company's own comments; competition from grocery chains and HelloFresh in the Nordics has not been examined and gives 0.
