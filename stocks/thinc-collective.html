    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin
      Adjusted EBITDA fell to kr 3.6m (6.6) in Q2 and 5.2m (9.0) in H1, i.e. a margin of 3.1% (6.0%) of net revenue. Reported EBITDA was −4.9m in Q2 after a non-cash revaluation of 8.4m on the consolidation of Safir. Over 12 months, operating profit is −kr 6.5m and net margin −3.7%; adjusted EBITDA of approx. kr 9m includes leasing, which costs 4.4m a year. The target of 7% is not until 2030.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • ROIC
      Returns are low even in profitable years: ROIC was 2.8% in 2025, 0.4% in 2024 and −0.05% in 2021, and only 2023 (14%) was good. Goodwill of kr 50m is larger than the parent company's equity of kr 21.6m, and tangible equity is −36m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Net revenue fell 6.4% in 2025 and 11% in 2023. The growth of 19% in Q2 2026 is partly due to Safir Communication (approx. DKK 20m in annual revenue) being consolidated from Q2; organic growth is not reported separately.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat
      Thinc is a collection of small advertising, media and PR agencies in a fragmented market with pressured profitability. The company itself writes that Swedish media agencies grew only 1% in 2025. A large part of revenue is won in public tenders, where price counts, and AI is pressing the price of creative work.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The group is built from partly owned subsidiaries: minorities own kr 21.7m of kr 43.3m in equity, so a large part of the profit does not accrue to the shareholders. The purchase of a further 15% of Safir was financed with a 67% vendor note and gave an accounting loss of kr 8.4m. The dividend was resumed (76% of net profit for the year), even though FCF after leasing was −kr 6.1m in 2025.
      mfn.se
      −4
