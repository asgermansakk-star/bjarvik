    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 2 analysts cover the stock (S&P Global), so estimates and price targets are thin. According to the company, the stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo, not on Oslo Børs' main list; the entry has been kept with the trading venue 'Oslo Børs'. The accounts follow NGAAP, so leases are not capitalised, and lease payments are already in the operating cash flow. stockanalysis states insider ownership at 1.86%, but Cortex AS (the Lillebø family) controls approx. 59% of the shares. ROIC of 54.8% is due to negative project working capital and has not been used. The peer comparison is based only on Tomra and Alfa Laval. Customer concentration per customer is not disclosed, and no points have been given for it.
