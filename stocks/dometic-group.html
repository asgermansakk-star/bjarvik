    In plain words

    The score is 58 out of 100: a Swedish supplier to caravans and boats, whose sales have fallen four years in a row and which wrote down SEK 4.2bn on past acquisitions in the third quarter. Debt is 3.4 times operating profit before depreciation, and the dividend was withdrawn. The shares are cheap relative to expected profit. Nine people in management and the board bought shares.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
