    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −26 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Preliminary revenue in Q3 2026 is EUR 8.3m, 29% below last year (11.6). The target of double-digit organic growth in revenue and adjusted EBITDA for 2026 has been abandoned, and an impairment of EUR 30–35m on intangible assets is coming. Management explains this by generative AI search permanently reducing the organic search traffic that the affiliate model lives off.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Margin
      Adjusted EBITDA in Q3 was only EUR 0.7m (2.9), and this includes a reversal of bonus provisions of 0.7m. The underlying operations are thus going to zero, after adjusted EBITDA margin fell from 30% in Q4 2025 to 22%, 13% and now approx. 0%. Net income is negative (TTM −EUR 5.8m) and becomes so again in Q3 after the write-down.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Moat
      88% of revenue in H1 is CPA payments and only 7% revenue share. Over 90% of new customers are acquired on CPA deals, so there is almost no recurring income when Google rankings and operators' marketing budgets fall. The company competes with low-relevance content that the search engines push up.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      Repeated impairments on acquired assets: EUR 34m in 2023, 41m in 2024 and 16.5m in 2025. Market cap has fallen from SEK 3.9bn in 2021 to about SEK 225m. Now the cash is being spent on a new platform (TraffiqX, launch Q1 2027), prediction markets and a minority investment in the collectibles platform Repackz, all outside the core that has delivered returns.

    • Hybrid capital and capital structure
      The perpetual hybrid CATME H01 has a nominal EUR 43.7m and deferred interest of EUR 7.0m (10/7-2026). The interest has risen to 3M STIBOR + 12%, so the deferred interest grows by about EUR 6m a year, roughly all of the normal FCF. Dividends to shareholders require the deferred interest to be paid first. The hybrid claim is about 2.5 times the market cap.

    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow in Q2 was only EUR 0.03m (1.0), and investments in intangible assets rose to 0.6m (0.3) in the quarter for the new platform. With adjusted EBITDA near zero in Q3 and a buyback programme of up to SEK 28m for incentive programmes, the cash of EUR 13m is being eroded.

In addition, the necessary majority was lacking at the general meeting in May (the company is domiciled in Malta), so an extraordinary general meeting had to be held.
