    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −20 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating profit before depreciation and value changes was only SEK 4.3m in Q2 2026 on operating income (excl. value changes) of 29.2m (approx. 15%), and net profit was −2.1m (H1: −1.7m). The TTM operating margin is −0.9%. The profit of 4.1m in 2025 was due to a one-off payment of 10.8m from Gadd's performance fee and 8.9m in value increase on equity holdings; without them the year was a loss.
      storage.mfn.se

    • Quality of growth
      Commission income rose 112% in Q2, but most of it was bought: Fund and asset management went from SEK 11.5m to SEK 35.6m with Gadd, and Luxembourg/Switzerland contributed SEK 7.9m from zero. Commission expenses take about half of gross income (SEK 27.0m of 55.7m). Organically, the custody service grew 40%, while insurance brokerage stood still (5.1 vs 5.1m), and the number of custody clients grew only 5.4% in a year.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Earnings quality (performance fees)
      A large part of earnings depends on Gadd's performance-based fees (SEK 38.4m for 2025, on average over 20m a year) and on returns in own funds. This is not included in the 2027 target of SEK 110–120m. The GADD SMP fund is only up 2.5% in H1 2026, and its assets fell 0.8% in a year to SEK 2,052m, so 2026's performance fee may be far smaller.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat
      The platform for independent advisers competes in Sweden with much larger players, and the client relationships sit with affiliated agents and franchisees, who can move. On 1/10-2026 the agent Pensionera bought part of Alpcot's own advisory business (about 15% of Alpcot AB's advisory revenue). The move means the clients are owned by the advisers and not by the platform.
