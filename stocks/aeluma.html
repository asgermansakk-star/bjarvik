    In plain words

    Aeluma gets 45: the share has fallen about 62% since the previous analysis and is cheap against its own history, but revenue is falling and comes only from government development contracts. The loss is growing, there are still no commercial orders, and the CEO has sold shares. The US government only has a letter of intent for a grant of up to $30m, to be paid for with new shares of the same value; at today's price that is about 31% more shares, and the money is not certain. On the plus side, over half of the market value is cash. Watch whether the first deals with datacom customers are signed.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). Since 29 Sep: the Q1 FY2027 results (quarter to 30 Sep, published 12 Nov) and the 10-Q have come out, the CEO has sold 200,000 shares and stopped his 10b5-1 plan (21 Oct), and the share price has fallen from 13.12 to about USD 5.0 (market cap USD 96m). The government stake is NOT final: on 29 Jul 2026 Aeluma only signed a letter of intent (LOI) with the Commerce Department's CHIPS R&D Office for up to USD 30m, partly prepaid and the rest after milestones; on a final agreement Aeluma issues shares 'with a total value equal to the grant'. The 10-Q (12 Nov) says that due diligence and negotiation are still ongoing and that the money is not a secure source of liquidity. The price basis for the shares has not been disclosed; in the D-Wave deal it was the lowest closing price on three dates minus 15%. The stake is treated as one finding in T3. The partner finding from the previous analysis has been removed (the CHIPS part is now in T3, and Tower/Sumitomo are still not commercial orders). Fiscal year July–June. Only 3 analysts. Options (2.0m), RSUs (0.3m) and USD 29.3m left in the ATM programme are a dilution overhang.
