    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 8 findings    −24 of −35
    • Biology and guidance
      Algae damage to the gills caused elevated mortality at one site in September and a biomass loss of about 1,000 t. Harvest guidance for 2026 was lowered from 31,000 to 29,000 t and cost guidance raised from kr 67.5 to 71.0/kg, only five weeks after harvest guidance had been raised. The 2025 generation also had biological problems in H1, and the share of superior quality fell to 59%.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Salmon price and feed
      A sharply rising global supply pressed the salmon price and price realisation through H1 2026, and the feed price rose into Q3; the company has offset only half of the increase. With a cost of kr 71/kg, any price fall flows straight through to the result.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Cash flow
      FCF after lease payments was −DKK 828m TTM and negative four years in a row (−1,372m in 2023, −1,090m in 2024, −661m in 2025); operating cash flow was −DKK 212m TTM.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Debt and financing
      After the dividend of approx. NOK 4bn, net debt is NOK 1.54bn (incl. leasing), and there is a further hybrid bond of NOK 750m at NIBOR + 390 bp. Debt/EBITDA is 9.1x and interest cover negative (−2.4x). The old hybrid was bought back at 105.75%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Competition law (EU cartel case)
      Grieg is among the Norwegian farmers that the EU Commission suspects of illegal exchange of price and volume data; a fine of up to 10% of revenue is possible.

      All production is now Norwegian and subject to ground rent tax (effectively 25%) on top of 22% corporate tax once the margin turns positive again; the basic deduction helps a small farmer, but the regime may be tightened politically.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Earnings follow salmon price, currency and feed prices (cyclical). The company sold Finnmark and Canada to Cermaq at the end of 2025 and paid out about kr 35.6 per share (about kr 4bn) in April 2026; trailing P/E (1.5), ROE (9.3%) and net income are therefore distorted by the gain on the sale, and the 52-week price fall (−67%) is largely the dividend. T2 is based on forward estimates and the remaining Rogaland business. Grieg now reports only half-yearly; the extended Q3 trading update comes about 12/11-2026 and is not included. stockanalysis shows −11.65% in share count, but the balance sheet shows an unchanged 112.24m shares, so it gives no points. Traffic light status for Grieg's production areas in Rogaland and the salmon price in Q3 2026 have not been examined. The EU cartel case and Cermaq's claim are unresolved. Only 7–8 analysts.
