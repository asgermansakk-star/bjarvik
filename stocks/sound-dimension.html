    In plain words

    The score is 28 out of 100; the company has three employees and almost no revenue, and the price has risen sharply even though revenue has fallen. The share costs about 172 times a year's sales. All sales go through one partner, and there is no paying customer yet. In its favour, patents have been granted and management has bought shares; cash lasts about 12 months, says the company.
