    • Loan to the spun-off IVISYS
      Athanase has lent SEK 20m out of a SEK 40m facility to IVISYS, which is now outside the group. The loan carries 18% PIK interest and matures in 2029 with security in Ivisys Sweden. The borrower has negative equity and large losses, and the brochure itself warns that IVISYS may not be able to repay. IVISYS is now owned by the same shareholders, with the chairman's and CEO's companies holding about half, so it is also a related-party transaction.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company that, after the spin-off of IVISYS (July 2026) and the sale of Zalaris, consists almost only of cash. Net asset value is set equal to book equity (SEK 365.7m at 30/9-2026). The company does not publish a separate NAV per share. The tables in the Q3 report PDF could not be read by machine, so the value of the individual unlisted holdings has not been verified. The price of SEK 18.00 is from 27/10-2026, i.e. before the Q3 report with the write-down of 23.5m. No analysts and no estimates (stockanalysis /forecast/ returns 404), so upside and revisions cannot be measured. stockanalysis' insider ownership of 3.06% is outdated: since May 2026 the chairman's and the acting CEO's companies together own approx. half. The board's proposal for delisting from October 2024 was only postponed in February 2025, not withdrawn. This is not a special situation, but a risk. Dividends and buybacks have not given points, because FCF is negative.
