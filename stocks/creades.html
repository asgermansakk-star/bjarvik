    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The A share on Nasdaq Stockholm (CRED A); the B shares (1/10 vote) are unlisted and owned by the Hagströmer family. Investment company: assessed on price vs net asset value (NAV) and on the underlying holdings. Stockanalysis' revenue, margins, P/E (5.8), ROIC and FCF are value changes and dividends received and thus meaningless, so the PEG formula may give a skewed result; there is no forecast page, no analysts and no price target. The latest official NAV is kr 90 at 31/10-2026; based on Avanza's rise and Silex' fall to 6/11 it is estimated at about kr 90 unchanged, corresponding to a discount of about 7% at a price of kr 83.95. The unlisted holdings (StickerApp, Instabee, Inet, Lumene etc.) and the holdings in the active management (Sdiptech, Exsitec, Vitec) have not been reviewed individually. A possible additional purchase price from the sale of Pricerunner (after Klarna's case against Google), which Creades estimates could exceed 1% of NAV, is not included.
