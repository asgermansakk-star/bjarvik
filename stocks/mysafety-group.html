    In plain words

    The score is 54 out of 100: the company sells protection products such as card blocking, but sales are falling and the share count has quadrupled since 2022. The dividend exceeds free cash flow, and the chairman and CEO now jointly own about 14%. In its favour, about 88% of revenue is recurring subscriptions. A report is due on 17 November, and the board is exploring a possible sale.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small Swedish distributor of insurance-based security products (card blocking, ID theft, income protection) with 87 employees and a market cap of about SEK 387m. No analyst coverage: neither forward P/E, price target nor EPS estimates exist, so PEG and upside are n/a. Reported EBIT is pressured by approx. SEK 28m of annual non-cash amortisation of customer contracts from the reverse acquisition in 2023; the findings use both reported EBIT and the company's own EBIT before this amortisation. The Q3 report comes on 17 November 2026 and is not included; the latest figures are H1 2026. On 24 March 2026 the board began a strategic review with Pareto as adviser (sale of all or part of the company, takeover offers etc.); there is no bid or agreement, so it is not a special situation, but the price may be affected by the outcome. Peers have not been examined systematically, as there is no pure listed Nordic comparable company (the criterion is therefore 0). The regulatory risk of the sales channels (direct sales via web and phone) and claims development have not been quantified. The stock is very illiquid (about 13,000 shares a day).
