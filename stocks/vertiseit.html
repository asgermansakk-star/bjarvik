    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC is 2.7% (TTM) vs 7.3% in 2024. Four acquisitions in five years (Grassfish, Visual Art, Muse Content and Scala for approx. kr 265m) were paid for with loans and directed share issues, and the share count has risen from 18.6m (2021) to 33.6m (+81%). Goodwill and intangible assets make up 81% of the balance sheet. ARR per share has, however, doubled since Q4 2022 (kr 6.82 to 13.80).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' gross margin (36%) uses a different definition from the company's own (75%). Forward P/E (25.7) and the EPS estimate of SEK 2.13 for 2026 are adjusted figures without amortisation of acquired intangible assets; the IFRS result for the first nine months of the year is SEK 0.1m. Scala is consolidated only from 1 June 2026, so the TTM figures understate the running level, and Q3's working capital build-up (approx. SEK 15m) is expected to reverse in Q4. FCF is calculated after leasing and purchases of intangible assets/capitalised development based on stockanalysis. Only 2–3 analysts cover the stock, and liquidity is low (approx. 6,900 shares, under SEK 0.5m, per day). Insider trades have been checked only in Finansinspektionen's register under the issuer name. Share classes and voting weight (A/B) have not been investigated, and governance has therefore not been given points. The move to Nasdaq Stockholm's main list at the turn of the year has not given points.
