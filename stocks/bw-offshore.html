    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    BW Offshore reports in USD, while the stock trades in NOK on Oslo Børs. stockanalysis shows a 7% fall in share count and a buyback yield of 7%, but the H1 report shows 181.2m shares outstanding, unchanged from 2025, and the cash flow shows no buybacks. The figure has therefore not been used. The trailing dividend yield of 9% includes the Q4 2025 dividend of USD 0.183; with the current quarterly dividend of USD 0.0625 the yield is about 6–7%. stockanalysis' debt (USD 1.6bn) includes the financing liability on BW Opal (USD 1.43bn), while the company's own net interest-bearing debt without it is USD 101m. The strategic review from 5/12-2025 is still running with due diligence from interested parties, but there is no bid, so it is not a special situation. The Q3 report comes 25/11-2026, so the latest figures are from H1 2026. There is no forecast page on stockanalysis (404), so analyst coverage and price targets have not been investigated. Insider trades in 2026 have not been investigated beyond the Newsweb list, which shows no purchases. The contract terms for BW Pioneer and BW Adolo have not been reviewed separately.
