    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company: assessed on price to net asset value (NAV) and on the underlying holdings. Stockanalysis' revenue, margins, P/E (4.5), ROIC and FCF are fair-value changes and dividends and thus meaningless, so the PEG formula may give a skewed result; there is no forecast page and no price target. The short-term debt of approx. kr 3.6bn is the interest-free liability for Silex Transfer Shares (19.1% of Silex), which is matched by the assets at market value. It is not real debt, and it is not included in NAV. The latest official NAV is kr 334.0 at 4/11-2026; based on the price development in Mycronic, Yubico, Vitrolife and Xvivo up to 6/11 it is estimated at approx. kr 336, and Silex' price has not been updated. NAV five years back is based on stockanalysis' equity per share (kr 322.9 at end-2021). The unlisted holdings (Atle, Bure Growth, Mentimeter, Allgon) have not been reviewed individually. The Silex agreement with SMEI (Silex Transfer Shares) has only been read in outline. Cavotec and Yubico report on 6/11. Industry: the closest match for an investment company is 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage'. Not flagged cyclical, in line with the other investment companies, even though approx. two thirds of NAV is in the semiconductor-related Mycronic and Silex.
