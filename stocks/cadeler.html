    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Accounts in EUR, price in NOK (EUR/NOK approx. 11.3 based on stockanalysis' conversion of net debt). Cadeler plc (ISIN GB00BN49H029) has traded in Oslo and on the NYSE under CDLR since 5/11-2026 after moving its domicile from Denmark to the UK via a 1:1 exchange offer (92.06% acceptance). It is a restructuring of the same company and not an acquisition, so there is no special situation for the plc stock; the remaining 7.94% of Cadeler A/S is being redeemed compulsorily for cash (estimated at approx. NOK 1.8bn at the current price, not included in net debt). stockanalysis still uses the A/S share count (385.88m); the plc has approx. 8% fewer shares, but net debt rises correspondingly on the redemption. The figures are from H1 2026 (30/6). The Menck acquisition (EV EUR 501m, partly financed with an acquisition facility of EUR 380m) falls after the balance sheet date, so net debt/EBITDA pro forma is calculated roughly. The Q3 report comes only 23/11. FY 2025 includes termination fees of EUR 111m, so 2025 multiples and ROIC are lifted; TTM is adjusted. stockanalysis' insider ownership (0.07%) is wrong: BW Altor (the chairman's company, 28.1%) and Scorpio (the deputy chairman's, 12.8%) together own approx. 41%. Day rates, contract terms and related-party transactions with BW/Scorpio have not been examined. Only 6 analysts. No industry fits exactly; 'Grid & electrical equipment' has been chosen as offshore wind's supply chain (together with Vestas and NKT). Marked cyclical because earnings follow supply and demand for installation vessels (day rates and utilisation), as for shipping companies and offshore services.
