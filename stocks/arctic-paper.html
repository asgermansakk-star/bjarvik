    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis has no Stockholm line for Arctic Paper (quote/sto/ARP gives 404, and quote/sto/ARCTIC is Arctic Blue Beverages), so the analysis and market data are based on the Warsaw line WSE:ATC in PLN; the stock is also traded in Stockholm (ARP, SEK). The group accounts consolidate Rottneros 100%, although Arctic Paper owns only 55%; P/B is therefore measured on the parent's share (book value PLN 19.91 per share). stockanalysis shows a dividend of PLN 0.70 (14%), but this is outdated: no dividend is paid for 2025. The insider ownership of 4.08% at stockanalysis is wrong; Thomas Onstad controls approx. 68% directly and via Nemus Holding. Only 2–3 analysts cover the stock, and the 2026 estimate (EPS −0.58) is above the −PLN 1.07 the company has already lost in Q1–Q3. The state aid of PLN 34.3m for energy costs in 2025 is a one-off item in Q4 2026. Not investigated (gives 0): customer concentration among paper wholesalers, contract terms and price agreements, and Rottneros' own debt structure.
