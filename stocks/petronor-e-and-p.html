    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −23 of −35
    • Corruption indictment and lawsuits
      Økokrim has indicted the subsidiary Hemla Africa Holding, which owns the stake in PNGF Sud, for corruption in Congo in 2017. The trial begins 23/11-2026, the first verdict is expected in Q1 2027, and with appeal the case can drag on to Q4 2028. No provision has been made for fines, Hemla is also sued civilly for damages in Norway, and the company writes that the legal uncertainty limits access to capital.
      petronorep.com
      −10
    • Controlling shareholder and related parties
      Petromal owns 33.8%, and the chair is CEO of a sister company of Petromal. The three largest owners hold approx. 50%, and free float is 53m of 142m shares. The company has an interest-free receivable of USD 14.9m from Aje Production AS with no fixed repayment.
      petronorep.com
      −3
    • Jurisdiction and partners
      All earnings come from Congo-Brazzaville, where the state takes royalty and tax oil in kind. In Nigeria the partner PR Oil & Gas defaulted on its cash calls, and the increased stake of 59.6% awaits approval from NUPRC.
      petronorep.com
      −3
    • Execution
      Net production fell in Q3 2026 to 4,298 barrels/day from 5,045 in Q2 because of an unusually large number of wells requiring workover. Production efficiency fell from 93% to 90%, and a third crew has been hired.
      petronorep.com
      −2

      The TTM result is based on one lifting in April at a realised price of USD 116.1/barrel vs 61.3 in 2025, when P/E was 20.5. There are no analyst estimates, and the company expects no oil sales for the rest of 2026.
      petronorep.com
      −3
