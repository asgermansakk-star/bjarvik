      Revenue fell 9% in 2025 (kr 269m, incl. smaller sales to a Nordic key customer) and rose only 2.5% in H1 2026. Order intake in Buildings rose 31% (12-month rolling), but Products fell 12% and Safety 21%. From 2021 to 2025, revenue grew approx. 6% a year.

      Climatic bought a fall-protection firm in Jutland in January 2026, but already in August the board decided to sell all of Climatic A/S, which dragged earnings down by DKK 3.1m in 2025. In 2025 a dividend of DKK 5.95m was paid on net profit of DKK 0.9m, and in H1 2026 DKK 15m was borrowed from the bank.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 8 findings    −24 of −35
    • Guidance downgrades
      Guidance has been cut two years in a row: in July 2025 (EBITDA kr 36–41m → 22–27m) and in May 2026 (EBITDA 45–55m → 30–40m, revenue 290–310m → 285–305m). The strategic targets for 2026 are thus lost. With EBITDA of 3.8m in H1, H2 must deliver 26–36m. The announcements also state EBITDA margins that do not match the figures (16–17% for 45–55m).

    • Debt and liquidity
      Net debt incl. leasing is kr 74m and NIBD/EBITDA 2.5 (target under 2.0). Equity is only 25.7m, and the equity ratio is 15.5% (target over 30%). Cash is kr 4.6m, bank debt of 43.8m is short term, and current ratio is 0.88. Operating cash flow was −kr 3.0m in H1 due to built-up working capital. All assets are pledged as floating charge, and approx. kr 20m of investments in Herford financed with mortgage loans are planned.

      CEO Erik Boyter controls the majority of the shares via three holding companies and sits on the board himself. The company has a receivable of kr 3.4m from his Berkshire Boyter Holding (joint taxation), which equals about 13% of equity and is not expected to be settled within 12 months.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company only publishes half-year accounts, so there is no Q3 figure; the latest accounts are H1 2026 (21/8). No analysts and no forward estimates on stockanalysis (PEG n/a), so T2 is based on trailing multiples and the company's guidance (EBITDA kr 30–40m incl. leases). The company's EBITDA is after IFRS 16 and is far above stockanalysis' EBITDA (13.8m), because leases and amortisation of intangible assets are treated differently. Reverse share split 100:1 in December 2025, so price history before that has been recalculated. The web search budget was used up, so the research is based on stockanalysis, the company's own reports and Nasdaq's announcement archive; peer multiples for Nordic ventilation and building product companies (Systemair, Lindab) have not been looked up, and no press or analyst coverage was found. Possible upcoming news: sale of Climatic A/S (no agreement yet).
