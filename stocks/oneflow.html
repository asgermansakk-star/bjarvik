    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −15 of −30
    • Margin
      EBIT was −SEK 8.8m over 12 months (approx. −4.5% of revenue of 193.9m) after −30% in 2025 and −61% in 2024. Q3 2026 gave the first positive EBIT of 5.4m (11%). The EBITDA margin of 38% is lifted by 8.1m of capitalised own work in the quarter (16% of revenue), so EBIT is the accurate measure.
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue growth has fallen from 45% (2023) and 35% (2024) to 17% in Q3 2026. ARR rose 16% to 208.4m, but 6.9 of the year's ARR increase of 25.3m was currency effects. New ARR in Q3 was 7.8m vs 8.5m a year earlier, and ARR fell from 208.4 to 207.9m in October.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat and scale
      Oneflow is a small player (117 employees, approx. USD 21m in ARR) in contract management and e-signature vs DocuSign, Scrive and Penneo, and AI tools can lower the barriers further. Gross retention of only 88–89% shows limited switching costs for B2B software.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation and dilution
      The share count has risen from 18.3m (2021) to 28.4m (+55%) through the listing in 2022 (SEK 290m) and a directed issue of 88m in August 2024. The accumulated deficit was 349m at the end of 2025. There has been no dilution since 2024.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −15 of −35
    • Cash flow (FCF after capitalised development and leasing)
      Operating cash flow was approx. 56.5m over 12 months. After 46.6m in capitalised development, 0.5m in tangible investments and 8.9m in leasing, FCF is approx. 0.5m, and the company itself reports net cash flow of 1.0m over 12 months. In 9M 2026, 15.7m came from working capital (receivables fell 11.3m).
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Balance sheet and financing needs
      Cash of 52.2m is less than the 98.4m in prepaid subscriptions, and current ratio is 0.66. The board assesses liquidity as sufficient, but the risk section mentions that share issues may become necessary if the plan slips.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Guidance and execution
      The long-term target of over 30% ARR growth (repeated in February 2026) has been replaced by vaguer targets: 'sustainable EBIT profitability' and Rule of 40, which is only 11% measured on EBIT. The CEO himself writes that growth is 'below our long-term ambitions'.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Customer behaviour and churn
      Gross retention is 88.8% and net retention 99.0% in Q3 (98.5% for 9M), i.e. below 100% for a full year. The number of paying customers fell 2% to 4,400. Growth comes from a higher price per customer (ARPA +19%), not from more customers or upselling.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Liquidity in the stock
      On average about 12,300 shares are traded a day (about SEK 0.2m), and free float is only 12.5m shares because the ten largest owners hold 89%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Governance (related parties)
      The exclusive distributor in North America is owned 30/30 by companies linked to chairman Lars Appelstål and board member Bengt Nilsson, and Oneflow owns only 20%. Oneflow has an option on the rest at a minimum of SEK 20m or 3x ARR, which creates a clear conflict of interest between the board and the minority shareholders.
