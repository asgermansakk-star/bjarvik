    In plain words

    GE Vernova gets 92. The share is very expensive: the price is about 66 times the profit analysts expect this year, and the wind-turbine division loses money and is shrinking. On the plus side, the order book is a record $176.3bn, expectations were raised, and the company has $9bn more cash than debt. Watch that part of the orders are only reservations, and that demand rests on AI data centres.

    In the 70+ zone: a score above 70 with more than 20% upside to the median analyst target.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated after the report of 2026-10-28. Only two to three years of its own multiple history (spin-off from GE in April 2024). GAAP net profit (USD 10.4bn TTM) is inflated by a revaluation gain of USD 4.0bn on Prolec GE, a 0.4bn gain on the sale of Proficy and a 1.4bn tax benefit – operating profit is only 3.6bn – so trailing P/E 21 is misleading; forward P/E is used. FCF (17.1bn TTM) and cash (16.4bn) are carried by customer prepayments: prepayments (current unearned revenue) have risen from 20.2 to USD 45.7bn in a year, more than the entire FCF. Therefore neither the net cash nor the buybacks (6.4bn TTM) give a Plus any longer (Bjarvik data hygiene 1.3), even though Fitch raised the rating to A- in August. The Vineyard Wind case is settled (amount not disclosed) and has been removed as a finding. The purchase of Trench Group (USD 9.0bn) does not close until late 2027. SoftBank's write-down on power equipment (10 Nov) came after the report; whether it hits GE Vernova's orders is unresolved. The share of orders from data centres is not disclosed. Insider trading has not been checked against Form 4.
