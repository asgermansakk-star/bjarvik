    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Cyviz is listed on Euronext Growth Oslo (the entry says Oslo Børs). Loss-making, so P/E, forward P/E and PEG do not exist, and no analysts cover the stock (stockanalysis' forecast page gives 404), so upside and revision points become 0. The Q3 report comes 12/11-2026, and it will show whether the covenant waiver is extended and how the extra facility of NOK 25m, which matured on 30/9, has been handled. This has not been examined. stockanalysis' gross margin (20%) is based on a different classification from the company's own (55–65%); the company's figures have been used. The peer comparison is based only on Barco. Insider trades have only been checked via Newsweb from June 2025, and customer concentration has been inferred from order announcements because the company does not disclose revenue from its largest customers. Very low turnover in the stock (approx. 1,100 shares a day) makes price and multiples noisy.
