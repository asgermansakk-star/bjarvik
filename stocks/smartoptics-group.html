    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin swings sharply: 7.9% in Q1, 12.5% in Q2 and 20.6% in Q3 2026 (14.5% for 9M). Q3 is above the company's own medium-term target of over 16% and is lifted by large deliveries in Solutions. The gross margin has been flat at 46–50% for five years (48.0% for 9M), so the improvement comes from scale, not pricing power.

      Smartoptics is a challenger with approx. USD 109m in revenue against Ciena, Nokia and Cisco, and management itself calls the market highly competitive. 68% of sales go through partners and resellers, and Devices (transceivers) is standing still with 0% growth in Q3.

      Cash is USD 7.5m vs USD 14.2m in deferred revenue (prepaid service agreements) and USD 6.1m in lease debt, so cash adjusted for prepayments is effectively negative. Inventory has risen to USD 22.8m (inventory turnover 2.7) and receivables to USD 25.6m. The unused credit line of kr 200m dampens the risk.
