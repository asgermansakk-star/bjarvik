    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −10 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBITA margin is only 6.3% R12 (7.7% vs 8.0% in Q3), and the operating margin of 5.9% TTM is about half of Orkla's. The gross margin fell to 39.9% (41.3%) in Q3 due to mix, tougher competition and currency. The target of an EBITA margin of over 8% in 2029 is far from reached, and the company made a loss in 2022 and broke even in 2023.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 18.1% in Q3, but EBITA only 13.4%. Gårdschips' growth of 25.8% was partly driven by a one-off clearance of potato stock at low margin, and management expects the effect of the Coop agreement on loose-weight sales to fade in the coming quarters (loose weight only +3.5% in Q3).
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Moat and scale
      Gårdschips and Exotic Snacks are small brands up against big players such as Orkla. The market share in snack nuts is only 6.5% (no. 3), and management itself cites increased competitive pressure as the reason for the lower gross margin. Bargaining power towards the Swedish grocery chains is limited.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Growth has tied up capital: FCF was negative in 2025 (−kr 5.0m) and in 2022–2023, yet the company paid a dividend in 2025. Only R12 to September 2026 has solidly positive FCF.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −12 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      Approx. 91% of revenue is in Sweden (export SEK 14.8m of 171.4m in Q3) through a few grocery chains. The company itself names developments in larger customer relationships and customer agreements as one of the three biggest risks, and the central listings had to be regained in August's range review.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Commodities and currency
      A significant share of purchases (nuts and fruit) is in EUR and USD, and the stronger EUR and USD squeezed the gross margin in Q3. In addition, a high gas price burdened the Laholm factory, and the unrest in the Middle East may raise freight costs.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Management change
      CEO Henrik Strinning, who was behind the turnaround, resigned in May 2026, and Peter Eriksson took over on 1 September. The CFO position also changed at the same time: the previous CFO left, the appointed successor withdrew before taking up the post (November 2025), and a new CFO only took up the post in February 2026.

      The installation of a new fryer (over 50% more capacity) in Q1 2027 causes a temporary production stop in Laholm and requires an inventory build-up in Q4, which ties up capital and creates delivery risk for Gårdschips.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (market cap about SEK 404m) with no analyst coverage: there is no forward estimate or price target, so PEG and upside are n/a. Earnings have only been positive since 2024 (loss in 2022, zero in 2023), so the multiple history is short. FCF after leases (about SEK 27m LTM) is an estimate: operating cash flow of 42.5m minus capex of about 10.5m and lease repayments estimated at about SEK 5m. Q4 is weighed down by inventory build-up ahead of the fryer installation and by the investments in the plant in Laholm. The split of customers across the individual chains (ICA, Coop, Axfood) is not disclosed, and the board's and management's own trades in 2026 have not been examined beyond the owner list. The stock is down 28% over 52 weeks.
