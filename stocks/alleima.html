    In plain words

    Score 57 out of 100: a Swedish steel producer with a solid position but weaker earnings. Revenue has fallen three years in a row, the return on invested money is low, and the share is pricier than in most years relative to earnings. The company has more cash than debt and is winning nuclear power orders. Analysts see 13% upside. Demand for oil and gas pipes has almost stopped.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Alleima reports both EBIT and EPS with and without metal price effects; reported EPS (TTM 4.13) is lifted in 2026 by metal price effects (+SEK 314m in 9M), while adjusted EPS for the last four quarters is added up from the quarterly reports (1.06 + 1.14 + 1.57 + 0.46 = about 4.23). Industry set to 'Commodities & uranium' like Outokumpu and SSAB; cyclical = yes, as a large part of earnings follows nickel and molybdenum prices (metal price effects) and the inventory cycle in the short-cycle business, and OCTG follows oil and gas drilling. Only 5 analysts, and price targets were last updated 20/7-2026, before the price fall from about SEK 122 (end of August) to SEK 90.55 after Q3. Net cash of SEK 535m is stockanalysis' figure excluding pension obligations; the company's own net debt definition (incl. pension and leasing) has not been recalculated. Customer concentration, litigation and tariff effects in the US have not been examined in depth.
