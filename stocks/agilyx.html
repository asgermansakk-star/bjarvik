    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company has been transformed in 2026: GreenDot (50.1%) has been consolidated from 20/4, Cyclyx and all US joint ventures have been abandoned, and three mechanical recyclers have been bought. Under rule 1.3 on pro forma for 12 months after a merger, the stockanalysis history (P/S 251, P/B 13.5 etc.) has not been used in T2; the price is assessed against the group's own figures, GreenDot's transaction value and peers. The half-year figures (H1 2026) are unaudited, and net income of EUR 9.7m is driven by EUR 30.2m in non-cash one-off gains, so forward P/E of 88 (EPS EUR 0.01) is based on a noisy foundation. The peer comparison has only been made against Tomra; other European recycling and EPR companies have not been examined. The industry list has no category for recycling/waste; 'Construction & materials' was chosen as the closest (recycled plastic). Not flagged cyclical, because approx. 80% of revenue is EPR licence income, but the company itself describes the recycling sector as exposed to cyclical volatility. Only 2 analysts; the price targets (kr 31.8–39.7) are over 130% above the price and are probably outdated. The insider share of 3.99% at stockanalysis understates the ownership of the CEO's circle (Saffron Hill Ventures, 33.8%). A Q3 update is expected 11/11-2026 and is not included. Customer concentration in the EPR business has not been examined (0 points).
