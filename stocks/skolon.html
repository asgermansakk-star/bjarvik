    In plain words

    The score is 53 out of 100: a digital school platform whose growth is slowing and whose operations still lose money. Growth fell to 5.7% in the third quarter from 37.6% in 2025, and the share is expensive relative to operating earnings. On the plus side, it has over 1.1m paying users and renewed framework agreements. Watch the Swedish government's focus on printed books and less screen time.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Revenue is gross incl. resold licences (trading goods approx. 74%), so EV/sales and stockanalysis' gross margin (6%) are misleading; the company's own gross margin is approx. 26%. Stockanalysis shows +2.3% in share count, but the company has had 26,544,000 shares unchanged since 2023 and no issue in the cash flow, so no dilution points were given. Forward P/E 36 is based on very thin analyst coverage (no forecast page on stockanalysis), and the 2026 figure is lifted by capitalisation of deferred tax of approx. kr 7.2m (accounting only, no cash flow). The cash of kr 36m is in reality a working-capital float from customers and licence suppliers. Concentration on individual customers is not disclosed and has not been investigated further; insider trades after June 2025 were not found. Very low liquidity in the stock.
