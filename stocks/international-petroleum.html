    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Nasdaq Stockholm (SEK) and TSX; stockanalysis only has the TSX listing (quote/tsx/IPCO, CAD), so price, price target and multiples are in CAD, while the accounts are in USD. Blackrod phase 1 is ramping up (about 10,000 of 30,000 barrels/day), so trailing figures understate and 2027 estimates assume full plateau. Trailing earnings include a one-off gain of USD 45m from a divestment in Q3. Buybacks give no plus, because FCF (TTM −USD 51m) does not cover them. The Lundin case in Sweden, CO2 regulation of Canadian heavy oil and French phase-out of oil extraction have not been examined (0 points), so the grade is a ceiling. The ownership stake of Nemesia (the Lundin family) varies between sources (about 34–38%).
