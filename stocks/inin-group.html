    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo and not on the main list. The company only publishes half-year reports, so the figures are from H1 2026 (at 30/6), and H2 figures only come in spring 2027. No analysts cover the stock, and there are no forward estimates, so PEG and upside lack data. stockanalysis' trailing P/E of 1.4 and EPS of 2.35 are due to the gain of NOK 366m on the sale of Qben Rail. That gain has been removed, and continuing operations show a loss. stockanalysis' FCF (NOK 94m TTM) and P/B 0.93 also include the sold Rail and the minority interests. According to the statement of changes in equity, Inin's shareholders own NOK 343m of equity (NOK 2.63 per share), while the balance sheet shows NOK 397.6m, so the report is internally inconsistent. Inin and the parent Qben Infra (scored separately) have largely the same operating assets. Interest, collateral and maturity on the loan of NOK 166m to group companies are not disclosed. No valuation of the Power platform has been found, so sum-of-parts vs NAV has not been assessed. There are no insider trades in the last 12 months. CEO Øivind Horpestad bought 130,000 shares in October 2025, but he is no longer CEO. Customer concentration per customer is not disclosed. Qben Infra owns over 90% and can therefore squeeze out the minority, but no squeeze-out has been announced.
