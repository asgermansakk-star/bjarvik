    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share; the equity certificate fraction is 62.3%, so 37.7% of equity is self-owned (the savings bank's fund and gift fund). It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.79 and book value of NOK 182.39 per share cover total equity including self-owned capital and hybrid capital and have not been used directly; T2 is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (1.26 = price 151.6 vs book value NOK 120.2), and the history before 2025 is scaled from stockanalysis's P/B series. Figures from before August 2024 are Sandnes Sparebank before the merger with Hjelmeland Sparebank. stockanalysis's financial figures are only updated to 30/6-2026; the Q3 figures are taken from the quarterly report. stockanalysis shows +5.9% in share count, but the equity certificate capital is unchanged at NOK 250.3m (25.03m certificates including 1.95m own), so no points are given for dilution. Insider trades in 2026 are small and mixed (the CFO sold 1,607 certificates in March, a board member bought 1,250), 0 points. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. Only 3 analysts cover the security, and about 4,000 certificates are traded a day.
