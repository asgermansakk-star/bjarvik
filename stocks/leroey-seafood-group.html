    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −16 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Operating EBIT over the last 12 months was approx. kr 2.2bn (Q3-25 15m + Q4-25 758 + Q1-26 858 + Q2-26 574) on kr 33.6bn in revenue, i.e. approx. 6.6% vs 15–16% in 2021–2022. ROCE before fair value was 8.0% in Q2 vs approx. 15% earlier, and ROIC is 4.9%, below WACC of 6.3%. Market Operations earns only a 3.5% margin.
      leroyseafood.com
      −6
    • Cost position vs peers
      Farming earned only kr 5.3 per kg in Q2 2026, and Lerøy Sjøtroll lost kr 0.4/kg because of sexual maturation of trout and weak price realisation. SalMar earned kr 15.1/kg in the same quarter. Lerøy is among the most expensive of the large Norwegian farmers.
      leroyseafood.com
      −3
    • Moat
      Salmon and whitefish are commodities where the market sets the price; the vertical integration with sales and processing does not lift the total EBIT per kg across the value chain, which fell to NOK 11.3 from NOK 12.4.
      leroyseafood.com
      −2
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 11% in Q2 and harvest 8%. Harvest guidance for Norway is 195,000 t in 2026 vs 195,600 t in 2025, so there is no volume growth in Norway; the small growth comes from Scottish Sea Farms. Operational EBIT fell from kr 2.96bn (2024) to 2.50bn (2025).
      leroyseafood.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The dividend has been held at kr 2.50 (kr 1.5bn a year), even though cash flow after investments, interest (0.77bn) and lease repayments was only about kr 1.2bn in 2025 and negative in 2024. Net debt incl. leases has risen from kr 4.6bn (2021) to 10.5bn.
      stockanalysis.com

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 8 findings    −21 of −35
    • Salmon price, currency and feed
      The salmon price (SSI) was flat at NOK 71.7/kg in Q2, while the strong NOK weighed on revenue. The price of marine feed raw materials has risen sharply (El Niño), and this feeds through to cost price from Q4 2026 and especially in 2027.
      leroyseafood.com
      −4
    • Resource rent tax
      Approx. 90% of the harvest is Norwegian and pays resource rent tax (effectively 25%) on top of 22% corporate tax; only the largest farmers are above the threshold deduction, and the regime could be tightened politically.
      sticos.no
      −4
    • The traffic light system
      After the traffic light assessment in 2026, Lerøy Sjøtroll risks losing a further approx. 1,560 t MTB. In total, approx. 8,120 t of capacity may have been withdrawn, equal to approx. 15% of Sjøtroll's original capacity.
      leroyseafood.com
      −2
    • Competition law (EU cartel case)
      Lerøy is among the Norwegian farmers that the European Commission in January 2024 accused of exchanging sensitive data on spot sales 2011–2019. A fine of up to 10% of revenue is possible, and British supermarket chains and a class action demand compensation in the UK.
      salmonbusiness.com
      −3
    • Debt
      NIBD is DKK 8.4bn excluding leasing; including leasing net debt/EBITDA is 3.4x vs 1.1–1.6x in 2021–2022, and interest cover is only 2.5x. The equity ratio is 47% (covenant 30%), and the rating is BBB.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Biology and execution
      Low sea temperature has slowed growth at Lerøy Sjøtroll, sexual maturation of trout caused losses, and more lice treatments in Q2 raise costs. The harvest forecast for Sjøtroll has been lowered.
      leroyseafood.com
      −2
    • Guidance vs estimates
      The company expects operating EBIT in H2 2026 in line with H2 2025 (kr 773m), when EPS before fair value was approx. −kr 0.19 (2.12 for the year vs 2.31 in H1). With kr 1.76 in H1 2026, this points to 2026 EPS of around kr 1.6–2.0 vs analysts' kr 2.97, so the estimates look too high.
      leroyseafood.com
      −3
    • Governance
      Austevoll Seafood (the Møgster family) owns 52.7% and controls the company; the 20 largest own 76.6%, so minority shareholders have limited influence.
      leroyseafood.com
      −1

    Caveats
