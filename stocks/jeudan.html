    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −10 of −30
    • ROIC and returns
      ROIC is 2.3%, below WACC of 3.0%, and has been 2.4–3.3% since 2021. The ongoing return (EBVAT/equity) is 4.6% pre-tax, approx. 3.6% after tax. ROE of 7.6% is lifted by revaluations: in H1, kr 517m came from property write-ups vs kr 303m in ongoing earnings.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      EBVAT has been almost flat for eight quarters (kr 137–152m per quarter from Q3 2024 to Q2 2026), although net interest-bearing debt rose from kr 21.7bn to 23.0bn in a year. The growth in revenue (+4%) and EBIT (+7%) comes mainly from debt-financed acquisitions (kr 1.12bn in 2025 and 278m in H1). Revenue in Projekt & Service fell 18% to kr 301m.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The company buys properties at a yield of approx. 4.3% with mortgage loans, while its own shares trade at 0.83 × book value. Own shares are bought only for employee schemes (kr 16.7m in H1), and the dividend has stood at kr 3 since 2022 (1.5% yield).
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −17 of −35
    • Debt and interest rate sensitivity
      Net interest-bearing debt is DKK 23bn, approx. 60% of the property value of 38.2bn. Net debt/EBITDA is 20.4 (Fast Ejendom Danmark 8.8), interest cover is only 2.1 ×, and the equity ratio is 33%. At the end of June DKK 838m of the credit facilities was drawn.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −5
    • Letting and tenants
      The terminations in H1 corresponded to an annual rent of DKK 97m vs DKK 36m a year earlier and exceeded the new contracts of DKK 78m. The occupancy rate is approx. 95, and the company itself expects the occupancy rate in the Copenhagen office market (95.7) to fall.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow fell to DKK 205m in H1 from DKK 291m (CFPS 7.4 vs DKK 10.5 on an annual basis), partly due to tax paid and fewer prepayments. In 2025 operating cash flow was DKK 645m, but after improvements (74m), projects (490m) and fixtures (15m) only approx. DKK 66m was left vs a dividend of 166m. Dividend and growth are financed with new mortgage debt.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −2
    • Valuation of properties
      The properties are valued every quarter based on net income and yield requirement, and the average yield requirement has been held unchanged at 4.3%, even though the ECB and Nationalbanken have raised interest rates. Price and value adjustments fluctuate strongly: −DKK 632m in Q3 2024 vs +262m in Q4 2025 and +DKK 517m in write-up of properties in H1 2026 (2023 gave a net loss of DKK 828m).
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −2
    • Governance and ownership concentration
      Two foundations (William Demant Invest and Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker) own over 80% of the shares. Free float is only 8.3m of 55.4m shares, and insiders own 0.10%. Minority shareholders have no real influence, and any squeeze-out will take place on the main shareholders' terms.
      lex.dk
      −2
    • Liquidity in the stock
      Average turnover is only 3,651 shares a day (about kr 0.7m) at a market cap of kr 11.1bn. That makes it hard to get in and out of a position.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Geographic and segment concentration
      Over 96% of the portfolio is in Copenhagen and Frederiksberg, and it is dominated by office properties. The company is thus fully exposed to one office market, where working from home and weaker Danish growth are pressuring demand.
      inderes.dk
      −1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: price vs intrinsic value is the main multiple (P/B 0.83 at a price of 200 and intrinsic value of DKK 242 at 30/6-2026). Intrinsic value is after deferred tax of DKK 3.06bn (approx. DKK 55 per share), so the discount to the value before tax is larger. The latest financials are H1 2026 (12/8). The Q3 report comes 12/11-2026 and is not included. stockanalysis' P/E 11.1 and ROE 7.6% are lifted by value adjustments. Ongoing earnings are estimated as EBVAT 2025 (DKK 579m) less 22% tax, approx. DKK 8.2 per share. LTV is calculated as net interest-bearing debt (DKK 23bn) divided by the properties (DKK 38.2bn), approx. 60%; the company does not itself state an LTV in the report. Whether the properties are valued by external valuers has not been investigated (the report only mentions the quarterly valuation). The two foundations' exact stakes (William Demant Invest and Chr. Augustinus Fabrikker, together over 80% according to lex.dk) have not been confirmed in a major shareholder announcement. Tenant concentration (the largest tenants' share of rent) has not been investigated. Insider trades in 2025–2026 have not been investigated. No analysts cover the stock, and stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E, PEG and upside are empty. The search budget ran out during the research, so the 2025 annual report and 5–10-year EBVAT per share have not been reviewed; the P/B history only covers 2021–2025.
