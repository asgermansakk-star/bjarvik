    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Scored on the B share (SWEC.B). Engineering and architecture consultant; 'Construction & materials' is the nearest industry, as demand follows construction and infrastructure investment. P/TBV is meaningless (goodwill 12.0bn vs equity 13.5bn). stockanalysis' net debt (6.76bn) includes lease debt; the company's own net debt is 3.16bn (0.8x EBITDA). Sweco gives no financial guidance. The share of public customers, contract terms (fixed price vs time and materials) and insider trades from Finansinspektionen's register have not been examined in depth; stockanalysis' insider share of 2% underestimates the Nordström family's control. The significance of AI for hourly-based consulting work is an estimate. Only 6 analysts cover the stock. A new CEO has not yet been appointed.
