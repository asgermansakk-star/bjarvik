      Revenue after 9 months is SEK 31.2m vs 44.0m (−29%), and TTM is −27%. Q1 fell 56% and Q2 27%, while Q3 was only on a par with the year before (12.9 vs 12.6m). Norway has been weak, and customers have postponed deliveries. The growth story from 2022–2025 is broken.

      A small niche producer of propane-based cooling and heating systems with 21 employees. It competes against NIBE, Beijer Ref, Systemair and Munters, which all sell natural refrigerants. The technology itself is not exclusive, and the company has not yet shown that it can scale.

The 2025 money has been spent on a new factory, an IT switch and establishment in the Netherlands and Belgium. The investments were expensed while sales fell, and cash has gone from 37 to 5m in 18 months.

      Cash was approx. SEK 5m at 30/9, and FCF is −SEK 14.8m TTM. The inventory loan from the main owner Weland has risen from SEK 5m (turn of the year) to SEK 10m (Q2) and SEK 20m (Q3), and the equity ratio has fallen from 77% to 49%. The board guarantees operations without a capital injection only 'throughout 2026', i.e. under two months ahead. A new issue or more owner loans in 2027 is likely.

After 9 months the figures are 31.2m and −11.6m, so Q4 must deliver 35m, almost three times Q3. At the same time the company says production cannot keep up with orders.

      Inventory is SEK 28.5m vs 18.2m at the turn of the year and TTM revenue of 43m (inventory turnover 0.82). The company has produced standard products for stock while customers postponed deliveries. This creates a risk of write-downs if demand does not come.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro company (market cap about SEK 267m, 21 employees), and the stock is very illiquid (about 730 shares a day). The only research is a paid commissioned analysis from Carlsquare (March 2026), whose forecast for 2026 is already far behind. Forward P/E 89.8 on stockanalysis is based on an outdated estimate. stockanalysis shows 'zero debt', but the company has pledged inventory for SEK 20m with principal owner Weland Stål; net debt is therefore about 15m and EV about 282m. Weland's and the insiders' exact ownership stakes have not been found (Weland owned 30.5% in 2017), so insider ownership scores 0. The terms of the Weland loan are not disclosed. The order book for Q4 is described as 'heavily filled', but no figures have been given. Q4 results 26/2-2027.
