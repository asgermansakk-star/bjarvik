    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The TTM figures include a refund of US tariffs (IEEPA) of approx. kr 336m in Q2 and kr 45m in Q3. Getinge does not book it as a one-off item, so trailing P/E, margin and FCF are adjusted by estimate. The status of the FDA consent decree and of Cardiosave's 510(k) application in 2026 has not been confirmed directly with the FDA; the latest FDA source is from 2024. The second patent case against Abiomed is still ongoing and has not been assessed. Carl Bennet's voting share is calculated from the number of A shares on stockanalysis (approx. 42% of the votes) and not from the ownership register. Of the insider trades, only Bennet's purchase in April 2026 has been examined. Customer concentration has not been examined in detail beyond the fact that customers are hospitals in over 135 countries. 11 analysts cover the stock, and JP Morgan has Sell with a price target of kr 193.
