    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Idun reports under K3 with goodwill amortisation, so reported EPS (5.40) and trailing P/E 59.6 understate earnings; the company's goodwill-adjusted EPS is NOK 16.5 LTM (P/E about 19.5). Forward P/E 16.8 is presumably based on adjusted EPS, and stockanalysis has no forecast page (few or no analysts beyond a commissioned DNB Carnegie analysis), so upside and PEG data are thin. Under K3, leases are probably not capitalised, so FCF after leases could not be isolated separately. Minority owners in the group companies have about 17% of EBITA; any purchase obligations towards the minorities have not been examined (0 points). Low liquidity (about 6,400 shares per day).
