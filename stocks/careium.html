    In plain words

    74 points out of 100. The stock is reasonably priced, but operating profit is only about 6.5% of revenue and cash flow is weak. The return on the money invested in the business just matches what the capital costs. Revenue is growing fast, 72% comes from services on multi-year contracts, and the new CEO and the CFO have bought shares. Customers are mostly public bodies, won through tenders.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small First North company (market cap about kr 686m) with low liquidity. stockanalysis has no forecast page and no analyst coverage, so upside and revision points may be missing; forward P/E 10.6 comes from S&P. Peers have not been examined (Tunstall and Legrand-owned Appello are not listed as pure companies) and give 0. FCF is measured using the company's own definition (after intangible investments) and then less loan/lease repayments; stockanalysis' FCF of kr 79m does not count capitalised development and leasing and has not been used. The hybrid loan was booked as equity, so net debt rose on redemption. Active industry chosen as 'Health insurance & services', because 72% of sales are alarm and monitoring-centre services to municipalities; MFN classifies the company as electronic equipment.
