    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

ROIC (25%), ROE (44%) and P/B 6.0 are lifted by negative working capital and customer advances and have not been used as a plus. NOK 160m of 785m TTM) is interest on cash, so P/E understates the price of the contracting business itself. stockanalysis shows +10.4% in share count year over year; this is mainly due to the capital increase of NOK 230m and the conversion of synthetic shares at the 2025 listing (share count 95.8 → 100.4m in 2025), not ongoing dilution. The acquisition of 70% of Thule Fastighetsutveckling (EV SEK 65m) is small and has not been assessed separately.
