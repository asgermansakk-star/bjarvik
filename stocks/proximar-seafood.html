    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is in an acute liquidity crisis: liquidity was exhausted by the end of October 2026, interest on the convertible loan has not been paid, and a standstill has been requested on all interest-bearing debt in the parent company. Trading was suspended on 27/10 and resumed the same day (no special situation as of 6/11), but a debt restructuring or compulsory composition could wipe out or heavily dilute the current shareholders, and Nomura's strategic process could end in a sale of the Japanese operation. There is no analyst coverage and no forward estimate; stockanalysis' forward P/E of 0.63 is based on an outdated estimate and has not been used. The Q3 accounts are not due until 27/11, so the latest figures are from 30/6. Peers (Salmon Evolution, Nordic Aqua) have not been compared separately on multiples, because the equity is effectively an option on a restructuring.
