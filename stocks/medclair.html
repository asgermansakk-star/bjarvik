    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −19 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin has fallen from 10.9% in 2025 to about 0.9% TTM (EBIT SEK 0.5m on revenue of 56m), and Q3 produced an operating loss of −0.7m. Operating costs rose far faster than sales (staff +51%, external costs +77% vs sales +28% in Q3). EBITDA looks better because 4.0m of development was capitalised in 9M. The gross margin is stable at 65–66%.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • ROIC/ROE
      ROE is 0.6% and ROCE 1.4% TTM vs 9.1% and 10.6% in 2025. The company has been profitable for one year (2025), and 2021–2024 gave negative returns. The return is far below the cost of capital.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is highly volatile: −19% in 2024, +62% in 2025, +10% in 9M 2026 and a fall of 10% in Q2 2026. Sales consist mostly of one-off equipment orders; recurring revenue is only approx. SEK 3.6m a year (approx. 6% of revenue).
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Market and moat
      Nitrous oxide destruction is a small niche with regulatory tailwind (limit values for the working environment and climate), but the company has only 21 employees and must go through a technology switch: the new photocatalytic platform will not reach the market until H2 2027. The total market of SEK 17bn is the company's own estimate.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analyst coverage and no forward estimate, so PEG is n/a and there is no price target. stockanalysis' statistics page shows outdated TTM figures (P/E 136, FCF +1.4m); the findings are based on the Q3 report (5/11-2026) and stockanalysis' TTM accounts to September 2026. The merger with the subsidiary was completed 21/10, and in the parent company the patents now stand at approx. SEK 33.6m. It is unclear how this feeds through to the accounts for Q4 and onwards (higher amortisation and equity); it has not been factored in. There are no listed peers that purely destroy nitrous oxide, so the peer comparison uses Swedish small medtech companies. Customer concentration has not been quantified, and short data is missing.
