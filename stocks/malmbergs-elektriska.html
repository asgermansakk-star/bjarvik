    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin
      Loss three years in a row: the operating margin is −2.3% TTM, −3.1% (2025) and −3.9% (2024) vs 6.9–7.1% in 2021–22 and a target of over 10%. Q2 2026 gave an operating loss of −SEK 9.5m on revenue of 95m, and the Swedish segment (approx. 80% of sales) lost 15.5m in H1. The gross margin holds at 45%, so the problem is costs relative to falling volume.
      mb.cision.com
      −9
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen four years in a row: from SEK 669m (2022) to 444m TTM (−34%), and it fell again 6.8% in the first half of 2026 and 7.6% in Q2. Norway fell 13.5% in the half-year. The company itself describes demand as wait-and-see, and investment decisions take longer than normal.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Moat and segment
      Malmbergs imports and sells mainly own-brand goods (EMV) in lighting, installation material and cables directly from manufacturers in Asia. Competition comes from the large electrical wholesalers and from e-commerce, and the business rests on price and logistics. There are no signs of pricing power when sales fall four years in a row.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Equity has fallen from SEK 379m (2022) to 295m (30/6-2026) through losses and dividends. SEK 8m in dividend was paid in April 2026 after a loss-making year, even though the target is 30–50% of profit after tax. FCF after leasing (11m TTM) only just covered the dividend.

    • Quality of cash flow
      Operating cash flow fell to SEK 8.7m in H1 2026 vs 31.5m a year earlier. Cash flow before change in working capital was only 6.2m. FCF in 2025 (69m before and 34m after leases of 35m) came mainly from an inventory reduction of 47m, which cannot be repeated indefinitely.

    • Inventory and write-down risk
      Inventory is SEK 209m, or 47% of the balance sheet, with an inventory turnover of only 1.1, which corresponds to about a year of cost of goods. Inventory rose again in H1 (from 205m), even though sales fell. With falling volume this increases the risk of write-downs and price pressure.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Macro and demand
      Sales follow Nordic construction and installation. Management reports unchanged, wait-and-see market conditions and long decision times, and there is no guidance. The Swedish core business is losing money in the current market.
      placera.se
      −3
    • Governance and ownership
      The Folke family controls the company through A shares (0.8m A vs 7.2m B), approx. 70% of the votes. Chair Johan Folke is from the family, and Pernilla Folke was newly elected to the board in 2026. Minority shareholders have no real influence. Stockanalysis puts insider ownership at only 4.25%. The voting control partly sits in a deceased estate, which could mean a change of ownership.
      inderes.se
      −3

      Average volume is 2,279 shares a day (about SEK 63,000), and free float is only 3.3m shares. The stock is hard to buy and sell in larger blocks.
