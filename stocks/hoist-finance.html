    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Debt purchaser with bank status (credit market company under Finansinspektionen, funded with SEK 48.8bn in deposits): EV, ROIC and FCF from stockanalysis are not meaningful (FCF includes collection on portfolios) and have not been used; the assessment is based on P/B, P/TBV, P/E and ROE. TTM EPS of 16.60 includes a VAT refund (SEK 163m) and a reversed tax liability (SEK 43m); adjusted EPS is about 15. Only 2 analysts, and price targets and estimates are from May 2026, before the price rose to about SEK 200. Major shareholders and insider trades beyond the 2026 share investment programme have not been investigated, and country distribution (Italy etc.), debt collection regulation and customer behaviour by market have only been looked at superficially. Debt collection does not fit the list; 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage' chosen.
