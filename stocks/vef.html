    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company: P/E, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, so the valuation is price to net asset value (NAV) and a look-through of the largest holdings. NAV is reported in USD; in SEK, NAV per share is almost unchanged this year (kr 3.94 vs 3.93), because the dollar has risen. Liquidity after the redemption of the bonds on 15/10 is calculated by us from the quarterly balance sheet and the RCF agreement, not stated by the company. The insider trades are from Insiderscreener, where the price and amount fields look wrong; the extent is therefore uncertain. Only 3–4 analysts cover the share. Creditas' latest transaction price and its date have not been examined.
