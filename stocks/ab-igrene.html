    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −24 of −30
    • Margin and earnings
      Revenue of only SEK 4k in fiscal 2025/26 vs an operating result of −SEK 8.3m (−8.5m a year earlier). The company has posted losses every year for at least five years, and ROE is −27%. Costs are mainly consultants for regulatory processes.

    • Business model and growth quality
      The gas in the Siljan Ring may not be extracted: the Riksdag banned natural gas extraction in May 2022. Länsstyrelsen rejected the application for an environmental permit for a demo plant in 2025, and the Land and Environment Court rejected the appeal, citing the ban in the Environmental Code. The wells are closed, and there is no timeline for production.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Capital allocation and strategy
      The strategy has changed several times in a year. In January 2026 the company changed its articles of association towards the ColdSpark technology at Seid AS, and the issue in April was partly meant to finance more investments in Seid. In October the board put the investments on hold and returned to traditional gas extraction. The investment of NOK 3m in Seid may itself be written down, according to the company.
