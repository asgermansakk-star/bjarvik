    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The ownership fraction is only 28.59%, so approx. 71% of equity and profit belongs to the primary fund (self-owned). Economically the certificate is equivalent to a share, and it is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.31 is based on total equity, so T2 uses the bank's own book equity per certificate (NOK 61.77 at 30/9) and the price at the balance sheet date from the annual report. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. Not examined: the size of individual customers in the corporate book and the composition of the board of trustees. Only 3 analysts cover the certificate, and turnover is low (approx. 13,000 certificates a day).
