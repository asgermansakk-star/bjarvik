    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: the main multiple is price vs EPRA NRV (SEK 26.19 at 30/9-2026); EPRA NTA is not stated, but equity plus deferred tax minus goodwill gives approx. SEK 25 per share. Running earnings are measured as förvaltningsresultat (run rate SEK 1.58 per share, adjusted 1.74). stockanalysis's P/E 51.7 and ROE 2.4% are distorted by value adjustments and currency effects on NOK and EUR debt, and their FCF is n/a. The history is short and not comparable: the company was Norwegian (NOK) until 2026, the SocialCo portfolio from SBB has been consolidated from 16/12-2025, and the share count has quadrupled, so the P/NRV history covers only 2025-2026 (0.92 → 0.62). The share count rises again with the SBB deal and the bids for Preservium, Origa Care and One Publicus (approx. 60m new shares, issued at NAV). Only 4 analysts cover the stock. Peers in social infrastructure property (Hemsö, Stenvalvet etc.) have not been compared systematically. Insider trades have been checked only via MFN announcements.
