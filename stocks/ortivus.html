    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The holding is the A share (ORTI A), but stockanalysis only has the B share (ORTI.B), which has been used for all figures; A and B have the same economic rights, and the A share is very thinly traded (about 1.6m of 88.6m shares). Ponderus Invest's mandatory bid at kr 1.22 expired 12/1-2026 without extension, so there is no special situation now, but Ponderus owns 80.3%, and a later bid or squeeze-out (requires 90%) cannot be ruled out. The company has dropped the Q3 report (switch to half-year reporting), so the latest figures are from H1 2026; the next report is 19/2-2027. No analysts cover the stock, and stockanalysis has no forecast page. stockanalysis shows no lease payments for 2025; FCF after leasing has therefore been estimated from the lease debt. Insider trades beyond Ponderus' purchases in the mandatory bid have not been examined.
