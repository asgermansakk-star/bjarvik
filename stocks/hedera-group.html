    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −24 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating loss of SEK 11.0m TTM (EBIT margin −2.2%) vs +3.4% in 2022 and +2.4% in 2023. Even adjusted for one-offs, operating profit (rörelseresultat) was only SEK 0.7m (0.2%) in the nine months of the year and −SEK 1.9m in 2025, so the company earns practically nothing on revenue of just under SEK 500m.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen three years in a row: SEK 678m in 2023, 556 in 2024, 516 in 2025 and −6.5% in the nine months of 2026 (365.9 vs 391.2). Staffing fell 23% to SEK 57.1m in nine months because of fewer consulting managers, and the assistance business lost users in 2025 and has had weak net intake of new customers.

    • Capital allocation and ROIC
      Intangible assets from the acquisitions (including Libra Assistans 2022) amount to SEK 132.3m, more than equity of SEK 99.0m and about 6 times the market cap, but yield an ROIC of −9.5% and ROE of −10.5%. The company paid a dividend of kr 0.20 (about SEK 3m) in 2024 and had to do a rights issue of SEK 27.4m the year after.

    • Moat and pricing power
      No pricing power: the assistance business receives the state's flat-rate compensation (schablonersättning), which rose only 1.5% in 2026 vs wage increases of 3% (almost SEK 2m in Q2 alone), and the company itself writes that the industry has thin margins and tough competition. The regions are cutting agency staff, and the new Life Science leg had weak revenue in Q3.

The stock is an illiquid microcap (approx. 57,000 shares or approx. SEK 30,000 per day).
