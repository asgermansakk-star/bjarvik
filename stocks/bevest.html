    In plain words

    The score is 58 out of 100: a holding company with stakes in, among others, the packaging firm BEWI and a salmon farm. The main drags are that it runs at a loss, the parent has a lot of debt and almost no cash, and one family has great power. The price is about 26% below the value of its holdings, and BEWI proposes an extra dividend. Watch the debt.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
