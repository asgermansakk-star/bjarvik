    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Reinsurance: FCF is not meaningful; payouts are measured against net income. Trailing P/E of 9.6 is low because H1 2026 had unusually few large claims (4.9% vs a budgeted 18%) and high investment returns; forward P/E of 11.5 is more representative. Stockanalysis' revenue forecast (+12% in 2026) does not match the company's own downgrade to 38bn in reinsurance revenue. Insider ownership is not disclosed. ERGO and US casualty reserves have not been examined separately. Hurricane season runs to November; Q3 results 12 Nov.
