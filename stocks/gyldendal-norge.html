    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Gyldendal ASA (Oslo, GYL, price 412) is not the Danish Gyldendal. The company only publishes half-year reports, so the TTM figures run to 30/6-2026, and the first half is seasonally negative (over 60% of revenue and all of the profit come in the second half). Cash and overdraft at 30/6 are therefore not comparable with year-end. stockanalysis' FCF (283m TTM) is before leasing; after repayments (167m) and interest (about 21m) on leasing, FCF is about 96m TTM and about 121m in 2025, and those are the ones used. stockanalysis' share count +5.9% does not match the share capital (2,352,240 shares, 45,109 treasury shares, unchanged), so no points were given for dilution. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates (the forecast page does not exist), so PEG and upside are without data. Insider trades beyond the ownership structure have not been investigated and give 0. The sale of the competitor Aschehoug is unresolved.
