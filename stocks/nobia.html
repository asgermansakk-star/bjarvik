    In plain words

    The score is 38 out of 100, and the model found several serious problems. The kitchen maker earns very little relative to sales, has no growth and negative cash flow, and has used nearly all its credit line. A March issue more than doubled the share count, and all top managers have been replaced. Management has bought shares, and two analysts have price targets about 39% above the price.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Kitchen manufacturer; 'Construction & materials' was chosen as the closest industry because demand follows construction and renovation (B2B and B2C). The UK (Magnet etc.) was sold in March 2026 and is included as a discontinued operation, so stockanalysis' history before 2026 mixes the old and the Nordic group; the findings are based primarily on the company's own figures for continuing operations in the Q3 report. The share count is affected by a rights issue (675 → 1,516m shares) and a 10:1 reverse split in May 2026; stockanalysis shows 151.4m shares after the reverse split. stockanalysis' forward P/E of 11.85 rests on only 2 analysts and cannot be reconciled with their EPS estimate for 2026 (SEK 0.09); it is used only as background. stockanalysis' insider share (0.01%) does not include Nordstjernan, which is the largest owner and has the chairman; the exact ownership share has not been investigated. Customer concentration has not been investigated in depth (broad B2C and B2B base, but not documented). The company gives no guidance. Only 2 analysts cover the stock, and the price has fallen 73% over 52 weeks (26% on the day of the Q3 report), so the price target is probably outdated.
