    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has only gone from kr 8.30bn (2023) to kr 8.66bn TTM (approx. 1.6% a year), and Branded packaged products fell from kr 6.15bn (2023) to 5.97bn (2025). Organic growth was 2.3% in Q3, below the long-term target of 3–4%, and price dragged in the quarter ('fair pricing'). The progress is margin, not growth.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • ROIC and balance sheet
      ROIC has risen from 7.7% (2021) to 12.2%, but equity of kr 6.2bn consists almost solely of goodwill and trademarks (P/TBV 42.9). The return on total capital is only moderate for a brand company.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Market and the segment of the future
      The core markets are the mature Nordic countries and the Netherlands. The expansion in Germany (five CandyKing pilots by year-end) and the USA (Ahlgren's cars at Target) is still at pilot level and costs marketing: Pick & mix's adjusted EBIT fell from kr 62m to 59m in Q3 because of the expansion.

      −2

    • Execution and Q4
      Management expects a temporary effect on growth in Q4 from IT outages at a large customer in Central Europe and a third-party logistics provider in the Nordics in October. At the same time the increased strategic marketing continues in Q4, and Q4 is the season's largest quarter.

      The adjusted EBIT margin of 13.6% (R12) is a record, vs 9.4–10.5% in 2021–2024. Management itself points to increased price volatility in many raw materials and high inflation expectations, while the company lowers prices. The margin is vulnerable to a normalisation.
