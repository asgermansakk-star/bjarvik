    • Margin and earnings

    • Project stage
      Hennes Bay has only a conceptual mine study (2025); PEA, environmental impact assessment and processing concession are in progress, and the concession application is only planned for the end of 2027. Bidjovagge in Norway has only a PEA from 2021. It is many years until any production.

    • Quality of growth
      The updated resource (90.7 Mt @ 1.03% CuEq, 0.75% Cu and 20.9 g/t Ag) is +72% in copper equivalent, but the cut-off has been lowered from 0.8% to 0.6% CuEq; at the same cut-off Dingelvik grew only 26%. 70% is Inferred, and a copper grade of 0.75% is low for an underground mine.

      Copper and silver are in-demand metals, and the project has a railway, motorway and 400 kV power close by. But the company is a pure explorer with no technological advantage and is at the mercy of the copper price.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Exploration company with no revenue (formerly Arctic Gold; 1:10 reverse split in March 2025). P/E, PEG, ROIC and FCF margin are not meaningful, and there is no analyst coverage on stockanalysis (the forecast page gives 404). The company reports only semi-annually; latest accounts are as of 30/6-2026, and cash at 30/9 has not been published, so runway is an estimate based on spending in H1. 'Other income' of SEK 9.3m in H1 corresponds to capitalised exploration work (9.2m), so the reported loss understates cash burn. There is no PEA or other economic assessment of Hennes Bay; the resource is 70% Inferred. The shareholder list and any insider sales have not been investigated beyond the issue in April 2026. The September quarter activity report had not been published at the time of the analysis.
