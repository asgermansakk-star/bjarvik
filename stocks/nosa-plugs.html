    In plain words

    A score of 55 out of 100 points to a small company with both weaknesses and bright spots. It has never made an operating profit over a full year, has dropped its 2026 targets, and cash has fallen to 11.1m kr. On the plus side, its customers are spread out, and last quarter showed a profit before depreciation. Watch for new shares: the count has almost quadrupled since 2022.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • Gross margin
      Gross margin fell to 63% in Q3 (74%) and 9M (73%) because Nozoil is bought in from an external manufacturer, unlike the in-house nose plugs; work to raise Nozoil's margin has been put on hold until the technical file is approved.

    • Quality of growth
      The 180% growth in Q3 is largely due to the acquisition of Pharmacure/Nozoil (consolidated from September 2025): B2C rose from kr 0.8m to 6.0m, while B2B grew 46%. Smell Training sells below plan, has not obtained the expected reimbursement status, and sales are also expected to disappoint in Q4.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat
      The core product Odor Control is a menthol-scented nose plug that is neither a medical device nor protective equipment and cannot be CE-marked; Nozoil is a sesame-oil-based nasal spray, which is bought in. The patents give protection in a narrow niche, but the scale is small (9 employees), and sales go through distributors.
      storage.mfn.se

      Pharmacure was bought for about kr 27.2m (47.5% in cash at closing, the rest over 27 months) with a promise of significant synergies and payback within three years; a year later the synergies are delayed because Nozoil's technical file is still not approved.

      The company has abandoned the 2026 targets of kr 50m in revenue and a 15% EBITDA margin (9M: 27.3m and 4%).
