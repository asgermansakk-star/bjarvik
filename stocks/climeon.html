    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

SEK 155m, and these are the figures the findings are based on. Stockanalysis' P/TBV (1.54) is not comparable with history, because capitalised development was moved from 'deferred charges' to intangible assets in 2026. Stockanalysis' insider ownership of 34.7% is not confirmed: according to the owner list, board members Thomas Öström and Olle Bergström own approx. 2.7% of the capital. Insider trades outside the issues and peers (other ORC suppliers) have not been investigated in depth. The ownership interest in Baseload Capital (SEK 20.8m) is valued at level 3 and cannot readily be realised.
