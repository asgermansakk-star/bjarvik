    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a small residential landlord whose share trades at about an 18% discount to property value after debt. Returns are low, growth was bought with loans and new shares, and only 14% of debt has a fixed rate, so rising rates could hurt. The share barely trades. The chairman has bought shares for almost 40m kroner, and the outlook has been raised.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • Margin and returns
      Operating return is low: EBVAT relative to average equity has been 3.5–4.1% every year 2021–2025 (3.62% in 2025), and ROIC is 3.3% vs a WACC of 3.9%. Return on equity swings with the revaluations: 38%, 22%, −15%, 1.6% and 16% in 2021–2025.
      storage.googleapis.com
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      Growth was bought with debt and new shares. Property values rose about 35% in 2025, and interest-bearing debt grew from kr 579m to 761m (principal) in a year. The company itself writes that the EBVAT margin has fallen from 47% to 32% because of interest. Intrinsic value per share has risen only from kr 7.57 (2021) to kr 9.52 (June 2026), about 5% a year.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Moat and scale
      A small portfolio of DKK 1.46bn (approx. 57,000 m²) with 7 employees. The company has no scale advantages against larger landlords like Jeudan, and 57% of residential leases are on cost-based rent or the value of the rental, so pricing power is regulated.

      −3

      The acquisitions are partly financed with 40% preference shares, issued at kr 8.35 with kr 0.40 in annual dividend. That corresponds to approx. 4.8% after tax, while the portfolio's required return is 4.62%, so there is only a thin added value for ordinary shareholders. In the October buyback, kr 5.0m went to preference shares at kr 8.35 and only kr 0.1m to ordinary shares.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: price vs intrinsic value is the main multiple. The score applies to the common share (CPHCAP ST). The company also has a listed 40% preference share 2032 without voting rights (approx. 15.9m outstanding after the buyback), which receives DKK 0.40 in annual dividend vs DKK 0.06 for the common share. stockanalysis calculates market cap (384.8m) and P/B 0.63 on the common shares only, but equity of 623.3m covers both classes. The company's own intrinsic value (DKK 9.52 per share on all 65.5m shares) is used instead: P/NAV approx. 0.82. Trailing P/E 4.2 is based on value adjustments and a one-off tax asset of approx. 15m (NTR Holding) and is not used. No analysts cover the stock (no price target, no forward estimate), so PEG and upside are empty. The periodic announcement for Q3 2026 only comes 10/11-2026 and is not included. The full terms of the preference shares (redemption price, conversion in 2032) have not been investigated. The tenants' split between residential and commercial and the exact vacancy are not stated (the company only writes 'low vacancy').
