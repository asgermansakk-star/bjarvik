    • Concentration, macro and currency
      Almost all of the NAV is one minority stake of 38.22% in a Brazilian small cap. The Bemobi price and BRL feed directly into the stock: a fall from the average of the last 50 days (R$27.92) to R$24.93 has already cost approx. 10% of NAV. Brazil's high interest rate and the currency are a direct risk.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −5
    • Realisation risk
      The strategy is to realise Bemobi, but the stake of 32.7m shares equals approx. 33 trading days at average volume (approx. 0.99m shares per day). An exit therefore requires a block discount or a buyer for the whole company, and no concrete sale is under way.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Cash flow in the core asset
      Bemobi's free cash flow fell 53% in a year to R$45m TTM after leasing (R$113m in 2024). That is only about 29% of net income (R$160m), and P/FCF is 44. Distributions to Otello are therefore small (USD 1.3m in 1H26 vs 4.4m in 1H25).
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Financing of buybacks
      Otello's own operating cash flow is negative (−USD 0.9m in 1H26), and the buybacks of USD 14.3m in 2025 and USD 5.7m in 1H26 are paid from cash, which fell from USD 15.9m to 10.6m in the first half. At the same pace the cash is used up within about a year, unless Bemobi shares are sold or the distributions increase.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

      Oslo Børs put Otello on the 'Penalty Bench' 4/5-2026 because the annual report came too late. The annual report at the same time changed the 2H25 figures on five points: option costs, recognition of Bemobi distributions (−USD 5.2m) and revaluation of Life360 and Alliance Venture. That is weak reporting quality for a company with one asset.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Holding company with no operations of its own (2.5 FTEs): about 90% of the value is the 38.22% of Bemobi Mobile Tech (BMOB3, B3 in Brazil). P/E, ROIC and FCF for Otello itself are not meaningful, and Bemobi is accounted for under the equity method, so P/B understates the net asset value. Assessed on price vs a self-calculated look-through NAV: 32.72m Bemobi shares at R$24.93 (6/11) at USD/BRL 5.50 and USD/NOK 9.43 give about NOK 1.40bn; plus an estimated cash position of about NOK 86m (the 30/6 cash adjusted for buybacks, the patent instalment and the Bemobi distribution after 30/6), Life360 and Alliance Venture for about NOK 38m and a deduction of about NOK 30m in option and severance obligations. NAV about NOK 22.2 per share and about 21% discount are our estimate, not stated by the company. The historical discount (about 31% at end-2024 and about 37% at end-2023) is based on P/B, while Bemobi was written down to market value. No analysts cover the stock, and stockanalysis has no forward estimate, so PEG is n/a. Bemobi reports 3Q26 on 12/11, after this analysis. Not examined (0 points): customer concentration in Bemobi (historically telecom operators) and competition in the Brazilian payments market.
