    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Strong seasonal variation: the R12 figures (EBIT margin 18.4%, ROIC 30%) are lifted by a record Q4 2025, while 9M 2026 gave only an 11.1% EBIT margin. Analysts' FY2026 estimate (EPS 5.48, revenue SEK 2.0bn) is below the TTM figures and may be outdated; forward P/E 25.3 rests on a steep rise in NTM earnings. Only 6–7 analysts cover the stock. Peer multiples for Saab, Kongsberg and Hensoldt are from their latest Bjarvik scores, not fetched anew. Customer shares per state have not been compiled, and the ownership structure beyond the insiders' 1.28% has not been investigated.
