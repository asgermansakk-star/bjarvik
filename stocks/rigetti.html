    In plain words

    Rigetti gets 28, mostly because of the price: the share costs about 217 times revenue and is the most expensive of the three quantum companies on next year's figures. Revenue fell 28% in the third quarter, the loss is growing, and management has only sold shares. In September the US government received about 2.3% of the shares, without voting rights and without paying cash, in return for up to $100m in support, but only $43.9m is secured, and nothing had been received by 30 September. On the plus side, the company has about $497m in cash and no debt. Watch whether the customer C-DAC approves the system in the fourth quarter, which the full-year target rests on.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). Since 29 Sep: the Q3 report and the 10-Q (10 Nov) have come out, the government stake is final and the number of shares has now been checked in the 8-K, and the market cap has fallen from about $4.7bn to $2.7bn. Government stake (verified in the 8-K of 8 Sep and 10-Q note 16): on 4 Sep 2026 the subsidiary Rigetti & Co entered into an OTA under the CHIPS Act for up to $100m ($43.9m made available immediately, then $29.9m and $26.2m when the department approves the milestones at its own discretion); as an 'inducement' Rigetti issued 7,739,938 shares to the Commerce Department on 8 Sep at an implied price of $12.92 (about 2.3%), with no cash payment. The department votes only on mergers and changes to the shares' rights, may only sell shares corresponding to support paid out, has registration rights, and a buyer of the company must assume the obligations. If the department terminates, Rigetti can buy back shares corresponding to unpaid support for $1; in case of material breach, amounts paid out can be reclaimed as debt. At 30 Sep nothing had been received. The stake is treated as one finding in T3; the grant must be spent on project costs and gives no Plus. Losses: P/E and PEG are meaningless; the GAAP result (−$3.1m in Q3) is flattered by a $25.3m gain on warrants, so operating profit is used. 11.7m warrants at $11.50 expire on 2 Mar 2027 and are out of the money. Annual guidance (about $20m) assumes that C-DAC accepts the 108-qubit system in Q4.
