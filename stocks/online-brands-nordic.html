    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company accounts under K3 with scheduled amortisation of goodwill (approx. SEK 24m a year). Reported net income and P/E are therefore near zero or n/a, while the EBITA level is markedly higher; transition to IFRS is planned for 2027. History before 2022 is not usable (1:100 reverse split in December 2021 and very small revenue in 2020), so multiples are only compared with 2023–2025. There is no analyst coverage on stockanalysis (no forecast page), so there is no forward estimate; the price assessment uses the company's own forecast for 2026 (approx. SEK 740m in revenue and SEK 65m in EBITDA, pro forma). The peer comparison is based on other Nordic e-commerce stocks scored the same day (Boozt, BHG, Apotea), not on a full peer group. Customer concentration in the retailer channel of Tanrevel and Frank Dandy (e.g. the framework agreement with Matas) has not been examined in detail. The share is very illiquid (approx. 17,000 shares a day).
