    In plain words

    Bjarvik gives 84 out of 100 to a small Norwegian savings bank (the security is an equity certificate). Profit per certificate fell and growth outran capital, so the bank is issuing new certificates worth about 250m Norwegian kroner at a 10% discount. On the plus side, the yield is about 6% and only 0.32% of loans are problem loans. The issue needs approval on 10 November.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Savings bank with equity certificates (equity certificate ratio 52.1%). stockanalysis' P/B (0.60) and ROE are calculated on total equity including the savings bank's fund; T2 therefore uses the bank's own figures per equity certificate (book value NOK 291). No goodwill, so P/TBV = P/B. EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful for a bank, and the dividend is measured against net profit. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), and analyst coverage is thin or absent. The directed issue (833,333 new certificates at NOK 300) has not yet been completed: it requires approval at the general meeting 10/11-2026 and Finanstilsynet's approval, and delivery is expected approx. 18/12; a repair issue of up to 186,666 certificates and an employee issue of NOK 6m may come on top. No separate figure has been found for the stage 2 share. Customer concentration beyond the sector split and any new insider trades after 5/11 have not been investigated further.
