    • AI disruption of IT services
      The share fell 8.4% to a 52-week low of EUR 89 in June 2026 when Accenture cut guidance; the sector is being sold off on fears that AI agents will pressure hourly-based contracts and prices. The share is −13% over a year and −31% since 2023.
      investing.com
      −6
    • Earnings decline and restructuring
      Net income fell 31% to €498m and normalised EPS 11.9% to €5.29 in H1 2026; the Fit for Growth restructuring costs €628m in H1 of a framework of about €700m, and organic FCF in H1 was only €37m.
      capgemini.com
      −3
    • Debt
      Net debt rose from €3.3bn (2024) to €6.45bn at June 2026 after the WNS purchase and a €800m bond in May; net debt/EBITDA has risen from 1.1 to about 2.6 (including leases), and higher financing costs drag net income down.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

      The subsidiary Capgemini Government Solutions entered into a contract with the US immigration police ICE (framework of over USD 365m); the group acknowledged a lack of control over the unit and is now selling it to ITC Federal after public criticism.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    IT consulting and outsourcing do not fit the list exactly; nearest industry is 'Software, cloud & internet'. Forward P/E 7.8 is based on analysts' normalised EPS (€12.7 for 2026), while reported EPS TTM is €7.88 (trailing P/E 13.1) – the difference is mainly restructuring (Fit for Growth, €628m in H1) and amortisation of acquired intangibles. Net debt €6.45bn at 30/6 according to the company (€7.9bn incl. leasing at stockanalysis). Growth of 11.3% at constant exchange rates includes about 6.5 points from acquisitions (WNS, Cloud4C). The sale of Capgemini Government Solutions to ITC Federal (0.4% of revenue) has not yet been completed. The 13th employee share programme (ESOP) is subscribed in November and dilutes if not offset by buybacks. Customer concentration and insider trades have not been examined in depth.
