    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company with listed Swedish small caps: assessed on price to net asset value (NAV), which Svolder publishes weekly (kr 63 per share on 30/10 2026, B share traded at a 1% discount). Stockanalysis shows 'revenue' and earnings that consist of dividends and fair-value changes, so P/E, P/S, ROIC, margins and PEG are meaningless, and the PEG formula (n/a) may give a skewed result. Stockanalysis' P/B of 1.15 is based on equity at May 2026 and is outdated; the NAV discount has been used instead. No analysts cover the share (the forecast page does not exist), so there are no upside or revision points. The financial year runs from September to August; the figures are from the year-end report and the 2025/26 annual report, the Q1 report is due in December. The individual portfolio companies' accounts have not been examined separately, only their contribution to NAV. The annual meeting on 23/11 2026 will consider voluntary liquidation, as every year; the board proposes continued operation. Svolder is not subject to a bid.
