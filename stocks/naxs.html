    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company (fund of funds in Nordic private equity): P/E, PS and EV/EBIT are based on value changes and are not meaningful; the valuation is price vs net asset value (NAV). About 86% of NAV is fund interests at level 3, which the managers value themselves. No analysts and no forward estimate on stockanalysis. Not examined (0 points): peers' discounts among Swedish PE funds of funds, the development of the underlying JAB consumer companies (about 25% of NAV) and any ties between the board and the manager Mimir. The ownership stake of Molcap/Oliver Molse is from the proposal for the extraordinary general meeting in November 2025 and may have changed.
