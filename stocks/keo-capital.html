    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Formerly Maha Capital (oil company), which on 2/4-2026 bought the fintech company KEO World. Q2 2026 is the first quarter with the fintech operation, so there is no comparable history; stockanalysis history and multiples from before the acquisition (P/B 0.83–1.61 in 2021–2025) cover a different company and are used only as background. Accounts are in USD, the share trades in SEK. The Q3 report comes on 24/11-2026, and the purchase price allocation (goodwill/intangible assets) is still preliminary. The value of the stake in PetroUrdaneta (Venezuela) cannot be assessed until the first reserve report is available (expected H2 2026), and there is no NAV. No analysts cover the stock, so there is no forward P/E, price target or PEG. The precise customer concentration in the loan book is not disclosed and has not been examined (0 points, so the grade is a cap); only the related-party receivable is included. KEO Aggregator owns approx. 40%. A non-binding LOI with Lionheart on listing KEO Energy in the USA expired on 31/8 without an agreement.
