    In plain words

    The score is 58 out of 100: a large US railroad that Union Pacific has agreed to buy. The main drags are shares that are expensive relative to earnings and cash flow, and a dividend that eats about three quarters of free cash. Record revenue and a strong position in the eastern US help. Watch the regulator's merger ruling, due no earlier than the second half of 2027.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
