    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio is 51.9% (48.4 at year-end), net debt/EBITDA 8.4× (7.9), and interest cover only 2.7× vs a cap of 1.8. Debt maturity is 2.5 years (2.8) and the fixed-interest period 1.9 years (2.3) with 67% hedging (72.8). About kr 4.4bn (42%) of loans fall due in 1-2 years and kr 4.8bn (46%) in 2-3 years. The company has no credit rating, 92% of debt is secured bank loans, and the Velux purchase for kr 1.0bn after the quarter adds further debt.
