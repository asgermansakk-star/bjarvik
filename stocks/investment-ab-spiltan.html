    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company on NGM Main Market: assessed on price to net asset value (NAV), which Spiltan publishes monthly (kr 270.96 per share on 31/10 2026 or kr 274.52 per outstanding share; a price of kr 182 on 5/11 gives approx. 33% discount). Stockanalysis' revenue, margins, P/E, ROIC and FCF consist of fair-value changes, dividends and sales of holdings and are meaningless for an investment company, so the PEG formula (n/a) may give a skewed result. There is no forecast page, no analysts and no price target, so there are no upside or revision points. 47% of NAV is unlisted holdings, which Spiltan values itself (level 3); the 30-plus unlisted contracting companies, Spiltan Fonder's latest 2026 accounts and the property companies have not been reviewed individually, only their contribution to NAV. The board's and family's combined ownership share is calculated from the list of owners at 31/12 2025. Spiltan is not subject to a bid.
