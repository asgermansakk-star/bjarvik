    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −27 of −30
    • Margin
      Revenue of only SEK 10.8m (TTM), almost all from the stem cell bank Cellaviva, vs an operating loss of SEK 42.6m (operating margin −395%). The loss has been SEK 35–43m a year since 2022, and costs rose 17% in H1 2026.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Pipeline and quality of growth
      ProTrans-Young (NXTCL-T1D) did not meet the primary endpoint (C-peptide difference +0.043, p=0.46), and no secondary endpoints were significant. 12–21-year-olds showed no effect after either 12 or 24 months. The 'numerical' advantage in 7–11-year-olds is descriptive, and 4 of 15 treated are missing 12-month data. The paediatric programme now has to be redesigned.
      mfn.se
      −10
    • Moat
      The whole path to approval in adults rests on one small randomised study and its long-term follow-up. The treatment is an MSC cell therapy from umbilical cord, a class with many late failures, and the new candidate is simply ProTrans with a new name (NXTCL).
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The company has cash of SEK 21m and burns SEK 11m a quarter, but has at the same time set up an office and subsidiary in Hong Kong, taken part in trade missions to Japan and built up the QVance laboratory. Costs are rising while the core programme lacks funding.
      mfn.se
      −4
