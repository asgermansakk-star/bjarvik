    In plain words

    A score of 66 is middling: the share is cheap, but the company is heavily indebted and earns little. Profit is low (4.4% of revenue), the return on invested money is only 3.3%, and debt is 3.2 times earnings against its own ceiling of 2.5. The chair and CEO have bought shares, and cash flow rose. The two analysts' targets are about 37% above the price; watch the debt.
