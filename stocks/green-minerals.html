    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Exploration company in seabed minerals with no revenue, licences or own resource; the stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (stockanalysis shows Oslo Børs). P/E, PEG, ROIC and FCF margin are not meaningful, and there is no analyst coverage (the forecast page gives 404). The company reports only semi-annually; latest figures are as of 30/6-2026, so cash and the bitcoin holding today are an estimate. The large resource in the presentation is the Norwegian Offshore Directorate's national estimate for the Norwegian shelf, not the company's own. Ownership behind the largest shareholders (Telinet Invest 14.7%, Anderson Invest 9.5%) has not been investigated, so insider ownership may be higher than the 3.9% stockanalysis shows. The strategic review from December 2025 may lead to a sale, a merger or a change of business; no bid or agreement has been published. Customer concentration is not relevant without revenue.
