    In plain words

    The score is 54 out of 100. The company builds ambulances and hearses, but sales have fallen three years running and profit has only recently turned positive. Cash is almost zero and the owners support operations. The order book has grown, and a new deal of about SEK 51m with a Swedish region helps, though such deals do not commit the regions.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen three years in a row: from SEK 264.5m in 2023 to SEK 223.0m (−16%), 172.4m (−23%) and 76.1m in H1 2026 (−15%). Management calls it 'lönsamhet före volym', but the company has become smaller, not stronger.

    • Scale and moat
      Nilsson is a small coachbuilder in Laholm that converts purchased chassis and wins ambulance contracts in price tenders. Sales outside the Nordics fell from SEK 49.8m in 2023 to SEK 19.9m in 2024 and SEK 1.4m in 2025. Exports are thus effectively gone, and the Volvo V90 hearse is being discontinued.

    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow was −SEK 1.5m in H1 2026 (SEK 2.2m the year before) because inventory and trade receivables rose. FCF fluctuates strongly with working capital (−6.1, +14.6, +0.7 and +SEK 6.1m in 2022–2025), and the 2025 cash flow came from an inventory decline of SEK 10.9m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Customer concentration
      The ambulances are sold through public tenders to individual Swedish regions. One framework agreement such as Kronoberg (up to 25 vehicles, about SEK 51m over two years) equals about a sixth of two years' revenue. The agreements apply to 'up to' a number of vehicles and do not commit the regions, so a lost tender is felt immediately.
      mb.cision.com

      Shortage of critical components limited production in Q1 2026. The company depends on chassis from Volvo and Mercedes, and the V90 hearse is being replaced by the electric ES90, which is still under development. Production is to move to a new factory in mid-2027.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap on Spotlight (market value about SEK 22–24m, 29 employees) with no analyst coverage and no forward estimates, so PEG is n/a and there is no upside. stockanalysis shows a price of 4.72 from 30/9-2026, while MFN shows 5.20 after the Kronoberg agreement on 2/10, so the price data may be outdated. Trailing P/E of 3.6 includes a waiver of shareholder loans of SEK 2.0m in 2025 (financial income); adjusted P/E is about 5–6, and the company pays no tax because of tax loss carry-forwards. The interim reports have not been reviewed by the auditor. The Q3 report comes on 13/11-2026 and is not included. The owner OnWheels Mobility AB (29.9%) has not been mapped, and it has not been examined whether it is related to the board. Insider trades after 2024 have not been examined. Cyclical is set to false: the company builds ambulances and hearses on purchased Volvo and Mercedes chassis, and demand follows public tenders, not a price or inventory cycle.
