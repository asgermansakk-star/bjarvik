    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a Swedish collection of small food and health brands, priced low against earnings but with thin margins and high debt. Acquisitions brought heavy dilution and a 604m Swedish kronor write-down, and debt is 3.7 times operating earnings. In its favour, management has bought shares since March. Watch the new chief executive.
