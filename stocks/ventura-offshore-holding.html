    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −14 of −30
    • Margin
      The underlying operations are thinner than the reported figures: adjusted EBITDA was USD 21.9m in Q2 on revenue of 59.8m (37%), and after depreciation of about USD 35m a year the EBIT margin is about 20%. The reported operating margin of 41% includes USD 11.8m of non-cash contract income in Q2. Analysts expect a loss in 2026 (EPS −USD 0.27), because two of three own rigs are in the shipyard from July/September to January 2027.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Return on capital
      ROIC of 21.5% (stockanalysis) is based on earnings including non-cash contract income. Without it, operating profit is approx. USD 50m on approx. USD 470m of rigs and equipment, equal to a return of only approx. 8–9% pre-tax vs WACC of 6.4%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Moat and scale
      Only three own rigs (DS Carolina, SSV Victoria and SSV Catarina) plus two drillships under management (Atlantic Zonda and Deep Value Driller). Contract drilling is a uniform service where the price is set by day rates. The company is far smaller than Odfjell Drilling and the US drilling companies.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Future segment
      The entire business is deepwater drilling for oil and gas in Brazil and Indonesia. Demand follows the oil companies' investments, and there is no growth leg outside fossil energy.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue from the company's own rigs has stood still at USD 53–56m per quarter since Q2 2025, and analysts expect −36% in revenue in 2026 because of the yard stays. The jump in 2027 (+68%) comes from re-contracting the same rigs, not from more assets.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −25 of −35
    • Capex, cash flow and refinancing
      Victoria (USD 78–81m), Carolina (27–30m) and spare parts (up to 19m) require about USD 125–130m in capex in total, and analysts expect FCF of −USD 60m in 2026. The USD 190m bond (10%) is repaid with a USD 250m bridge loan from DNB (SOFR + 500 bp, 12 months, bullet) and a USD 30m RCF. The refinancing has thus only been pushed out until after the contract start in January 2027. Current ratio is 0.80, and the Altman Z-score is 1.89.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Execution
      Two of three own rigs are at the shipyard in Guanabara Bay at the same time, and both the financing and the merger assume that the contracts start in January 2027. The capex estimate was due to be updated in Q3, and a large part of Victoria's bill is paid only after contract start. Carolina was also stopped by the regulator ANP in January–March 2025.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Customer concentration
      Three customers accounted for 56.9%, 22.1% and 21.0% of revenue in H1 2026, and USD 101.8m of USD 141.3m in gross revenue came from Brazil, where Petrobras is the customer for all three rigs.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Recontracting of SSV Catarina
      Catarina has secured work only into December 2026, with an extra well for about USD 21.7m for Searah (Eni/PETRONAS). There is no firm contract from 2027 for a third of its own fleet.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Merger and governance
      Non-binding LOI for a pure share merger with SED Energy Holdings, in which the Ventura owners get about 45% via 605m new shares (5.5x). The exchange price can be adjusted for capex and contract starts, and Ventura will be delisted. The buyer itself has contract expiries for half of its rigs and a lost PTTEP tender. Ventura's chairman Gunnar Eliassen is to be chairman of the combined company. The Ventura owners are swapping into a weaker and more concentrated business, and the promise to distribute 100% of FCF from 2027 now depends on the merger.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Macro
      Earnings depend on oil companies' offshore capex and day rates when the contracts are to be renewed. The Brazil contracts run to 2029 and beyond, so the risk lies mostly in Catarina and in the day rates at the next renewal.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list), and the company reports in USD. Reported earnings are inflated by non-cash income from amortisation of an unfavourable contract liability (USD 72m over the last four quarters), so trailing P/E 3.7, ROIC 21.5% and EV/EBITDA 2.6 at stockanalysis have not been used; the valuation builds on the company's adjusted EBITDA (USD 85m TTM). Non-binding LOI (11/9-2026) on a pure share merger with SED Energy Holdings (5.5 ENH shares per Ventura share, adjustable for capex and contract start). It is not a special situation under 1.4, but the price partly follows the exchange ratio. The Q3 report only comes 27/11-2026, so the capex update for Carolina and Victoria is not included. Insider ownership and trades have only been partly examined (chair Gunnar Eliassen owns about 2m shares); no new insider purchases have been found. Only 3 analysts cover the stock. The history is short (listed June 2024).
