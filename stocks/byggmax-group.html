    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −13 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin is 6.0% over 12 months (EBITA 6.4%) vs 11.1% in 2021 and still below the company's own target of at least a 7% EBITA margin. The recovery from the bottom of 2.0% in 2023 is a rebound, not a new level.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC is 6.65% vs WACC of 7.16%, and ROE is only 10%. Goodwill of kr 2.0bn from the acquisitions (Skånska Byggvaror, Right Price Tiles, Lavpris Træ) makes up over two thirds of equity of kr 2.8bn (P/TBV 6.7), without the group having grown.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue over 12 months is kr 6.29bn vs 7.65bn in 2021. Like-for-like sales grew only 1.6% in the first nine months of the year (5.1% in Q3), sales in Other Nordic rose only 0.1% currency-adjusted, and the number of stores has fallen from 212 to 210.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Moat
      The business model is a low-price model with a selected range of building materials, where the customer collects the goods. That gives limited differentiation against other builders' merchants and discount chains, and management is now launching a new 'next step in the transformation' to become relevant to more customers.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
