    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (market cap approx. DKK 24m) with no analyst coverage, no forward estimates and no PEG; the price on stockanalysis is from 2/11 and trading is very thin. Movinn sublets apartments with up to 3 months' notice (lease obligation DKK 11.9m at the end of 2025, not on the balance sheet under the Danish Financial Statements Act); stockanalysis' debt of DKK 22.7m includes drawings on the credit facility and other long-term debt, while deposits for the apartments (DKK 15.3m) are shown as an asset, so the real EV is lower than the DKK 46m. Q3 2026 is not reported until 13/11, so H1 2026 is the latest accounts. Customer concentration is not disclosed in the reports and has not been examined (0 points). The web search quota was used up, so the research is based on company announcements via Nasdaq and stockanalysis; Danish press has not been reviewed. The master lease for the aparthotel (95 units, opening May 2027) is not known in detail (term, rent, guarantees).
