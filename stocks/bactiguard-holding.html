    In plain words

    The score is middling: high profit on each item sold, but almost no profit at the end. Revenue is standing still, the share is expensive relative to the tiny profit, and over half of sales come from one partner. The bank loan expires in February 2027. In its favour, the third quarter was the strongest growth in ten years and the chairman's company bought shares. The share trades very little.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
