    In plain words

    NOBA gets 92. The bank mostly lends unsecured consumer loans, and losses are near their lowest level, so a weaker economy or higher rates could hit hard. Lending is growing 15%, deposits only 6.5%, and capital sits at the bottom of its target. On the plus side, the quarter beat expectations and the bank earns about 30% a year on its capital. Watch owner Nordic Capital, which keeps selling shares.

    • Business model and moat
      Earnings rest on unsecured personal loans and credit cards (kr 128bn of 151bn in loans) with an interest margin of 8.3% and credit losses of 2.4% of loans. The product is a standard loan sold largely via loan brokers in competition with other niche banks and fintechs; the moat is credit models and costs, not customer lock-in.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −11 of −35
    • Credit risk and macro
      The credit loss level has fallen ten quarters in a row to 2.4%, and provisions amount to kr 13.5bn (8.3% of gross loans). Management itself mentions rising market expectations of rate hikes in the Nordics; unsecured consumer loans are the first place a weaker economy and higher interest rates show up, and credit losses are close to the cyclical bottom.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital and funding
      CET1 fell from 14.1% to 13.2% – at the bottom of the 13–15% target and 290 bp above the requirement of 10.3%. Lending grew 15%, while deposits grew only 6.5%, so growth is increasingly financed via repo and secured funding (24.9 vs 20.9bn) and bonds (6.4 vs 4.1bn); LCR fell from 171% to 144% and NSFR from 112% to 108%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Governance and ownership overhang
      The private equity fund Nordic Capital owns 51.6% and controls the bank; Sampo sold 14.7m shares at kr 85 in September, and the owners already sold about 10% of the shares earlier in 2026, before the lock-up expired. A new 90-day lock-up expires around the turn of the year, so there is a persistent selling overhang. Insiders own only 0.45%.

      Chief Technology Officer (CTO) Adam Wiman is leaving the bank after Q1 2027, shortly after the migration to the new core banking platform, and chairman Hans-Ole Jochumsen is being replaced at the extraordinary general meeting in November.
