    • Quality of growth
      Profit from property management fell to SEK 156m (161) in 9M, and net operating income fell to SEK 386m (395). The rise per share to SEK 1.13 (1.11) is due to buybacks. Earnings capacity per share fell to SEK 1.63 at 1/10-2026 from SEK 1.82 three months earlier and SEK 1.68 a year earlier, because annual financing costs rose from 229 to 256m. Comparable rent rose 3.0%.

    • Future segment and portfolio quality
      The portfolio is mainly in small and mid-sized municipalities outside the big cities (e.g. Falköping, Oskarshamn, Sundsvall and, until now, Skaraborg). Residential vacancy is down to 4.4% (a record low for the company), but still clearly higher than residential peers in the Stockholm area such as Heba (0.3%) and John Mattson (1.3%), and the total economic occupancy rate is only 93.9%.

      −2

      Selling Skara, Skövde and Mariestad at 16% below book value to buy back shares at approx. 0.36 × NRV is arithmetically value-creating per share (kr 142m bought back in 12 months, 5.1% of the shares). But it is done with a loan-to-value ratio of 50% and an interest cover of 1.8 ×, where debt reduction would have strengthened the balance sheet.
