    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a small, debt-free software company with steady income but limited room to grow. Growth has fallen from 17-19% to 9-11%, the company has one product for one industry, and few shares trade each day. In its favour, it has no debt, 92% of sales repeat and the dividend has risen every year. Watch that the share is hard to trade.

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 2 findings    −6 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Growth is organic and subscription-based (92% recurring), but it has fallen from 17–19% in 2021–2023 to 9% in 2025 and 11% in 9M 2026; Q3 showed 14% and ARR +14% to SEK 103.9m, driven by a net 26 more paying customers in a year. One strong quarter does not restore the earlier growth level.

    • Market and moat
      One product (transport planning system) for one industry in the Nordics with just over 600 customers; the financial targets apply only to the Nordic market, so the growth room is limited. The system is the hub of customers' operations (high switching costs), but international TMS providers and AI-based order handling may put pressure on it over time.
