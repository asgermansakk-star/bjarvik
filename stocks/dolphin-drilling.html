    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list). The accounts are kept in USD while the price is in NOK; converted at about 9.9 NOK/USD from stockanalysis' TTM figures. The latest report is Q2 2026 (30/6); Q3 comes 27/11-2026. The sale of Blackford Dolphin (agreed 26/10, closing expected about 5/11) is not yet in the balance sheet, so net debt, EV and order book (USD 557m firm at 30/6) are before the sale. The 300:1 reverse split in January 2026 makes stockanalysis' historical share counts and P/B history unreliable; dilution is therefore measured on the company's own announcements. Only 2–3 analysts cover the stock, and forward P/E 12.3 builds on EPS of about USD 0.02 for 2027. The rigs' build years and the exact day rates have not been examined. The non-binding settlement proposal from GHL (Nigeria) is only mentioned as background. The company is in 'preliminary, non-binding discussions' about a possible merger; there is no bid.
