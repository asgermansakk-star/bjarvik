    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The multiple history is short because Sats only made money again from 2023. Industry set to the consumer category, as the list has no category for leisure and fitness. The owner list (Modular Finance) could not be read, so major shareholders and the board's ownership have not been examined beyond stockanalysis' insider share of 6%. Possible GDPR fines after the data breach in March have not been quantified. Only 5 analysts cover the stock.
