    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    ALNG ASA is the former Awilco LNG (name change in June 2026). The shares are traded in NOK, but the accounts are kept in USD (about NOK 10/USD). There is no analyst coverage (stockanalysis has no forecast page, P/E and forward P/E are n/a). The market value of the two TFDE ships from 2013 has not been found; book value is USD 279m, so P/B 0.58 may be too optimistic if broker valuations are below it. stockanalysis' P/FCF of 5.85 is before lease repayments; after repayments, FCF was negative (TTM). stockanalysis shows +14% in share count (average), but the company issued 86.4m new shares (+65%) in April and June 2026. The LNG trading has not started, and there are no figures for it. Short data has not been found. The Q3 report comes on 18/11-2026.
