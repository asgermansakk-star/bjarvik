    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Singulus is NOT in restructuring: the bond (4.5%, 2016/2026, EUR 12m) and the old loans were repaid in 2026 and replaced by a secured loan of EUR 29m (to March 2031) and a secured convertible of up to EUR 3.558m from an unnamed global technology partner. The ordinary calibration is used. The share count is 8.895m (Triumph's 1,489,997 shares = 16.75%); stockanalysis shows 10.0m at the balance sheet date, which is not confirmed. Market cap is calculated at EUR 96.1m (8.895m × EUR 10.80, about DKK 717m), above Nordnet's DKK 685m. The conversion price (about EUR 1.98) is derived from EUR 3.558m / up to 1.8m shares and not confirmed in the actual terms. Equity is negative (EUR −62.6m according to stockanalysis), partly due to large customer prepayments (38.4m). The partner's identity, the loan's interest rate and security, and the buyer of Triumph's stake are unknown. Short interest has not been examined. The price target from 3 analysts (avg. EUR 7.40) is below the share price and was not used in the findings.
