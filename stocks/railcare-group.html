    In plain words

    Railcare Group scores 62 out of 100. The record quarter came partly from an unusual number of track renewals, so it may not last. The five largest customers make up 78% of revenue, and after paying for leased locomotives almost no free cash is left. On the plus side, the order backlog is 1.40bn kr., about twice annual revenue.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
