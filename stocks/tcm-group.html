    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a business under pressure at a fairly ordinary price (score 78 out of 100). Pulling down most: high debt, a return on invested money below what that capital costs, and heavy management turnover, with three finance chiefs and a new CEO in 18 months. Pulling up: order intake above last year and a dividend of about 6.6% of the share price. Watch the report due in November.
