    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −27 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin is −78% TTM (operating loss of SEK 40m on revenue of 51m), and there have been losses of 45–68m every year since 2021. Q3 improved (−3.9m vs −8.2m) due to lower costs, but gross margin for the first nine months fell to 58% (62%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has been flat at SEK 46–57m since 2021 and has halved since 2020. In January–September, sales fell 6.8% to 33.2m, and in Q3 they fell 2.9%, partly because of inventory adjustments at the Swedish pharmacies. Sweden accounts for 77% of sales.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has risen from 149m (2021) to 243m after repeated issues (incl. SEK 159m in 2024), and the accumulated deficit is SEK 542m. The money has been spent on operations, without revenue having grown.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Moat
      The company depends on one product, a mouth spray for colds in a competitive OTC market, with a 6.5% market share in Sweden. The MDR class III certification gives some barrier, but the large consumer health groups sell cheaper alternatives in pharmacies.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
