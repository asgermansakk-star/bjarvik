    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    JM reports primarily under the segment principle (successive settlement, project financing of housing cooperatives off balance sheet), while stockanalysis shows IFRS: IFRS debt of kr 14.2bn includes kr 11.6bn of project financing, and IFRS FCF (−kr 861m) is therefore not comparable with the segment's operating cash flow (+kr 1,740m TTM). Forward P/E of 16.9 is stockanalysis' figure; consensus EPS for 2026 of kr 3.45 corresponds to a P/E of approx. 37. Only 3 analysts cover the share, and the median price target (kr 130) is close to the price. The largest owners have not been examined; the insider trades are from insiderscreener, where some rows (Anders Wimmerstedt, prices of kr 23–490) look erroneous and have not been used. Housing development is cyclical, but the company is not flagged cyclical (like YIT, Peab and NCC). Q3 report 21 Oct 2026.
