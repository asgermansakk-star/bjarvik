    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company reports in EUR, while the share trades in SEK; stockanalysis converts. The adjusted FCF of approx. EUR 52m is our own estimate: TTM cash flow includes Q4 2025, when the largest VOI seller paid USD 31.9m, and this appears to have been booked in operating cash flow (Q4 2025: EUR 55.6m vs 22.6 in Q3 2026). Customer concentration among US veterinary chains has not been examined. The same applies to a reported Fidelio purchase of SEK 145m in August 2026, which could not be confirmed. Fidelio owns approx. 59%, and free float is only approx. 189m shares. Only 6 analysts cover the share, and the median target is at the current price.
