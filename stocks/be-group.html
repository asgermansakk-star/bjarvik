    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −20 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Fourth year in a row with a loss: the underlying operating margin is −1.1% over the last 12 months and 0.3% in the first nine months of the year, and the return on invested capital (excluding IFRS 16) is −2.2%. At stockanalysis ROIC is −2.7% vs 13.8–25.1% in 2021–2022. Q3 delivered only SEK 4m in underlying operating profit on revenue of 974m, and management itself calls the level unsatisfactory.
      storage.mfn.se
      −9
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from SEK 6.9bn in 2022 to 4.0bn over the last 12 months. The 13% growth in Q3 comes from a weak comparison in Finland, where the introduction of a new business system in 2025 pressed the figures. In Sweden tonnage fell 5%, and the group's tonnage is at the same level as the year before after nine months.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat
      BE Group is an independent distributor of steel, stainless steel and aluminium with a gross margin of approx. 12%. According to the company, competition and price pressure remain significant. The Swedish business area had an underlying operating result of −SEK 12m in Q3 (−1), and the rebar business must be restructured again.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      In 2025 goodwill was written down by SEK 409m (219m in Sweden and 190m in Finland) together with capitalised expenses for the new business system. Earlier acquisitions and IT investments have thus destroyed value, and equity per share has fallen from SEK 126 in 2022 to 51, also because the issue increased the share count.

    • Cash flow
      FCF after leasing has been negative three years in a row: −SEK 28m in 2024, −127m in 2025 and −98m over the last 12 months, with lease payments of 94–97m a year. Interest-bearing net debt excluding IFRS 16 has risen to SEK 341m (278 the year before), even though the company raised 135m in the 2025 share issue.

    • Debt and financing
      Net debt including leasing is SEK 667m vs EBITDA around zero over the last 12 months, and interest cover is negative (−2.3). The financing at SEB runs to 2028 and consists mostly of factoring, with covenants on net debt ratio and available liquidity. Liquidity including overdraft has fallen to SEK 196m (247), and the equity ratio to 39.4%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Dilution
      The rights issue in Q3 2025 issued 6.49m new shares, so the share count rose from 12.98m to 19.48m (+50%) for net proceeds of SEK 135m. This is confirmed by the cash flow statement. In addition there are 250,000 warrants to management (approx. 1.3%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Execution and management
      The CEO was replaced in December 2025 (the CFO was acting CEO until Johan Wiig took over in 2026). The turnaround consists of repeated restructurings: Poland has been wound down, in Finland plate processing is being consolidated in Lappo with full effect only in 2028, and the reinforcement business in Sweden is being restructured in Q4. The result in Sweden was weaker than expected.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Macro and steel prices
      The construction market remains weak, and residential construction is at a low level. Customers plan short-term, and the weaker krone hit Sweden in Q3. Earnings swing with the steel price through inventory gains and losses (inventory gains of SEK 5m in Q3, while underlying operating profit was only 4m).
