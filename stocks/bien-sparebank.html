    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bien Sparebank ASA is a joint-stock savings bank with ordinary shares (5,680,198, unchanged), so there is no equity certificate ratio. stockanalysis' P/B 1.09 and ROE are based on equity incl. NOK 40m of hybrid bonds; T2 uses P/B on equity without hybrid capital (NOK 723m, NOK 127.3 per share, P/B about 1.15), and the history has been recalculated the same way. P/TBV equals P/B, as the bank has no goodwill. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are Q2 2026; the Q3 report is due 11/11-2026 and is not included. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so there is no real analyst coverage, and forward P/E 13.87 has an uncertain basis. The capital requirement (CET1) has not been examined, so the capital buffer has not been scored. The peer comparison is based on other Norwegian savings banks scored on the same day. Customer concentration in single exposures has not been examined (0 points).
