    In plain words

    Syrah Resources gets 39: the auditor points to material uncertainty about the company's ability to continue, and loans from two US government bodies are in default, so all debt can be called. The mine in Mozambique loses money on every tonne, and the anode plant in the US has not yet sold commercially. The US government owns no shares yet: a non-binding proposal would turn part of the loans into shares and warrants diluting about 20%, and the rest into convertible loans at 11% interest added to the debt. The share is at the bottom of its history against book value, but most of the company's total value is debt. Watch whether the deal with the government becomes binding, and whether Tesla approves the plant.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    New in Bjarvik (24 Nov 2026), scored in light of government equity stakes. Listed only on the ASX (price about A$0.062); in the US it trades OTC as SYAAF (ordinary) and SRHYY (ADR), which Nordnet's search API does not show. Accounts in USD, but stockanalysis converts to AUD (revenue A$71.5m TTM). IMPORTANT: The government 'stake' has not been completed. DFC's proposal of 25 Mar 2026 is non-binding and still was at the Q3 report on 29 Oct 2026: $31m of the DFC loan converts into 423m shares at A$0.105 in two tranches (about 15% of the current share count after conversion; about 20% with warrants), warrants for 5% fully diluted at A$0.0001, a board seat, and the rest of the DFC loan becomes a secured convertible loan (11% PIK, conversion at A$0.1982, 3 years). DOE converts $40m of its loan into the same type of convertible loan and defers interest and repayments as well as its forbearance to 20 Apr 2029. Both tracker figures ($31m converted and $40m DOE convertible) are thus correct, but it all requires binding documents, FIRB and a shareholder meeting. No analyst coverage of the ASX line on stockanalysis (the ADR has sporadic price targets). Insider trades have not been examined. The Q3 report's full cash statement (4C) has only been read via the presentation.
