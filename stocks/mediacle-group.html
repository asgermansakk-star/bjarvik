    In plain words

    A score of 61 shows a very small company selling marketing to the online gaming industry, whose share costs less than its cash. The discount has a reason: the founder owns 84%, so other owners have no say over how the money is used. Sales and margins have fallen, quarterly reports are not reviewed by the auditor, and the share barely trades. On the plus side, there is no debt.
