    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analyst coverage (stockanalysis has no forecast page), so there is neither a price target nor forward P/E; PEG is assessed by the formula only. The figures are TTM to Q2 2026; the Q3 report comes on 12/11-2026 and will include the gain of about NOK 80m on the sale of Viking Reach (Eidesvik owns 50.1%), which is a one-off and must not carry a multiple. EV and net debt include construction loans for two newbuildings that are not yet earning money, which makes EV/EBITDA higher than ongoing operations alone justify. Customer concentration is assessed only from contract announcements (no breakdown per customer found). Extremely low liquidity in the stock (about 5,400 shares per day). The broker values apply to the consolidated fleet, so part of the surplus value accrues to the minorities.
