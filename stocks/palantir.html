    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a fast-growing software company, but at an extremely high price. The share costs 73 times annual sales and about 99 times expected profit, leaving no room for error. Top executives sold tens of millions of dollars of shares and nobody bought. 42% of sales come from the US government. In its favour are 93% sales growth, record new contracts and net cash of 9.2bn dollars.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
