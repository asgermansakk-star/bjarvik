    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −32 of −35
    • Governance and accounting fraud
      EY's forensic investigation found fabricated agreements for four of five properties examined. Short-term investments of kr 170m did not exist, a reported land sale in Hallsberg had never happened, and a prohibited loan of 174m to a former board member has only been recovered with 32.8m. The corrections gave −kr 206m before tax and −323.9m on equity. The auditor issued a qualified audit opinion and a remark on internal control, and police complaints have been filed. The largest owner, Polar Structure (26.2% of capital, 29.9% of votes), was the counterparty in the unfavourable transactions.
      mfn.se
      −14
    • Liquidity, debt and financing need
      Cash was kr 8.4m at the end of Q3 vs bank debt of about 600m, of which 127.8m is short-term. Operating cash flow was −145m in 9M, and Altman Z is 0.79. The company is getting by on bridge loans from major shareholders (35m, of which 16m drawn), and after the quarter 92.9m has come in from the settlement, recovery and a deposit. The risk of new emergency loans or an issue at a low price is high.
      mb.cision.com
      −9

      The SMARTCargo project in Sigtuna has been stopped because no solution could be found for access to the site. The company may have to repay all or part of the kr 113.7m in EU support, equal to approx. 27% of market cap. The EU's decision is expected within approx. three months.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analyst coverage and no forecast page on stockanalysis (404), so PEG and upside have no basis. Stockanalysis' TTM figures include the restatements of the 2025 accounts, which the company has not allocated to quarters; quarterly comparisons with 2025 are therefore based on the original, partly incorrect figures. The adjusted net asset value (kr 16.5 per share) is the company's own and has market values only for selected properties; after the fraud case it is an uncertain anchor. The Q3 report has not been reviewed by the auditor. Insider trades, the observation status on First North and the full annual report for 2025 have not been examined (0 points). The Hallsberg sale is conditional on subdivision and infrastructure and is only settled on takeover in up to nine months. The EU decision on repayment of the SMARTCargo aid is expected within approx. three months.
