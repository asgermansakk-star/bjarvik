    The score is 70 out of 100. The share has become expensive while profit stands still: the price is up 33% in a year, and interest income fell 5.6% amid fierce competition. Analysts' price targets are about 3% below the price.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B, P/TBV and P/E, and dividend/buyback is measured against net profit. The merger between SR-Bank and Sørøst-Norge (1/10-2024) distorts the stockanalysis history: EPS growth (+27% TTM, +30% in 2025) and share count (−19% in a year) are merger effects and not used; the actual buyback is 1% (3.7m of approx. 372m shares). Only 6 analysts cover the stock (consensus Hold), and stockanalysis has only 2 EPS estimates for 2026. The peer comparison with SpareBank 1 SMN and Sparebanken Norge has not been made (stockanalysis rejected several calls); only DNB is used. The board writes that it will meet the structural consolidation in the savings bank sector 'proactively', which may mean new mergers; this is not scored.
