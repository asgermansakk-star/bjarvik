    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −26 of −30
    • Margin
      Gross loss even in the record quarter Q2 2026 (metal sales USD 6.1m, gross profit −0.4m, EBITDA −0.7m) at a gold price around USD 4,500/oz; H1 2026 produced an operating loss of USD 2.3m on 7.5m in sales (about −30%), and FY2025 an operating margin of −336%.
      goldroadplc.com
      −8
    • Resource base and documentation
      No valid resource or reserve estimate: the only NI 43-101 report (2018) showed 214,000 oz solely 'inferred', has not been validated, and management judges that drilling to confirm resources is not worthwhile. The mineral property is booked at zero, and the mine plan is based on an internal block model.
      goldroadplc.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth and scale
      Gold poured fell from approx. 1,350 oz in Q2 to approx. 750 oz in Q3 (198, 214 and 341 oz) and 468 oz in October – a run rate of approx. 3,000–5,600 oz per year vs the target of 8,000–10,000 oz. Most of the feed material is still tailings at approx. 0.7–1.2 g/t, and the mine has been placed on care and maintenance in both 2015 and 2021.
      goldroadplc.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation and dilution
      The share count has gone from approx. 4m in mid-2025 via 36.1m at the end of 2025 and 45.3m in June to 57.3m after the issue in July 2026; plus 2.95m options for management and advisers. The issue proceeds mostly went to repay shareholder loans, leasing and royalty rather than build capacity.
      goldroadplc.com
      −5

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −26 of −35
    • Cash flow and financing need
      Operations used USD 2.8m and investments USD 4.3m in H1 2026; cash was USD 1.0m at 30/6 with current liabilities USD 4.3m above current assets, and the accounts have a going concern note. The information document set aside only USD 1.25–2.85m of the issue as a buffer, so a new issue is likely if production does not rise quickly. No interest-bearing debt after July.
      goldroadplc.com
      −7
    • Execution
      The restart of the ball mill and the second filter press has been postponed three times: from late August/early September to late November and now to installation at the end of December 2026. The mill's hourly throughput fell in September and October because the ore requires longer grinding.
      goldroadplc.com
      −6
    • Governance and accounting quality
      The Q2 report corrects three errors: an intragroup loan that was never translated (IAS 21), erroneous depreciation in Q1 and warrants that were omitted from the annual report. 90% of the mine's silver revenue has been sold for the life of the mine to related party Chancery Royalty (a board seat on both sides) for USD 1m in cash plus unlisted CRL shares valued at 3.6m. The company has moved from Alberta to Malta.
      goldroadplc.com
      −6
    • Gold price dependence
      Even at gold prices of around USD 4,000–4,500/oz, operations are loss-making; a fall in the gold price hits a mine with a high fixed cost level and low grade.
      goldroadplc.com
      −3
    • Customer concentration
      All revenue comes from one customer: doré is sold to Asahi's refinery, and the company may only sell to Asahi at an agreed price unless Asahi gives written consent.
      goldroadplc.com
      −2
    • Lock-up expiry
      The lock-up for the board, management and the large pre-subscribers expires six months after the listing on 1/7-2026, i.e. around the turn of the year; about 57.6% of the shares before the issue were covered. The price (NOK 7.02) is already 30% below the issue price of NOK 10.
