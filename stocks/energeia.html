      Revenue has fallen from NOK 77.9m (2022) to 53.4m (TTM) and was flat in H1 2026 (30.6 vs 30.4m). Almost all revenue comes from the Dutch installation business ASN, whose solar market is slowing because net metering ends 1/1-2027, grid queues and new feed-in fees.

      −5
    • Capital allocation and dilution
      The share count multiplied in 2025 (from 0.47m to 9.3m split-adjusted shares) through directed issues, subsequent offerings and an underwritten rights issue at NOK 0.03 per share; the cash flow confirms NOK 64m in new equity in 2025. Retained earnings have fallen from +NOK 8.6m (2022) to −NOK 84m, and equity is only NOK 98m despite NOK 162m in paid-in premium.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7

      Only one producing plant (Drachtsterweg, 12.1 MW in the Netherlands); the Norwegian portfolio of about 553 MWdc is mainly projects without a concession. Solar project development has low barriers to entry, and part of equity is intangible assets (trademark 20.9m and capitalised development 44m).

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −28 of −35
    • Funding and going concern
      The board highlights uncertainty about going concern. Cash was NOK 12.7m 30/6-2026 after net spending of 9.7m in H1, and Energeia's share of the equity requirement in Seval Skog is about 103m – about 1.4 times the market cap. The alternative to a large issue is that Eidsiva takes a larger part of the project and dilutes Energeia's share of the value creation.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −13
    • Execution of Seval Skog
      The total capital requirement has risen from approx. NOK 312m (June 2025) to approx. NOK 403m (October 2026), partly due to a larger battery. The investment decision is conditional on a final PPA (only a letter of intent for approx. 30% of production), bank financing and equity; operation is expected only in late 2027 or in 2028.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −4
    • Governance and ownership conflict
      The chairman and a board member from the largest shareholder (Obligo Nordic Climate Impact Fund) left in August 2026, after the merger with Solgrid fell through, stating that Energeia lacks a critical mass of producing parks. The entire board has been replaced since July, and the managing director was changed twice in 2025.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −4
    • Lawsuit over PPA in the Netherlands
      The buyer of the power from Drachtsterweg is claiming approx. EUR 0.38m (approx. NOK 4m, 6% of market cap) for 2023–2025 and a new price formula for negative hours for the rest of the 10-year agreement; nothing has been provisioned, and the first court hearing is in November–December 2026.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −2
    • Concession and regulatory risk
      The concession for Store Nøkleberg (32 MWdc), the company's second large concession, has been appealed, and NVE has forwarded the objection and appeal to the Ministry of Energy; the timeline for the rest of the pipeline is uncertain.

      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro-cap company (market cap about NOK 72m) with no analyst coverage and no forward estimates; the company reports only half-yearly (latest figures 30/6-2026). The share actually trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (the company applied to delist from there in 2025 and withdrew the application), although stockanalysis states Oslo Børs. The share count is not comparable over time due to the reverse split of 1:250 in December 2025. The board has made a going concern reservation. The investment decision for Seval Skog is conditional on PPA, bank financing and equity; a possible issue had not been announced at 6/11-2026. Solar peers in the Nordics are few and not directly comparable. Sum-of-the-parts/NAV for the project portfolio has not been examined (scores 0).
