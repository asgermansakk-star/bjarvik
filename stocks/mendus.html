    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −23 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue. The operating loss is SEK 98.7m (TTM), and the company has posted losses of SEK 101–133m a year in 2021–2025. The accumulated deficit is over SEK 1bn. Costs have fallen after the Q4 2025 reorganisation, but that is savings and not earnings.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth / segment of the future
      The whole company rests on vididencel. The proof of concept is based on ADVANCE II, a single-arm phase 2a study with only 20 AML patients. Five-year survival is promising, but without a control group. The randomised CADENCE (phase 2b) and the CML studies are small and early, and there is no partner or licence agreement after many years with the programme.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat
      Orphan drug status (EU/US), Fast Track and an in-house cell line (DCOne) give some protection. But maintenance treatment of AML is a field with approved oral azacitidine and venetoclax, and in CML cheap TKIs dominate. A cell product produced at an external partner (NorthX) is expensive to scale.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      With 19 employees and about SEK 60m in cash, the company is spreading itself over four clinical tracks: CADENCE and DIVA in AML, VITAL-CML and VITAL-TFR2 in CML, and ALISON in ovarian cancer. It is an expansion into a new indication instead of focusing the little money on the most advanced AML programme.
      mfn.se
      −5

      The market cap is SEK 344m. With the Fenja loan and leasing, EV is about 330m, which is far above Swedish cancer biotechs such as Elicera and Alligator (about 130m).     Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −26 of −35
    • Funding and debt
      Cash was SEK 59.9m at 30/6 vs operating spending of SEK 33m in H1. The company itself says the money lasts only into Q2 2027, and that requires the full Fenja loan of SEK 46.5m to be drawn (interest STIBOR + 8%, floor 2%). The loan falls due 30/4-2027, so a new issue or refinancing is necessary within a few months.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Dilution
      The share count has risen from 10.0m (2021, adjusted for the 20:1 reverse split in 2024) to 64.8m, i.e. +550%, and +27% in a year. In November 2025 a directed issue of 10.5m shares was made at kr 5 (9.7% discount), and in July 2026 the company issued 2.2m C shares for bonus and board fees.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Execution and guidance
      In November 2025 the company promised the first CADENCE data in H1 2026, the first DIVA data in Q3 2026 and enough money to the end of 2026. DIVA only started in September 2026 with topline in H2 2027. There is still no CADENCE data, and the promise about the money only held because the loan was extended.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Clinical and partner risk
      All programmes are based on the same molecule, and the coming readouts are small: VITAL-CML has 24 patients, DIVA has 24 and CADENCE is in a safety phase with 20–40 patients. A weak result in one study hits the whole company, and no big pharma partner has taken vididencel on.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Governance
      The general meeting has given the board the right to receive its fees in shares at 150% of the cash amount, and employee bonuses are paid in newly issued shares 'to preserve liquidity'. Both transfer value from the minority shareholders.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Liquidity and share price risk
      Van Herk (35.7%), Flerie (22.6%) and AP4 (9.3%) together own approx. 68%, so the free float is small, and typically only SEK 0.06–0.3m is traded per day. The share price has fallen 22% in a year and approx. 94% since 2021, adjusted for the share consolidation.
      mfn.se
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Clinical biotech without revenue: P/E, PEG, ROIC and FCF margin are not meaningful. The Q3 report comes on 11/11-2026 and is not included; the figures are at 30/6-2026 plus announcements to 6/11. stockanalysis' debt figure (17.9m) covers only leasing and a small loan, not the Fenja loan (SEK 30m at 30/6, now SEK 46.5m), so the true EV is higher than the 302m shown. Only 2 analysts with price targets of kr 17.9–50 (upside over 500%), so the price targets are effectively worthless. Insider trades outside the issue in November 2025 and the status of the ilixadencel programme have not been examined (0 points), so the grade is a ceiling.
