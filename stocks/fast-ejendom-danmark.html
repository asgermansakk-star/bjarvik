    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: price vs intrinsic value is the main multiple (P/NAV 0.72 at a price of 232 and intrinsic value of DKK 323.55 at 30/9-2026). The company is in a gradual wind-down: the board decided 23/10-2025 to sell the portfolio over 'the next couple of years' without a fixed timeframe. It is not a special situation under 1.4 (no offer, no delisting), but the value depends on the sale prices. stockanalysis' P/E 5.8 and ROE 13% are lifted by value adjustments and fair value gains on the debt. Ongoing earnings after tax are estimated at approx. DKK 34m a year (profit before tax and value adjustments of DKK 39.9m in Q1–Q3 minus DKK 6.9m in price gains on loans, annualised and less 22% tax). Whether the four sold properties (DKK 86.9m) were sold above or below book value has not been stated and has not been investigated. No insider trades have been found in 2025–2026. The major shareholders' names and stakes have not been investigated; an anonymous forum comment mentions two large shareholders. No analysts cover the stock (no price target, no forward estimate), so PEG and upside are empty. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404). The stock is very illiquid.
