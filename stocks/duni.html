    • Debt
      Interest-bearing net debt has risen to kr 2,855m (1,458 a year ago), and leverage is 3.88 × EBITDA vs 1.81. Interest cover is 3.0 and Altman Z 1.85. Net financial items are −kr 102m (−40) because favourable interest rate swaps have expired.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Execution
      The move to an external logistics partner in Meppen failed: profit warning in Q2 (negative effect of kr 50–70m), backorders, double warehouse costs, higher freight and kr 65m in restructuring in 9M. The German volumes are only fully moved in Q4.

      −4
    • Macro and customer behaviour
      Restaurant visits are falling in several European countries (Germany −6.8%), HoReCa customers are focusing on costs and switching to cheaper products, and sales in the Middle East are hit by geopolitics.

    • Regulation
      The EU's packaging regulation PPWR applies from 12 August 2026 and gradually introduces requirements for reuse, materials and producer responsibility. That gives ongoing transition costs in Food Packaging, even though Duni has phased out PFAS and has Relevo for reuse.
