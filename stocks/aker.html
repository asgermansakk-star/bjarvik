    In plain words

    The score is high (90 out of 100). Aker is an investment company, so its share price is compared with the value of its holdings. Pulling it down: the earlier discount has almost vanished, about 26% of the value sits in unlisted Nscale, and oil prices affect several holdings. In its favour, the Cognite sale brings about NOK 14.7bn in cash, and total return was 53% in the first half.

    • Quality of NAV growth
      NAV per share rose to NOK 1,509 in Q3 (Q2: 1,429; end of 2025: 905), and NAV reached a record NOK 111.2bn. The Q3 increase of NOK 5.1bn came from Aker BP (+NOK 5.8bn incl. dividend) and Solstad (+1.7bn), while Aker Solutions and real estate subtracted 1.9bn. Of the year's NAV growth of NOK 44bn, however, 22bn comes from the revaluation of Nscale to a private Series C price and 22bn from oil shares and dividends, i.e. still marks and the oil price rather than underlying cash flows.

    • Capital allocation (dividend vs cash flow)
      The dividend for 2026 is DKK 58 per share (DKK 4.3bn) against expected dividend income in the holding company of over DKK 6.0bn, which must also cover operations and interest (DKK 353m in Q3 alone).     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated after the report of 2026-11-04. Investment company: stockanalysis' earnings figures (P/E 2.9, net income NOK 27.9bn TTM) are distorted by revaluations and the consolidation of, among others, Solstad Maritime, and stockanalysis only shows half-year figures (latest June 2026), so the valuation rests on net asset value (NAV) and holdings figures from Aker's own Q3 report and presentation. NAV per share was NOK 1,509 at the end of Q3 and NOK 1,550 on 3 Nov (before the NOK 29 dividend, ex-date 5 Nov); the discount is calculated against Q3 NAV less the dividend. Nscale (NOK 32.1bn, 25% of gross assets) is still carried at the Series C price from March (USD 14.6bn) and does not include dilution from the new convertible loans; the reports of a delayed or lower IPO are press sources (Telegraph via Silicon UK), which neither Nscale nor Aker has confirmed. The Cognite sale (about NOK 14.7bn) has not yet been completed. Industry set to 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage' as for Investor AB and Exor, even though the portfolio is dominated by oil (Aker BP) and AI infrastructure (Nscale). Not marked cyclical, as energy makes up about half of gross assets. The historical NAV discount is backed by quarterly figures from Q3 2024 to Q3 2026, not a full 10-year series.
