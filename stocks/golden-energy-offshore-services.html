    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list), and the company only publishes a full report half-yearly; Q1 and Q3 are simplified updates, and the Q3 figures only come 20 Nov. stockanalysis' P/B 0.67, EV NOK 555m and net debt NOK 115m are from before the cash distribution of NOK 4 per share (about NOK 282m, paid 14/8). Adjusted, that gives net debt of about 385m, equity about 373m (P/B about 1.2) and an estimated NAV of about NOK 8.7 per share on the broker valuation from June; those figures are own calculations. The TTM result includes a one-off gain of NOK 171.5m from ship sales, which has been stripped out. The price fall of 63% in a year is partly due to the distribution and the issue at NOK 8. The two largest shareholders (Clear Ocean and Pelagic) started a strategic review in May that may end in a sale of their shares; it is not a bid and does not give a special situation. stockanalysis' insider share (2.1%) understates that owners close to the board hold over 60%. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates. Peer levels are from the contemporaneous analyses of Eidesvik, Solstad Offshore and Sea1.
