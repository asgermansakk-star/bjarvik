    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Industrial holding company: about 75% of profit before tax (YTD) is share of results from Wallenius Wilhelmsen and Hyundai Glovis (equity method), so stockanalysis' P/E, ROIC and FCF figures are distorted. The NAV discount is our own estimate (market value of the two listed holdings, net cash less about USD 50m invested in the WAWI issue in October, and operations at about 8x EBITDA); the company publishes no NAV. Stockanalysis' insider ownership of 3.5% is wrong: CEO Thomas Wilhelmsen controls 54.6% via Tallyman. No analyst forecasts on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist), so upside and revision points lack a basis. USTR's proposal to extend the suspension of the port fees to 9 Jan. 2027 had not been finally adopted at the time of the analysis. Customer and pricing conditions in Ships Service have not been examined separately.
