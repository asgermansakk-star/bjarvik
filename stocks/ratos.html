    The score is 71 out of 100 for a Swedish investment company with 13 mid-sized businesses. Several earn a low return on their capital, and a large write-down on Plantasjen caused a loss in 2025. On the plus side, adjusted operating profit rose 29% in the third quarter, the CEO has bought shares, and cash flow covers the dividend about 2.6 times. The finance chief is leaving.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Ratos has since 2026 called itself an investment company (Ratos 2030), but consolidates its subsidiaries and publishes no net asset value; price vs intrinsic value is therefore measured as P/B on equity to shareholders (SEK 46 per share) and not vs a NAV. stockanalysis' TTM figures are distorted: the loss (SEK −0.72 per share) is due to the goodwill writedown on Plantasjen in Q4 2025, and EBITDA (371m) and ROIC (−0.4%) do not match the company's adjusted EBITA of SEK 2,230m; the company's own figures from the Q3 report have been used. FCF is calculated after lease repayments (SEK 889m LTM) with Q1–3 2026 plus Q4 2025. The 0.92% fall in share count on stockanalysis is not confirmed by the report (327.39 → 327.51m shares) and earns no points. Only 4 analysts cover the stock and only 2 with an EPS estimate. Ratos gives no guidance, so guidance has not been assessed (0). Sum-of-parts for the unlisted companies has not been examined.
