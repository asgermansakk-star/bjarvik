    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −8 of −30
    • ROIC
      ROIC 8.6% vs WACC 7.2% and 8.0–9.9% over the past five years: a capital-heavy business with lift, snow and housing investments that only just earns the cost of capital, and with no improvement.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Ski days and activity days were flat (6.26m vs 6.25m); the organic growth of 5% came from prices and mix, and 2 percentage points of the growth came from the acquisition of Sälens Högfjällshotell. No property sales in the year; property development is at a standstill.

      −2
    • Future segment
      The business rests on snow in the Scandinavian mountains; warmer winters require continuous large investments in snow cannons (489 new before the season) just to maintain the season, and summer operations still contribute only marginally (Q4 operating result −SEK 274m).

No formal guidance; only booking figures (+3%). Management's purchases in late October may be connected with an incentive programme; the notice of the general meeting on 4/11 has not been read. Share count is unchanged (78,376,056). Customer orientation and competition from Norwegian and alpine destinations have not been examined further.
