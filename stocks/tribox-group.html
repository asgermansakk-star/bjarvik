    In plain words

    The score is 53 out of 100: a small acquisition-driven company where owners have been heavily diluted. The share count rose 82.8% in a year, and the return on invested money is below the cost of capital. Cash flow is positive, though, after four years in the red. Watch the base-oil supply, secured only to year-end, and the fact that new acquisitions probably need more shares.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap on First North (market cap about SEK 76m) with no analyst coverage, so there is no forward estimate, and PEG shows as n/a. The accounts follow K3, where goodwill is amortised over 5 years (about SEK 8.8m a year after Fleet Tec), so EBIT, net profit and P/E understate underlying earnings, while EV/EBITDA is the most accurate measure. The TTM figures contain only two quarters with Fleet Tec (consolidated from 1/1-2026). The Q3 report comes on 13/11-2026 and is not included. Insider transactions beyond participation in the issue in December 2025 and the split of customers in Elite Oil and Fleet Tec have not been examined (0 points). The stock is very illiquid (about SEK 40,000 a day).
