    • Quality of growth and evidence
      Sevuparin failed in the randomised phase 2 study in sickle cell disease (144 patients, no difference in time to end of crisis). The new track, anaemia in chronic kidney disease, rests on an open-label phase IIa study at two Italian centres with biomarker endpoints (haemoglobin, hepcidin) without a control group.

      Sevuparin is a patented heparinoid, but anaemia in kidney disease is today treated with ESAs, iron and HIF-PH inhibitors from large companies. A company with 2 employees and preclinical/open data has no documented advantage.

      The company has switched indication from sickle cell to sepsis and now renal anaemia and malaria and lives off share issues: a unit issue in 2025, TO 2026 (9.5m) and a directed issue of SEK 7.6m in September 2026 at kr 0.38 with a 14.8% discount without pre-emption rights.
