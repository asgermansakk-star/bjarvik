    In plain words

    The score is 53 out of 100: a small shipping company with eight vessels whose figures look cheap but rest on a strong market. Returns have been below the cost of capital, growth was bought with borrowed money, and cash flow is heavily negative. The share costs about 6 times earnings. Watch management's warning that the next 6-9 months will be challenging, with six of eight ships on market rates.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • ROIC and margin over the cycle
      ROIC was 7.9% (TTM to June), 4.4% in 2025, 4.0% in 2024 and negative in 2021 and 2023. The company itself uses a WACC of 10.5% in the impairment test, so the return has been below the cost of capital through the cycle. The operating margin of 28% seen now is a market peak.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose to SEK 1,128m year to date (646). According to the company, the increase is due to the purchase of Ben Viking and Tor Viking and a favourable AHTS market. Growth has thus been bought with borrowed money and spot rates, not organic; six of eight vessels were on the spot market in the North Sea in Q3.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat
      A shipping company with eight AHTS vessels and 6 employees, where crewing, technical and commercial operation are outsourced to Sea1 Offshore. The ice class gives a niche, but the company has no pricing power, and management itself calls the market cyclical.

      The company has bought two ships in a strong market: Ben Viking (built 2012) in 2025 and Tor Viking (built 2019), which was bought from the main shareholder Kistefos on the terms Kistefos itself had obtained. Investments were SEK 1,160m (TTM to June) vs operating cash flow of 273m, and no dividend is paid.

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 report came on 6/11, and stockanalysis' key figures are still based on TTM to June (P/E 10.8). With Q3, TTM EPS is about SEK 26.9 (24.7 year to date + 2.2 in Q4 2025), P/E about 6.2, P/B about 0.92 (equity SEK 182.4 per share) and EV/EBITDA about 5.6 (net debt SEK 1,326m, TTM EBITDA about 631m); the figures are calculated from the report. stockanalysis shows insider ownership of 0.05%, but Kistefos owns 82.8% of the capital via Viking Invest and a sister company. No analysts cover the stock, so there is no forward P/E, price target or estimates. Peers (Sea1, DOF, Solstad) are only compared on stockanalysis' key figures. Contract coverage, day rates and the ships' broker valuation vs book value (NAV) have not been examined. Kistefos owns 82.8%, so a squeeze-out (from 90%) is possible, but there is no bid.
