    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin is −23.9% in 2025/26 (EBIT −DKK 7.3m on revenue of 30.7m) and has been negative in four of the last five years; only 2023/24 gave +2.5%. EBITDA −3.5m vs +1.3m the year before, and gross margin has fallen from 78% (2021/22) to 68%, partly because two larger projects required far more resources than budgeted. ROE −67% and ROIC −22% are not counted again.
      stockanalysis.com
      −8
    • Quality of growth and order intake
      Revenue fell 17% to DKK 30.7m, order intake fell 69% from DKK 37.8m to 11.9m, and the order backlog has halved from DKK 39.7m to 18.1m. Only 60–65% of the backlog is expected to be recognised in 2026/27, so approx. half of the guidance (DKK 27–34m) must come from tenders not yet won.
      rovsing.dk
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      Development costs are capitalised on an ongoing basis (DKK 2.7m in 2025/26), and intangible assets of DKK 13.4m make up 87% of equity; tangible equity is only DKK 2.1m. At the same time a 'buy-and-build' strategy with acquisitions is being launched, even though the company cannot finance its own operations without loans from the board.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Moat
      A real niche in test equipment (EGSE) and simulator software for ESA missions at Airbus, Thales Alenia and OHB, but with 30 employees Rovsing is a small subcontractor that depends on ESA's geographical return principle and on the prime contractors' tenders; pricing power is weak (inflation in materials could not be passed on).
