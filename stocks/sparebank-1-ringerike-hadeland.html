    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share, but the equity certificate fraction is 97.34%, so the self-owned share is small and the security is economically equivalent to a share. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. T2 is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (1.45) and history from the Q3 presentation; stockanalysis's P/B of 1.31 counts hybrid capital in equity. stockanalysis's +2.96% in share count has not been used: the number of certificates is 15,650,405 according to note 12, and there is no issuance. Only 2 analysts cover the stock (both Hold), and liquidity is very low (approx. 2,500 certificates a day) despite a market-making agreement with SpareBank 1 Markets. One-offs: 2025 included a gain of NOK 58m from the sale of SamSpar units, so the underlying decline is smaller than in net income. Insider holdings in management have not been examined beyond the reported trades. The representative council meeting on 9/11 has not been examined.
