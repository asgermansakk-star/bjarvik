    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Financial year ends 31 March; FY 2026 at stockanalysis is 2025/26. stockanalysis shows 30.77m shares and −20.65% in the quarter, which is a data error: Matas has 37,484,455 shares and 475,678 treasury shares at 30/9-2026 (approx. 37.0m outstanding), so the share count has not given points. The buyback (up to DKK 100m) and the dividend are not covered by FCF after leasing and therefore give no plus. Forward P/E 7.9 rests on an EPS estimate of 9.81 (+17%) from only 3 analysts, which looks outdated after Q2 (adjusted profit in H1 −15.7%). Web search was used up in this run, so the analysis is built on the company's own reports (Q1, Q2, annual report 2025/26), company announcements and stockanalysis; Danish press and customer data (customer concentration is not relevant for retail) have not been reviewed, and only one insider trade (a person closely related to the CEO, 7/9-2026) has been found. Strategy update and new long-term targets come 12 March 2027.
