    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share; the equity certificate fraction is 80.0%, so 20% of equity is self-owned. It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.85 and book value of NOK 211.95 per share cover total equity including self-owned capital and hybrid capital and have not been used; T2 is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (NOK 152.2). Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. No trades by primary insiders found on Newsweb since June 2025 (0 points). SpareBank 1 Nord-Norge owns 26.7% after a purchase in August 2026 at NOK 180; there is no bid or merger agreement, but the ownership may support the price. Only 2 analysts cover the security, and about 3,400 certificates are traded a day.
