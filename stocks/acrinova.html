    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The holding points to the B share (ACRI B), but stockanalysis only has the A share (quote/sto/ACRI.A), so market data and multiples are for the A share (kr 7.60 on 6/11). The B share trades lower (kr 6.94 vs kr 8.14 for A at 30/9). Property company: the main multiple is price vs net asset value (NAV kr 9.67). Trailing P/E 10.7 is based on realised sales gains from 2025 and has not been used. Stockanalysis' FCF (operating cash flow of 383m) is distorted by the property sale and has not been used. Dividends and redemption are financed by property sales, not by FCF, so they do not add points. Insider trades and tenant concentration (the largest tenants' share) have not been examined. No analyst coverage and very low liquidity in the stock.
