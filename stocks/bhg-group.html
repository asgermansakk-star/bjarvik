    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 3 analysts cover the stock (S&P Global), so price targets and estimates are thin. Forward P/E is based on adjusted EPS excluding amortisation of acquired intangible assets, while trailing P/E uses reported EPS, which in Q3 included a tax-free one-off gain of kr 30.8m. The company's own net debt (kr 1,046m) excludes leases, acquisition-related obligations and deferred tax; stockanalysis' net debt of kr 1.89bn includes them. Approx. 30% of the group's net profit accrues to minority owners in subsidiaries. The board's independence, management remuneration and peers such as Boozt and Rusta have not been investigated directly (peers only from calibration data), and Q4 (Black Month) is the year's most important quarter and not yet reported.
