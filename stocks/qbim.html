    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

TTM revenue (about SEK 5.1m) is calculated with Q3 2025 as the residual of the full year. The Q3 report comes on 11/11-2026 and is not included. Customer concentration (largest customer's share) and the price per Q-Sales licence are not disclosed and have not been examined; both criteria score 0, so the grade is a ceiling.
