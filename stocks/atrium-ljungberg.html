      Rental income rose 4.3% in 9M, but only because of completed projects. In the comparable portfolio, rent fell 1.3% (Q3: −2.5%) and net operating income 2.9% (Q3: −5.0%). The economic occupancy rate fell to 85.7% from 89.7% a year earlier, the surplus ratio fell to 71.4% (72.5), and förvaltningsresultat per share fell 3% in 9M and 7% in Q3.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −14 of −35
    • Debt and interest
      The loan-to-value ratio rose to 44.5% (42.0), just below the company's own cap of 45%. Net debt/EBITDA rose to 13.9× (12.6), and interest cover fell to 2.8× in Q3 (3.2). The fixed-interest period has fallen to 2.2 years (3.0), and the floor in the policy has been lowered from 2.0 to 1.5 years. Loans of kr 2.7bn at 1.6% are to be repriced in 2026, while the five-year swap rate rose from 2.5% to 3.2% in Q3. 26% of debt falls due in 2026–2027.
      storage.mfn.se
      −6
    • Cash flow and financing need
      Operating cash flow was DKK 1,529m over 12 months (of which +362 in working capital), while investments in own properties and acquisitions were DKK 3,386m. The dividend of DKK 694m is therefore debt-financed. The Ericsson project at approx. DKK 6.2bn comes on top, while the loan-to-value ratio is at the ceiling. That raises the risk of property sales or an issue at a price far below net asset value.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: the main multiple is price vs långsiktigt substansvärde (NAV SEK 54.68 per share at 30/9-2026). At a price of 22.15, P/NAV is approx. 0.41 and P/B 0.49. Ongoing earnings are measured as förvaltningsresultat per share (SEK 2.02 rolling 12 months). stockanalysis' trailing P/E of 23.3 is affected by value adjustments of properties and derivatives and is not used. stockanalysis' FCF of SEK 1.50bn is before investments in properties, and operating cash flow is lifted by +SEK 362m in working capital. The insider share of 3.74% at stockanalysis underestimates the Ljungberg family's ownership. The exact ownership stakes of Ljungberg, Coop Östra and Holmström have not been investigated. Tenant diversification has not been scored: the ten largest tenants make up 20%, but the Ericsson agreement from 2031 gives one tenant approx. 12% of the current rent. Only 6 analysts cover the stock, and Handelsbanken cut its price target to SEK 23 after Q3.
