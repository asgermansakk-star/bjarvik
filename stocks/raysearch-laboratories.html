    In plain words

    Bjarvik gives RaySearch 81 out of 100. The software company has a record order book, orders rose 30%, and customers are spread over 1,200+ clinics. But growth is weak, cash flow is very low, and the company has gone from net cash to net debt. The share fell 15% on the latest report. Watch the fourth quarter: the 25% margin target needs a strong quarter.
