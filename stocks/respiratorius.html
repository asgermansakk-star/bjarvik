    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −26 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue of significance (SEK 30k TTM) vs a net loss of SEK 6.65m (TTM) and 6.95m in 2025. The company has never had income from VAL001.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      VAL001 has only one completed phase I/IIa study from three Swedish university hospitals. The next step is a phase III study with 800–1,000 patients, which the company cannot finance itself. There are no new clinical data, only a pre-IND meeting with the FDA and a pharmacokinetic formulation study.
      mb.cision.com
      −7
    • Moat
      VAL001 is a new oral formulation of sodium valproate, an old generic drug. Protection rests on combination and formulation patents (expiring 2032 and 2037) and orphan status, and the DLBCL market has strong new treatments from large companies.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The company has no employees and is run by consultants, including the CEO. It has lived off repeated rights issues with an underwriting premium of 14–18% and a 100:1 reverse split in 2024, while the search for a partner has gone on for several years without result.
      mfn.se
      −5

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −28 of −35
    • Funding
      Cash was only SEK 2.93m at 30/6-2026 vs spending of SEK 3–4m per half-year. The company itself says the money lasts until February 2027, and the auditor has noted material uncertainty about going concern. New capital must be raised within 6–12 months if no licensing deal materialises.
      mfn.se
      −9
    • Dilution
      The share count rose 55% from 5.10 to 7.90m in the 2025 issue (+43.8% in a year; confirmed by SEK 7.46m in share issuance in the cash flow statement). The issues in 2024 and 2025 were only 50–55% subscribed, and the underwriters took a large part. TO2 at SEK 13.83 was not exercised.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Partner
      The entire business model is to out-license VAL001, but there is still no agreement. The CEO himself writes that the partner discussions are drawn out and that not all counterparties have completed the first evaluation after BIO Europe.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Clinical risk
      The value depends on a large phase III study in first-line DLBCL on top of standard treatment, where the effect must be shown against a strong comparator. Even with a partner it will be many years before data arrive.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Governance and key person
      The company is entirely dependent on CEO Johan Drott, who is a consultant, sits on the board and owns shares via Valcuria Holding, which the founder of the VAL001 project is also behind. Board member Kristina Drott left the board in March 2026 for health reasons. The largest owners are dispersed private individuals and Avanza Pension (8.3%).
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Liquidity and share price risk
      Average turnover is about 2,400 shares a day (about SEK 5,000). The price has fallen from SEK 8.68 in June 2025 to SEK 2.17, far below the issue price of SEK 3.70, and a new issue would hit a very thin market.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
