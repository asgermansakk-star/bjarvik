      The EBITA margin fell to 5.6% in Q3 (7.2%) and 6.4% in January–September (7.5%) vs the target of at least 10%; the EBIT margin was only 3.6% in Q3. Even adjusted for the recruitment effort in Sweden (−20m), the margin is 7.0% vs 8.0% in 2025. The net margin is 3.4% (TTM).

      −4
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen to 8.5% (TTM) from 11.4% in 2025 and 13.9% in 2022, and ROE to 8.7% from 14.5% in 2022.

      Acquisition-driven growth: goodwill has grown from SEK 0.75bn in 2021 to 1.97bn, almost all of equity of 2.19bn, while EPS has not grown. The acquisitions for 252m in 2026 have been financed with bank debt and an in-kind issue, while ROIC is falling.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The merger with Multiconsult (Rejlers is the acquirer and issues 0.9725 B shares per Multiconsult share; Rejlers' shareholders get 46% of the combined company) was approved by both general meetings on 19/10-2026 and is expected to complete around the turn of the year, subject to competition approval. Special situation is not set because the Rejlers share price is not tied to the deal terms but follows the combined company; after completion the pro forma rule applies for 12 months, and a rescoring on the combined figures is necessary. Engineering consultant; 'Construction & materials' was chosen as the closest industry, in line with Sweco, AFRY, Norconsult and Multiconsult. P/TBV is meaningless because goodwill (1.97bn) almost equals equity (2.19bn). Stockanalysis' net debt (1.14bn) includes lease debt; without leasing it is about 0.85bn. FCF after leasing (about SEK 55m TTM) is an estimate from operating cash flow, investments and lease payments. Stockanalysis has no forecast page for the stock (404), so the number of analysts, price targets and the basis for forward P/E 11.2 (assumes about 60% EPS growth) have not been investigated. The Rejlers family's exact ownership share and contract types (fixed price vs time and materials) have not been investigated in depth.
