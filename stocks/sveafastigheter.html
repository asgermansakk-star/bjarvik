    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −17 of −35
    • Debt and refinancing
      Loan-to-value is 45% (KlaraBo 49% before the merger), and net debt/EBITDA is 16x vs the policy ceiling of 15. Interest cover is 1.9x (KlaraBo 1.8x), and the average debt maturity was only 2.5 years, with 44% of the debt maturing in 2027 as of 30/6. KlaraBo's debt was only 61% interest-hedged.
      storage.mfn.se
      −7
    • Large owner and governance
      The troubled SBB owns about 58.5% after the merger and was on both sides of the transaction: SBB sold its portfolio to KlaraBo for shares and voted the merger through. SBB has pledged 63% of its Sveafastigheter shares for a credit facility, so a forced sale could pressure the price. The free float is small, and insiders own only 1.3%.
      marketmate.se
      −5
    • Management and execution
      CEO Erik Hävermark had to leave on 14/10-2026, two weeks after the merger, and a board member is acting CEO while a new one is sought. This comes in the middle of the integration, on which the synergies and thus the value creation of the merger depend.
      realtid.se
      −2
    • Cash flow and dividend
      Operating cash flow fell 34% to SEK 444m (TTM), and after investments of 1.1bn, free cash flow is −SEK 668m. No dividend has been paid since the listing (SEK 0.00 in 2025 and 2026), so the projects are financed with debt and sales.
      stockanalysis.com

The weighted yield requirement is 4.54% (unchanged), and +0.25 percentage points costs about SEK 1.3bn in property value, about 8% of the long-term net asset value.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: valued on price vs long-term net asset value (NAV) and förvaltningsresultat, not on P/E. The merger with KlaraBo and the purchase of the SBB portfolio were registered 30/9-2026, so under the rule on pro forma for 12 months, the company's own pro forma figures from the merger document are used. Stockanalysis' market cap (6.3bn), P/E, PEG and estimates are based on 200m shares and figures from before the merger, but the share count is now 305.5m (incl. 20m unlisted A shares), so market cap is approx. SEK 9.6bn. NAV per share for the combined group has not been published; SEK 81.80 is Sveafastigheter's own figure at 30/6, and the exchange ratio was set from NAV, so the level should be roughly the same. The Q3 report comes 18/11-2026 and will contain a one-off gain from negative goodwill (approx. SEK 2.7bn pro forma), which must not be included in earnings multiples. Only 3 analysts and one EPS estimate. Insider trades and external checks of the property valuations have not been investigated.
