    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank/broker: stockanalysis' FCF (incl. movements in client funds), debt and EV are meaningless and not used; the assessment is based on P/E, P/TBV/P/B and ROE, and dividend is measured against net profit. The insider trades in September (small sales in connection with option exercise) and the possible dilution of 1.7% from the option programmes did not give points. Regulation (ISK taxation, AML, Finansinspektionen) and the credit quality of mortgages and securities credits have not been examined. Interest rate increases from the Riksbank will lift net interest income (+SEK 1,100m per percentage point according to the company), but this has not been given a plus.
