    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −14 of −35
    • USTR port fees
      The US port fees on all foreign-built car carriers are only suspended until 9 November 2026. Wallenius Wilhelmsen itself estimated an exposure of about USD 100m per quarter before mitigation, about 25% of annual EBITDA, if it cannot be passed on to customers.
      wfw.com
      −6
    • Overcapacity
      The global ro-ro fleet grows from 4.1m CEU (2023) to about 5.9m in 2030. The order book equals about 21% of the fleet, and about 60 ships are delivered in 2026 alone, which threatens today's high rates (time charter about USD 90,000/day).
      investing.com
      −5
    • Bunker fuel and the Middle East
      Adjusted EBITDA fell 7% q/q to USD 361m because of bunker prices up to about USD 2,000/t. The Middle East costs about 2% of shipping revenue plus USD 2m a month, and the Red Sea route is not being resumed.
      bairdmaritime.com
      −2
    • Ownership structure
      Only about 261m of 423m shares are free float, because two family/shipowner groups control the rest. Small shareholders have limited influence, and liquidity in the stock is low.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1
