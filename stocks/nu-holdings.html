    In plain words

    Nu Holdings gets 90. The bank is growing fast but with rising risk: loans grew 37% in a year, nearly all are credit cards and unsecured loans, and the share of loans over 90 days late rose to 6.9%. On the plus side, it earned over $1bn in the quarter and the share is cheaper than in recent years. Watch taxes and interest rates in Brazil, home to most customers.
