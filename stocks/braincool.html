    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −24 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin is −71% TTM (EBIT −SEK 33m on revenue of 46.5m), and gross margin is only 27% TTM and 21% in Q2 2026 (31% at constant exchange rates). That is low for medtech with disposable products and far below 66–69% in 2021–2022. Production has been outsourced, and a large share of the disposables is still made manually.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Net revenue rose 72% in Q2 (SEK 11.3m vs 6.6), and the number of single-use items sold rose over 125%. The order book is approx. 42m. But net revenue fell from 40.0m in 2024 to 36.2m in 2025, and the base is small and fluctuates a lot from year to year (−12% in 2023, +72% in 2024).
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat
      BrainCool System is a surface cooling system sold via ZOLL, and competes with established systems for targeted temperature management. The low gross margin suggests weak pricing power towards the distributor. RhinoChill, which is the real differentiation in stroke and cardiac arrest, is still in clinical studies.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has risen from approx. 64m (weighted, 2021) to 394.9m through issues every year (SEK 56–116m a year in 2021–2025). The latest rights and over-allotment issue in December 2025 of 70.7m was at approx. SEK 0.50 and diluted by 35.8%. Intangible assets (capitalised development) make up 83.7 of 159m in equity.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −23 of −35
    • Funding
      Cash was SEK 43.1m at 30/6-2026 with no debt, while operating cash flow was −SEK 23.5m in H1 and −SEK 49.7m TTM. That lasts about four quarters, so another issue in 2027 is likely unless growth in single-use products turns cash flow quickly.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Customer concentration
      Almost all sales of the BrainCool System go through one global distributor, ZOLL (USA, DACH and Asia; agreement to 2030), and approx. 85% of net revenue came from countries outside the EU in 2025. The ZOLL order of at least SEK 39m from February 2026 corresponds to more than all of 2025 revenue, so the company is heavily dependent on one customer's inventory decisions.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Governance
      Ekobrottsmyndigheten has charged the former CEO Martin Waleij with gross breach of trust (payments from the company's bank account in September 2023) and gross insider offence (share sales in March 2024). Three of the board members declined re-election in 2026, and the board and management have been almost completely replaced within a year.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Insider selling
      Board member Oscar Engellau sold 8.79m shares via a related party on 17/3-2026, three months after subscribing in the issue, and left the board at the annual general meeting in May.
      marknadssok.fi.se
      −2
    • Execution (production and studies)
      Demand has periodically been limited by production, and investment in automation at the production partner continues. The COTTIS 2 study of RhinoChill in stroke is delayed by staff turnover at several German centres, and a US study has been postponed, so RhinoChill revenue is several years away.

      −3

      Most sales are in USD, while purchases are in SEK. The weak dollar pulled the gross margin down from over 31% to 21% in Q2 2026 and revenue from SEK 13.1m to 11.3m.
