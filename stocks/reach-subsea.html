    The score is 57 out of 100: a Norwegian offshore vessel company whose profit has vanished. The net loss is NOK 76m over the past year, and free cash after leasing is negative. The share count has grown sharply, and a dividend was paid despite the loss. The order book has grown, and a vessel sale brings over NOK 200m. Watch the 17 November report and the strike.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analyst estimates on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist), so forward multiples cannot be compared with forward history. The latest report is Q2 2026; Q3 is published 17 November 2026, and the effect of the SAFE strike from 17 October is unknown. The company gives no concrete guidance. Customer concentration has not been examined in detail (Equinor is a central customer for Reach Remote), and 0 points have been given. FCF is measured after lease payments (IFRS 16 charter of vessels), which stockanalysis' P/FCF does not deduct. The gains of approx. kr 70m from the sale of Viking Reach in Q4 2026 are one-off items. The spin-off of Reach Remote may change the valuation significantly, but the terms are unknown. Insider ownership of 2.4% according to stockanalysis does not include North Energy (15.5%), which is a related party to the chairman.
