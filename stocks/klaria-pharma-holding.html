    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    A company with no revenue and negative equity, so P/E, forward P/E, P/B and PEG do not exist, and there is no analyst coverage (upside and revision points become 0). The rights issue of up to SEK 147.6m closed 6/11-2026; the outcome (expected 9–10/11) is not known, and how large a share the underwriters and set-off of loans take could change both cash and dilution. Price and multiples are before the issue (245.98m shares); after full subscription there are approx. 492m shares plus TO1 warrants and underwriting fee in units. The Q3 report (26/11) and the prospectus's working capital statement have not been read. The company's own 'external valuation' of approx. SEK 812m (SEK 1.88 per share) and an estimate of peak sales of USD 500m are commissioned material and have not been used as a basis. The cooperation with BDO on 'strategic and financial alternatives' may end in a sale of the company or the asset, but there is no bid. Insider trades in 2026 and the board's own subscription in the issue have not been examined (0 points). The royalty rate from CNX is only disclosed as 'double-digit'.
