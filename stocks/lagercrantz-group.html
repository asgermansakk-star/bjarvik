    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Serial acquirer of niche technology companies (85 companies, five divisions: Electrify, Control, TecSec, Niche Products, International); 'Machinery & automation' was chosen as the closest industry, as for Addtech, Indutrade and Lifco, although Electrify (approx. 25% of sales) could also belong under power grids and electrical equipment. Non-calendar financial year (April–March), so FY2026 = April 2025–March 2026. stockanalysis' ROIC includes goodwill; the company's own measure P/WC (72%) excludes goodwill and looks far higher. The marginal return is calculated as EBIT growth over growth in intangible assets. Acquisition multiples are not disclosed per deal and have not been examined. Customer concentration on individual customers is not disclosed; assessed from segment and geographic breakdown. The Børjesson family (chairman Fredrik Börjesson) holds 5.6% of capital and 29% of votes via A shares, CEO Jörgen Wigh 1.2%; stockanalysis' insider share of 1.31% is too low. The insider purchases are small, and management had its 2022/26 option programme settled in cash in June 2026. The price targets on stockanalysis were last updated 20 May 2026; only 7–8 analysts. Litigation and short interest have not been examined. Next report 3 February 2027.
