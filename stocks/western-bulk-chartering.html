    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are in USD (N-GAAP, half-year reports only), while price and multiples are in NOK. The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo, not the main list. TTM at stockanalysis ends 30/6 2026; Q3 2026 is known only from the dividend announcement (USD 4.9m, up to 80% of net profit, i.e. a Q3 profit of at least approx. USD 6m on unaudited management figures), so the trailing P/E of 94 overstates the price. Forward P/E (29.5) is based on very few estimates; only Arctic and DNB cover the stock, and stockanalysis has no forecast page. NAV is not relevant for an operator without its own ships. The extent of the charter-in commitments (T/C length and duration) and the FFA book's net position have not been examined in detail, so the risk may be greater than reflected. Stockanalysis' insider share (5.34%) does not include Kistefos' 68.7%.
