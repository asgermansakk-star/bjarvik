    • Quality of growth and minority structure
      In Q2 revenue rose 16.2% and EBIT 16%, but EPS to the parent company's shareholders was almost unchanged (3.73 vs 3.71 kr).     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Listed on First North in 2024, so the multiple history covers only 2024–2025. The accounts follow K3 with amortisation of goodwill, so P/E is lower than for IFRS peers such as Compass and Sodexo. Orifo is only 40% owned but fully consolidated, and a growing share of earnings goes to minorities; Albisabores (51%) was acquired on 1/10 and is not included in the figures. stockanalysis has no forecast page and no visible price target, so the source of forward P/E 12.9 (corresponds to EPS of about kr 20 vs 15.46 TTM) has not been verified. Low liquidity (about 9,000 shares a day). The Q3 report comes 12/11 and is not included. It has not been investigated how large a share of Försvar revenue is export (SFD) relative to Försvarsmakten's restaurant operations.
