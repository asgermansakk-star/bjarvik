    • Moat and competition
      Agillic is a small Nordic provider of marketing automation with 35 employees and 114 customers (122 in 2023). Competitors are large international MarTech platforms with far more resources. ARPA has been fixed at DKK 0.5m for five years, and about 70% of revenue comes from Denmark, so pricing power or scale effects cannot be documented.
