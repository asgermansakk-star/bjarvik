    • Moat and pricing power
      The product is a commodity whose price follows global salmon supply: the average price in Q2 was EUR 6.94/kg, only 8% above the Sitagri index, and pressured by high global supply. Land-based salmon (RAS) is a young and unproven industry with several failed facilities; the advantage is mainly proximity to the market and local political support.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Accounts in EUR, price in NOK; the stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list). The Q3 2026 report comes 12/11-2026, so financial figures are from Q2 2026 (30/6) and the Q3 operating update. Earnings are affected by fair-value adjustments of the biomass; operating EBIT has been used for the margin assessment. Customer concentration and sales channels in China have not been examined. Salmon is marked cyclical like Mowi and SalMar.
