    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −6 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Growth is strong (Q3: +38% at constant exchange rates), but uneven: H1 2026 gave only +20% reported and +31% at constant rates, and 2025 was a weak year in which the share halved. Growth is carried by one product (CERAMENT G) and by US reimbursement.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Segment of the future and pipeline
      The platform's next leg in the US, CERAMENT V, is delayed: the FDA moved the application from 510(k) to De Novo in December 2025 and asked for more biocompatibility data in 2026. The spine product is still under development, so the diversification away from CERAMENT G has dragged on.
      efn.se
      −2
    • Margin and moat
      Gross margin is 92% and ROIC 53%, but in the US 32% of revenue goes to commission to independent distributors (Q3: SEK 110.7m). The reported operating margin (25.5% TTM) is clearly below the adjusted (34% in Q3), because the LTI programmes are a continuing cost.
