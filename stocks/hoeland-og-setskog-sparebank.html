    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The equity certificate fraction is only 13.69%, so approx. 86% of equity is self-owned; the security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.07, net income of −NOK 39.5m (TTM) and forward P/E of 55 do not match the bank's own figures (profit after tax approx. +NOK 3.2m over the latest 12 months before interest on perpetual bonds) and have not been used. T2 is based on the bank's own book value per certificate (NOK 152.83 at 30/9-2026) and own P/B figures for 2024–2025; P/B history before 2024 has not been calculated. There is no analyst coverage or forward estimates, and guidance is only qualitative ('weak result' for 2026). Very low liquidity (approx. 137 certificates a day) and a market cap of approx. NOK 83m. The change of auditor in May 2026 (RSM to Revisorkonsult) is because RSM is ceasing to audit savings banks, and has not been deducted.
