    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −11 of −35
    • Debt and financing
      Operational net debt is SEK 1.61bn (1.42) plus pensions of 444m and leases of 358m, in total about 3.2 times EBITDA. Interest cover is 4.4, and financial items eat about 24% of EBIT. FCF after leases (303m) did not cover acquisitions (366), dividend (112) and buybacks (64) TTM, so the acquisition model is partly debt-financed. In addition there are contingent considerations of 97m and option liabilities to minorities of 72m.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Execution against own targets
      The '500/ten/45' target (EBIT of SEK 500m and an EBIT margin above 10% in 2025/26) was not met: the result was EBIT of SEK 413m and a margin of 8.3%. The growth target of 15% a year over a cycle is far from reality. PPE & Utilities' EBITA fell 45% in H1 (margin 3.8%), with restructuring costs in the rest of Skydda, and Luna remains weak.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Estimate risk
      Consensus expects EPS of 10.65 in 2026/27 (+26%), but adjusted EPS rose only 10% in H1 (4.45 vs 4.05). That requires approx. 40% growth in H2. The estimate is based on 3–4 analysts with a range of 9.18–14.03, and the company gives no guidance.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Macro and customer behaviour
      Nordic construction is on hold, especially in Norway after the autumn interest rate rise (Luna, Teng Tools), and dealers are holding back orders. Customers are also delaying investment decisions on machinery and equipment.

    • Customer concentration
      The ten largest customers accounted for approx. 25% of revenue in 2025/26. They are especially resellers in building materials and industry, and one of them is Ahlsell, which has taken over Skydda Nordic and is now selling off the acquired inventory at the expense of the group's PPE sales.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The financial year ends 31 March; TTM is at 30 September 2026 (Q2 2026/27). Trailing EPS 9.85 includes a one-off gain (items of a one-off nature +SEK 31m over 12 months, incl. the sale of Skydda Nordic to Ahlsell); adjusted EPS is 8.85, so stockanalysis' trailing P/E of 26.2 is too low (adjusted approx. 29). It may distort the PEG fallback. EV/EBITDA has not been used, because stockanalysis' TTM EBITDA (619) and annual figures do not add up. Only 3–4 analysts cover the stock, and the forward estimate (EPS 10.65, +26%) ranges from 9.18 to 14.03. The company gives no guidance. Credit rating and any covenants have not been examined. Net debt is stated here incl. pensions (444m) and leasing (358m).
