    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −11 of −30
    • ROIC
      ROIC is 5.4% and ROCE 5.9% vs WACC of 9.0%, and ROIC was 5.0–5.4% in 2024–2025. The figures are distorted by Fontis, but the company has not earned its cost of capital in any of the years it has been listed.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Moat and business model
      After the sale of Archer (2025) and Fontis (2026), Paratus is a holding company with one activity: 50% of Seagems, which has six pipelay vessels (PLSV) in Brazil. The niche has high barriers to entry, but Paratus does not control the JV and lives off its distributions.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      The 2026 growth (Seagems revenue +23% in H1) comes from new Petrobras contracts, which all vessels have already moved onto. The order book has fallen from about USD 1.3bn (Q4 2025) via 1.2 to 1.1bn (Q2 2026), and the company has become smaller, not larger: Fontis and Archer have been sold off.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • History and balance sheet
      The company was spun out of Seadrill after the 2022 restructuring and has an accumulated deficit of USD 1.1bn. Equity is only USD 95m (30/6-2026) vs 257m at the end of 2024, because dividends have exceeded profit.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −26 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      100% of earnings come from six vessels working for one customer (Petrobras) in one country (Brazil), and the day rates have a BRL component.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Recontracting
      The order book of about USD 1.1bn (100%) equals about two years of revenue (USD 142m in Q2). In the Petrobras tender for contracts starting in 2028, Seagems bid with Jade and a chartered-in third-party vessel, but only the third-party vessel was accepted (18/9). Jade thus has no follow-on contract.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Seller credit
      USD 237m of the sale price of USD 400m is a 2.5-year non-recourse vendor credit to a company owned by Borr and CME, secured on the jack-up rigs. Four of the five rigs were laid up in H1 after the Pemex contracts. The company deducts the credit from net debt (USD 282m vs approx. USD 519m without), and a defaulted loan gives only laid-up rigs as collateral.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Debt
      Gross debt was USD 829m in Q2 (2029 bonds 500, 2031 bonds 250 at 8.125% and 79m in Seagems debt). Net interest expenses (USD 32m in H1) eat up more than half of operating profit (60m). Altman Z-score is −0.33, and the partial redemption in September cost a make-whole premium of USD 8.7m.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Cash flow and dividend cover
      The dividend is USD 0.22 per quarter (about 18% direct yield), but in H1 USD 71.6m was paid out vs FCF of 35m after interest and Seagems distributions. Distributions from Seagems fell from 41.3 to USD 30.2m in Q2, and stockanalysis shows an FCF payout ratio of 468%.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Management and governance
      CEO Robert Jensen stepped down on 17/4-2026, and CFO Baton Haxhimehmedi has been acting CEO since then. There is no permanent chief executive seven months later, and the board was changed again on 23/9. The company is registered in Bermuda, and management does not control Seagems alone.
      mfn.se
      −3

      Technical utilisation fell from 98.3% to 93% in Q2 due to maintenance and operational incidents on Esmeralda and Jade. Seagems EBITDA fell from USD 48.1m to 43.5m (Paratus' share).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are kept in USD, while the stock trades in NOK on Oslo Børs (approx. NOK 9.9/USD in stockanalysis' figures). The Fontis sale (completed 29/7-2026) makes stockanalysis' TTM figures unusable: the TTM column is effectively 2025 incl. Fontis, so trailing P/E 9.4, ROE 76%, interest cover 0.53 and EV of NOK 13.8bn have not been used. Findings are instead based on the company's own figures for continuing operations (Paratus' 50% of Seagems plus the holding company) and the pro forma net debt. Seagems is a 50/50 JV, accounted for under the equity method, so margin and ROIC are seen only indirectly. Insider ownership and the largest shareholders have not been examined (stockanalysis shows n/a) and give 0 points. Contract expiry per vessel has not been verified beyond the order book and the Petrobras tender for 2028. The Q3 report comes 27/11-2026. Only 4 analysts cover the stock.
