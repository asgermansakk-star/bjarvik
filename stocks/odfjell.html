    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The holding is the B share ODFB (non-voting), but stockanalysis only has a page for the A share (quote/osl/ODF); price, multiples and price targets are therefore the A share's. The two classes have the same dividend rights and trade at almost the same price (ESPP price NOK 101.38 for A and NOK 101.49 for B in September). The accounts are in USD, price and multiples in NOK. Q3 2026 (4/11) is included in TTM. Q2 included USD 6.9m from an arbitration case, and the sale of Bow Elm and Bow Lind gives a one-off gain of USD 9.6m in Q4 2026/Q1 2027; neither has been used in the multiples. Only 4 analysts cover the stock, and stockanalysis only shows estimates for 2026. Odfjell Terminals is a small part of earnings (about USD 2m per quarter). Insider trades are only small ESPP purchases; no actual insider purchases or sales have been found. Customer concentration in the COA portfolio has not been examined (0 points).
