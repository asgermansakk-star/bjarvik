    In plain words

    The score is 45 out of 100: a brand-new shipping company with no ships, staff or sales yet. Eight oil tankers are delivered only in 2028-29, and the company is short of money: about USD 112m must be found before August 2027. The ships were ordered below today's prices. Two analysts' price targets sit about 33% above the share price. Watch the financing, as cash runs out in January 2027.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −26 of −35
    • Funding need and dilution
      Yard instalments of USD 162.2m fall due before August 2027 vs cash of about USD 50m, so the shortfall is USD 112.2m (about 55% of market cap), and the cash runs out in January 2027. USD 874m (4.3 times market cap) is missing for the whole fleet, and no external debt is secured; there are only indicative offers on sale-leaseback of 80–85% of parts of the fleet. PwC has added an emphasis-of-matter paragraph on going concern. If the instalments cannot be paid, the yards can keep the instalments paid and claim damages beyond the equity.
      storage.mfn.se
      −15
    • Governance and related parties
      The company has no employees. The CEO and CFO are hired in via Peak Maritime at cost plus 13% and shared with Bruton and Himalaya Shipping; the accounts are kept by Himalaya Shipping Management. Tor Olav Trøim's Magni has a support agreement with a related party, and Trøim's Drew owns 19.74% and Koch Shipping 26.52%, while the board owns under 1%. The structure is Bermudan with NDRs.
      storage.mfn.se

      The company will only enter charters close to delivery, so the value depends entirely on VLCC rates and ship prices in 2028–29. Today rates and prices are lifted by the conflict around Hormuz, and both rates and ship values are historically very volatile.
