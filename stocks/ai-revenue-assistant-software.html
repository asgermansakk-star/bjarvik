The burn is rising with the sales team, so more issues may follow.

    • Execution
      The whole case rests on Aira for Fortnox, which was only launched 1/11-2026. Management aims for 200–400 new customers a month with a fully built-up team in 2–3 quarters and a payback time of 4 months. These are unproven targets for a company with 6 employees whose earlier products only reached SEK 1.85m in ARR.
