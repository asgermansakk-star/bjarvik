Revenue is expected to fall from kr 164.7m to 86–88m in 2026, and 2027 has no project handovers (Moje Bielany 3 is only completed in Q4 2028).

    • Margin in rental
      The core business is approx. 31,500 m² of warehouse buildings from the 1980s. EBITDA from rentals fell to kr 2.7m in H1 2026 from kr 3.3m despite higher rental income, because maintenance, external services and group costs rose; the full year is expected at only kr 7.3–7.8m, approx. 3.8% of the investment property value of kr 198m.
      via.ritzau.dk
      −4
    • ROIC
      The group's EBITDA guidance of kr 16.5–17.2m for 2026 gives approx. 5.8% before tax and approx. 4.5% after tax on invested capital of kr 290.9m, below a WACC of approx. 6.6%. ROIC of 12–13% in 2025 was due to the one-off project sale; in 2021–2024 it was 1.8–2.5%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Future segment
      Building rights are secured only on a small scale: about 232 apartments (Moje Bielany 2 and 3) and a self-storage development up to 2029 on a 15.4 ha site. For most of the area there is no local plan, and Warsaw's new general plan, which may change the permitted use, has not yet been adopted. Moje Bielany 1 did, however, deliver an EBITDA margin of about 23.5%.

    • Execution and housing market
      The supply of new homes in Warsaw rose 7.1% in Q2 to over 16,000, sales fell 9.4% from Q1, and buyers are negotiating discounts. This hits pre-sales in Moje Bielany 3, which according to the budget is to deliver a margin in line with Moje Bielany 1.

    • Customer behaviour
      Occupancy fell to 83.46% at the end of H1 2026 from 85.8% a year earlier, below the company's own target of 85–87%. The vacancy is in the large warehouse units, where Cemat competes directly with modern warehouse halls, and the demolition for Moje Bielany 3 removes rental income.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property and development company (land and older warehouse buildings in Warsaw's Bielany district), so price vs net asset value is the main multiple. Trailing P/E 4.25 on stockanalysis is distorted by value adjustments (DKK 46.0m in 2025) and the one-off sale of Moje Bielany 1, so earnings multiples are calculated on the company's guidance before value adjustment. The net asset value after the write-up of approx. DKK 19.9m in September is an estimate (approx. DKK 1.03/share); a new overall valuation comes in December 2026. The company files only half-year reports, so there is no Q3 report. No analysts, no price target and no forecast page on stockanalysis (forecast gives 404). stockanalysis shows 7.21% insider ownership, but according to the annual report the board with related parties owns 38.4% (EDJ-Gruppen 32.5%). Multiple history exists only for 2021–2025. Not investigated: status of the pre-sales in Moje Bielany 3 (started at the end of September, no announcement on the approx. 25% pre-sales that the financing requires), the largest tenants' share of rental income and Gist Holding's role.
