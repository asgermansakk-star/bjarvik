    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company: assessed on premium/discount to net asset value (NAV). EPS and P/E (4.2) consist of unrealised price gains, and forward P/E and PEG are missing, so the PEG formula may give a skewed result. Stockanalysis only has statistics and accounts for the A share (quote/sto/INDU.A); the path quote/sto/INDU.C gives 404 on the statistics page. The C share (the holding) trades close to the A share (525.40 vs 528.50 on 6/11). No forecast page and no analyst coverage on stockanalysis. Net asset value is at 31/10 (kr 509); the NAV history for premium/discount is based on P/B at year-ends and the report's own figures, not a full monthly series. Peers compared on P/B, which for Lundbergföretagen does not correspond to NAV. Insider trades and the ownership chain (the Handelsbanken sphere) have not been examined. Industry: the closest match is 'Finance, data & insurance brokerage' as for Investor and Kinnevik; not flagged cyclical, even though 59% of the portfolio is Sandvik and Volvo.
