    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin is 1.4% adjusted over the last 12 months (1.8% in H1 2026). Over 12 months there is a net loss of −kr 46m (adjusted +20m). Even the best year in 2022–2025 produced only a 1.2% EBIT margin, so the target of 3% is far off.
      mb.cision.com
      −8
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen three years in a row, from kr 4,888m (2023) to 4,658m (last 12 months). B2B fell 12.9% in H1 because of construction. The CEO himself writes that the improvement so far has been driven by savings, not growth. Currency effects added kr 15.5m to gross profit in H1, which corresponds to approx. 40% of the period's EBIT of 38.6m.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • ROIC
      ROIC was negative or near zero in 2023–2025 (0.6%, −3.0%, −0.7%). It is now 7.5% measured over the past 12 months, only slightly above a WACC of 5.9%, and not yet a stable level.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Moat
      Elon sells white goods and home electronics through a voluntary chain of stores. It is a commodity-like category with tough competition from larger chains and online players. Finland had a clear sales decline in Q2, and Norway is being wound down. The gross margin of approx. 17% gives a small buffer.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The dividend of kr 18.6m was paid in 2025, even though FCF for 2024 was −kr 5.5m. The dividend was then stopped. The wind-down of the unprofitable parts of Norway and the closure of Din Elon gave kr 66m in write-downs and provisions in 2025, of which 28m on loans to stores.
      mb.cision.com
      −3

    • Peers
      Elon has EV/EBIT 18.5 and P/B 1.84. Currys trades at EV/EBIT 9.2, P/E 9.9 and P/B 0.61, and that with a higher operating margin (2.6%) at the same P/S (0.16). Komplett (NetOnNet) trades at P/B 0.64. Elon is thus more expensive than its closest peers, even though the margin is lower.
      stockanalysis.com

      Adjusted EPS over the last 12 months is kr 1.37, which gives a P/E of approx. 36 at a price of 49.6. There are no estimates. FCF after leasing and investments (123.0 − 21.3 − 44.3) is approx. kr 57m, i.e. approx. 8% FCF yield, but FCF was −kr 5.5m in 2024 and fluctuates a lot with working capital.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −18 of −35
    • Governance
      After media allegations in November 2025, lawyer Biörn Riese carried out an inquiry. It found no basis for the allegations of criminal conduct, but did find repeated shortcomings in the documentation of decisions and that individual senior employees did not follow the code of conduct. The CEO himself calls the criticism 'very serious'.
      news.cision.com
      −4
    • Chemical tax
      A previously unpaid chemicals tax debt of kr 88.8m was paid in Q1 2026. That made operating cash flow in H1 negative at −161m. The company had to take out a loan of kr 70m to secure liquidity and also pays interest on the tax correction.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Debt and liquidity
      Cash is only kr 20m, and liquidity including unused credit facilities is 143m. The equity ratio is 23.1%, quick ratio 0.62 and interest cover 3.1. Net debt is kr 340m including leases and 122m without (1.1 times adjusted EBITDA).
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Loans to stores
      Elon lends money to its dealers, and those loans are continuously written down: SEK 11.8m in Q2 2025 and SEK 3.0m in H1 2026, in addition to the SEK 28.3m written down in connection with Norway. This gives a credit risk on the chain's own stores.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Main shareholder and liquidity
      Elon Group Holding owns 54.7% of capital and votes, and free float is only approx. 4.4m shares. On average approx. 48,000 shares trade a day (approx. SEK 2.4m), which makes the price vulnerable after a rise of 170%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Management turnover
      The CEO has only been in post since September 2025. Chief Product Officer Onur Dogay leaves at year-end, and both a new Chief Logistics Officer and a new Chief Procurement Officer were appointed in 2026. Management is thus undergoing heavy turnover in the middle of the turnaround.
      news.cision.com
      −2
    • Macro
      B2B depends on a weak construction sector, and Finland is declining. The company itself cites tariffs, freight costs and rapid currency swings as risks to the purchase price of imported goods.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
