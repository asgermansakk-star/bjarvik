    In plain words

    The score of 76 shows a bathroom furniture maker whose growth comes from an acquisition, not its own sales. Sales excluding acquisitions fell, margins are below target, and debt has doubled. Cash flow dropped sharply, and UK costs are temporarily high. On the plus side, the customer base is broad, the stock is cheaper than its closest rival, and the chairman has bought shares. Targets: about 28% above the price.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    UBC has been consolidated since 8/6-2026, so the TTM figures on stockanalysis mix periods with and without the acquisition, and the organic growth is the company's own. Net debt of SEK 1,148m is the company's own and includes leases (429m, up from 89m due to the new distribution centre in the UK) and option debt of 92m for the last 20% of UBC. P/TBV (42) is not meaningful because goodwill and intangible assets (1,658m) almost equal equity. Customer concentration is based on the 2025 annual report (before UBC). The insider purchase is registered on the chairman as a closely related party; it has not been checked whether it is Stena Adactum or a private company. Analyst coverage is very thin (1 recommendation and 3 estimates on stockanalysis). Competition and price pressure have not been examined separately; the company gives no guidance.
