    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −27 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue. The operating loss (EBIT) was DKK 6.9m in H1 2026 and DKK 17.2m in 2025. The H1 result of −DKK 2.7m is only small because a write-up of the Portinho receivable of DKK 5.6m was booked as income; it is a one-off item.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      By its own figures the company has six candidates in phase 2, but spent only kr 1.8m on research in H1 2026 vs kr 5.1m on administration (2025: 5.1 vs 12.1m). The money thus goes mostly to the stock market listing, lawsuits and management, not to clinical data.
      files.quartr.com
      −7
    • Moat
      RNX-011 combines known substances (GM-CSF, fosfomycin and metronidazole), and protection is formulation and use patents to 2035/2040. No licence partner has signed yet, so the commercial value is not validated.
      files.quartr.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Reponex was contributed in 2023 for kr 1.5bn in shares (exchange ratio kr 1.57), while the market value is now kr 83m. A shareholder refused to grant discharge, citing the exchange ratio. At the same time, management wants to build an investment company with several acquisitions (LOI on Otiom), even though the cash is empty.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −7

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −30 of −35
    • Funding and liquidity
      Cash was only kr 29,000 at 30/6-2026, and operations are continuously financed with loans at 10% interest and convertible loans. The auditor has made capital readiness a key audit matter, and the 2026 budget assumed kr 19.9m from licences and new credits, which have not arrived.
      files.quartr.com
      −8
    • Dilution from convertible loans
      Convertible instruments of gross kr 23.2m are always redeemed in shares at kr 0.10. That corresponds to approx. 232m new shares (approx. 19%) before interest, plus subordinated convertible loans of kr 4.5m. In April the board issued another convertible loan of up to kr 9.6m (up to 127m shares).
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −5
    • Execution and guidance
      In March the company promised first patient in RNX-011 in Q1 2026 and a licence agreement for RNX-051 in H1 2026 with study start in Q2/Q3. In August RNX-011 had been pushed to late 2026, RNX-051 to late 2026/early 2027, and there was no licence agreement. The revenue guidance of kr 3.0–8.6m presupposes precisely a licence agreement.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −5
    • Earnings quality
      The Danish Business Authority ordered a new measurement of the Portinho receivable in November 2025. It was treated as a correction of an error: the 2024 result was changed from DKK −24.4m to DKK −40.6m, and corrective information was issued for the 2024 annual report and H1 2025.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −5
    • Governance and related parties
      The law firm Loeven, which is linked to chair Christian Vinding Thomsen, received a fee of DKK 1.1m in 2025 (1.3m in 2024). That equals about 9% of administration costs. The CEO owns no shares, and the whole management owns only 2.4%.
      files.quartr.com
      −2
    • The Portinho receivable
      In June the company won an arbitration case for EUR 10.5m plus interest, but the counterparty (Interpatium, Madeira) challenged the award in September at the court in Helsingør. The money has been overdue since July 2023, and the receivable is pledged as security for loans. The outcome is binary for the company.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Share liquidity and price below par
      The stock turns over about kr 40,000 a day (577,760 shares × about kr 0.07), and the price has fallen 25% in a year. The price of kr 0.068 is below the nominal value of kr 0.10, so a cash issue at market price requires a new capital reduction like in October 2024.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analysts (stockanalysis forecast 404); only paid coverage from HC Andersen Capital. Web search was exhausted during the research, so the analysis is based on stockanalysis, the company's annual report 2025, the H1 report 2026 and company announcements retrieved directly from Nasdaq. News after the announcement of the challenge to the arbitration award (7/9-2026) has not been found; next report 16/11-2026. Insider trades after 30/6, the owner behind Finansmanagement ApS (16.2%) and the status of the LOI on Otiom have not been examined. stockanalysis' change in share count (+4.9%) is not used: the share count has been 1,227.6m since 2024, and the dilution sits in the convertible loans. Booked equity consists almost only of the Portinho receivable, so P/B is not a meaningful measure for the pipeline.
