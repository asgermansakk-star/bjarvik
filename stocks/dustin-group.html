    In plain words

    The score is middling: a business with very thin profits but a cheap share if the improvement lasts. The company has lost a lot of money on past acquisitions, has issued many new shares, and debt is at the top of its target range. Sales fell in the last quarter. In its favour, adjusted profit rose 44%, costs have been cut and management has bought shares.
