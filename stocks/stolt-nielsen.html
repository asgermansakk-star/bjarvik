    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are in USD, while price and multiples on stockanalysis are in NOK; the financial year ends 30 November, and TTM covers the period to 31 August 2026 (Q3). Earnings include one-off gains (USD 15.4m on the sale of 50% of Avenir LNG in Q3 2026 and USD 75.2m on write-ups in 9M 2025), so the underlying earnings are lower than reported. The capex expectation for 2027 comes from a transcript of the Q2 call and has not been checked in the company's own report. stockanalysis states only 0.02% insider ownership, but a free float of 18.7 of 53.1m shares suggests that the Stolt-Nielsen family controls the majority; the exact ownership stake has not been checked. Peers (Odfjell and others) have not been compared, so Step 2 is based only on own history. The insider purchase by CEO Udo Lange (April 2025) is more than 12 months old and gives no plus. Only 5 analysts cover the stock.
