    In plain words

    The score of 55 describes a very small media company with losses and almost no cash. Cash is below 0.4m kronor, sales fell, and traffic depends on Google, which changed its rules. The shares barely trade, and the accounts are not audited. On the plus side, earnings before write-downs turned positive in the third quarter, and costs have been cut. Its cash is the key thing to watch.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
