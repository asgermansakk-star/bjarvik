    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The ownership fraction is only 11.84%, so approx. 88% of equity is self-owned (the savings bank's fund and gift fund). It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.14, book value of NOK 2,152 per share and net income of NOK 13.7m mix the whole bank's equity with the certificate holders' share of earnings and have not been used. The share count change of +2.87% is a data error: the bank's own figures show an unchanged 632,500 certificates (of which 13,450 own). T2 is based on the bank's own book value per certificate (NOK 249.30 at 30/6-2026) and earnings per certificate. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are H1 2026. The Q3 report is due on 13/11-2026 and is not included. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 15.96 has an uncertain basis and has not been used, and there is effectively no analyst coverage. No insider trade notifications have been found on Newsweb for 2025–2026, but insiders' holdings have not been mapped (0 points). Customer concentration in single exposures has not been examined (0 points). The peer comparison is based on figures from other Norwegian savings banks scored the same day.
