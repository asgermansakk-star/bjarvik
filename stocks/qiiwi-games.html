    In plain words

    Bjarvik gives a low score because the company is very small and loses money. Revenue has fallen by almost three quarters since 2021, and nearly all of it comes from a single game. Cash has shrunk to SEK 12.3m. On the plus side, there is no interest-bearing debt, and the chairman owns about 21%. The Q3 report (12 Nov 2026) is not included.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap on First North Stockholm with a market cap of only about SEK 41m, 4 employees according to stockanalysis (7 after the cuts in 2025) and no independent analyst coverage. The only coverage is paid commissioned research from Modern Research (motivated value SEK 6.50), and no forward estimate exists on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist). The Q3 report comes on 12/11-2026 and is not included; TTM figures run to 30/6-2026. stockanalysis shows −2.2% in share count over a year and −6.75% in the quarter, but the balance sheet shows an unchanged 9.25m shares, and the cash flow has no buybacks, so it is a data error, and no points are given for it. The terms of Plan A Games' performance-based consideration (interest, success fee, any warrants) have not been published. ROAS, LTV and cohort data for Wordington Solitaire have not been published beyond the company's own comments. Insider trades have only been checked in FI's insider register (latest trade July 2025).
