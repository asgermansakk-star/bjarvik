    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Holding-like structure: the capital reserve (DKK 1,066m, of which about DKK 990m in securities) is larger than market cap (about DKK 880m), so EV is negative and trailing P/E 4.7 is driven by DKK 174m in securities returns YTD; multiples have therefore been assessed on ordinary operations (EPS excl. securities returns). No analyst coverage and no forward estimate on stockanalysis (the forecast page returns 404), so PEG is n/a and there is no upside score. stockanalysis shows −2.7% in share count, but the holding of treasury shares is unchanged at 2.03m shares and no buybacks were made in 2025–2026; hence no points for buybacks. The majority shareholder's exact ownership share (Baunegård ApS) was not found; it only appears that the company is majority-owned. The web search was used up in this run, so the research is based on the company's own announcements and stockanalysis: customer concentration (the large grocery chains in FK Distribution), regulation of unaddressed advertising and a possible damages payment in the competition case have not been examined and give 0, so the score is a ceiling. The company reported Q3 on 4/11-2026.
