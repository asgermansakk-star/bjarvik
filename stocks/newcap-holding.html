    In plain words

    A score of 41 out of 100 points to a weak overall picture. The stock is expensive relative to expected profit, and almost all income comes from two funds managed by one subsidiary. The CEO controls 65.2% of the shares. On the plus side, the company has no interest-bearing debt, raised its revenue outlook twice in 2026, and the funds' assets grew 19%. Figures are affected by a reverse takeover.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Reverse takeover on 30/12-2025 (NCI Advisory A/S in effect took over NewCap) and a 2000:1 share consolidation in May 2026 make the stockanalysis figures distorted: P/E 22,663, EPS 0.02, share count +3,736% and insider ownership 4.28% are wrong and not used. Under Method 1.3 (4) the company's own figures and guidance are used, not the stockanalysis history. Market cap checked: approx. 250,220 shares × DKK 515 = approx. DKK 129m. The company only publishes half-year and annual reports; the next announcement is expected 20/11-2026, and H2 with the result-dependent fees is therefore not included. No analysts and no forward estimate, so PEG is n/a and there are no price targets. The web search was used up in this run, so Danish press (Børsen, Ugebrev etc.) was not searched; the research builds on the company's own reports and announcements at newcap.dk and on stockanalysis. The funds' own fee agreements and related-party relations have not been reviewed in detail.
