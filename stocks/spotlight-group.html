    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Special situation: Kriptown SAS (parent of Lise SA, which operates the Lightning Stock Exchange) made a public cash offer on 17/6-2026 at SEK 33 per share, which the independent bid committee recommends. Approx. 94% of the shares have accepted, and the acceptance period has been extended to 9/12-2026 because the Swedish FSA's ownership review is still ongoing. The price (SEK 32) therefore follows the offer and not the company; before the offer the price was around SEK 17. If the offer lapses, the price may fall sharply, and if it is completed, compulsory redemption and delisting follow. stockanalysis shows cash of only SEK 1.3m because the bank deposit (SEK 19.9m, 'utlåning till kreditinstitut') is placed under receivables. The analysis uses the report's figures, so the company has net cash and not net debt. The stockanalysis price is from 30/9-2026, and trading is thin (approx. 3,400 shares a day). The TTM figures on stockanalysis also include divested and discontinued activities. The Q3 report comes on 9/11-2026 and is not included. No analysts cover the stock. Insider trades beyond the bid acceptances have not been examined (0 points). Customer concentration has been assessed only on the basis of the number of listed companies.
