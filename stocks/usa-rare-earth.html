    In plain words

    USA Rare Earth gets 32: revenue was only $3.0m in the third quarter against an expected $6.5m, operations lose a lot of money, and several timelines have slipped. The share count is up 136% in a year, and the price is far below the $19.95 per share the company paid in stock for Serra Verde. The US government has received 16.1m shares plus warrants, about 8.6% in total, in return for up to $1.6bn in grants and loans, but the money is only paid out at milestones, and the company has not yet asked for any. On the plus side, net cash is $431m and there are government minimum prices for Serra Verde's output. Watch whether more money must be raised, and whether the new Congress majority puts the deal on hold.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Re-evaluated 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). Since 29 Sep: the Q3 results (9 Nov) clearly disappointed (revenue $3.0m vs $6.5m expected, adjusted EPS −$0.44 vs −$0.19, operating loss $141.4m), Serra Verde was consolidated with $306m in notes and $362m in royalty obligations, Barbara Humpton was replaced as CEO by Thras Moraitis on 1 Oct, several timelines have been pushed back, and the 2027 EBITDA target has not been confirmed. The share price fell from $14.42 to $7.02 (23 Nov), four analysts have cut their price targets, and William Blair has downgraded to Hold. The government stake is verified in the 8-K of 3 Jun 2026 and at NIST: the final agreements with the Commerce Department (CHIPS) provide up to $277m in grants and up to $1.3bn in senior secured loans, about $1.6bn in total. In return the government gets 16.1m shares and about 17.6m warrants. That equals about 4.3% of the current 375m shares and about 8.6% with warrants; CNBC wrote 8–16% based on the share count in January. The warrant's exercise price is not in the sources read. The money is paid out only as reimbursement at milestones; no request has been sent yet, and management says it may not reach the capital-raising milestone. Serra Verde's offtake goes to a $1.55bn SPV in which the Department of War has put $750m (price floor). The government funds are scored in facets: the financing and the floor as Plus, conditions and dilution as T3, and the election result and the Lutnick/Cantor case as political risk. Net profit in Q3 (+$11.7m) is due to a fair-value gain of $136.6m on warrants/earnout. stockanalysis' P/B of 0.55 rests on equity that is mostly acquisition values. Short interest is 15.5% of the shares. Cyclical: Serra Verde's basket price follows NdPr/Dy/Tb (about $43/kg at floor prices vs about $97/kg at market prices).
