    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Residential developer with two accounting principles: IFRS (revenue on handover) and segment reporting (revenue as the project progresses); both are mentioned, and there is no double counting. Forward P/E 7.1 and the EPS estimate for 2026 are based on only 2 analysts and have probably not been updated after the guidance cut on 22/10; the price targets (kr 10–18) imply an upside above 100%, which is outdated. Cash and net debt have not been adjusted for customer prepayments, and management's expectation of markedly lower net debt after about 1,000 handovers in Q4 has not been verified. Shareholder structure (A/B shares, largest owners) and governance have not been examined (0 points), nor has concentration on individual investor buyers. Restructuring costs of about kr 75m are expected in Q4.
