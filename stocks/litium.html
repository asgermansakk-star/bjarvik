    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (market cap approx. SEK 184m, 37 employees) with no forecast page on stockanalysis, so there is no price target. Forward P/E 31.7 on stockanalysis is based on an unknown, probably single estimate and is not used in the findings. The organic growth is our own estimate: Geins (approx. SEK 9m in annual revenue according to the company) was acquired 16/10-2025 and is included in Q3 2026, but not in Q3 2025; the company itself does not disclose organic growth or Geins' contribution. Stockanalysis' gross margin (54.9%) differs from the company's (69%), and its FCF (22.7m) is before capitalised development; the company's own FCF after investments is used. Stockanalysis' quarterly change in share count (+9.9%) is not real: the share count has been 19,349,265 since the issue in kind in October 2025. There are no lease items in the cash flow (rent is expensed). Churn, net retention and the breakdown across the largest customers are not disclosed and have not been examined further; partner dependence (implementation partners and Monitor ERP) has not been assessed separately either.
