    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −28 of −35
    • Regulation (MiCA)
      Finansinspektionen rejected Goobit AB's application for a MiCA authorisation on 2/7-2026 and refused to reconsider it. The case is before the administrative court. If the company loses, the BTCX business may, according to the company itself, have to be wound down or restructured, and the auditor refers to the rejection in the audit report.
      mfn.se
      −10
    • Liquidity and financing need
      Cash was SEK 0.94m at 31/7-2026, equity 1.4m and the equity ratio 9% (37%). The issue gives only about 9m in cash net (11.3m minus 2.3m costs; 13.8m is set-off of debt), while operating cash flow was −10.5m TTM, and Futureproof must be marketed. New capital is probably required within about 12 months, or full exercise of TO 2 (May 2027). There is also an interest-free loan of 5.5m that can be redeemed in shares.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Governance and related parties
      SEK 13.8m of the issue is paid by set-off of claims from CEO Gustav Buder's AB XXI Capital, CMO David Leeb, board member and former CEO Christian Ander and Blockchain AB. Insiders are thus both lenders and buyers at SEK 0.02. The CEO changed in August 2026, the MLRO and board members left in 2026, and the compliance functions were only fully staffed after the MiCA rejection.

      Futureproof rests entirely on Kvarn Capital (MiCA licence from the Finnish FIN-FSA) and Kvarn Metals. The company itself writes that it builds on its own interpretation of the rules, that Goobit does not need its own licence, and that a different assessment by an authority could delay or prevent the launch and lead to sanctions.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro-cap company (market cap about SEK 8.5m before the issue, price SEK 0.023) with no analyst coverage and no forward estimates. The rights issue (SEK 0.02 per share) ended 5/11-2026; the outcome is announced on 9/11, and all figures after the issue are our own estimates assuming full subscription (about 1,627m shares, about 2,010m fully diluted with TO 2 and the option programme). Revenue is not comparable over time: OTC went from gross to net accounting in 2025/26, and the whole exchange business moves to net accounting from Q2 2026/27, so the P/S history on stockanalysis has not been used. P/B of 6.15 is based on equity of only SEK 1.4m before the issue and has not been used either. The outcome of the MiCA appeal case in the administrative court is unknown. Insider trades over the market, short interest and customer concentration in figures (beyond missing OTC trades) have not been examined.
