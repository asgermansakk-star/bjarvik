    In plain words

    The score of 55 describes a small software company with losses and little cash. Sales grow slowly, and the shares have become pricier although there is no real profit. Management and the board have seen turmoil, and cash lasts about two years. On the plus side, about 80% of revenue is recurring subscriptions, customers hardly ever leave, and there is no debt. New shares may be needed.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
