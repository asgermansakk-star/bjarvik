    In plain words

    The score of 67 describes a beauty retailer with low profit, high debt and disappointing results, but signs of improvement. The profit margin is only 3.1%, and debt is 4.5 times operating profit. A savings programme lifted operating profit to 58m crowns in the latest quarter against 2m a year earlier. Watch that the low price relative to expected earnings requires a big improvement in the fourth quarter.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Beauty retail (online and stores) does not fit the industry list and is placed under 'Consumer: food, beverages & restaurants', as for Boozt and Apotea. Only 4 analysts cover the share, and forward P/E 11.9 is based on an EPS estimate of SEK 6.78 for 2026 and 10.78 for 2027 – that requires an EBIT in Q4 2026 of around five times the Q4 2025 level (SEK 16m), so the low forward multiple is uncertain. The price has almost doubled since May (the CFO bought at SEK 60.30) and is approx. 48% above the 200-day average, but is −12% over 52 weeks. Ownership data come from the nomination committee's composition as of 30/9-2025 (the four largest owners approx. 83%); an updated shareholder list and the Finansinspektionen insider register have not been fully reviewed, and Rickard Lykos' private trades have not been examined. stockanalysis shows no data for insider ownership, short interest and change in share count; the share count is unchanged at 15,310,012 according to the Q3 report. Supplier dependence on the large brand houses and covenants on the bank facilities have not been examined and score 0.
