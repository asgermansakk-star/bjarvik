    In plain words

    Score 77 out of 100: a high-quality luxury brand, but growth has slowed and the price assumes it will return. The biggest drags are profit per share barely growing since 2023 and weak sales in China and the Middle East. In its favour are a steady profit margin around 40% and about 10bn euros more cash than debt. Currency moves cut roughly 4 percentage points from growth.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.
