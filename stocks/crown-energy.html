    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Holding company with two legs: property and services in Angola (almost all revenue) and the healthtech company Accyourate/SmarTee in Italy (85%, most of the balance sheet), so the industry is set according to the company's own strategy (medical technology). No analysts and no forecast page on stockanalysis, so PEG and price targets are missing. The Iraq sale gives an accounting gain when the liability of about SEK 1.38bn (prepayments) is released on the transfer of Crown Energy Iraq AB; it lifts equity sharply, but the money has largely been spent already on the SmarTee purchase, so P/B after the transfer has not been used as a sign of cheapness. An earn-out of up to USD 270m is not expected in the short term (the licence expired 28/9-2026) and is not included. A rough estimate of net assets excluding Accyourate (cash incl. the last instalment, property, receivables minus the SmarTee instalment in February 2027 and the debt to the former Accyourate owners) is above market cap, but depends entirely on the contingent remaining purchase price of EUR 49.5m and on whether cash can be taken out of Angola; the estimate has not been used for points. Insider trades have not been examined (no Insynsregister lookups). The Q3 report comes 27/11-2026. Free float is only about 8%, so the stock is illiquid.
