    In plain words

    A score of 61 shows a Nordic IT consultancy with falling sales and low margins. Revenue fell two years running, a large write-down followed earlier acquisitions, and the dividend was cut. Management has changed, and AI squeezes consulting hours. On the plus side, the margin rose in the third quarter, debt is low, and managers bought shares. The share is cheap on sales but dearer than peers on earnings.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
