    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −17 of −30
    • Margin
      In continuing operations the operating margin is only 6.1% over 12 months (EBIT 43 of kr 701m). Marketing Partners' EBITDA margin has fallen to 16.4% in Q3 from 21.3% and to 14.5% for Jan–Sep from 21.2%, because the acquired agencies have lower margins, delivery costs have risen and Local Search accounts for less. ROE is 4.2%.
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Acquisitions account for 30 of the kr 33m in growth in Q3. Organic growth was kr 4m (approx. 2.5%) and, according to management, the first in over ten years. For Jan–Sep, acquisitions (SST, Qwamplify, Aste, Medialuotsi) accounted for kr 65m of growth of 64m, so organic development was roughly zero. ARR rose only from 537 to kr 548m in 2026 despite the acquisitions.
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Moat and future segment
      Eniro has roots in the old directory brands (eniro.se, gulesider.no, krak.dk), which are now being gathered under Robin. Management writes that Local Search is falling while SEO is growing. A large part of the offering is resale of ads through partnerships with Google and Facebook to small businesses, where switching costs are low. AI-based search threatens both local search and SEO.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Dynava (DKK 318m in revenue in 2025) was sold for DKK 102m with an accounting loss: the discontinued operations gave −DKK 34m including exchange losses. Only DKK 50m was paid in cash, and DKK 23m in cash went with the company, so the net effect on cash was DKK 27m. At the same time four acquisitions have pulled the margin down.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −13 of −35
    • Debt: pension obligation
      The defined-benefit pension obligation of kr 263m corresponds to about 55% of the market cap and exceeds the net cash of kr 100m. It costs about kr 8m a year in interest, and its value fluctuates with the discount rate (4.25%). Tangible equity is negative (−kr 178m).
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Cash flow and payouts
      Operating cash flow was −kr 3m in Q3 and kr 23m for Jan–Sep (46 the year before). Even so, the company is distributing kr 72m in 2026 plus buybacks under a programme of up to kr 40m. The new policy pays out 60% of operating cash flow, i.e. before investments, leasing and pension, and thus exceeds FCF. Cash fell from kr 189m to 133m in 2026 and falls further with the dividend in November.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Credit risk on vendor loans
      kr 52m of the price for Dynava (about 11% of market value) is a 24-month vendor loan to the small buyer Servana. Servana financed the cash part with a secured loan of kr 40m from Moank plus a targeted issue and a convertible loan, so Eniro's claim effectively ranks behind the secured lender.
      inderes.se
      −2

      Eniro lost the Kapatens case on the redemption of the B preference shares in 2022 in two instances, and it ended in a settlement of SEK 17m and legal costs of about 15m in 2026. In May 2026 other former preference shareholders filed a new case for SEK 28.7m plus interest, about 6% of market cap.
