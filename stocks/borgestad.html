    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company with two consolidated holdings (Agora Bytom 100%, Höganäs Borgestad 69.7%); stockanalysis' P/B, EV and ROE are based on the group's total equity including minority interests (approx. kr 82–88m), and EV does not include the minority. Agora Bytom is carried at cost with a value-in-use test (EUR 62.8m at 7.2% WACC), not fair value, so intrinsic value is estimated from equity attributable to the parent's shareholders (kr 673m at Q2) and may differ. Forward P/E of 4.5 probably includes the one-off gain of approx. SEK 112.6m from the Bjuv sale, which is booked in Q4 2026, and has not been used in T2. No price targets or estimates on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist); three brokers cover the share according to the company, including Analyst Group, which is typically paid analysis. The price of 15.90 is after the ex-dividend date 3/11 of kr 1.50; stockanalysis' dividend of kr 0.50 has not been updated. Cyclical is set to false, because refractory materials are maintenance consumption in heavy industry and rental income weighs in the result, but demand follows activity in the steel, cement and paper industries. The size of related parties' insider purchases in April 2026 has not been examined (attached form not read). Comparison with Polish shopping centres (cap rates) has not been examined.
