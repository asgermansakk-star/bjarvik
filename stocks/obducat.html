    In plain words

    Bjarvik scores it 40 out of 100. The company loses money (operating margin -49%), and the share count is up 260% in 2026. The shares are expensive relative to revenue, and the exchange fined it for poor disclosure. On the plus side, the order book is 147m SEK, and a new 37.3m order arrived on 6 November. Watch that 78% of the order book is one customer.
