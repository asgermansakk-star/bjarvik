    • Moat and competition
      Monsenso is a very small provider of digital solutions for psychiatry and clinical trials with 9 employees. Management itself writes that parts of the Danish public healthcare sector are increasingly developing their own solutions rather than buying finished products, and that customers' changed focus has delayed projects. Certifications (CE, ISO 13485) are an entry barrier, but not enough to show pricing power.
