    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −18 of −30
    • Margin
      The first profit ever (EBIT NOK 22.7m in Q3 2026) comes entirely from a positive fair value adjustment of the biomass of NOK 45.2m. Before it, operating profit was −NOK 22.6m in Q3 and −NOK 83.6m year to date (−47% of revenue). Net loss was NOK 103.6m TTM, and there were operating losses in all years 2021–2025.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −8
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue year to date fell to NOK 177m from NOK 322m (−45%) because the harvest fell from 5,986 t to 2,556 t. In January the 2026 harvest was revised down to 5,800 t vs 7,723 t in 2025 due to extraordinary mortality and delayed stocking of fry. The price per kg rose 29%, but that is due to the market, not volume.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −3
    • Moat
      Cod farming is a young industry that has yet to be proven profitable at large scale. The company depends on one exclusive fry supplier (Havland, five-year agreement), and the cod is sold at a premium of approx. 19% over wild cod. The price thus follows the catch quotas for wild cod.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The accumulated deficit is NOK 1.14bn vs paid-in capital of NOK 1.42bn. ROE is −50% and ROIC 1.3% (TTM). Growth is increasingly financed with leasing: right-of-use assets rose from NOK 160m to 312m in a year, and lease interest rose from NOK 1.3m to 6.6m in the quarter.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −25 of −35
    • Debt and liquidity
      Interest-bearing net debt has more than doubled: NOK 608m at 30/9 vs 284m at the turn of the year. Cash is NOK 4.5m, the overdraft NOK 301m (short-term), and there are NOK 54m in available funds incl. a new loan from Innovasjon Norge. Interest cover is 0.31. The planned green bond of NOK 750m was abandoned because the terms were not attractive enough.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −8
    • Dilution and funding need
      The share count rose from 43.8m (end of 2024) to 79.2m (+81%). About NOK 395m was raised in four issues in 2025–2026, most recently 8.5m shares at NOK 10 in May 2026, and the cash flow statement confirms the figures. The targets of 8,000 t of biomass by year-end, a new site in Q1 2027 and 25,000 t harvested in 2029 require more working capital, while cash is almost empty.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −5
    • Biology
      The bacterium Francisella was found at the Mausund site in September for the first time. There is neither vaccine nor treatment. It triggered extraordinary mortality of NOK 6.5m in Q3. In 2025, Vibrio mortality at Jamnungen cost NOK 92.5m in one-off items, so the 'one-offs' are recurring.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −4
    • Customer concentration and related parties
      All sales, all marketing and all logistics go through Sirena Group (agreement to 2031). Sirena's CEO Boe Spurré sits on the board. Strategic partners and potential customers (Jerónimo Martins, High Liner Foods) also have board seats and subscribed for shares in the issue in May.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −4
    • Macro and cod price
      Norway and Russia have raised the cod quota in the Barents Sea for 2027 by approx. 14% to approx. 325,000 t, the first increase in six years. The record-high price of wild cod, which has lifted Norcod's price per kg by 29%, may therefore ease. The fair value of the biomass is based on the company's own price expectations.
      bairdmaritime.com
      −4

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo, not the main list, and the quarterly accounts are not audited. Stockanalysis has no analyst estimates (the forecast page does not exist), so there is neither forward P/E, price target nor EPS revisions. The Q3 profit rests on a fair-value adjustment of the biomass (level 3, the company's own price expectations). There are no listed pure cod farmers, so the peer comparison is made against land-based and sea-based salmon. The company has not repeated the harvest guidance for 2026 (5,800 t) in the Q3 report. The ownership stakes of the largest shareholders have not been checked in the share register.
