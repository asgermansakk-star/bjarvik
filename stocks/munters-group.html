    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a company with a full order book but weaker earnings and high debt. Orders on hand are up 178% to 27.9bn Swedish kronor, but the margin has fallen and returns are just below the financing cost. Debt is 3.4 times annual operating earnings. The low price rests on analysts expecting almost double profit in 2027. Watch the new CEO and reliance on US data centres.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
