    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −20 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBITA margin for continuing operations was only 0.5% in 2025 and about 2.8% over the last 12 months (EBITA about SEK 7.9m on revenue of about 285m), vs an operating margin of 12.8–21.3% in 2021–2022. H2 2025 produced EBITA of about −5m, so Q2 2026 (15.1%) is the seasonal peak and not a new level for the whole year.
      mb.cision.com
      −6
    • ROIC
      ROIC is 0.7% and ROE −0.45% TTM, after −3.3 to −4.8% in 2023–2025; even on normalised earnings from continuing operations the return on invested capital is only approx. 4–5%, below WACC of 6.8%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisitions have not held up: in 2025 goodwill was written down by SEK 26.6m (Optiheat 9.2m, bought 2022, and Nomaco 17.4m, bought 2021). Optiheat is now being wound down completely, and the discontinued business lost 15.6m in 2025 and a further 2.5m in H1 2026. That corresponds to approx. one fifth of equity.
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Moat
      The group is primarily a distributor: Park & Miljö sells others' brands (Melex, Pellenc, Oeliatec, Boschung), and Hem & Trädgård lives off pools, greenhouses and saunas sold via dealers. There are low barriers to entry and a risk of losing agencies. The gross margin of 25–29% points to limited pricing power.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      The 13% growth in H1 2026 comes entirely from Park & Miljö (+37%), while the largest business Hem & Trädgård fell 1.5% in H1 and, even according to management, 'is not where it should be'. Park & Miljö had EBITA of −SEK 1.8m in 2025, so the improvement is only one strong quarter old.
      news.cision.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −13 of −35
    • Option debt and dilution
      The sellers of the remaining 49% of Nomaco can exercise a put option from May 2027. The option liability is booked at SEK 25.0m and is settled 50% in cash and 50% in new shares, calculated as 6x average EBITDA for 2025–2026. The strong performance in Park & Miljö in 2026 increases the amount. It requires approx. SEK 12.5m in cash (half of the cash of SEK 26.0m) and approx. 9% dilution at the current share price, and the board already has an authorisation for an issue of 10%.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow was −SEK 5.9m in H1 2026 (21.8) because inventory rose by 16.1m. FCF after leasing is −9.8m TTM, and cash fell from 38.5 to 26.0m. The wind-down of Optiheat cost approx. 10m in cash flow in H1, according to the company's own estimate.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Management and governance
      CEO Peter Bäck left the role in February 2026 after eight years, and chair and major shareholder Gaétan Boyer (26%) took over as group CEO. Bäck continues as managing director of the largest subsidiary. Owner, management and board are thus closely intertwined, and part of the external loan financing comes from related parties.
      news.cision.com
      −2
    • Liquidity and ownership concentration
      The market cap is about SEK 139m, and on average only about 2,300 shares (under SEK 40,000) a day are traded. Three owners (Boyer with family 26.1%, Axel Lindgren AB 17.5% and Nordnet Pensionsförsäkring 13.7%) hold 57% of the shares.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Macro and season
      Pools, greenhouses and garden products are discretionary spending that depends on the weather and the housing market, and Hem & Trädgård earns almost all its profit in Q2–Q3. Park & Miljö sells to municipalities and property companies, whose budgets follow the business cycle. The company gives no forecast for 2026.
      mb.cision.com

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' latest price is SEK 15.90 from 30/9-2026; there is no trading data after that, so the multiples are based on that price. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates, so PEG and upside are missing. Stockanalysis' TTM figures (P/E 1,240, ROIC 0.7%) mix in the wound-up Energi & Klimat (Optiheat) and one-offs from 2025: a goodwill impairment of SEK 26.6m and a gain of SEK 26.0m on revaluation of the option liability. The company's own figures for continuing operations have therefore been used: EBITA TTM approx. SEK 7.9m (1.3 − 6.4 + 13.0) and EV approx. SEK 167m incl. interest-bearing debt, leasing and option liability. The Q3 report is due 12/11-2026, after the analysis date. Customer concentration in Hem & Trädgård (the dealer network) and the term of the agency agreements in Park & Miljö have not been investigated and score 0. The company is so small and illiquid that the price can move a lot on a few trades.
