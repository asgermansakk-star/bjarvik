    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Mangold is a small Swedish securities firm (Investment Banking and Private Banking) under capital adequacy rules, so EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful: stockanalysis' FCF of SEK 192m and 'net cash' of SEK 763m are customer deposits (deposits from the public rose to SEK 1,297m) and have not been used as a plus. The valuation is based on P/B and P/TBV. No analysts cover the stock, and stockanalysis has no forecast page, so there is neither a price target nor a forward estimate; the company gives no guidance. The stock is very illiquid (about 51 shares a day), so the price of SEK 1,220 may be noisy. Insider trades in recent months have not been examined (0 points). The SEK 27.0m that Mangold was awarded in the lawsuits is not counted as earnings, because the counterparties have gone bankrupt.
