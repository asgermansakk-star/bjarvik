    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −27 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue. The operating loss was SEK 36.4m and operating cash flow −SEK 31.4m (TTM). The company has posted a loss every year (SEK 37–58m annually since 2021).
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      The entire value rests on two small, open proof-of-concept studies with tasquinimod in myelofibrosis (HOVON 172 and MD Anderson). Both had to stop recruitment to change the protocol, and in the first cohort under the new protocol one of three patients had a serious dose-limiting side effect. Tasquinimod did not prolong survival in phase 3 in prostate cancer, and as monotherapy in myeloma it gave no partial responses.
      storage.mfn.se
      −8
    • Moat
      Tasquinimod has orphan status in myelofibrosis (FDA 2022) and an exclusive licence from Oncode, and patents have been granted on the formulation (tasquinimod in the USA, laquinimod eye drops in Japan). The molecules are old, so protection rests on formulation and use patents. The market has four approved JAK inhibitors.

    • Funding and going concern
      Cash was SEK 36.3m at 30/9. According to the company, it lasts only into the beginning of Q4 2027, and new capital must be raised within 12 months to complete phase 2. The auditor (PwC) highlights a material uncertainty about going concern. At the issue in December 2025, management promised that the programmes were fully funded to the end of 2027.
      storage.mfn.se
      −8
    • Dilution and share price
      The rights issue in December 2025 (SEK 70.3m at SEK 0.05, equivalent to SEK 5.00 after the reverse split) diluted by 53%. It was subscribed only 82.8%, and the underwriters took 17.3%. The share count has risen 124% in a year, and the price of 3.60 is 28% below the subscription price, 54% lower than a year ago and 29% below the 200-day average. With a market cap of 95m, every new SEK 50m requires approx. 50% more shares.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Execution and clinical risk
      Recruitment in both myelofibrosis studies stood still during the protocol amendment, and the European study only resumed in July 2026, 17 months after the first patient (February 2025). Interim data were promised in 2026 and later 'late in 2026', while the Q3 report mentions no timing. Efficacy data are expected only towards the end of 2027, after the cash runs out.

      The laquinimod eye drops have been ready for phase 2 since the phase 1 programme was completed in 2025, but there is still no partner. Partner NeoTX's study with naptumomab is not currently recruiting patients. The business model requires partner agreements, and there is no licence income.
