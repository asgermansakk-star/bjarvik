    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a well-run property company, but the share is expensive and debt high. The price is about 1.28 times the value of the properties after debt, higher than at comparable companies. Debt is 6.6 times annual operating earnings, and cheap loans expire in 2027-2028 and must be replaced by dearer ones. In its favour are many tenants, a raised profit outlook and the founder owning 16.1%.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.

    • Debt and refinancing
      Net debt/EBITDA has risen to 6.6x from 5.2x in 2023, and interest cover has fallen to 4.2x from 7.1x in 2022. The average interest rate is 2.8% (2.5), but cheap bonds from 2020–2021 of EUR 875m with coupons of 0.8–1.1% mature in 2027–2028, while new loans cost 3.4–4.4%. 32% of the debt matures in 2029, and the fixed-rate period is only 2.4 years.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: price vs long-term net asset value (NAV SEK 144.49 per A and B share at 30/9-2026) is the main multiple; at a price of 185.20 (the B share 6/11), P/NAV is approx. 1.28 and price/equity per A and B share approx. 1.48. Ongoing earnings are measured as förvaltningsresultat per A and B share (SEK 12.92 rolling 12 months); stockanalysis' trailing P/E 12.9 is lifted by value adjustments and an accounting gain of SEK 683m on reclassifying Retail Estates as an associate. stockanalysis' share count (494.7m) and +1.9% in share count include the D shares (30m new D shares in June); the number of A and B shares is unchanged at 338.4m, so no points are given for dilution. The stockanalysis path quote/sto/SAGA.B only gives a price overview (the subpages give 404), so the path is set to the A share quote/sto/SAGA.A, which trades at the same price (185.00) and carries the company's key figures. Insider trades have not been verified: a search shows a headline that the chairman sold shares for SEK 259m in February 2026 and a purchase of SEK 8.6m by a board member, but this could not be confirmed in Finansinspektionen's register, so no points are given. Hedin Group (among the five largest tenants) has not been credit-assessed here. Only 2 analysts with price targets on stockanalysis.
