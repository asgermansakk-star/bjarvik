    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −15 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin is 5.7% (TTM) and 6.2% in 2024/25 vs 11.2% in 2020/21 and 9.5% in 2022/23. Gross margin has fallen from 43.3% to 32.9%, because the acquired and newly started electrical wholesalers and installers (Ølgod Elektro, Elektro Gruppen, Stampes Elektro, West Electric Trading) have lower margins than the core.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC has fallen from 21.3% (2020/21) and 16.2% (2022/23) to 9.3% (TTM); the company's own return on invested capital excl. goodwill is 16.0% vs 28.1%. Acquisitions and start-ups have increased goodwill from kr 4.3m to 15.3m, minority interests from kr 2.1m to 17.4m and lease debt from kr 2.4m to 32.2m, but have not raised earnings per share.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has risen 84% from kr 191.7m (2020/21) to kr 352.0m (2024/25), but EPS has fallen from kr 286.8 to 253.3. Growth comes primarily from the start-up of West Electric Trading and acquisitions, not from the core. In H1 2025/26 revenue rose 8%, while EPS fell from kr 116.6 to 107.2. Bigger, not stronger.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −4
    • Moat
      The company is a trading and agency business (including Bonfiglioli gears, motors, pneumatics and linear technology), and management itself writes that it is sensitive to loss of agencies and dealer agreements and to customers relocating production. It has no technology of its own, and 91% of revenue comes from Denmark.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −3

    • Guidance and execution
      Guidance has been cut two years in a row, both times because of weaker subsidiaries. On 5/12-2025 pre-tax profit for 2024/25 was cut from DKK 22–28m to 18–20m. On 22/5-2026 2025/26 was cut from 25–30 to 18–23m (net profit to shareholders from 15–20 to 13–17m). Q3 maintained the lower guidance.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −5
    • Accounting quality and management
      Key figures for 2020/21–2024/25 have been restated for errors in the inventory write-down, and in H1 2025/26 2024/25 was restated again for errors in work in progress and financial write-downs. The half-year report was postponed by a day because of 'unforeseen circumstances' in the accounting process, and it is neither audited nor reviewed. CFO Charlotte Bender left at her own request in April 2026.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −4
    • Liquidity in the stock
      Only about 10% of the capital is free float. Average trading is 7 shares a day (about kr 30,000), and at the general meeting on 20/1-2026 a shareholder noted that the stock is illiquid. It can be hard to get out on bad news.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −3
    • Governance and control
      Fritz H. Schur and his companies own 89.95% of the capital and have 97.59% of the votes via unlisted A shares with 10 times voting rights. Minority shareholders effectively have no influence, and the owner is just below the 90% threshold for compulsory redemption.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −2
    • Macro and customers
      The customers are machine builders and manufacturers, almost only in Denmark (DKK 318.9m of 352.0m in 2024/25), so earnings depend on Danish industrial investment. Net debt has gone from net cash of DKK 23m (2021) to net debt of DKK 34m (March 2026), mostly leasing.

      −2

Stockanalysis only has data up to and including H1 (March 2026), so its trailing P/E of 17.2 is based on EPS of 244; calculated with the Q3 announcement, TTM EPS is approx. 271 and P/E approx. 15.5. Stockanalysis' P/B of 1.57 uses equity incl. minorities (kr 17.4m); on the parent company's share, P/B is approx. 1.8. FCF is the group's and includes the minorities' share. Historical figures have been corrected twice for accounting errors, so the older multiples are based on restated figures. Insider trades and customer concentration on revenue have not been examined (0 points); the company only states that there are no major credit risks on individual customers. Fritz H. Schur owns 89.95% of the capital, just below the 90% threshold for compulsory redemption; there is no offer. Not flagged as cyclical: the company is a distributor of components (like Addtech and Indutrade), and profit before tax has been stable at kr 16–21m for five years. The annual report for 2025/26 comes 17 December 2026.
