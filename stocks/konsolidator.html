    In plain words

    The score is low because the company has lost money every year since listing in 2019 and owes more than it owns (equity of minus DKK 7.1m). Its earnings forecast was cut to DKK 0-1m. Recurring revenue is growing 18%, but existing customers shrink overall. On the plus side, the chairman's company bought shares, and there are about 290 customers. Watch that the managers themselves have lent the company money.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −18 of −30
    • Margin
      The company has made a loss every year since the stock market listing in 2019. TTM the operating margin is −11.9%, the net margin −14.7% and EBIT −kr 3.3m. EBITDA guidance for 2026 is only kr 0–1m. It is propped up by capitalised development of approx. kr 3.0–3.4m per year, which is larger than the amortisation of intangible assets (approx. 2.1m). The gross margin of 94% shows that the problem is scale, not product economics.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      CARR rose 18% to DKK 26.8m and revenue 19% in Q3. But net retention is only 98 (95 in H1), so existing customers are shrinking net. ARR churn was 13.4% in H1 and 9.8% in Q3, vs the 6–9% the company expects for the year. CAC payback is 21 months, and new sales in Denmark were below expectations. Growth is carried by new customers via partners and Iberia.
      feed.mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat and competition
      Konsolidator is a small provider of consolidation and FP&A with 33 employees and average subscription revenue of only DKK 89,000 per customer. Competitors are far larger CPM platforms, and sales increasingly depend on partners in the Microsoft D365 ecosystem (42% of new customers in H1). Scale effects or pricing power cannot be documented.
      info.konsolidator.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The expansion in Spain takes place through Konsolidator Iberia, in which local management owns 40%. Minority interests are negative at −kr 1.7m. According to the company, the investment in Iberia has deferred the group's profit, so shareholders pay for the loss but get only 60% of any gain.
      feed.mfn.se
      −2

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −25 of −35
    • Debt, liquidity and negative equity
      The group's equity is negative at −DKK 7.1m at 30/9-2026. The company has lost more than half of its share capital, and an extraordinary general meeting under section 119 of the Danish Companies Act was held on 27/10. Cash was DKK 0.25m and the credit line drawn DKK 1.8m at 30/6. Current ratio is 0.20, Altman Z 0.96 and interest cover −1.7. The company itself writes that liquidity remains tight and that short-term financing has been secured until the refinancing takes full effect.
      feed.mfn.se
      −8
    • Cash flow (FCF after leasing and capitalised development)
      FCF after leasing and capitalised development was approx. −DKK 5.1m in 2025 and approx. −0.8m TTM. The company's own positive FCF (0.1m LTM) is before interest and repayments; DKK 2.1m was paid in interest TTM. The improvement comes mainly from customers now prepaying a year (deferred revenue rose from 3.8 to 11.6m), and in Q3 the lower Danish sales hit liquidity squarely.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Dilution
      The share count rose from 15.5m (2021) to 24.6m (+59%) with issues every year, most recently +6.3% in a year, incl. a directed issue at a price of 3.79–3.87 in November 2025. In addition there are warrants: 810,000 in 2025 and 490,000 in June 2026 (approx. 5% in total). Management itself mentions capital injections as a possible way to restore equity.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Guidance and execution
      The EBITDA guidance was lowered on 28/10 from kr 1–2m to 0–1m. The causes are delayed contribution from ARR, weaker data warehouse consulting assignments and an adjustment of 0.4m under the Exsitec agreement. Revenue guidance was raised at the same time to 28–30m. Q3 EBITDA was 0.3m vs 1.4m a year earlier. The positive EBITDA promised since the start of the year has now been pushed down towards zero.
      feed.mfn.se
      −3
    • Governance and related parties
      The chairman, CEO and CFO have lent the company money in both refinancing loans (6.5m at 8% with interest capitalisation and maturity in June 2029, and 6m at 9% with maturity in September 2029). The lenders received 100,000 warrants, and the chairman's company has converted debt into shares. The founders and the chairman have an agreement on coordinated voting over 57.6% of the votes, in which the chairman controls capital increases and debt conversion. Minority shareholders therefore have effectively no influence.
      feed.mfn.se
      −3

      On average only about 6,700 shares are traded per day (20 days), approx. kr 26,000. Free float is only 5.6m shares (approx. 23%), and the founders have a lock-up until the end of 2027. A position can be hard to get in and out of.
