    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a buyer of overdue loans that wrote down its portfolios by about 30% because they were bought too dearly. Net debt exceeds market value, and new shares diluted owners who did not take part by about 60%. The share is twice as expensive relative to book value as in 2021-2025. On the plus side, debt is reduced and several executives have bought shares.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

The TTM figures (revenue −NOK 893m, net loss NOK 3.3bn) are dominated by a negative revaluation of the portfolios of EUR 320m in Q2 2026, and PEG is n/a. stockanalysis' P/B of 0.81 uses equity including minority interests (EUR 114.7m in the seed SPV), so P/TBV 1.51 (shareholders' equity) has been used as the main multiple; converted at about NOK 10.9/EUR. Forward EPS for 2027 (EUR 0.05) is based on one analyst and is rounded, so forward P/E of about 8 is uncertain. Only 3 analysts have price targets, and the price is 24% below the 200-day average and 39% lower than 52 weeks ago. Fortress' exact ownership share after the issue, the terms of the sale of the seed portfolio to the SPV with Fortress and Geveran, and customer concentration in servicing have not been investigated in detail.
