    • Financing and liquidity forecast
      The company burned SEK 5.8m in operations in H1. In March the message was that liquidity would last to the end of 2027; in August it had moved forward to mid-2027. The cash was then used to repay the EIFO loan of 7.2m and replenished with a directed issue of 9.2m. Management itself says the next capital injection is continually being assessed, and the company is not cash-neutral.

      −4
    • Guidance and execution
      Revenue guidance for 2025 was cut in July 2025 from kr 10–14m to kr 8–11m because a large pharma order was pushed out. Hardware sales disappointed in 2025 because customers took longer to decide, and the liquidity forecast was shortened by half a year in 2026.
      via.ritzau.dk
      −3
    • Customer concentration
      The largest customer, SLB, has cut orders two years in a row (first an integration after an acquisition, then a reorganisation), so Oil & Gas fell 58% in H1 2026. Pharma depends on a few large customers: the segment fell from 5.8m in 2023 to 1.7m in 2024.
      bactiquant.com

      The executive board and employees have received 1.4m warrants (December 2025, price 2.30) and 1.25m (23/9 2026, price 2.19), in total approx. 6% of the shares. The last grant came four weeks before a directed issue without pre-emption rights. Repeated errors in the announcements: the EIFO loan was stated as 9.3 instead of 7.2m, the proceeds target as 8.3 instead of 9.2m, and there are corrections of the H1 announcement and of the category of several announcements.
