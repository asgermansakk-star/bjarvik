    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −12 of −30
    • Development of net asset value
      NAV per share went from SEK 286 at end-2021 to SEK 308 at 30/9 2026; with approx. SEK 41 in dividends that corresponds to approx. 22%, or just over 4% a year, over almost five years. In 2025 equity rose only 4.6% (ROE), and in the first nine months of 2025 only 1% adjusted for dividends. 2026 (9% in nine months) is on a par with SBX (13%), not above.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Strategy and portfolio composition
      The active holdings have shrunk from 39% to 25% of assets after the sale of OEM International, the Nilörn Group and the Hifab Group; 72% is now in passive stakes in large companies (Essity, Elekta, Nordea, GN, Barclays), bonds and cash. The CEO himself writes that the need for new investments is rising and that the strategy is primarily new financial equity placements, i.e. an expensive detour to a near-index portfolio. Financial income fell to SEK 26m (42).
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation (Portmeirion)
      Traction participated with SEK 71m in Portmeirion's new issue in June and increased its ownership from 16% to 26%; the stake weighed on the result by SEK −7m in nine months and −5m in Q3. Money is being sent after a pressured UK consumer company instead of being returned.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
