    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Trailing EPS (1.87–1.89) includes a tax-free revaluation gain of SEK 102m from taking control of Fabri AG; adjusted trailing P/E is approx. 27–28 (own estimate). Fabri contributes only one month of earnings while all of its debt is included, so trailing EV/EBITDA, EV/EBIT and net debt/EBITDA are distorted; the company itself reports 2.9x pro forma vs 3.3x reported. stockanalysis' debt (SEK 5.8bn) is higher than the company's interest-bearing debt incl. leases (SEK 4.9bn plus SEK 349m of not yet integrated debt), presumably because of minority buy-out obligations; the company's figures are used. Steps 3 and 4 of the Fabri agreement (up to 49% more) are not priced in the report. Only 5 analysts cover the stock. The history on stockanalysis covers only 2021–2025, so 10-year multiples have not been assessed. The peer comparison is based only on Bravida. Instalco gives no guidance, so the guidance criterion scores 0. The stock is up 78% in 52 weeks.
