    In plain words

    5N Plus gets 78: the company has become much stronger since 2021, but the share is now more expensive than at any point in 2021–25 on both profit and operating earnings. Net profit fell 32% in the third quarter as pricier metals squeezed the margin, inventory has grown 30%, and about 34% of sales go to First Solar. The US government holds no shares: the Department of War gave an $18.1m grant to expand germanium refining, with no shares or warrants, so shareholders are not diluted. The order book has grown 25% to $447m. Watch China's export rules for gallium and germanium, where a pause expires on 27 November.

    In the 70+ zone: a score above 70 with more than 20% upside to the median analyst target.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    New 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). The US government has NO equity stake in 5N Plus. The claim of "$18.1m grant + equity" comes from one weak tracker and does not hold up. The Department of War's US$18.1m award (DPA Title III, approved 15 Dec 2025, announced 29 Jan 2026) is a grant to expand germanium refining in St. George, Utah, to up to 20 t/year over 48 months. The company's announcement of 30 Jan 2026 mentions neither shares, warrants nor repayment, and the share count has risen only 0.2% in a year. The same applies to the US$14.4m grant from 2024 and the US$7.3m award for gallium arsenide (September 2026). The grants are therefore scored as non-dilutive support (Plus), with no political deduction and no deduction for government control. Price in CAD (TSX), accounts in USD (stockanalysis' statistics page converts to CAD; financials are in USD). Industry set to 'Semiconductors' like AXT, because 74% of revenue is semiconductor materials (CdTe/CdSe for First Solar and germanium substrates for space solar cells) and the list has no materials category. Not flagged as cyclical: earnings track contract volume for First Solar and aerospace with price pass-through, not mainly metal prices, and the margin rose steadily in 2021–25. Performance Materials (26%) is, however, sensitive to metal prices (gross margin from 53.1% to 31.2% in Q3). The share price has fallen from about C$43 in June to C$28.99 (12% below the 200-day average). Q3 (2 Nov): revenue +30% to US$136.4m, adjusted EBITDA +7% to US$31.1m, net income US$12.3m (US$18.2m a year earlier). Guidance for 2026 (adjusted EBITDA US$100–105m) was reiterated with the high end expected, but not raised. Share-based pay swings with the share price (US$7.6m in Q3 against a US$2.5m reversal a year earlier) and is kept out of adjusted EBITDA. Only 8 analysts. The First Solar customer share (about 34%) comes from a secondary source, not the annual report. China's suspension of the export ban on gallium and germanium to the US expires on 27 Nov 2026; the status after the extension of other pauses on 9 Nov is not confirmed.
