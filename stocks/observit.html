    • Liquidity and debt
      Cash fell from SEK 23.1m at the end of 2025 to 6.6m at 30/9. Bank debt is 26.6m (loan 15.0, short-term 6.9 and drawn overdraft 4.6), and other current liabilities rose to 18.5m from 2.2 (presumably the vendor note). On top comes an earn-out of up to 10m.
