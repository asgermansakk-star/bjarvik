    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a design and clothing company with no earnings growth in five years. Profit per unit of sales has fallen from 20.4% to 16.6%, and sales in Finland, over half the total, declined. The share is cheap against its own history, but that reflects missing growth. Prices have held up, and the dividend is covered by cash flow. Watch whether the second half reaches the year's target.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.
