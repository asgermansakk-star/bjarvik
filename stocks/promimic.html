    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −16 of −30
    • Margin
      Gross margin is high (83% TTM), but operating margin is −19% TTM (−SEK 8.6m on SEK 45.2m in revenue) and has been around −20% since 2023. There has been a loss every year, ROE is −13% and ROIC −9%. Gross margin fell from 98% to 83% because process costs are now booked as goods for resale.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue in SEK has largely stood still: SEK 44.0m in 2024, SEK 44.4m in 2025 (+1%) and +4% in H1 2026. At constant exchange rates, growth was +10% in Q2, and the core business (implant revenue) grew 14%. That is still far below the company's target of over 25% annual growth, and Q4 2025 fell 9%.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat
      HAnano Surface is in over 2.8m implants, is documented in over 30 studies and is patented, and two customers (ATEC and Curiteva) have received the FDA's Nanotechnology Designation. But Promimic is a small licensor with 17 employees and depends entirely on customers launching and selling implants. The new ceramic surface and coating of sutures do not yet give any revenue.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      In 2025 about SEK 7m (about 16% of revenue) was capitalised as development and patents, and SEK 1.8m went to plant, incl. in the 60%-owned joint venture NPI, where 40% of the value accrues to the partner Danco. Since the SEK 80m issue at the stock market listing in 2022, cash has fallen from 58.8 to SEK 19.8m without delivering noticeable growth in SEK.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −17 of −35
    • Funding
      Cash flow is approx. −SEK 8.6m TTM (operations −2.0 and investments −SEK 6.2m), and cash was SEK 19.8m at 30/6-2026 vs 28.4 a year earlier. Only SEK 9.1m is in the parent company; the rest is in, among others, the partly owned NPI. There is just under two years of cash, and the risk section still refers to the 2022 issue, so a new issue at a low price is a real risk.
      mb.cision.com
      −6
    • Customer concentration
      Only 14 of 23 customers have approved implants, and revenue comes mainly from a few partners: ATEC Spine (the largest customer), Curiteva and S.I.N. Implant System. Royalties are reported only 45 days after the quarter. A smaller customer withdrew from the US in Q1 2026, and S.I.N.'s purchase of its US distributor caused a negative one-off swing in Q2.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Currency
      All customer agreements are in USD, while costs are in SEK. The weak USD cost about 9 percentage points of growth in 2025 (+1% reported vs +10% at constant exchange rates) and 17 percentage points in Q1 2026.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Ownership structure and governance
      K-Svets Venture owns 38.2% and Karolinska Development's Kdev 12.3%, so two owners control more than half. Board member Johan Dighed (deputy CEO of Karolinska Development) is not independent of major shareholders. The chair of the board was replaced in May 2026.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Insider selling
      The founders sold 260,000 shares at kr 31.70 in March 2024 to repay a loan, and CEO Magnus Larsson sold 18,000 shares (15% of his holding) in February 2025. No insider purchases have been found after the price fall to about kr 8.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Liquidity in the stock
      Average turnover is about 2,700 shares a day (about kr 22,000), and free shareholders outside the largest owners hold under 10%. The company hired ABG Sundal Collier as liquidity provider in April 2026. It is hard to trade the stock without affecting the price.
