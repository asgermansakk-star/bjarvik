    • Margin and profitability

Q3 alone gave EBIT of −6.9m (−3.1). The company has had an operating loss every year 2021–2025, and the positive net profit of 19.6m comes solely from the Billecta gain.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 30% in Q3 on 88% higher brokered volume, but commission costs rose 74% (SEK 9.6m vs 5.5m), and average revenue per invested krona fell 5% to 5.3 øre. Growth is thus getting more expensive, and in H1 the consumer loan portfolio shrank by approx. SEK 75m because the company had to hold back on lending to protect liquidity.

      −3
    • Business model and moat
      Small credit platform with SEK 2.1bn in capital from savers, competing with banks' deposits with deposit guarantee, and with about 45–53 employees. The model depends on private individuals' trust in bearing the credit risk themselves, and the new strategy and all new financial targets require a licence as a credit market company, which has not yet been granted.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The Refino purchase gives the seller 45% of the business's profit before tax until July 2032 as royalty, and the seller can take the business back for free if Finansinspektionen rejects the licence. SaveLend thus bears the integration and build-up costs, while almost half of any gain goes to the seller.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    SaveLend Group is a small credit platform (SaveLend, SBL Finans, Fixura in Finland and now Refino) where private savers and institutions such as NordIX fund the loans; the company is not yet a credit market company, so it is valued on P/B, P/TBV and P/S instead of P/E. stockanalysis shows trailing P/E 4.4 and ROE 26%, but that is solely due to the gain of SEK 42.0m on the sale of Billecta; without it the result is negative. stockanalysis' debt of SEK 222m and net debt of SEK 186m include client funds (SEK 215.7m at 30/6), which are not the company's own money; the company's own net debt was SEK 35.2m at 30/6. No independent analysts cover the stock (only paid research from Kalqyl), and stockanalysis has no estimates, so PEG is n/a. Q3 is only a trading update without a balance sheet; equity and net debt at 30/9 are not disclosed. stockanalysis' date of 13/11 for the next report is wrong; the next report is the annual report on 24/2-2027. Not examined: the largest shareholders beyond the insider share of 35%, the credit quality and losses on the credits the platform's savers have invested in, and how large a share of the capital the largest institutional investors represent. The licence decision from Finansinspektionen is expected around the turn of the year 2026/27 and could change the picture in either direction.
