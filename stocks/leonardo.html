    In plain words

    Leonardo gets the top score of 100: a growing defence group, but at a high price. The share price is up fivefold since 2022 and expensive against its own history, and the Boeing aircraft-parts business still makes losses. The order book has grown 17% to €58.6bn, and expectations were raised. Keep an eye on debt, which rose to €3.5bn after several acquisitions.

    In the 70+ zone: a score above 70 with more than 20% upside to the median analyst target.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated after the report of 2026-11-04. The 9M results were approved by the board on 4 Nov and published on 5 Nov; Q3 beat consensus (revenue €5.34bn vs 5.22, EBITA €521m vs 482), but only the order guidance was raised (€29.5bn), while revenue, EBITA, FOCF and net debt were merely confirmed, and the stock fell on the day – also dragged down by Rheinmetall's fall of about 15% and uncertainty about the US defence budget after the midterm elections. Management will only update the financial targets with the 2027–31 plan in March 2027. Leonardo steers by EBITA and FOCF; forward P/E 17.9 rests on adjusted EPS (2026E €2.50), while reported EPS TTM is €2.18 (trailing P/E 23.0); adjusted net profit 9M €812m vs reported €792m. FCF is strongly seasonal: 9M FOCF was −€68m (+€22m excluding IDV), so guidance requires about €1.44bn in Q4 – the same pattern as 2025, no deduction given, but it is the single largest risk to the year's figures. Net debt at 30 Sep is €3.33bn vs guidance of €2.2bn at year-end. IDV is consolidated from 18 Mar, so organic growth must be read on the company's like-for-like figures (revenue +9%, orders +22.4%). The Italian state (MEF) owns about 30% and appoints management – mentioned, not scored. The NH90 settlement (over €110m paid out) is closed and no deduction given. The buyback (€47m in Q3) only covers incentive programmes and gives no plus. The Ukraine negotiations (US–Russia in Abu Dhabi from 10 Nov) and Bromo/GCAP are unchanged stable findings. The credit finding has been corrected: Fitch upgraded to BBB (stable) in June 2026, not BBB- positive as written in the previous analysis. Only 5 years of multiple history (2021–25) available.
