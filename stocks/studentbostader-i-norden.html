    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio is 67.7% (66.9) vs the company's own target of at most 60%, and interest cover is 1.20× over 12 months vs a target of 1.75×. The equity ratio is 28.5%, and net debt/EBITDA is about 20×. kr 1,380m (25% of debt) falls due within a year, the fixed-interest period is only 20 months, and 59% of debt is repriced within a year. Reaching the target requires property sales or a new issue.

    • Management and governance
      In October 2026 the board removed CEO Rebecka Eidenert and appointed the deputy CEO as acting CEO, and CFO David Juhlin resigned in November. Before that, major shareholder Otre Fund called an extraordinary general meeting to replace the board, and Nordstjernan's alternative proposal won. It is a showdown between owners in the middle of a refinancing.

      −4
    • State aid clawback
      Länsstyrelsen has demanded repayment of the entire investment grant of SEK 81.6m for Oxie in Malmö, equal to about 10% of market cap. The company announced on 6/7 that the amount would weigh on Q2, but reversed this in the report and made no provision, because it will appeal to Boverket.
      mfn.se
      −4

      Operating cash flow was only kr 3.2m in H1 2026 (6.3) and kr 75.8m in all of 2025, which leaves no room to reduce the debt of kr 5.6bn. The levered FCF was −kr 28m in 2025. There is no dividend, and the debt reduction depends on property sales.

      The required yield is 4.89%, and value changes were −kr 97m in H1. If the required yield rises by 0.25 percentage points, property value falls by about kr 390m, equal to about half of market value, because gearing is so high.
