    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Accounts in EUR, price in NOK (EUR/NOK approx. 11.35 based on stockanalysis' conversion of net debt). The figures are from H1 2026 (30/6); the Q3 report comes only 20/11-2026. IWS owns only approx. 75% of IWS Fleet (Sumitomo the rest) and 52.5% of ProCon, so EV and EBITDA include minorities (EV/earnings 20 vs P/E 10.5); stockanalysis' P/B 0.99 is on total equity incl. minorities, while P/B on the parent company's equity is approx. 1.3. stockanalysis' insider ownership (0.16%) is wrong: Awilco AS (closely related to two board members) owns approx. 39.5% and QVT (board member Dan Gold) approx. 9.5%. The exact breakdown of revenue by customer, day rates and the financing structure of the four newbuilds (incl. Sumitomo's share) are not disclosed and have not been examined; customer concentration has been assessed from the vessels' chartering in Q2. No industry fits exactly; 'Grid & electrical equipment' has been chosen as offshore wind's supply chain (as for Cadeler). Marked cyclical because earnings follow supply and demand for service vessels (day rates and utilisation), as at shipping companies and offshore services. Only 4 analysts, and the stock is very illiquid.
