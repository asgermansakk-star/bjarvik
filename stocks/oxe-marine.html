      Revenue fell 58% to SEK 65.0m in January–September (154.0m), and engine sales (propulsion) fell 72% to 34.2m. The gross margin of 59% in Q3 is due to product mix (spare parts and engineering services for GM), and management itself says it is not a normal level.

      The product is built on a purchased BMW diesel engine, which is being phased out; OXE has bought a final stock (all-time buy) and is to switch to a new generation with GM Duramax engines, which are still only at the development stage. The moat lies in the transmission, while the engine, and thus security of supply, is owned by others.
