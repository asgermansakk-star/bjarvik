    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −16 of −30
    • Quality of earnings
      The profit (SEK 9.9m TTM, SEK 8.0m in 2025) comes from short-term trades in small companies' issues, directed issues, warrants and block trades, i.e. realised and unrealised price gains that are not recurring. Revenue of SEK 40.6m gives only SEK 14.7m in gross profit, the Piotroski F-score is 1, and there is no cash flow data.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Scalability and capital allocation
      The high ROIC of 71% was earned on equity of about SEK 20m. The issue of SEK 66.5m gross (54.5m plus an expansion of 12m) quadruples the capital, but gains in special situations among micro caps scale poorly with size. Affärsvärlden wrote that it is crucial whether Tellus can be scaled and that the business is risky.
      placera.se
      −5
    • Business model and moat
      The investment side has no structural moat and relies on the network of two people and participation in over 100 deals since 2021. The data side Finance Lab (SaaS database, Nyemissioner.se, Providers.se) was founded in 2024, and its revenue and customer numbers are not disclosed. stockanalysis states 1 employee.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −16 of −35
    • Macro and market dependence
      All earnings depend on activity and price development in the Swedish market for issues in small companies, where the return comes from short-term gains. A weaker issue market or a small-cap bear market hits both the return in Tellus Equity and demand for Finance Labs data at the same time.
      foremont.se
      −5
    • Transparency
      There is no reporting of intrinsic value or of the portfolio, and Affärsvärlden criticised that the company does not describe in detail how it invests. Equity fell from SEK 27.3m to 20.8m and the balance sheet from 43.3m to 26.6m in H1 2026 despite a profit, which suggests a distribution or restructuring before the listing (not verified).
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Key person risk
      Operations rest on CEO Oscar Hentschel (founder of Tellus Equity) and investment director Niklas Estensson, who is also CEO of Finance Lab and runs advisory work through UBB Consulting on the side. If one of them disappears, the company loses both network and investment expertise.
      foremont.se
      −3

      Board member Mark Falkner is a partner at Eversheds Sutherland, which was legal adviser at the listing. The chair Robert Burén is a co-founder and major shareholder, and the group runs the information site Nyemissioner.se about share issues, while Tellus itself trades in the same issues.

      On average only about 11,900 shares are traded per day (approx. SEK 0.12m), and free float is 4.5m shares. When the lock-up on 91% of the pre-IPO shares expires, selling pressure may follow.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Newly listed 14/10-2026 on NGM Growth Market (IPO at SEK 10); there is no price history, no analysts and no forward estimate. stockanalysis has only two half-year periods (FY 2025 and H1 2026), no cash flow statement, and ratios such as P/B 4.1, P/TBV 24.7 and EPS decline of −70% are based on pre-issue equity measured against the post-issue share count, so they have not been used. Net asset value after the issue is our own pro forma estimate (equity of SEK 20.8m at 30/6 + SEK 66.5m gross from the issue ÷ 8.73m shares ≈ SEK 10.0 before issue costs, which are not disclosed). The information memorandum sits behind a paywall and has not been read, so the portfolio composition, issue costs, length of the lock-up period, Finance Labs' revenue and the explanation for the fall in equity in H1 2026 have not been examined. Insider trades after the listing have not been examined. The first quarterly report is due 12/11-2026. Affärsvärlden advised against subscribing for the stock in the IPO.
