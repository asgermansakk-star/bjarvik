    • Margin
      Adjusted EBITA margin of 7.0% R12 vs a target of 10%, and the operating margin (5.95% TTM) is below 8.9% in 2021. Gross margin fell to 26.0% (26.8%) in Q3, because larger data centre projects are exposed to price pressure.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Organic growth of about 11% in Q3 (14% at constant exchange rates, of which 2.9% acquired), but only about 2% for January–September. Revenue of kr 1,453m R12 is still below 1,573m in 2023, and the largest unit, Networks Centre (UK/Netherlands), fell 13% in Q3.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has risen from 18.3m (2021) to 24.5m through issues for acquisitions, while EPS from continuing operations (approx. kr 2.11 TTM) is still below kr 2.56 in 2021. The closure of Germany cost approx. kr 54m in write-downs of inventory, receivables and goodwill.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat
      The core business is distribution of passive fibre and copper products to installers and operators with a gross margin of around 26–27%; the price increases come from the suppliers, and the data centre projects are won on price. The company depends on others' products and has limited pricing power.
