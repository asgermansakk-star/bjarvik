    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Development company with no production before 2028: P/E, PEG, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and valuation is measured as price vs the project's NPV. The NPV figures (kr 4.4bn in the 2025 feasibility study; kr 7.8bn at USD 11,700/t copper from the Q1 2026 presentation) are the company's own and not adjusted for capex already spent. stockanalysis shows kr 158m in TTM revenue, but the company's report shows net sales of 0; the figure has not been used. No analyst price targets or estimates on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist). Peer multiples (P/NAV for other copper developers) have not been examined in detail. About kr 312m of the cash was tied up at 31/3-2026 as a deposit for closure costs. A new resource estimate will come with the Q4 report in February 2027.
