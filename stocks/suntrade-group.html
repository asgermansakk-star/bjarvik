    The company has only one employee and SEK 0.6m in cash against SEK 9.1m in short-term debt.     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (approx. SEK 78m, price approx. SEK 0.04) with 1 employee and no analyst coverage, so PEG and upside are n/a. The company was created through a reverse takeover into the Taurus Energy shell in September 2024, and the financial year was changed from a broken year to calendar year, so the history is short and not fully comparable; stockanalysis shows figures only to December 2025, and H1 2026 is taken from the company's unaudited Q2 report. The company's own announcements state Spotlight Stock Market as the trading venue, while the entry and stockanalysis state NGM. The price on stockanalysis is from 30/9-2026. Insider trades are taken from Börskollen (aggregator), not directly from Finansinspektionen's register; the link between Solcellskonsulterna i Stockholm AB and CEO Daniel Hedström appears only from the sales being reported in his name. The Q3 report is due 19/11-2026 and is not included. The prices of Swedish ancillary services (FCR/FFR), which Marmaverken is to earn from, have not been examined.
