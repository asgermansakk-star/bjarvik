    In plain words

    The score is 68 out of 100: a road contractor with a record order book, but very thin profits and high debt. Pulling it down: profit is only about 2.4% of sales, cash flow after leasing is negative, and net debt is 76% of market value. In its favour: SEK 7.29bn in orders, and the share looks cheap if the 2026 outlook holds. Watch the second half.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
