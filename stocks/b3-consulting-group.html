    In plain words

    The score of 65 is middling: a Swedish IT consultancy that looks cheap but has weak earnings and debt. Sales fell 6.9% over nine months, and interest takes almost two thirds of operating profit. Net debt of SEK 249m exceeds market value. On the plus side: earnings rose in the quarter and financing is secured until 2029.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Trailing P/E (94.7) is meaningless because net profit TTM is only SEK 2.4m and Q3 is burdened by SEK 6.6m in one-off refinancing costs; forward P/E 9.44 equates to an EPS of approx. SEK 2.6 vs SEK −0.40 for continuing operations in the nine months of the year and SEK 1.20 in 2025, so it is based on a sharp recovery. stockanalysis' forecast page does not exist (404), so the number of analysts and the estimate basis have not been examined. Habberstad AS has been reclassified as discontinued operations, so TTM figures on stockanalysis mix continuing and discontinued operations. The ownership group (the nomination committee represents approx. 25%) and B3's total insider ownership have not been checked in the share register; stockanalysis shows 1.27%. Customer concentration at single-customer level is not disclosed; only the industry breakdown has been used. B3 gives no financial guidance.
