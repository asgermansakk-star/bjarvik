    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis has no analyst forecasts for Inission (forecast gives 404), although ABG Sundal Collier follows the company; forward P/E 12.6 therefore rests on a thin and unknown estimate base, and upside and revision points may be missing. The TTM figures on stockanalysis run only to June 2026; the Q3 report (5/11) is used for revenue, margins, net debt and cash flow. FCF after leases (approx. kr 75m TTM) is calculated as stockanalysis' FCF minus lease repayments. The gross margin of 45% is due to the format of the income statement and is not comparable with EMS peers. Bavaria Digital Technik (bought 2/11) is not yet included in the figures and has no pro forma figures. The share of revenue from defence and data centres is not disclosed. Electronics manufacturing (EMS) does not fit the industry list exactly and is placed under Tech hardware & optics like Kitron and NOTE.
