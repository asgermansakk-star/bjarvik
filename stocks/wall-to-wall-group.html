    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small company (market cap about kr 356m) with very low liquidity. No analyst estimates on stockanalysis (forward P/E and PEG n/a). Reported FCF and P/FCF 2.9 are inflated by sales of receivables (factoring) in Q3 2026 and have not been used as an expression of cheapness. The share count was checked against the cash flow (small buybacks, −1.7% in a year); LTIP 2026 is delivered from own shares, but the board has a mandate to issue up to 10% in connection with acquisitions. Customer concentration (the largest customers' share) has not been examined in detail. The company started as a SPAC in 2021 and is now an operating company (Spolargruppen etc.).
