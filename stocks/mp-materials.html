    In plain words

    MP Materials gets 48: the company still loses money, and about two thirds of this year's adjusted earnings came from the government's price floor. Cash flow after investments is minus $537m, and the magnet business has yet to prove its earnings. The Department of War holds preferred shares and warrants equal to about 13.7% more shares at today's price, and the government must approve any sale of more than 15% of the company, which can limit a takeover premium. On the plus side, the government provides a 10-year price floor and buys all output from the new magnet plant for 10 years. Watch whether the new Congress majority from January 2027 touches the funding behind the deal.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). Since 29 Sep: the Q3 results (5 Nov) showed revenue of $147.7m plus $8.1m of PPA income, adjusted EBITDA of $41.1m and a net loss of $20.7m (adjusted EPS $0.04 above consensus), GM qualification of the magnets is complete, the share price has fallen from about $47 at the end of September to $35.54 (23 Nov; −11.9% in October after lowered price targets at Baird, Morgan Stanley and Goldman), three senior executives bought shares in November, and the Democrats won Congress in the midterm elections. The government stake is verified in the 8-K of 10 Jul 2025: 400,000 Series A preferred shares at $1,000 each ($400m, 7% PIK dividend, conversion at $30.03 = about 13.3m shares; liquidation preference $435.6m at 30 Sep) and a warrant for 11,201,659 shares at $30.03 for 10 years, about 15% as-converted at signing and with an ownership cap of 19.9%. On top of that come a 10-year NdPr price floor of $110/kg (the government gets 30% of the price above $110 once 10X runs at full capacity), a 10X agreement under which the government takes the entire output with Threshold EBITDA of $140m/year, and a $150m loan (10-year Treasury rate + 1%). The deal is scored in three facets: floor and offtake as Plus, dilution and the government's veto rights as T3, and the election result as a new, separate event in T3. Shenghe: China Rare Earth Group's purchase in October covers only Shenghe's smelting and separation business, not the MP stake (about 3%), and MP no longer sells to China, so no deduction has been given. Q3 was lifted by a one-off sale (SEG+ $31.6m). Short interest is 20% of the shares. The 10X plant's timeline and total cost are not in the Q3 material, and capex guidance of $500–600m for 2026 is only found in secondary summaries of the call. Cyclical: the selling price follows the NdPr market (about $100/kg) with the floor as the bottom.
