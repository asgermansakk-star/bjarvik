    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −10 of −30
    • Moat
      NOTE is a contract manufacturer (EMS) and builds to customers' own designs, so customers can move production. The gross margin is only 14.5% (TTM); it is delivery reliability and proximity, not proprietary technology, that carries the business.

    • Cash flow
      Adjusted operating cash flow after investments was only kr 23m in January–September vs kr 379m a year earlier, because inventory was built up due to component shortages. FCF is kr 71m (TTM), an FCF margin of 1.7% vs 10–13% in 2024–2025, and the FCF yield is 1.6%.

      −4
    • Debt and liquidity
      STI was bought for GBP 72.5m (approx. kr 822m) financed with a kr 900m loan from SEB. Net debt excl. leases rose to kr 1,312m (27), available liquidity fell to kr 386m (735), the equity ratio to 38.7% (49.6%), and financial costs have more than doubled. The dividend for 2025 was dropped.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4

      In March NOTE promised an operating margin of 9.5–10.5% for 2026 and continued strong operating cash flow. After nine months the margin is 9.3% adjusted and 8.3% reported, cash flow is near zero, and the Q3 report did not repeat the guidance. The stock fell 11% on the report day.
      mfn.se
      −3

      Sales from the factory in China fell approx. 30% in January–September, the older British units fell 11%, and the relocation of Lund and Hyvinkää to new factories caused production disruption in Q3.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 2 analysts cover the stock on stockanalysis (price targets SEK 195–196), so forward P/E 12.6 and the EPS estimate for 2027 (+24%) rest on a very thin basis. STI was bought in March 2026, so TTM figures and ROIC mix before and after the acquisition; NOTE has not published pro forma figures. P/TBV 13.4 is lifted by goodwill from the acquisitions and has not been used. Stockanalysis shows no lease payments in the TTM cash flow, so FCF after leases may be lower than the SEK 71m. The Q3 report does not repeat the March guidance for the full-year margin (9.5–10.5%); the assessment of guidance rests on the level after nine months (9.3% adjusted, 8.3% reported) being below. Electronics manufacturing (EMS) does not fit the industry list exactly and has been placed under Tech hardware & optics like Kitron. Governance beyond insider ownership and trades has not been investigated further.
