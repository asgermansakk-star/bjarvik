    In plain words

    Bjarvik scores it 40 out of 100. It loses money (about 60% of its income in 2025), and revenue has fallen from 78m to 39m kr. in two years. The share count rose 68%, guidance was cut, and the effect of a US lawsuit is unknown. On the plus side, cash of 24.8m kr. far exceeds debt of 0.3m. Watch that the half-year report is unaudited.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro company (market value about DKK 21m) with no analyst coverage and no forward estimates, so PEG and upside cannot be calculated. The company only publishes half-year reports, so there is no Q3 report; the latest figures are H1 2026 (12/8-2026). The report is unaudited and has not been reviewed by the auditor, and from 2026 reporting is under the Danish Financial Statements Act instead of IFRS, so H1 2026 cannot be compared directly with 2022–2025 (leases now sit above EBITDA). Cash of DKK 24.8m has been adjusted for customer prepayments of DKK 15.7m according to the rule. The web search quota was used up in this run, so the research is based on stockanalysis, the company's website, the H1 report and the announcements via Ritzau. Not examined (0 points): customer concentration (not disclosed), multiples vs peers, the extent of insiders' subscription in the issue and the content of the US lawsuit. The grade is therefore a ceiling.
