    • Margin
      Loss: adjusted EBIT of −SEK 32.9m (−3.2%) and net result of −46.4m over the last 12 months, ROE −8.9% and ROIC 1.4%, vs an 18.8% operating margin in 2021. Even the core business GARO Electrification fell to a 4.4% adjusted EBIT margin in Q2 (7.4% a year earlier), far from the target of at least 10%.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen four years in a row, from SEK 1,391m (2022) to 1,031m (TTM), a fall of 26%. Q2 growth of 2% came mainly from Ireland and the UK, while E-mobility fell 7%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation and exit from E-mobility
      After a strategic review, the charging box GARO Entity (about SEK 100m in annual revenue) is being phased out, and capitalised development, inventory and other assets are written down by about SEK 150m, equal to about 30% of equity. The years of investment in its own charging platform are lost, and the company is left with mature electrical distribution products for construction. The dividend has been stopped since 2023.

      Gross margin has fallen from 50.2% (2021) to 44.0% (TTM). The Electrification margin was squeezed in Q2 by currency and higher selling costs, and E-mobility has run a heavy price campaign to clear inventory.

      Net debt of SEK 213.7m without leasing (257.6m with) vs adjusted EBITDA of only 19.9m (R12).     • Management and execution
      CEO Jonas Klarén left the company in January 2026 to become CEO of Assemblin El. COO Joe Ree was acting until Tobias Byfeldt took over in the summer. At the same time a new ERP system is being implemented (inventory write-downs in Q2), subsidiaries are being merged, production is being moved from Poland and Sweden is being reorganised. That is many projects at once in a small company.
