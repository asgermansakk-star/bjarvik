      With 14 employees and DKK 57m in revenue, Loyal Solutions is a very small player in card-linked loyalty, where Visa and Mastercard themselves and larger platforms compete. PCI DSS Level 1, SOC2 and ISO 27001 as well as deep integrations create switching costs, but the moat is narrow.

      Selected shareholders, the board and management started a review of the capital structure with advisers in September 2025, which may include talks with new investors. No outcome has been announced. Restoring the negative equity through conversion or issue will dilute current shareholders.

      The customer base is a few large programmes, especially airlines (Qatar Airways, Emirates, Virgin Australia, SAS EuroBonus, Etihad) and Visa's VMLS platform; 78% of ARR growth came from existing customers. The split is not disclosed, but the loss of one large customer would hit revenue hard, and the airline industry is cyclical.

The finance function is outsourced with no CFO, and reporting is only half-yearly.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Danish company (A/S) listed on First North Stockholm; the stock trades in SEK, the accounts are kept in DKK (converted at approx. 1.48 SEK/DKK). Half-year reporting only, and the annual report contains no cash flow statement, so FCF is estimated from the change in cash and debt; stockanalysis' FCF (SEK 6.6m) and net cash (SEK 4.7m) have not been used, because loans of DKK 25.3m have been moved to 'other payables' and are missing from stockanalysis' debt and EV. No analyst coverage, no forward estimate and very low liquidity (approx. 2,500 shares a day). The outcome of the strategic review of the capital structure from September 2025 has not been found. Customer concentration is not disclosed in figures, and peers have been assessed qualitatively without retrieved peer multiples. The half-year report for H1 2026/27 has not been published (stockanalysis states 10/11-2026, the MFN calendar 11/2-2027).
