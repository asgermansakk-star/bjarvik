    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −16 of −30
    • Margin and return on capital
      EBIT margin 5.7% and net margin 2.3% (TTM) vs an EBIT margin of 12.1% in 2021; adjusted EBIT margin −2.7% in Q3 and 9.2% (9.5%) in the first nine months of the year, far from the target of over 10% across a cycle. ROIC has fallen from 17.5% (2021) to 5.3% TTM, below WACC of 8.8%, and ROCE is 6.3% vs the target of 15%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen four years in a row from kr 54.0bn (2022) to kr 44.8bn TTM (−17%). In Q3 sales fell 5% organically, and Forest & Garden – the largest division – fell 10% organically on weak European demand; only Gardena (+8%) and North America grew.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat and future segment
      Robotic lawn mowers, the group's most important growth area, had a weak quarter with competitive pressure, bad weather and excess inventories at dealers in Europe; management itself writes that development is below expectations. Husqvarna has complained to the EU about dumping by Chinese competitors, and Gardena's Powered Garden unit is under strategic review.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Acquisitions for kr 3.9bn in 2021 and a still expensive capital base have not delivered returns: ROIC has more than halved since, the dividend was cut from kr 3.00 to 1.00 in 2024, and the financial targets were lowered at the capital markets day in December 2025, where kr 1.5bn in restructuring costs towards 2030 was also flagged.
      mfn.se
      −2
