The cash of SEK 22m comes from the share issue and not from operations.

    • Guidance and credibility
      The targets for 2025 were lowered in September 2025 from SEK 120–150m in revenue and 5–8m in EBITDA to 110 and 2m, and the outcome was 103.9 and 1.1m, i.e. below even the lowered targets. The targets for 2026 (150–200m and EBITDA 8–12m) require at least 53m in revenue (+42%) and over 4m in EBITDA in Q4 vs about 0 in Q4 2025, and management itself warns that the EBITDA target may be missed.
