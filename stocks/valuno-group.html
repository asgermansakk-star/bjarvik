    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Formerly QuickBit eu AB. The accounts are kept in EUR with a July–June financial year, while the stock trades in SEK on NGM. Stockanalysis calculates the market cap (SEK 12m) on 217.6m shares, but the debt-for-equity issues in October 2026 raise the share count to 483.4m, and the Helena conversions may lift it further; the real market cap is therefore at least double. No analysts, no forecasts and no PEG. The cash balance at 30/6-2026 is not shown on stockanalysis and was not found in the report, so the liquidity finding is based on the control balance sheet, current ratio and cost level. Insider trades have not been examined (0 points). The VAT case of SEK 21.8m and the 2019–2022 transfer pricing case are undecided; the administrative court has granted a stay without security, but it can be appealed. The company is not in liquidation: the control meeting on 28/8-2026 decided to continue operations.
