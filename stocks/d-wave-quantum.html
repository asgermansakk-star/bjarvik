    In plain words

    D-Wave Quantum gets 30, mainly because the share is still very expensive: about 210 times revenue, even after a 64% fall in a year. The loss is growing, revenue over the last 12 months is less than half the 2025 level, and over half of equity is values from an acquisition. In September the US government received about 1.9% of the shares, without voting rights, in return for up to $100m in milestone-based support; the dilution is small, but nothing had been paid out by 30 September, and the deal can be cancelled or declared invalid. On the plus side, net cash is $446m and the order backlog has grown sharply. Watch the fourth quarter, where analysts' estimates require a large system sale.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). Since 29 Sep: the Q3 report (10 Nov) and the 10-Q (16 Nov) have come out, the government stake is final, and the share price has fallen from $15.79 (6 Oct) to $7.31 (23 Nov). Government stake (verified in the 8-K of 8 Sep, the 8-K/A of 9 Sep and the 10-Q): OTA under the CHIPS Act entered into on 4 Sep 2026 with the Commerce Department for up to $100m in tranches after milestones ($53.55m made available first, then $9.08m + $16.70m + $20.39m + $0.29m); SIA entered into on 8 Sep, under which 7,095,721 non-voting common shares were issued to the department at $14.093 per share (lowest closing price on three reference dates minus 15%) with no cash payment at issuance. The department votes only on mergers and changes to the shares' rights, may only sell in step with support paid out, has registration rights, and on termination 'for convenience' (60 days' notice) D-Wave can buy back the shares corresponding to unpaid support for $1. At 30 Sep nothing had been paid out. The stake is treated as one finding in T3; the grant must be spent on project costs and therefore gives no Plus. Losses: P/E and PEG are meaningless. Analysts' 2026 estimate ($37.4m) requires about $27m of revenue in Q4 vs $10.5m in the first nine months of the year, presumably from the system delivery to Italy; if the Q4 system sale fails to come, it will hit the estimates. Customer concentration for Q3 was not found in the 10-Q; the finding is based on the first half. Insider trading since August is only tax withholding (code F) and a small director sale; no purchases.
