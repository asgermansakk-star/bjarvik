    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Adjusted EBITA only kr 22.6m over 12 months (1.7% of revenue) vs an operating margin of 10.5% in 2021 and 7.8% in 2022; gross margin has fallen from 26% to 11% (reported), ROIC is −2.7% and the return on capital employed excluding goodwill only 1.7%.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue (kr 1,335m TTM) is on a par with 2022 (1,334) despite acquisitions of approx. kr 255m since 2021; the core renovation business has fallen from kr 1,163m in 2022 to 1,019m, and sales to bostadsrättsföreningar fell 19% in the first nine months of the year (442 vs 548m).
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The debt-financed acquisitions are being rolled back: TBO-Haglinds and Stora Fasad have been folded into other units, Holland has been closed, and Q3 carried SEK 88m in one-off items, of which write-downs of 79m. Net debt has risen from SEK 96m in 2021 to 446m, while earnings have vanished.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat and pricing power
      Market leader in the Nordics with patented solutions, but the fall in gross margin from 26% to 16% (adjusted) shows weak pricing power; growth comes from new-build and construction firms with lower margins, while the high-margin renovation for housing associations is weakening.
