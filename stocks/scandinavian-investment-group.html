    • Margin and returns

    • Quality of growth
      Of the profit of DKK 23.0m in H1 2026, DKK 19.2m came from price gains on equity holdings, primarily two illiquid strategic positions (SAGA Furs and Fable Media). Properties contributed 7.1m and the credit bonds a loss of 0.9m. Recurring earnings are far lower than the profit for the year.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Moat and scale
      A micro company with one employee and investments of approx. kr 384m. The investments are found with external advisers. Costs of approx. kr 4m a year correspond to approx. 1.8% of equity, before fees to managers. There is no documented advantage in any of the four asset classes.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation and strategy
      The strategy changes often. Mortgage deeds were added as a fourth business area in June 2025. In August 2026 the agreement with the manager NCI was terminated, and the credit bonds are to be wound down over 24–36 months after losses on restructurings and currency. The buybacks below intrinsic value have stopped.

      Interest-bearing debt has risen from kr 105.8m to 173.2m since 2024, and 45.9m falls due within a year. Equity ratio has fallen from 63.4% to 55.1%. The board itself receives warrants (1.42m shares at kr 3.38 in 2026 on top of 1.65m vested in 2023), and the company does not disclose which shares and credit bonds it owns. The half-year report has not been reviewed.
