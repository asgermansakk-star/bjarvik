    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      No operating history in the new form: AHTS AS only started in 2026, and the whole business was bought in in October 2026 with 1.15bn new shares (from 22.9m). All 'growth' is acquisitions, and there are no pro forma figures for revenue, margin or cash flow.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Moat
      No pricing power: day rates for anchor-handling vessels are set by the market, and the fleet is spread across about ten markets and three technical/commercial managers (UOS, DOF and Aurora). According to Baird Maritime, the vessels are too few and too scattered to exploit the high North Sea rates.
      bairdmaritime.com
      −5
    • Fleet age and future capex
      Almost all ships were built in 2009–2010 (average over 16 years); docking for an estimated at least USD 50m awaits around 2029–2030, and the business plan assumes a 30-year life, which few offshore vessels reach.
      bairdmaritime.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The ships were bought for approx. USD 27m each (USD 406m for 15) from sponsors who, according to Baird Maritime, bought the 11 former Hayfin ships for approx. USD 23m in July 2026; the sellers earned an estimated approx. USD 60m in two months at the listed company's expense.
      bairdmaritime.com

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is effectively a new company: the dry bulk fleet was sold in April 2026, the cash paid out (special dividend of NOK 129.5), and on 1 and 5 October 2026 11–15 large AHTS vessels were bought for new shares (reverse takeover; name change to AHTS proposed). stockanalysis' history (P/E 0.09, P/B 199, ROIC 1,190%, dividend yield 184%, −94% over 52 weeks) is based on the old dry bulk business and ship sale gains and has not been used (pro forma rule). Forward P/E 6.27 has not been used, because it is unclear whether the estimate covers the new AHTS business; stockanalysis has no forecast page. No pro forma accounts exist, so margin, ROIC and FCF after leasing for the AHTS business have not been examined (0 points; the grade is a cap). The market value of approx. NOK 10.2bn is the price of the only 22.9m old shares multiplied by all 1.23bn shares; the new shares trade on Euronext NOTC (2020T), whose price has not been found. The EBITDA estimate is Bjarvik's own illustrative calculation on the assumptions reported by Baird Maritime, not company guidance. Contract coverage and charter list have not been published. Q3 report 25/11 and prospectus expected late November/early December. No analyst coverage found. Exchange rate approx. 9.47 NOK/USD (NOK 2.83 = USD 0.299).
