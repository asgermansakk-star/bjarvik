    The score of 56 shows a mediocre business at a high price. Most weighing it down: the share price is up 123% in a year without earnings keeping pace, and returns on invested money are low. In its favour, sales to AI and cloud customers more than doubled, and cash exceeds debt. Watch cash flow, which was negative in the quarter.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
