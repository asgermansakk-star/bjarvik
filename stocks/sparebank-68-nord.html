    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The ownership share fraction is 33.21% at 30/6-2026, so approx. 67% of equity is self-owned (primary capital). The security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. The certificates trade on Euronext Growth Oslo. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.37, trailing P/E of 17.42, EPS of 11.94 and share count change of +37% have not been used: P/B is based on total equity including self-owned capital, EPS is based on the bank's erroneous 'earnings per equity certificate' of NOK 5.48 for H1 (the same figure as for Q2 alone), and the number of certificates has been unchanged at 2,992,721 since 2024 according to note 11 and note 38. T2 is based on the bank's own figures per certificate: book value approx. NOK 184–186 at 30/6-2026 (ownership share capital plus the fraction's share of the fund for unrealised gains and profit for the period), earnings per certificate of NOK 15.19 (2025) and approx. NOK 9.8 for H1 2026. The historical P/B at year-ends is an estimate based on the market cap on stockanalysis and the ownership share fraction. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are H1 2026; the Q3 report is due on 12/11-2026 and is not included. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 10.93 has an uncertain basis and has not been used, and there is effectively no analyst coverage. The regulatory requirement for common equity tier 1 (incl. Pillar 2) has not been found, so the capital buffer above the requirement has not been quantified. Insider trades have only been read from the owner lists in the annual report and the bank's own announcements about employee savings; no actual insider purchases or sales have been found for 2025–2026. No web search used; no announcements about a merger or bid have been found.
