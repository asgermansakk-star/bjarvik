    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −13 of −30
    • Development of net asset value
      NAV per share rose only from kr 147.00 (Q2 2025) to 151.50 (Q2 2026), +3.1%, or approx. 4.8% incl. the dividend of 2.52. That is below the company's own target of more than 7% annual NAV growth.

    • Capital allocation
      kr 970m in available capital (about 47% of net asset value) sits in equity and bond funds while the company waits for investments. The largest holdings are minority stakes of 24.5% (Sambygd, Dalakraft, Adven Siljan), where a partner owns 51%, so Siljansvik has no control over operations or dividends.

      The parent company's operating costs were kr 32.0m in 2025 (about 1.5% of net asset value) with 9 employees. The group's EBIT before associates is negative (−kr 20.9m TTM), and ROIC is −0.6%.

      −3

Electricity trading is a commodity-like business with thin margins; Kalmar earned SEK 8.1m before tax on SEK 215m in revenue in H1 (3.8%).

    • Governance and conflicts of interest
      Three of seven board members are or have been chairs of the municipal council in Rättvik, Leksand and Gagnef, the same municipalities that in 2025 bought 24.5% of Sambygd from the company and hold stakes in Sambygd and Adven Siljan.       The government has put grid tariffs under review and requires grid companies to abolish power charges.     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Siljansvik (formerly Dala Energi) is an investment company: NAV (SEK 151.50 at 30/6-2026) is book equity, where the associates are carried under the equity method and not at market value; P/B and discount to NAV are therefore used in T2. No analysts cover the share, and there are no forward estimates, so PEG is n/a. The 44.7% fall in share count is due to the redemption programme in 2025 (SEK 1,545m), which was financed by the sale of the electricity and fibre networks and not by FCF, so it adds no plus. Insider trades and the full shareholder list have not been examined (0 points). Affärsvärlden's analysis was behind a paywall and has not been read. The Q3 report comes on 12–13 November 2026.
