    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Listed 10 June 2026 (subscription price SEK 60), so there is no own multiple history; the criterion gives 0, and T2 rests solely on peers and FCF yield. Stockanalysis' share count (+427%) and EPS history are distorted by the capital structure before the listing and have not been used; dilution is measured on the IPO issue. Trailing P/E is n/a (loss after listing costs of SEK 32.9m and amortisation of acquired intangible assets); forward P/E 23.5 is on adjusted EPS. The company's ROIC is before tax and on adjusted EBITA. Only 2 analysts cover the stock, and they appear to be from the issuing banks (DNB Carnegie and Nordea), so the price target is thin. Lock-up terms for the former owners and insider trades after the listing have not been investigated. Customer concentration at company level (number of customers, largest customer) is not disclosed. MFN's company description (technical services/installation) is wrong; the company buys Nordic vertical B2B software companies.
