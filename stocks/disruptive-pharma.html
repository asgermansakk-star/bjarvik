    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −24 of −30
    • Margin
      Disruptive Pharma had net revenue of only SEK 0.9m in 2025 vs an operating loss of SEK 20.1m (2024: 0 and −30.5m). In Q1 2026 revenue was SEK 0.6m and operating profit −SEK 3.4m. The company has never made money.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is small payments for feasibility studies and evaluation projects. According to the bolagsbeskrivning, the company has not yet entered into any partnerships or licence agreements of commercial significance. The organisation has only four employees.
      disruptivepharma.se
      −5
    • Moat
      Formulite (mesoporous magnesium carbonate) is patented and tested with over 100 substances, but has been tried in only one clinical pilot study in humans. The technology must compete with established amorphous solid dispersions, which the large contract manufacturers already offer. The licensed base patents (DM1) run only to 2033, and only the newer applications reach to 2038.
      disruptivepharma.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Disruptive Pharma is financed by repeated issues: rights issues of SEK 25.1m (2024) and approx. SEK 10m (2025) and a directed issue of SEK 6.3m (April 2026). The auditor qualified for going concern in both 2024 and 2025. The accumulated deficit is SEK 112m, and access to the stock market and capital was bought via Guard's cash rather than a proper listing.
      disruptivepharma.se
      −5

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −24 of −35
    • Funding
      According to the CEO, cash without revenue lasts about 18 months, i.e. to around H1 2028, and the company has only declared that working capital covers 12 months. The company has no recurring revenue, and scale-up to commercial GMP production is planned for about 2029. A new issue is therefore likely in 2027, and at the current share price it will cause heavy dilution.
      storage.mfn.se
      −7
    • Dilution via consideration shares
      If Guard's guarantees turn out to have been breached before the end of February 2027, up to 20% additional consideration shares (about 4.9m shares, about 11% of the share count) can be issued to Disruptive's former owners. The board has already been authorised to do so.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Customer and partner concentration
      The only major project is an evaluation with one large pharma company of two phase II programmes (metabolic and cardiovascular). Continuation depends on the partner's own decisions and timetable. In addition there are only development projects with three smaller companies, and the agreement with a contract manufacturer (CDMO) is still missing. The company describes the partner variously as 'top-10' (Q2 report) and 'top-20' (company description).
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Licence and supplier dependence
      The right to the MMC material rests on an exclusive licence from Disruptive Materials Operations AB, which can end in a dispute or on the licensor's insolvency. Formulite is currently produced only at pilot scale under GMP-like conditions, and the company has no production capacity of its own.
      disruptivepharma.se
      −3
    • Execution and organisation
      The strategy requires several partnerships, a global CDMO agreement and scaling up at the same time, but the company has only four employees. A new board and a new CEO took office on completion of the reverse takeover 27/7-2026.

      The price is SEK 0.97, i.e. 61% below the transaction price of SEK 2.48 and 30% below the 200-day average (1.39). The price has fallen 28% over 52 weeks, and only approx. 15,000 shares a day are traded.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is the former Guard Therapeutics, which completed a reverse takeover (RTO) of Disruptive Pharma Holding on 27/7-2026 and trades as DPHA from 21/8-2026. Under the rule on pro forma for 12 months after a merger, stockanalysis' history, FCF (−65m, Guard's old trial expenses) and the missing multiples are not used as a basis; T1 and T3 are based on Disruptive Pharma's own figures in the bolagsbeskrivning (2024, 2025, Q1 2026), and cash is an estimate from Guard's and Disruptive's cash as of 31/5 (SEK 36.5m + 8.8m) less transaction costs and burn. There are no pro forma accounts and no analyst coverage; the Q3 report (first consolidated figures) is due 12/11-2026. Own price history against multiples is not meaningful and scores 0. The value of the royalty and milestone right to the sold GTX platform has not been quantified. Customer concentration has only been assessed from the company's own descriptions of the projects.
