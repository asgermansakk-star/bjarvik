    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: the main multiple is price vs net asset value (NAV SEK 65.00 at 30/9-2026, incl. deferred tax). At a price of 39.50, P/NAV is approx. 0.61 and P/B 0.75. Ongoing earnings are measured as förvaltningsresultat per share (SEK 2.0 rolling 12 months). stockanalysis' trailing P/E 8.6 is lifted by value adjustments of properties and interest rate derivatives and is not used; forward P/E 19.8 is based on adjusted EPS. The interest cover of 2.8 × includes the gain on the wind turbines (SEK 98m). The share count has fallen from 656m to 631m via buybacks and cancellation of 24m B shares; buybacks and dividends (SEK 772m TTM) are covered by operating cash flow (SEK 1,410m), but not after investments in new construction (FCF −SEK 1.48bn), so no plus is given for the buyback. The loans' capital commitment (maturity profile of the credits, not the interest rate fixing) is not stated in the report and has not been investigated; 89% of the interest-bearing debt is classified as short-term. The company has no public credit rating, as far as has been investigated. Peers are only compared with Castellum, Balder and Sagax, which were scored the same day; pure residential peers (Heba, K-Fast) have not been reviewed. Only 5 analysts cover the stock. Wallenstam gives no earnings guidance, only a target of NAV of SEK 80 per share in 2030.
