    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Salix Group was spun off and listed 15 June 2026, so stockanalysis' TTM figures (P/E 0.44, net income 4.1bn, EV/EBITDA 3.8, dividend yield 8.8%) mix in Salix and an accounting gain of approx. kr 4.1bn and have not been used; under the rule of 12 months after a spin-off, the findings are based on Volati's own figures for continuing operations in the Q3 report, and multiples vs own history have not been used in T2. The dividend of kr 2 is from before the spin-off; analysts expect approx. kr 0.11 going forward. EV is calculated with the preference shares at market price (kr 622 x 1.60m units) and net debt incl. leases. Only 2 analysts cover the stock, and price targets were cut sharply after the spin-off (Nordea kr 135 to 47 in June). Customer and supplier concentration beyond Tornum's Lantmännen deliveries has not been examined.
