    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating profit is −SEK 6.6m on revenue of SEK 12.1m (TTM), i.e. an operating margin of −54%. Gross margin is high (87%), but the company has had losses every year since the listing, and net loss was −SEK 5.5m in nine months in 2026 (−5.5m a year earlier). So it has not got smaller.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Growth has stalled. Revenue fell 2% to SEK 9.47m in January–September (9.64m a year earlier), Q2 fell 14%, and Q3 rose only 1.6% to SEK 3.38m, after revenue had grown 87% in 2025. The CEO himself writes that he is not satisfied with sales development in the US and Europe, and that orders from key customers have not yet come.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation and strategy
      The share count has risen from 9.6m (2021) to 26.2m (2.7x) through repeated issues. In 2026 the company changed its name and broadened its purpose to consulting and asset management as a 'holding, investment and development company', where future investments need not be based on TSE. This is an unclear strategic direction for a company with 1 employee and cash of SEK 1.9m.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat
      TSE is patented and has over 60 installed systems and over 5,000 treatments sold in Animal Care. It is, however, a small niche within veterinary electroporation, where customers switch from older systems from other suppliers, and the company has no documented pricing power.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Future segment
      Human Care (cancer treatment of humans in India) is effectively at a standstill. Recruitment to the study is slower than expected, and a new sponsored study with Tata Memorial depends on funding. There is no revenue from Human Care, and all revenue comes from Animal Care.
      mb.cision.com
      −2

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −30 of −35
    • Funding
      Cash was only SEK 1.9m at 30/9 (SEK 4.3m the year before), and operating cash flow was −SEK 2.5m in Q3 and −SEK 5.4m over nine months. The overdraft had only SEK 0.5m available. The board writes that further financing may be needed within 12 months, and that going concern is based on loans and new issues. The company is effectively dependent on new capital within a few quarters.

    • Debt and equity ratio
      The group's equity has fallen to SEK 133k (SEK 6.2m the year before) and is below half of the share capital. The company also has loans of SEK 8.0m from external lenders and board members, incl. SEK 3m of a SEK 6m growth loan that is free of repayments for 8 months. The book value consists mostly of capitalised development and patents (SEK 3.0m).
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Dilution
      The share count rose 10.7% in a year and 2.3% in the latest quarter. New shares are typically issued at a discount (the directed issue in May was 10% below the price), and the TO4 and TO5 warrant programmes may give a further 4.8%. With cash of SEK 1.9m, another issue is likely at a price that is now 36% below the issue price of SEK 3.35.

In March the CEO swapped 220,000 B shares for A shares. In addition there are loans from board members.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Neem's Hill AB was called Scandinavian ChemoTech until May 2026 (TSE electroporation for cancer treatment, Animal Care under the vetIQure brand and Human Care under IQwave). Micro-cap with a market value of approx. SEK 56m. There is no analyst coverage, so upside and revision points are 0, and P/E and PEG do not exist because of losses. Note that stockanalysis' EV of SEK 54m does not include the loans of SEK 8.0m, so the real EV is approx. SEK 62.5m. stockanalysis' TTM net result (−SEK 8.0m) differs from the company's own nine-month figures (−SEK 5.5m over 9 months). The Q3 report is unaudited. Customer concentration has not been quantified, because the company does not disclose the share from the largest chain customers. The Human Care study in India and the terms of the second tranche of the growth loan have only been read as the report describes them. No binding bids or special situation.
