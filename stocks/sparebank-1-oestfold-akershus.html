    In plain words

    The model sees a regional bank with a steady dividend, but falling profit and a share at the high end of its own history. Profit fell 9.3% and costs rose 10.3%. The dividend yields 5.4%. Almost 80% of business lending is property, which the bank itself calls exposed, and the capital buffer is only about 1 percentage point above the minimum. Only two analysts follow the share.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share, but the equity certificate fraction is 93.1%, so it is economically almost fully equivalent to a share and is scored as such. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. T2 is based on the bank's own book equity per equity certificate (NOK 382.71) from the Q3 report, not stockanalysis's P/B of 1.05. stockanalysis's +4.9% (and −10.1% quarter) in share count is a data error; the number of certificates has been unchanged at 12,388,560 since 2022 and earns no points. P/B is below peers (SpareBank 1 Østlandet 1.49 and SMN 1.61 at the same ROE), but the discount probably reflects the very low liquidity and earns no plus. Only 2 analysts cover the security. Insider trades beyond the bank's own buybacks for the employee savings programme, and the bank's rating, have not been examined. The Sparebankkommisjonen's proposal for a new capital structure for savings banks has not been assessed separately.
