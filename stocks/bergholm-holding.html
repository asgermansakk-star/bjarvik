    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −13 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBIT margin is 4.1% TTM (3.6% in 2025 and −0.3% in 2024) with a gross margin of only 13.9%. In Q3 2026 EBIT fell to SEK 1.0m (3.5m), a margin of 0.8%, and even adjusted for the Norway start-up EBIT was only about 3.5m. The company's own target is an EBT margin of 5%.
      mb.cision.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is flat in 9M 2026 (SEK 430.8m vs 430.6m; Q2 −11.4%), while 4,834 new motorhomes were registered in Sweden in January–August vs 3,215 the year before. The 44% growth in 2025 came partly from batch purchases on attractive terms in bankruptcies in Europe, which will not be repeated.

      −4
    • Moat and future segment
      Bergholm is an importer and general agent for others' brands (Knaus, Weinsberg, Tabbert, T@B, Morelo, Roller Team) and owns neither product nor brand. The position rests on agency agreements; the new Norwegian agreement with Knaus Tabbert runs for five years.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 7 findings    −17 of −35
    • Supplier concentration
      The business rests on agencies from a few European manufacturers, especially Knaus Tabbert, which stands behind four of the brands and now also Norway. Knaus Tabbert has a net loss of EUR 20.6m TTM, an EBIT margin of 0.6% and debt/EBITDA of 9.9; if the agency is weakened or terminated, it hits the whole business.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Currency risk
      The cars are bought in EUR and sold in SEK. In 2025 the currency hedges swung from −SEK 21.1m in Q1 to +17.7m in Q2, which is larger than the whole year's profit before tax (17.2m). The new policy with a lower hedge ratio moves the risk into operations: −SEK 2.0m in exchange differences in Q1 2026.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Inventory and dealers
      Inventory rose to SEK 92.3m (74.5m the year before), equal to 87% of equity, and receivables from dealers are 49.0m. The network consists of approx. 30 Swedish and 15 Norwegian dealers; a model-year change or a weak season may cause write-downs and losses on receivables.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Execution in Norway
      The establishment in Norway cost approx. SEK 2.5m in Q3 with no revenue yet and tied up inventory. That equals approx. 10% of TTM EBIT in a new market where the company must first build a dealer network (15 dealers).
      news.cision.com
      −2
    • Governance and ownership concentration
      CEO Lars Bergholm's LABE Business AB owns 71.4%, and EntreprenörsVärden owns 16.3%. The group rents its premises from the majority owner's property company, and the interim reports have not been reviewed by the auditor.
      mb.cision.com

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is called Bergholm Fritidsfordon AB and was listed on Spotlight on 26/3-2026 at SEK 11 per share, so there is no own multiple history; T2 rests on peers and absolute levels. Stockanalysis' price (SEK 10.75) is from 30/9-2026 and may be outdated, and the stock trades very thinly. Net cash of SEK 49.5m is measured at a seasonal peak in trade payables (63.8m); at the end of Q2, cash was only 18.4m despite the IPO proceeds. Trailing P/E includes Q4 2025; Q2 2025 contained a one-off gain of 17.7m from currency hedging and Q1 2025 a loss of 21.1m, which however lies outside the TTM period. Knaus Tabbert's share of revenue is not disclosed. Insider trades after the listing, lock-up terms and dealers' credit quality have not been examined. No analyst coverage and no forward estimate. Not flagged cyclical (importer/distributor like Sonic Automotive), even though the RV industry has a clear inventory cycle.
