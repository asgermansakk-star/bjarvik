    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro-cap company with one property (Sunrise River Beach Resort in Aheloy, Bulgaria) and no analyst coverage, so there is no P/E, forward estimate, PEG or price target. The price is assessed as price against book equity per share. Stockanalysis' P/B of 0.42 includes the minority interests; here the majority's equity is used (kr 9.8m, kr 2.13 per share), and the history is adjusted for the reverse split of 1:100 in November 2025. There is no external NAV or valuation in the half-year report, so the property value has not been verified. The company is in a refinancing process with material uncertainty about continued operation. An indicative bank loan offer of up to EUR 6m (29/10-2026) is non-binding, and there is no offer for the share, so saerlig_situation is empty. The decision on the rent for 2026 (expected in mid-October) and the negotiations on the shareholder loan, which falls due on 30/11-2026, have not been found published. Peers have not been measured directly; the comparison is based on Agat Ejendomme, Annehem and Entra from our own analyses.
