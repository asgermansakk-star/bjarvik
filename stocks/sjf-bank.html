    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a Danish local bank with good capital and rising expectations, but a high price. The biggest drags are a share up 48% in a year and about twice as expensive relative to the bank's value as before 2025, while returns are only average. The 20 largest loans weigh far more against capital than six months ago. Expectations were raised twice, and management has bought shares.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −6 of −30
    • Efficiency and ROE
      The cost ratio is 52.1% in H1 2026 and was 58.4% in 2025 (53.4% in 2024) vs the bank's own target of 50% and Ringkjøbing Landbobank's 26%. Return on equity after tax fell from 13.5% in 2024 to 12.3% in 2025 and is 12.7% p.a. in H1 2026 – an average local bank, not a stronger one.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Profit before tax fell 5% in 2025 (kr 771.3m vs 814.8m), and EPS stood still (kr 34.0 vs 33.4), while net interest income fell 4%. In H1 2026 profit before tax rose only 5% (kr 428m vs 409m) and EPS 4% (kr 19.0 vs 18.3); core earnings rose 15%, but net interest income only 4%.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The bank ties up capital in minority stakes in competing local banks: over 20% of Lollands Bank (DKK 384m as an associate) and a larger stake in Møns Bank (15%), where the articles of association give only 1% of the votes. This gives a low return and no control.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −1

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −9 of −35
    • Large exposures
      The sum of the 20 largest exposures rose to 121.7% of common equity Tier 1 at 30/6 2026 from 78.9% at end-2025 and 81.4% a year before – an increase of 50% in half a year. The limit in the supervisory diamond is 175%, and the bank does not disclose which exposures are behind it.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Credit quality and agriculture
      Lending rose 13% in a year, while the accumulated impairment ratio fell from 3.5% (2024) to 2.6%. Agriculture makes up 10.2% of loans and guarantees, of which arable farming 5.3% and pigs 2.5%, and the bank itself writes that market conditions for pig producers and crop farmers are difficult and may require extraordinary liquidity. Guidance for 2026 assumed rising impairments.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −2
    • Interest rate sensitivity
      Deposits (DKK 26.0bn) exceed loans (DKK 15.6bn) by a surplus of DKK 10.4bn excl. pools, so earnings follow the interest rate: net interest income fell 4% in 2025, and 2026 guidance is based on an unchanged interest rate level from Danmarks Nationalbank.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −1
    • Governance and ownership structure
      No shareholder can vote for more than 7.5% of the share capital, and the board is elected by a committee of representatives of up to 51 members, so management is well protected against shareholders. The largest owners are the competitors AL Sydbank (9.96%) and Jyske Bank (9.8%) as well as AP Pension (7.9%), Nykredit (5.3%) and Fonden for SJF Bank (5.1%).
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −2
    • Liquidity and coverage
      No analysts cover the stock, institutions own only 1.94% according to stockanalysis, and approx. 24,000 shares are traded a day (approx. kr 12m). Better than the smallest local banks, but a price fall after +48% in a year can be hard.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; valuation on P/B (≈ P/TBV, only DKK 91m of goodwill) and P/E, and distribution is measured against net profit instead of FCF. P/B is calculated on the bank's own book value (DKK 298.5 per share at 30/6 2026); stockanalysis' P/B 1.56 and P/TBV 1.59 are based on equity incl. hybrid core capital and therefore understate the multiple. Latest report is the half-year report (13/8 2026); the interim report for the first three quarters will not come until 10 November 2026 (stockanalysis shows 9/11). No analysts and no forward estimate (stockanalysis forecast = 404), so P/E is calculated on guidance (profit before tax DKK 825-900m, 22% tax, approx. DKK 36m hybrid interest, approx. 16.1m shares). Insider ownership is n/a on stockanalysis; management's total holding has not been calculated. The price has risen from DKK 369.5 to approx. DKK 512 since 30/6; the cause (e.g. consolidation expectations with AL Sydbank and Jyske Bank as major shareholders) has not been examined, because web search was not available in this run, and no bids or agreements have been found. The company's own website and GlobeNewswire denied access; all announcements were read via Nasdaq. The interest rate sensitivity of the bond portfolio, the industry breakdown of the 20 largest exposures and the stage breakdown of impairments have not been examined in detail.
