    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is listed on Euronext Growth Oslo (MERK), not the main list. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates on stockanalysis, so PEG and upside are not based on data. The operational update for Q3 2026 is due 10/11, after the scoring date, so the covenant measurement as of 30/9 and any new financing are not included. P/B and P/TBV on stockanalysis (0.41/0.42) treat NOK 204m of capitalised development on HRO350 as tangible assets; without that item, equity is negative. Customer concentration has not been broken down by customer (only by region), and insider trades have only been found for October 2024. The stock is very illiquid.
