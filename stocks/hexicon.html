    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Hexicon has on 6/11-2026 called the first control meeting (kontrollstämma) for 25/11-2026, where shareholders are to vote on liquidation or continued operation. It is not a special situation under the rules (no bid, suspension or delisting), but the outcome could change everything. The control balance sheet shows a revalued equity of SEK 880.7m, but the auditor KPMG has abstained from giving an opinion on it, and no credit has been given for it. Stockanalysis shows SEK 145m in cash, but SEK 141.9m of that is loans to associates and accrued milestone payments; the actual cash was SEK 3.3m at 30/6-2026. Stockanalysis' share count (+4.9%) does not match the balance sheet (363.8m shares unchanged) and has not been used. No analyst coverage and no forecast page. Insider trades and customer concentration have not been investigated (0 points), so the score is a ceiling. The Q3 report comes 26/11-2026.
