    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The industry list has no education category; Lumi is placed under 'Health insurance & services' like AcadeMedia (welfare and education services). The company reports half-yearly, and the financial year in the guidance is the school year (July–June). The share price of kr 13.40 is from 5/11-2026, i.e. before the trading update on 6/11-2026; the market's reaction to it is not included. Forward P/E 12.4 is based on an estimate that is probably from before the update, and stockanalysis has no forecast page (number of analysts unknown, probably 1–2). The goodwill write-down in Sonans of kr 175–250m comes in the H2 2026 accounts and will make the reported 2026 result negative; it is non-cash and does not affect covenants. Insider trades and management's own shareholding have not been examined (0 points); stockanalysis shows no insider ownership, but Hanover Active Equity Fund III controls 60.1%. Political risk for private education providers in Norway has only been assessed via the NOKUT case.
