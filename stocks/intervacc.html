    • Margin
      Operating margin is −520% TTM: an operating loss of SEK 94m on revenue of 18m. Gross margin is −64% TTM (−57% in 2025), and the company has had negative or low gross margin every year since 2023, nine years after the stock market listing.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell to SEK 9.6m in H1 2026 from 11.6 (−17%). Strangvac sales in Sweden rose 54%, but in the rest of Europe (via Dechra) they fell 63%, and management calls the development 'far from satisfactory'. TTM revenue is −0.7%, and analysts expect only 21m for 2026.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has risen from 75.7m to 340.8m in the rights issue of SEK 225m at SEK 0.85 in February 2025 (78% dilution for those who did not take part), with issue costs of approx. 31m (14%). Already in 2023, 104m was raised. The price has fallen from SEK 39.64 (2021) to SEK 0.78.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Moat
      Strangvac is a patented recombinant vaccine with strong efficacy data, but the approved immunity is only two months, which management itself calls one of the biggest barriers to use. The application to the EMA for 12 months has been submitted, but not approved.

      Sales outside the Nordics go through one distributor, Dechra, where sales fell 63% in H1. The agreement was extended by only two years in October 2025, and Dechra is now running a new campaign, but Intervacc has little control over the sales effort in its most important market.

      Gross profit was −SEK 16.8m in Q4 2025, and inventory fell from 27.4 to 10.4m, which suggests a large inventory write-down after overproduction relative to demand. In 2024 Strangvac was sold out in several places. Production planning has swung between shortage and surplus.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4

      The CEO was replaced in February 2026 after almost two years (Jonas Sohlman), and the new CEO, Carl-Johan Dalsgaard, is a venture partner at HealthCap, which owns approx. 26% after the issue. The chairman was also replaced in May 2026. The major shareholder has thus effectively taken over management, and there is a risk of conflicts of interest in future issues.

      The US is called the company's most important future market, but approval at the USDA (CVB) requires both the ongoing safety study and a later efficacy study. No approval date has been given, so the value depends on a multi-year, binary process.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 report comes on 12/11-2026 and is not included; the figures are based on H1 2026. Only 2 analysts have estimates, and there are no meaningful P/E or PEG figures (loss). P/B and P/TBV on stockanalysis are misleading, because about SEK 109m of equity is capitalised expenses (deferred charges) and not cash. Peers within veterinary vaccines are not directly comparable (large companies such as Zoetis/Dechra are not listed small companies), so the peer comparison is made against Swedish loss-making medtech companies.
