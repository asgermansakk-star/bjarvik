    In plain words

    The score of 48 shows a car-antenna company with big losses and falling sales. Revenue fell 55.6% in the third quarter, and it loses money even on its products. Still, the technology is in series production at Valeo, and Valeo is ordering almost twice as many antennas in 2026 as planned. Watch the new share issue of about SEK 62.5m, open for subscription from 24 November to 8 December.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −25 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating profit is −SEK 55.4m on revenue of 66.8m TTM (approx. −83%), and gross profit is negative (−21.0m). Adjusted EBITDA was −14.2m in Q3 alone on revenue of 9.0m. There have been losses every year in 2021–2026.
      stockanalysis.com
      −12
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 55.6% in Q3 (9.0 vs 20.4m) and 39.1% in 9M 2026 because project and equipment revenue is disappearing. Product sales are growing (1.6m antennas in Q3 vs 1.2m in Q2), but do not offset the loss, and revenue per antenna produced is low, as a large share is HELLA's licence production.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The investment in the associate Sensrad (30%) has been abandoned: the stake is being handed over to Qamcom for SEK 1, and SEK 40.5m has been written down on the shares in 9M as well as SEK 18.0m on shareholder loans – approx. SEK 58m in total, corresponding to over 20% of market value.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat
      The patented waveguide technology from Chalmers is in series production at Valeo (via Frencken in China) and licensed to HELLA, which is real validation. But the company sells components to a few large Tier 1 customers with strong pricing power, and the value per antenna is still too low to give a positive gross profit.
