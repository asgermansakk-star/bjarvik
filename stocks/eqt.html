    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Asset manager: earnings consist of fixed management fees and volatile carried interest and returns on own investments. EQT reports full accounts semi-annually, so the TTM figures on stockanalysis run to 30/6-2026 (reporting currency EUR). Coller Capital was consolidated from 31/8-2026, and 80.4m new shares were issued. stockanalysis still shows 1.17bn shares (−0.63% in a year), but the real figure is 1.246bn. Market cap and multiples are therefore about 6% too low, and under the 1.3 rule the company's own figures have been used. stockanalysis' 'net cash' of SEK 42.6bn includes EUR 5.6bn in illiquid fund investments and carry. Cash vs bond debt gives net debt of about EUR 1.6bn. Insider trades in 2026 have not been checked in FI's register (0 points), and the partners' real ownership share has not been calculated either.
