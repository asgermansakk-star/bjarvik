    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list), and the accounts follow NGAAP with amortisation of goodwill, so ROE and P/B are not meaningful (negative equity). stockanalysis has no forecast page for Omda, so there is no visible analyst coverage beyond a forward P/E of 15.7. The Q3 report comes 18/11-2026 and is not included. The new bond of SEK 700m (September 2026) and the acquisition of Saab Public Safety Solutions (closed 30/10-2026) are not in the balance sheet at 30/6, so net debt after the refinancing has not been determined. Lease payments are not shown separately in the NGAAP cash flow. Peer multiples (Nordic vertical software consolidators), customer concentration on single customers and the owner list have not been examined, so they give 0.
