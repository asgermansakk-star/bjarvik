    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −16 of −30
    • Margin
      Gross margin 8.1% and EBIT margin 3.75% (TTM) vs 23–34% in 2021–2022, before the acquisitions changed the business into brokerage. Net margin is only 0.26%, because interest and bond premium take most of operating profit. The margin rose in Q2 (EBIT 3.7% vs 1.9%), but the level is thin.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen to 8.6% (TTM) from 24.3% in 2024 and 13.8% in 2025, and ROE is 8.0%. Goodwill of SEK 444m makes up 42% of the balance sheet, and tangible equity is −SEK 354m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has gone from SEK 74m (2023) to SEK 1.8bn (TTM) almost only through acquisitions (approx. 50 Swedish companies since 2022). Organic growth was 5.66% in Q2 (originally reported as 2.84%). EPS has fallen from 2.59 (2024) to 1.02 (2025) and SEK 0.83 (TTM), so the growth has not reached shareholders.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat
      Staffing and consultant placement have low pricing power. The subsidiary ITC had revenue of SEK 808m but EBITDA of only SEK 8m (about 1% margin, Apr 25–Mar 26), and subcontractor costs make up 61% of revenue in H1.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation and minorities
      Calviks typically buys 70–80% of target companies, and the Norwegian companies are gathered in Norvika, where sellers and minority owners swap in and can raise capital at subsidiary level. Already in Q2, SEK 1.6m of SEK 2.6m net income went to minorities, so a growing share of the value does not stay with Calviks' shareholders.
      mfn.se
      −2

    • Debt and financing
      Net debt was SEK 252m (3.3× EBITDA) in June, interest cover 1.2× and the equity ratio 13%. The old bond of SEK 295m had an effective interest rate of 19.5% and is redeemed at 120% (approx. SEK 59m in premium). The new bond of SEK 650m (STIBOR + 8.0%, 3 years, framework of SEK 1.2bn) more than doubles the debt. In addition there are contingent additional purchase prices of SEK 53m. Altman Z is 2.1.

    • Cash flow
      FCF after leasing was SEK 35m (TTM), while acquisitions cost SEK 96m. Operating cash flow in Q2 was SEK 14m vs EBITDA of 28.7m. Trade receivables (−93m) were offset by larger accrued liabilities (+76m), and prepaid costs rose to SEK 150m from 53m at the turn of the year.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Dilution
      The share count rose 11.8% in a year (4.97 → 5.45m, incl. non-cash issues for acquisitions) after −30% in 2024 and −9% in 2025. The founders themselves call it 'en rejäl utspädning'. Going forward, dilution is moved down into Norvika.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Serial acquirer in staffing and recruitment that has just closed five Norwegian acquisitions (Subshore, Drift Sør, Atrium People, NøkkelPersonell, Safe Bemanning) and refinanced with a bond of SEK 650m. The stockanalysis figures (TTM to June 2026) include neither the acquisitions nor the new debt, and the Q3 report comes on 12/11-2026. The pro forma figures (revenue SEK 3.5bn, EBITDA SEK 231m) are the company's own unaudited figures and are calculated at 100% of companies where Calviks typically owns only 70–80%. Forward P/E 7.2 is based on very thin analyst coverage (no forecast page on stockanalysis). Insider trading in 2026 has not been found. The industry does not fit the industry list; 'Transport & logistics' was chosen, as for Randstad. Not marked cyclical: staffing follows the economic cycle, but not a price or commodity cycle, although Subshore gives exposure to offshore.
