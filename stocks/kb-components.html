    In plain words

    A score of 54 points to a supplier of plastic parts to the car industry with falling earnings. Operating margin has fallen to 4.7%, revenue fell 5.3%, and the dividend equals 173% of profit, funded by loans. On the plus side, production for a new customer has started, and a new credit deal moved debt to long maturity. The main owner holds 70.1%. The next report comes on 18 November.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 report does not come until 18/11-2026, so the figures are TTM to 30/6-2026. The stock has only been listed since December 2024, so own multiple history covers only 2024–2025. Only two analysts (Handelsbanken and Carnegie) cover the stock, and stockanalysis has no forecast page; forward P/E 13.6 requires almost a doubling of adjusted EPS (SEK 1.64 TTM). stockanalysis' insider ownership (0.01%) is wrong: chair Stefan Andersson controls 70.1% via BrA Invest. Stockanalysis shows very low trading volume, which has not been verified. Customer split per customer is not stated in the reports. The planned move to Nasdaq Stockholm in 2026 had not been completed as of Q2.
