      Operating cash flow before changes in working capital was −SEK 9.2m over the latest 12 months. At 30/6 cash was SEK 18.4m vs debt of SEK 25.5m (of which SEK 18.8m short-term bank debt), and that triggered the issue. If break-even does not hold, new capital is needed.
