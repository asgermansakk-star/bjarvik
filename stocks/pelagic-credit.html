    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −16 of −30
    • Return (ROE)
      The net result in Q2 was USD 0.66m on equity of USD 74.8m, equal to approx. 3.5% annual ROE. This is because USD 49.5m (44% of assets) sat as uninvested cash after the listing. The projects' IRR of approx. 14% does not yet reach shareholders at all.
      storage.mfn.se
      −6
    • Cost structure
      Administration costs were USD 0.66m in Q2, or 39% of interest income. The sponsor receives a fixed fee of USD 500 per ship per day, also while ships are under construction. The fee thus grows with the number of ships, not with returns, and the agreement can only be terminated to a limited extent in the first 36 months.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat and scale
      The company is a small financing platform for sale-and-leaseback of ships with four projects and a market value of approx. USD 58m. The market is competitive, with Chinese leasing houses, banks and credit funds, and the advantage lies mainly in the sponsor's network.
      pelagic-credit.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Growth comes solely from new capital: equity rose from USD 17.6m to 74.8m with the issue. No new transactions were closed in Q2, and management writes that a further capital increase may be needed to scale.
      mfn.se
      −3

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 8 findings    −22 of −35
    • Sponsor control and governance
      The sponsor's funds (Marevia Holding I–III) own approx. 64% of the shares. Chairman Atef Abou Merhi and CEO Niels Hartmann are both founders and managing directors of the sponsor Pelagic Partners. The board has only one independent member, and on Euronext Growth no governance code applies.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Related parties
      The largest investment of USD 47.4m (Project Holly) is leased to a subsidiary of Hartmann Group, which is run by board member Niels Hartmann. The pipeline contains several deals with related parties (Harmony 2, Pelagic Wind), so the company partly finances its own owners' ships.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Customer concentration
      The backlog of USD 107.1m is spread across only four projects: dry cargo (Hartmann) 39%, MPP 34%, offshore 23% and chemical tankers 4%. If one counterparty defaults, it hits a large part of earnings.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Dilution and funding need
      The general meeting has authorised the board to issue share capital of up to USD 250m (currently 37m) over five years without pre-emption rights. Management mentions a possible new issue, and at a price of about 0.8 times book value an issue would dilute.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Dividend coverage
      The first quarterly dividend of USD 1.48m (USD 0.04 per share) was more than double the Q2 profit of USD 0.66m and is covered only by the H1 profit. The dividend policy (the lower of profit and free cash) makes it unlikely that the level can be kept before the cash is invested.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Liquidity in the stock
      The stock turns over on average 3,284 shares a day (about NOK 50,000), and free float is only 13.3m of 37m shares. The price often stands still for several days.

      According to the CEO, the company is investing at a historically high level for ship values. If ship prices fall, the risk on purchase options and residual values increases, especially with dry bulk and offshore in the portfolio.
