    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −9 of −30
    • ROIC and returns
      ROIC is 2.3% vs WACC of 5.2% and has been 1.8–3.1% since 2021 with no improvement. ROE of 6.7% over 12 months is lifted by revaluations. The direct return on the portfolio is only 3.1–3.3%, while the average interest rate has risen to 2.88% (2.69).
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Rental income rose 4.4% and net operating income 5% in 9M, primarily because of acquisitions and rent increases. Förvaltningsresultat rose 7%, and per share it rose 11%, but about 4–5 percentage points of the per-share growth come from buybacks. Net financial items rose to −SEK 137.5m (−126.2), and intjäningsförmåga assumes −SEK 197m on an annual basis.
      storage.mfn.se
      −1
    • Future segment
      74% of rent comes from housing in the Stockholm region and Mälardalen with regulated bruksvärdehyror. This gives stable demand but limits pricing power. Samhällsfastigheter (care and nursing homes) are to make up at least 30% of net operating income and today account for 28%.
      mfn.se
      −1
    • Capital allocation
      Management aims for a 5% return on new projects. At the same time the company buys own shares at under 0.5 × NRV (SEK 329m since 2025) and has a project programme of SEK 2,835m, of which only SEK 500m has been incurred. In addition, Heba owns 50% of JV projects worth SEK 3,080m. The total programme is far larger than the actual market value of about SEK 3.6bn and is financed with loans, while the loan-to-value ratio rises towards the new ceiling of 50%.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −16 of −35
    • Debt and interest
      The loan-to-value ratio has risen to 47.8% (46.2 at year-end), and interest cover is 2.3× vs the company's own floor of 2.2×. Net debt/EBITDA is 17.3×. Of interest-bearing debt, kr 2,035m is short-term, of which kr 1,275m is commercial paper. Debt maturity is 3.0 years and the fixed-interest period 2.3 years. According to the CEO, long-term rates have risen sharply, and the direct yield of 3.1–3.3% is close to the cost of debt.
      storage.mfn.se
      −7
    • Cash flow and financing need
      Operating cash flow was kr 208m over 12 months, while investments were kr 808m. The dividend (kr 85m) and the buybacks are therefore effectively debt-financed, and net debt rose kr 629m in 12 months. About kr 2.3bn remains to be invested in own projects, and Heba's share of the JV projects comes on top.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Valuation of properties
      Only about a third of the portfolio is valued externally. The yield requirement on social infrastructure properties is 3.3%, and the direct yield on the portfolio is 3.1%. If the yield requirement rises by 0.5 percentage points, the property value falls by over kr 1.9bn, which corresponds to about 29% of equity.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Project and sales risk
      The JV projects with Åke Sundvall comprise approx. 440 tenant-owner flats (340 in Stora Sköndal and 100 in Skarpnäck), and here Heba carries the sales risk on owner-occupied flats. It is a new type of risk for a pure rental company.
      storage.mfn.se
      −1

      Industricentralen has gone from 18.66% of the capital in September 2024 to 9.99% as of 30/9-2026. This has caused sustained selling pressure in a stock with a free float of only about 83m shares.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: the main multiple is price vs EPRA NRV (SEK 51.64 per share at 30/9-2026). At a price of 23.55, P/NRV is approx. 0.46 and P/B 0.60. EPRA NTA/NDV has not been investigated, and they are lower than NRV, because NRV adds back deferred tax. Running earnings are measured as förvaltningsresultat per share: SEK 1.50 rolling 12 months and SEK 1.60 on the current earnings capacity (SEK 247m divided over approx. 154.4m outstanding shares). stockanalysis's trailing P/E of 8.6 is lifted by value adjustments of properties (+SEK 141.5m) and derivatives (+SEK 57.0m) in 9M and is not used. stockanalysis's market cap of SEK 3.89bn includes 10.7m treasury shares (6.48%), so the real market cap is approx. SEK 3.64bn. The 4.78% fall in the share count matches buybacks of SEK 309.6m in 2025 and SEK 19.7m in 2026 in the cash flow statement. These buybacks are not covered by FCF and therefore give no plus. There is no forward estimate and no coverage with price targets on stockanalysis. Insider trades in Finansinspektionen's insider register have not been investigated. It has also not been investigated how large a share of the votes the A shares give, and which of the family owners sit on the board. The rent negotiations for 2027 have not been investigated.
