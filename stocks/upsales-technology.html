    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Aira was spun off (Lex ASEA) in Q2 2026, so stockanalysis' history and TTM figures contain Aira costs. Under the rule of 12 months after a spin-off, T2 therefore builds on the company's own pro forma figures (EBITDA run-rate of about SEK 60m) and on peers, not on its own multiple history. The Q3 margin of 46% is seasonally high (holidays). No analysts on stockanalysis (the forecast page gives 404), so PEG and upside are missing. Insider ownership of 10.5% at stockanalysis is outdated: the founder sold the rest on 26/10. Number of customers and churn have not been examined, so no plus has been given for a diversified customer base. The buyback mandate requires a law change on 5/12-2026 and has not been used.
