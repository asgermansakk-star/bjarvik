    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −14 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Operating margin has fallen from 34.3% in 2021 and 20.1% in 2022 to 7.6% in 2025 and is only partly back at 13.1% TTM. ROIC has gone from 71.7% and 26.1% (2021–2022) to 7.6% in 2025 and 12.7% TTM, close to WACC of 8.6%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue was EUR 162.4m in 2021 and 162.0m in 2025, i.e. no growth in four years. Q3 2026 grew 23%, but that covers EUR 4.2m in resold data costs from the World Cup, 1.7m from a stronger Colombian peso and operator-friendly sports results. Turnover grew only 3%, the gross margin fell to 80.9% from 85.8%, and consensus expects only 3.9% growth in 2027.

      Development of EUR 25–28m a year has been capitalised (about 16% of revenue), and acquisitions of EUR 62m were made in 2021–2023 (Abios, Shape Games, Tzeract), including a write-down of EUR 12.4m in 2023. The investments have not lifted revenue since 2021.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −16 of −35
    • Customer behaviour and migrations
      FDJ UNITED (formerly Kindred, historically the largest customer) is moving to its own sportsbook before the end of 2026, and LeoVegas follows in 2027. Both are moving to the cheaper Odds Feed+. Turnover in Europe fell 3.1% in Q3 because of the migrations. Guidance of approx. EUR 26m implies an adjusted EBITA of approx. 4.8m in Q4 vs approx. 7.3m in Q4 2025. The pattern repeats itself: DraftKings and Penn (online) have also left the platform.
      storage.mfn.se
      −7
    • Regulation and tax
      Kambi earns a share of operators' gaming profit and is therefore hit by their taxes. Brazil banned online betting on 25/9 (a low single-digit percentage of revenue, but the growth potential may disappear). Colombia has introduced an extra GGR tax of 16%. The UK is raising the tax on remote betting to 25% from April 2027. Illinois has introduced a per-wager tax, and the Netherlands has raised its tax to 37.8%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Sports results
      Revenue follows the operators' trading margin, which was 12.5% in the first nine months of the year vs 10.7% a year earlier and a budgeted level of approx. 11%. 2026 has thus been helped by favourable results and the World Cup, which makes the comparison base for 2027 difficult.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Unprovisioned liability
      A performance-based final payment is owed to the consultants behind the AI trading system, which the company estimates at a high single-digit million amount in EUR. It has not been provisioned and is expected to be recognised in 2027. That equals approx. 20% of the cash.

      An extraordinary general meeting in June 2026 gave the board authority to issue up to 10% new shares without pre-emption rights as payment for acquisitions. That opens the door to dilution and new acquisitions, after the earlier acquisitions have not created growth.

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.

Insider ownership (0.45% on stockanalysis) may be underestimated if chair Anders Ström is behind the major shareholder Veralda Investment; this has not been investigated and gives 0. Peers have only been checked superficially (Sportradar forward P/E about 28, Betsson about 8), and Kambi sits in between, so there is no peer finding.
