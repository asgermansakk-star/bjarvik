    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small IT consultancy (market cap about kr 640m) on First North with thin analyst coverage; stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 10.9 is based on few estimates. TTM FCF on stockanalysis (kr 27m; 8m after leasing) is distorted because the cash portion of the Clara acquisition is booked as capex; operating cash flow minus leasing has been used instead. Underlying EBITA excluding Clara is an estimate based on Clara's EBITA margin of 22% in 2025 and the acquired revenue (6.1% of kr 621.5m). The name of the largest customer (21%) is not disclosed. Clara is consolidated from 1/4-2026, so the TTM figures mix pre- and post-acquisition.
