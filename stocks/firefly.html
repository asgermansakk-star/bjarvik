    In plain words

    The score of 77 shows a small, debt-free supplier of spark detection for industry at a fair price. Growth has been low in recent years and profit swings a lot between quarters. Customers are delaying investments, and a strong krona eats into earnings. The shares trade rarely, no analysts follow them, and the chairman owns 38%. On the plus side, the customer base is broad.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.
