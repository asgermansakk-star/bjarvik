    In plain words

    A score of 61 covers a micro company with four employees that sells a sensor for dialysis. Sales are flat since 2023, operations still make a small loss, and cash is small. Sales go to a few large US customers, and an important approval process with US regulators has been delayed. On the plus side, there is no debt, and sales of consumable parts rose 26% in the second quarter.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
