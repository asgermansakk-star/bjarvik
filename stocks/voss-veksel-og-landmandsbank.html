    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (stockanalysis' negative FCF and net debt are loan growth and deposits), so the valuation is based on P/B and P/E, and the dividend is measured against net profit. stockanalysis' P/B 1.10 is based on total equity incl. NOK 50m of additional tier 1 bonds; the bank's own book equity per share without hybrid capital (NOK 367.79) is used, which gives P/B 1.17. The 9M figures for net interest income are not comparable with 2025, because NOK 12.3m of interest from liquidity funds has been moved from price gains to interest income; the comparison uses the bank's figures without the reclassification. stockanalysis shows a forward P/E of 10.49, but has no forecast page, so analyst coverage is thin or absent. Insider trades have only been checked in Newsweb from October 2025 (no reportable trades found). The peer comparison is based on other Norwegian savings banks scored the same day. The stock is very illiquid.
