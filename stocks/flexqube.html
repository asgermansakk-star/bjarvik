    In plain words

    A score of 54 points to a small company whose good figures come almost entirely from one record quarter. Most sales went to one US customer, new orders were only about a third of sales, and there were operating losses every year in 2021-2025. A co-founder sold shares before the quarter. On the plus side, it won an order against large competitors, and the founders and board own over 40%.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
