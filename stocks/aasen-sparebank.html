    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The ownership share fraction is only 25.87%, so approx. 74% of equity is self-owned. The security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.27, book value of NOK 480 per share and net income of NOK 14.1m mix the whole bank's equity with the certificate holders' share and have not been used. T2 is based on the bank's own figures per certificate from the Q3 presentation (book value NOK 126.5 and earnings NOK 7.8 per certificate at 30/9-2026) and the price of NOK 141 (5/11-2026). Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 12.18 has an uncertain basis and has not been used. Analyst coverage is effectively non-existent. The conversion costs for the new IT platform (NOK 10m at Q3) are treated as a one-off item in the P/E comparison. The total capital requirement (approx. 15.5% common equity tier 1) is my own estimate based on Pillar 2 of 2.6% and the standard Norwegian buffers, as the bank does not disclose the figure in the report. Customer concentration in single exposures has not been examined (0 points). The new issuance authorisation of 20% has not yet been used.
