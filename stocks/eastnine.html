    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −17 of −35
    • Debt and interest
      The purchase of The Bridge (EUR 241m) is debt-financed. The loan-to-value ratio rose to 58% (47), close to the company's own ceiling of 60% and above the target of approx. 50%. Interest cover in the earning capacity is 1.8 × vs the company's own floor of 2.0 × (2.1 × after full occupancy), the debt ratio rose to 11.6 × (8.2), and the equity ratio fell to 36% (45). The average interest rate rose to 4.6% (4.3), 21% of the debt is unhedged, and the interest-rate fixing period is only 2.6 years and the capital tie-up 3.5 years. A VAT loan of EUR 57m must be repaid no later than Q1 2027.
      mb.cision.com
      −9
    • Tenant concentration
      The ten largest tenants account for 55% of rent and the three largest (Erste Bank Polska, Warta and Allegro) for 29%. In The Bridge, which is now approx. a quarter of property value, Erste Bank and Visa together rent 76% of the area.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Letting and execution
      Net letting was negative in 9M (−EUR 435,000 in annual rent), and approx. 6,000 m² will be vacated on 30/6-2027. The average remaining lease term is only 4.1 years. Only 57% of The Bridge was occupied at the takeover, and full occupancy depends on Visa's fit-out until Q4 2027. At the same time the company takes over the management in Warsaw itself in 2027.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Macro and geography
      The entire portfolio is in Poland and the Baltics, with Vilnius and Riga close to Russia and Belarus. Income and debt are in EUR and PLN, while the stock is traded in SEK, so the exchange rate affects NAV in kronor. Interest rates are variable and tied to Euribor and Wibor.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Governance and liquidity
      Peter Elam Håkansson controls 17.6% and CEO Kestutis Sasnauskas 9.7%, after East Capital Holding distributed its shares in December 2025. That gives good ownership alignment, but also a concentrated shareholder base and a free float of approx. 63m shares. Management was also granted warrants in May 2026.
