    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 42% in 2025 to SEK 35.0m and 10% in H1 2026 to SEK 13.5m. Sales are lumpy and depend on a few robot deliveries, and the Saudi order of SEK 16.5m in August was not new: it is a reallocation of Aerotech's existing order from 2025 and does not increase the order book.

      Niche product (aircraft washing robots) with 21 employees. According to the CEO, the need for automated aircraft washing in Europe is limited (longer washing intervals and many narrow-body aircraft), and the new leg in train and bus washing is still under development with SNCF, where the final configuration does not yet meet the requirements.

At the same time rising development spending is being capitalised (intangible assets in progress SEK 5.7m vs 1.1m) while the company borrows for operations.

      IAG dropped the supply agreement for washing robots for British Airways at Heathrow in August 2026, after Aerowash had received the award in March. IAG cited rising jet fuel prices, which have led several airlines to postpone investments. The share price fell 28% on the day.
