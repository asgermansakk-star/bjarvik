    • ROIC and returns
      ROIC is 3.8% vs WACC of 5.6%. The net operating income yield is 5.6% (6.2% excluding projects and land, down from 6.4%) vs an average interest rate of 4.0%, so the spread is thin. ROE of 10% is below the target of 12% and is lifted by revaluations and derivative gains.

      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Profit from property management rose 26% in 9M (SEK 346m), but net operating income in the comparable portfolio rose only about 2.5% (SEK 15m). The rest comes from acquisitions of SEK 1,711m in 9M, completed projects, currency effects and lower loan margins after the refinancing in 2025.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      Growth is primarily debt-financed: interest-bearing debt has risen from SEK 6.9bn to 10.6bn since 2023, and net debt per share from SEK 238 to 318. In addition a directed issue was made in 2025 (SEK 292m) and hybrid bonds of SEK 700m issued at Stibor + 3.75–5.50%, while no dividend is paid.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −15 of −35
    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio is 54% (53% a year ago), net debt/EBITDA 12.2×, and interest cover only 2.1× (1.9× with one-offs) vs a target of at least 2.0×. Average loan maturity has fallen to 2.6 years (2.8 years), and 74% of loans fall due in 2028–2029. Loans of kr 1,233m falling due in 2026–2027 face liquidity of kr 670m, and 41% of debt has a fixed-interest period through 2027. The hybrid bond of kr 300m at Stibor + 5.50% has its first call date in May 2027.
      mb.cision.com
      −9
    • Letting and projects
      Two speculatively built logistics buildings completed in July and September 2026 (Viby 19:66 and Fotocellen 5, 9,100 sqm, kr 188m) are 0% let. All ongoing projects are also 0% let, with a remaining investment of kr 805m, of which kr 407m is for a data centre in Almnäs with no tenant and with power capacity not yet secured. The occupancy rate by area is 91%, and the average remaining lease term has fallen to 3.9 years (4.2).
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Governance and ownership concentration
      Stendörren Real Estate AB owns 37.7% and Altira AB 10.3% of the shares. There are A and B shares with different voting power. Board member Carl Mörk (through a related party) pledged 500,000 A shares and 2.69m B shares in November 2025, equal to approx. 10% of the capital. This creates a risk of forced sale in a price fall.
      marknadssok.fi.se
      −2
    • Macro and value sensitivity
      Management itself points out that long rates have risen since before the summer. With loan-to-value of 54%, equity is hit hard by higher yield requirements: +0.25 percentage points costs about SEK 777m in property value (about SEK 24 per share, about 11% of net asset value). Beta is 1.25, and about 55% of rent comes from the Stockholm region.
