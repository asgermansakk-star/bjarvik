    • Cash flow and receivables
      Receivables from revenue-sharing agreements rose from EUR 2.0m to 11.3m in 2025, and no provision has been made for losses because management considers the amounts recoverable. Total receivables have grown to EUR 18.5m (57% of TTM revenue) at 30/6-2026. Operating cash flow was EUR 1.49m in H1 vs a profit of EUR 4.91m, and FCF was EUR 2.05m TTM vs net profit of EUR 7.9m. The board proposed no dividend for 2025 despite record profit.

      According to the annual report, the group depends heavily on one subsidiary, which accounts for a substantial part of revenue, and it comes from an undisclosed number of B2B partners.
