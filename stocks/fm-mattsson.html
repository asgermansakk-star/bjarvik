    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The acquisition of Bristan (28/7-2026, SEK 684m incl. earn-out) is under 12 months old, so the history on stockanalysis is not comparable, and the company's own pro forma figures are used where they exist (EPS approx. +SEK 1.03 or +30% for the 12 months to June 2026). The acquisition analysis is preliminary. The stockanalysis net debt (156m) and EV (5.21bn) are too low: the company's own interest-bearing net debt is SEK 795.7m incl. leasing, pensions and earn-out, so EV is approx. 5.85bn. The forward P/E history on stockanalysis (13.79 in all years) is a fill-in and has not been used. FCF after leasing is an estimate (lease repayments set at approx. 37m as in 2025). stockanalysis has no forecast page and no price targets, so analyst coverage is thin or absent. The company gives no forecast for 2026. No insider trades in 2026 in the FI register; the Mattsson family's total ownership has only been partly determined.
