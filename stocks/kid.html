    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −8 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Operating margin is 12.3% (TTM) vs 17.5% in 2021 and 14.7% in 2024, and ROIC is 10.9% vs 18.7% and 15.5%. Even analysts' EPS estimate for 2026 (kr 8.31) is below 2024 (9.81) and 2021 (9.46), so the company is bigger but not stronger than five years ago. Hemtex has a lower EBITDA margin (19.0% in 9M) than Kid Interior (25.6%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Like-for-like growth of 14.6% in Q3 comes against a weak comparison (−1.7% in Q3 2025, when inventory problems hit product availability) and is partly based on more campaigns and more discounted sales in Kid Interior. Online sales fell 4.1% in constant currency, and the online share fell from 14.4% to 11.9%.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −2
    • Moat
      Gross margin has been stable around 61–62% since 2021 thanks to own design and own purchasing, but home textiles and interiors is a competitive market (Jysk, IKEA, H&M Home, Lindex, Temu), and Hemtex had like-for-like of −1.3% in 2025.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −10 of −35
    • Debt and liquidity
      Net interest-bearing debt is DKK 2,243m including leasing (approx. 2.1x LTM EBITDA) and 964m excluding IFRS 16 (1.60x). Cash is zero, operations run on an overdraft (214m drawn), the quick ratio is 0.08 and interest cover 2.9x. The dividend of 305m in 2025 was partly financed with loans, as FCF after leasing and interest was negative.
      api3.oslo.oslobors.no
      −3
    • Execution: warehouse and systems
      The move to the shared warehouse in Sweden caused stock shortages and lost sales in 2025, and the empty warehouse in Lier is still without a permanent solution: temporary sublease agreements cover only approx. 65% of capacity, after a deal fell through in 2025 and triggered a write-down of 25m. At the same time the entire systems landscape is being modernised.
      api3.oslo.oslobors.no
      −3
    • Purchasing, currency and freight
      The improvement in gross margin in Q3 was, according to the company, due to favourable currency effects; goods are bought in foreign currency, and freight rates rose in H1 2026. A weaker NOK or higher freight hits the margin directly, and revenue is concentrated in Norway and Sweden.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −2
    • Seasonality
      Earnings are concentrated in the Christmas quarter: Q4 2025 accounted for kr 440m of the year's kr 949m in EBITDA, while Q1–Q3 2025 gave net income of only kr 7m. Weak Christmas sales can therefore move the full-year result markedly.
      api3.oslo.oslobors.no
      −1
    • Insider ownership
      Insiders own only about 0.06%: the CEO has 14,327 shares, the CFO 3,100 and the chairman 7,000. No insider trades have been registered since November 2025.
