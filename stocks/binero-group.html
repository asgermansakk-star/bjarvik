    A score of 51 out of 100 is mid-range: a small IT services company with weak earnings, where only 2.7% of revenue becomes adjusted operating profit. Debt is the main thing to watch.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Binero reports only semi-annually; the Q1 and Q3 figures are unaudited trading updates (management accounts), and stockanalysis' TTM is from 30/6-2026. The gross margin on stockanalysis (17%) is based on a different definition from the company's (42%) and has not been used. Organic growth is not disclosed; the estimate (about −10 to −13% in Q3) is calculated as Q3 revenue of SEK 130.3m vs 128.4m minus Hibox' about SEK 19m per quarter (EUR 6.8m annually) and includes the deliberate wind-down of the Redbridge consulting business. The price of the data centre property in Vallentuna has not been published. Customer concentration has not been examined (0 points). No analyst price targets on stockanalysis (the forecast page gives 404), and forward P/E 15.9 is based on a thin estimate. The stock is very illiquid (about 2,600 shares on a daily average), and Tiliaflore AB (Malcolm Lindblom) controls 50.8%.
