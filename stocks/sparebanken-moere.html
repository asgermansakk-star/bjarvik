    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share; ECC holders have 49.1% of equity, the rest is self-owned (grunnfond). It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and stockanalysis's negative FCF reflects loan growth. The valuation is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (book value NOK 85.2, P/B approx. 1.32); stockanalysis's P/B of 0.59 and book value of NOK 174 per share count the self-owned capital and have not been used. P/TBV history per certificate is not publicly available, so P/B is used (no material goodwill). Only 3 analysts cover the security. The Savings Bank Committee's proposal on regulating equity certificates has not been examined specifically for Møre (0 points). A primary insider purchase in September (the chief risk officer, 1,473 certificates at NOK 110, approx. NOK 0.16m) is too small for a plus. The extraordinary general meeting on 26/11 deals only with routine matters (buyback authorisation, SNP authorisation, remuneration).
