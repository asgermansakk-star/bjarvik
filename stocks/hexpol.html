    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 7 analysts cover the stock, and the price targets (median SEK 105) and the EPS estimate for 2026 (SEK 6.10) are from 22/9, i.e. before the Q3 report; 9M adjusted EPS is SEK 4.53, so the 2026 estimate looks high. Trailing P/E (17.9) is on reported EPS including SEK 116m of one-off costs for the management change; adjusted P/E is about 17.1. The automotive industry is the largest end-customer group, but the company does not disclose an exact share; the stock is not flagged as cyclical because customers are spread across automotive, cable, construction and industry, but earnings have followed the industrial cycle (EPS fell 2024–2025). Vipa (EUR 143.5m, consolidated from 1/10) and Symplast (EUR 54m, closing by Q1 2027) are not in the TTM figures. Insider trading is checked in the FI register (latest trade Aug. 2025). Melker Schörling AB has 25% of the capital and 46% of the votes.
