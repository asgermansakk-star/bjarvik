    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a consumer loan bank making progress but with many risks, and the price is up 36% since June. Over 15% of loans are in default, and only 43% of those are covered by provisions. The MedMera Bank acquisition added 36% more shares and dilutes owners. On the plus side, costs are low and loan losses are falling. Watch the 12 November report, which first includes MedMera.

    • Credit quality
      Non-performing loans were kr 2,752m or 15.3% of gross lending at 30/6, with a provision coverage of only 43% (kr 1,195m). The Norwegian and Finnish NPL portfolios (about kr 1.5bn) are simply being sold at book value, and management expects an NPL ratio below 5% only once the sales have been completed.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Integration and dilution (MedMera)
      The bank's largest acquisition (SEK 1.96bn, approx. 1.06x P/B) increased the share count by 36% from 231.6 to 316.1m via a rights issue at SEK 11.50 and a contribution-in-kind issue to Kooperativa Förbundet at SEK 11.96, financed in addition with AT1 and T2. The integration includes the divestment of MedMera's IT and organisation, which requires regulatory approval, and the synergies of approx. SEK 150m will not be fully realised until the end of 2028. The first quarter with consolidation is reported 12/11.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (negative operating cash flow is due to loan growth); valuation on P/TBV, P/B and P/E. Morrow Bank AB is the former Norwegian Morrow Bank ASA (Oslo: MOBA), which moved to Sweden and was listed in Stockholm 9/1-2026; stockanalysis therefore has no multiple history, and figures before 2026 are converted from NOK. The acquisition of MedMera Bank (1/7-2026) is not yet in the financial figures (latest report is Q2; Q3 with first consolidation comes 12/11-2026), so P/TBV approx. 1.9 is an own estimate: equity SEK 2,932m minus AT1 of 564m plus the contribution-in-kind issue to KF of 392m and minus intangible assets incl. estimated excess value in MedMera, spread over 316.1m shares. stockanalysis' P/B 1.73 and P/TBV 1.77 include AT1 in equity. Own P/B history from the Oslo period has not been retrieved in figures. Only 2 analysts cover the stock (price targets SEK 20.00–20.50). Insider ownership of 24% at stockanalysis is primarily the major shareholder Kistefos.
