    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The paper is an equity certificate (ECC) listed on Euronext Growth Oslo, not an ordinary share. The equity certificate ratio is only 22.7% (2025), the rest is self-owned. Economically it corresponds to a share and has been scored as such. stockanalysis' P/B 0.18 and book value cover all equity incl. self-owned capital and perpetual bonds and have not been used. T2 is based on ownership capital per certificate (kr 122.7m / 1,128,571 = 108.7 kr at 31/12-2025, about 111.8 kr with the certificate holders' share of the H1 2026 result) and profit per certificate (8.00 kr in 2025). Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net profit. The latest accounts are H1 2026; the Q3 report comes 12/11-2026 and is not included. The letter of intent on a merger with Stadsbygd Sparebank (8/9-2026) is not binding; Nidaros is the acquiring bank, the exchange ratio and degree of conversion (30–40% of the primary capital to a new foundation, ownership ratio 40–50%) have not been set, and the merger plan is expected to be considered in mid-November 2026. Hence no special situation, and the economic effect of the merger for the certificate holders is not scored. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 11.03 has an uncertain basis and has not been used; analyst coverage is effectively non-existent. The bank itself writes that the biggest strategic risk is the Norwegian authorities' work on phasing out the equity certificate; this applies to all Norwegian savings banks with certificates and is not scored. No reportable insider trades have been found on Newsweb since 2023; stockanalysis' insider share of 14.68% has not been verified. The sector breakdown of the corporate book beyond property has not been examined in detail.
