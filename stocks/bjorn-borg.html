    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −10 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Net revenue rose only 0.2% in 9M 2026 (1.1% at constant currency) and 0.5% over 12 months, far from the target of at least 10% a year. Footwear fell 14%, bags 23%, own stores 20% and the Netherlands, the second-largest market, 9%. Analysts expect only +0.75% in EPS in 2027.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Margin and earnings quality
      Operating margin is 12.8% (TTM) in the company's own figures, but kr 16.9m of the operating result of 111.8m in 9M (21.2m the year before) is other operating income, primarily unrealised currency gains on receivables and trade payables. Without them, operating margin is 10.5% according to stockanalysis, only just above the target of 10%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Moat and future segment
      The core is underwear (47% of sales) in a market with many competitors. The push into footwear and the distribution of shoes in the Netherlands is not working, the US e-commerce was closed in 2025 because of tariffs, and the annual report itself says the brand to some extent depends on the person Björn Borg. Sportswear, the designated growth engine, grew only 3% in 9M.

      CEO Henrik Bunge, who has led the company for twelve years and driven the shift from underwear to sports brand, steps down on 31 March 2027. The board has just started recruitment, so the strategy must be carried on by a new chief in the middle of a period without growth.
