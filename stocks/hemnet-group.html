    In plain words

    The score of 55 describes a strong position under pressure. Revenue fell almost 22%, and more homes are now sold without going through the site. Strategy has changed quickly, the board has seen turmoil, and debt has risen, with part due in April 2027. On the plus side, the shares are cheaper than before, and cash flow is still high. Analysts' price targets are about 14% above the share price.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −20 of −30
    • Moat
      The network effect has broken up: published ads fell 18.9% in Jan–Sep 2026, and according to Fastighetsbyrån approx. 35% of homes are now sold entirely outside Hemnet vs under 10% in 2023, driven by the pre-market, Booli and dissatisfaction with prices. Hemnet is now responding with free ads for agents and automatic indexing of ads from agents' own sites – a clear sign that the platform no longer has pricing power across the whole market.
      realtid.se
      −8
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 21.9% to kr 920m in Jan–Sep and 17.8% in Q3; paid ads −34% in the period. The only growth driver is ARPL (+11%) via price increases and premium packages – exactly the pricing policy that has driven sellers away. Consensus expects −18% in revenue and −28% in EPS for 2026.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Margin
      EBITDA margin 44.1% (LTM) vs 52% in 2024 and a long-term target of over 55%; the operating margin has fallen from 46.7% to 39.6% in Q3. Still high, but the trend is going the wrong way, and free ads and a new compensation model for the agents are squeezing it further.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Buybacks of kr 592m and dividends of kr 87m over the last 12 months exceeded FCF of kr 415m and were partly loan-financed (net borrowing +kr 257m), while the price fell from above 300 to approx. kr 75. The buyback was paused on 14 September.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −15 of −35
    • Dependence on brokers
      The estate agents are gatekeepers for the listings and are increasingly channelling homes to the pre-market; Hemnet must now introduce a new compensation model for estate agency firms from 1 November and let partners such as Svensk Fastighetsförmedling use 'Under the radar'. Bargaining power has shifted to the agents.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Execution
      Strategic U-turn in a short time: free basic ad (20 October), indexing of others' ads, new packages (Bas/Premium/Max where Bas gets the Plus features at no extra price), new compensation model, restructuring with cutbacks, new commercial director and new board. The risk of cannibalisation of paid ads is considerable and unquantified.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Governance
      The activists Vor Capital and Sprints (14.1%), together with Mäklarsamfundet (10.9%), forced through an extraordinary general meeting on 2 September, where four members, including the chair, left the board. Mäklarsamfundet represents Hemnet's own customers, which creates a conflict of interest; insiders own only 0.39%.
      placera.se
      −3
    • Debt and refinancing
      Net debt has risen from kr 516m to 718m (1.3x EBITDA vs 0.7x); 870 of the credit facility of kr 900m is drawn, and kr 270m falls due in April 2027. Current ratio 0.61, and the equity ratio has fallen from 51% to 39%.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Insider trades, customer concentration in individual broker chains and the actual ad volume of Boolis/Boneos have not been investigated and give 0. The revenue decline is partly a timing shift, because 'Sell first, pay later' is only recognised as revenue on sale (about 7,000 published, not yet paid ads in Q3). Restructuring costs of kr 36–37m come in Q4 2026 and are not included in the TTM figures. Press coverage of a possible takeover (e.g. REA Group) is rumour without a bid and does not affect the score. Consensus expects EPS +30% in 2027, which assumes the free ads do not cannibalise the paid ones.
