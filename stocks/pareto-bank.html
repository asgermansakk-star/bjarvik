    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful. Stockanalysis calculates with 145.54m shares and a book value of NOK 35.98 per share, but the bank has 76,782,423 shares (Q3 presentation), and the actual book value is NOK 61.03 per share without hybrid capital (AT1 NOK 550m). P/B is therefore calculated from the bank's own figures (approx. 0.81, P/TBV approx. 0.82). Stockanalysis' historical P/B includes AT1 and is therefore slightly too low. The +18% in share count is a data error and has not given points. Only 2 analysts cover the stock, and one is the sister company Pareto Securities. Forward P/E for 2026 (29.6) is not usable as a normalised multiple, because earnings are depressed by losses. The largest single exposures and the individual housing projects in stage 3 have not been examined in detail. The dividend expectation for 2026 (NOK 1.50 according to analysts) has not been scored on its own, because it follows from the losses.
