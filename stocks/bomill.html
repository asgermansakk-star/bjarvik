    In plain words

    The model sees a very small machinery company that has lost money every year since 2021 and has a serious funding problem. Cash was SEK 4.4m, all orders are delivered, and only one small order has come in since March. Shares in issue are up nine times since 2021. Positives: repeat buyers such as AB InBev and 31.6% insider ownership. Watch for new orders and fundraising.
