    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list), and the company only reports half-yearly; the latest figures are H1 2026 (30/6). No analysts cover the stock, and there is no forward estimate, so PEG is n/a. The chip's efficiency so far builds on simulations; first silicon came out of the fab on 26/9-2026, and no test results exist yet. Capitalised prepayments to the supplier (NOK 99.6m for mask sets and production equipment) make up most of equity, so P/B overstates the liquid substance. The JV LOI on Arctic AI is non-binding and is not counted as a plus. Customer concentration in ASIC sales has not been examined, because there are no sales yet. The Nordnet page has not been looked up.
