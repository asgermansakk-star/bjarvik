    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −18 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Adjusted EBIT margin is 4.3% R12 vs the company's target of over 8%, and the operating margin has been 0.9–4.1% in 2021–2025. ROIC is only 0.7–2.9% in 2021–2026 (2.1% now) vs a WACC of approx. 5.7%. The company does not earn its cost of capital.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from kr 3.90bn (2022) to kr 3.61bn TTM. The organic change was −3.7% in Q3 and −1.3% LTM vs a target of +3–5% a year. Own consumer brands were at −0.2% organic, so the Q3 growth of +1.7% comes only from the acquisition of Risenta and currency.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisition strategy ended with a write-down of approx. kr 0.5bn in 2022 (net income −kr 501m) and two share issues of kr 500m and 600m in 2021–2022, which doubled the share count from 72.7 to 145.4m. Goodwill and trademarks (kr 2.9bn) still make up almost all of equity of kr 3.17bn.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Moat and segments
      Only the Nordics really make money (EBIT margin 9.2% TTM). North Europe has a margin of 2.6%, and South Europe is around zero (0.8% TTM) after the fire. Many brands are undifferentiated vs retailers' own brands, and management is now reviewing the portfolio to prioritise harder.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −13 of −35
    • Customer behaviour and delistings
      A large grocery customer in North Europe has delisted a number of products from the start of Q3. Own consumer brands fell 17.3% organically in North Europe in Q3, and the segment's sales fell 10.2%. Sales go primarily through a few large grocery chains.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Execution
      According to the managing director, Q3 did not live up to ambitions. The consumer health strategy is moving too slowly, Spain is still not profitable after the fire in July 2025, and a written-off receivable in Norway cost kr 3m. The share fell 8% on the reporting day and is about 40% below the quarter's high of kr 15.50.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Governance and internal control
      Fraud has been uncovered in the Spanish subsidiary (−SEK 5m in Q3, not yet covered by insurance), and CFO Max Bokander left the company, so the finance chief has been replaced in 2026. Stena Adactum owns 48.1% of the capital, while management and board own only about 2.6%. Free float is only about 66m shares, and liquidity is low.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Costs and raw materials
      Transport, packaging and certain raw materials (drought in Europe) as well as the currency are pressing the gross margin, and the increases have not yet been passed on to customers. The margin improvement in Q3 came solely from product mix, and the adjusted EBIT margin stood still at 5.0%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Write-down risk
      Goodwill of kr 1.60bn and other intangible assets of kr 1.28bn stand against equity of kr 3.17bn and tangible equity of only kr 289m. With a ROIC of about 2%, there is a risk of new impairments as in 2022.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Trailing P/E (8.1) and FCF (TTM) are inflated by one-offs: insurance compensation after the fire in Castellcir (approx. SEK 64m in 9M 2026). Adjusted P/E has therefore been roughly calculated from adjusted EBIT R12 (approx. 13). stockanalysis has no forecast page, and forward P/E (9.84) is based on very few analysts. The business interruption insurance is still being processed, and any amount is not included. Stena Adactum owns 48.1% and has not been treated as an insider. The peer multiples for Cloetta and Humble are taken from today's analyses of them. Customer concentration is not quantified because the company does not disclose the share from the largest customer.
