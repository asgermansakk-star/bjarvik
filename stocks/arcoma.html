    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin
      EBIT margin 4.7% TTM and 4.8% in 2025 after 8.7% in 2024, 2.9% in 2023, 2.5% in 2022 and −7.5% in 2021; the net margin is only 3.9%. The gross margin of about 40% is stable, but little is left once a small organisation and amortisation of capitalised development (about 7m a year) are paid.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • ROIC/ROE
      ROIC 9.9% TTM and 11.6% in 2025 vs 23.7% in the peak year 2024 and 5–7% in 2022–2023; ROE only 6.4%, because a quarter of equity is held as cash. The return is around the cost of capital (WACC approx. 8%).
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from SEK 163m in 2023 to 157 (2024), 131 (2025) and 124m TTM (−24% from the peak). Q3 2026 fell 12% (Europe −25%), and the first nine months −7.5%. Asia has fallen from 14.2 to 5.4m, and North America fell 34% in 2025 on tariff uncertainty.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Moat and scale
      A small niche producer of conventional DR X-ray rooms with 31 employees against Siemens Healthineers, GE, Philips, Fujifilm and Canon. A large share of the systems is sold under the partners' own brand (OEM), so Arcoma has limited pricing power and end-customer contact. The market is expected to grow only approx. 6% a year.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Targeted issue of SEK 11m to Eiffel in June 2025 (+8.5% shares) to finance acquisitions and sales, even though the company already had net cash and positive FCF; the acquisition plan has come to nothing, and cash is now 30m. 4–7m a year is still being capitalised in development without visible growth.
      mfn.se
      −2

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −12 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      Sales go through a few OEM partners and distributors; the annual report names Canon USA (US), Konica Minolta (global) and Canon Medical Systems Europe as significant agreements, and the company itself states the risk of a partner ending the cooperation. The Americas (approx. 34% of sales in 2025) go largely through Canon USA.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −4
    • Cash flow and working capital
      Cash flow was −SEK 3.1m in Q2 and −SEK 0.6m in Q3 2026 (9.1 the year before), because trade receivables rose from SEK 13.2m to 20.7m since the turn of the year; orders are lumpy (one US order of SEK 8m equals approx. 6% of annual revenue).
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Management and execution
      The CFO left the company in Q3 2026 (interim CFO, new CFO only in Q4), and the sales director and COO have been replaced; the Certified Adviser has also changed. A small management with high key-person dependence, which the company itself cites as a risk.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Currency and tariffs
      Production and costs are in Växjö, while a large part of sales is in USD and EUR; the stronger krona cost about SEK 1.4m in EBITDA in Q4 2025 (about 40% of the quarter's EBITDA), and US tariff turmoil dampened sales in H1 2025.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Liquidity in the stock
      Average turnover is about 13,000 shares a day (about SEK 0.1m), and the three largest owners (Linc, Priego and Eiffel) hold about 46% of the shares; the stock is hard to trade in larger amounts.

      −2
