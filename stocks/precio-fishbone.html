      Revenue rose 7.1% to kr 37.7m in Q3, but Authority (bought in August) contributed kr 4.2m; organically, revenue fell approx. 5%. The nine-month growth of 5.6% is approx. 2% organic, and headcount rose only from 166 to 180 despite 17 new specialists from Authority. Revenue has fallen from kr 249m in 2021 to kr 169m in 2025.
