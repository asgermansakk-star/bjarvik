    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −17 of −35
    • Regulation
      EU member states voted on 3/11-2026 for the Commission's proposal not to renew the approval of Selektope under the Biocidal Products Regulation; the approval runs to 31/12-2026. The company says the EU accounts for only approx. 4% of revenue, but ABG points to a risk of spillover to other markets, and the substance is labelled as an endocrine disruptor.

      −7
    • Customer concentration and customer behaviour
      The market is dominated by nine large paint producers, of which seven buy commercially; Chugoku Marine Paints is the largest customer. One large customer cut its purchases by two thirds in 2025 because of financial problems, and 94% of sales go to Korea, Japan and China.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4

      Selektope is made by external production partners; the company itself writes that they cannot be replaced quickly or on economically acceptable terms if the cooperation ends.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small company (approx. kr 755m in market cap) with only 2 analysts on the estimates (ABG and Redeye). The EU vote on 3/11-2026 on not renewing the approval of Selektope (medetomidine) has not yet been formally adopted; transitional rules, a possible lawsuit and contagion to other authorities are unresolved. The largest customer's (Chugoku Marine Paints) share of revenue is not disclosed in the reports read, so customer concentration has been assessed from the market structure (nine large paint producers). Patent expiry for Selektope has not been examined (0 points). Revenue is uneven from quarter to quarter because the paint producers buy in large lots. No material lease debt; FCF is after capex.
