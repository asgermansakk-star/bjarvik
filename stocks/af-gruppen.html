    In plain words

    AF Gruppen scores 68 out of 100: a Norwegian contractor with a record order book but thin profits. The main drags are a margin of only about 5%, partly bought growth, and large fixed-price projects that can cause losses. In its favour are an order book of NOK 49.8bn, insider purchases and a dividend covered by cash flow.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Accounts and price in NOK; the latest report is Q2 2026 (Q3 is published 13/11-2026 and is not included). ROIC of 37% and P/B 5.5 are lifted by negative working capital (−NOK 3.7bn) and customer advances and have not been used as a plus; the net interest-bearing receivable of NOK 131m has likewise not been given a plus, because the cash is carried by trade payables and advances. The 2026 growth is partly bought (Claxton and H.A. Bygg), so organic growth has not been calculated precisely. Only 2 analysts cover the stock (price targets NOK 156–230). Customer concentration in the order book has not been quantified beyond the large individual projects.
