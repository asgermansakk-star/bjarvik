    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −28 of −30
    • Margin
      Gross margin is −135% and operating margin −227% (TTM): revenue of SEK 7.5m vs an operating loss of SEK 17m. There have been losses every year since at least 2021 (2024 was close to zero only because of a one-off order), and in Jan.–Sep. 2026 operating profit was −SEK 12.6m vs −6.8m the year before.
      stockanalysis.com
      −13
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell from SEK 21.8m (2024, carried by a one-off order from Stena Recycling Danmark) to 10.3m (2025) and 6.3m in Jan–Sep 2026 (7.8). Q3 grew to 2.1m (0.8), but the core business consists of only four recurring flows.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Moat and future segment
      CirChem calls itself the leading Nordic player in industrial solvent recovery, and the EU's waste hierarchy and the Circular Economy Act are tailwinds. But the plant in Vargön requires new adaptations and investment for each new flow, and the targets (SEK 250m revenue, 15% EBITDA margin) are far from 8–9m today.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The rights issue in June 2026 (SEK 0.68 per share) was subscribed only 66.6% and raised SEK 16.2m gross, but costs ended at approx. 4.4m (27%). The share count rose from 40.2m to 64.0m (+59%), and ROIC is −17%.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −25 of −35
    • Financing need and liquidity
      Cash was SEK 10.7m at 30/9, after the last issue instalment (8.2m) and 2.9m from Klimatklivet had come in. Operating cash flow was −12.7m in Jan–Sep, interest-bearing debt is about 16.8m (of which 4.8m short-term), current ratio is 0.97 and Altman Z 0.26. The Stena project requires new investment, and the board itself writes that the company depends on new financing. A new issue or more debt within 2–3 quarters is likely.
      mfn.se
      −10
    • Execution
      The Stena agreement (SEK 20–25m a year) was called a commercial breakthrough in May and was the reason for the issue. In Q3 it is still in technical verification and pilot phase, and the investment need has not yet been assessed. Two pilot projects in the automotive industry have been postponed by the customers, one of them to the beginning of 2027.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Customer concentration
      The ongoing business is four recurring flows: two closed loops (one automotive and one paint customer) and two open loops via one waste partner. The largest future source (Stena) and the revenue peak in 2024 both hang on Stena Recycling.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Price performance and market confidence
      The price has fallen 70% over 52 weeks to SEK 0.395, 42% below the issue price of 0.68 and at half the 200-day average (0.80). Beta is 2.35.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Governance and insider ownership
      Insiders own only about 1% of the shares. Eminova is Certified Adviser, issuing agent, financial adviser (Eminova Partners) and underwriter (SEK 13.2m, hedged with put options) all at once, which is a conflict of interest in a small First North company.
