      Profit after tax fell 11% to kr 68.1m in H1 2026 (76.6), and earnings per equity certificate fell 17% from kr 8.03 to 6.69.     • Profitability and costs
      Return on equity on ordinary profit fell to 8.05% (9.88%) vs the bank's target of over 10%, and cost/income rose to 48.8% (43.1%) vs the target of below 45%, because operating costs rose 6.5% with falling income. The board itself writes that ROE and the cost ratio are under pressure this year.
      api3.oslo.oslobors.no

    • Capital allocation for the equity certificate holders
      The equity certificate ratio has fallen from 32.63% (2024) to 30.70% (2025) and 28.88% (2026), because the certificate holders receive 88–93% of their share of profit paid out, while the ownerless savings bank capital retains its share. The certificate holders' share of the bank's value is diluted year by year.

      −2

      Small Eika alliance bank with about NOK 16bn in business capital and about 70 employees in Melhus and Trondheim, where it competes directly against SpareBank 1 SMN and the big banks; the board itself points to strong competition for customers and margin pressure as the reason for the decline.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful. stockanalysis shows P/B 0.30, but that figure uses total equity incl. the ownerless savings bank capital; P/B per equity certificate is calculated manually from equity without additional tier 1 bonds and the equity certificate fraction (the bank's own APM includes the additional tier 1 bond and therefore shows a slightly lower P/B). P/B history before 2024 has not been calculated. The Q3 report comes 13/11-2026 and is not included; figures are as of H1 2026. There is no known analyst coverage beyond a forward P/E on stockanalysis, so price targets and revisions are thin. No reportable insider trades were found on Newsweb in 2025–2026 beyond the bank's own purchase of certificates for the bonus programme. Very illiquid paper.
