    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −7 of −30
    • ROIC and returns
      ROIC is 5.1% vs WACC of 8.05% and has been 4.6-6.4% since 2021 with no improvement. ROE of 15.6% is lifted by revaluations (kr 386m in 9M) and derivative gains; the return on the properties (direct return 6.9%) has fallen from 7.2% a year ago.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Profit from property management per ordinary share rose 11% in 9M (SEK 13.67), but rent in the comparable portfolio rose only just under 3%; the rest comes from acquisitions of SEK 2.0bn in 9M (incl. Anguli for SEK 1.2bn). Earnings capacity per ordinary share rose only 8% since the turn of the year (SEK 18.41), and analysts expect lower EPS in 2026 and 2027.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      Growth rests on recurring share issues: ordinary shares have risen from 54.45 to 61.63m since 2021 (+13%, of which +7% in 2024), and 14m preference shares were issued in 2026 (12.75m at SEK 30 in May). The preference dividend of SEK 2 costs about 6.7% of the issue price, close to the properties' direct yield of 6.9%, and takes SEK 140m of the profit from property management a year.
      mfn.se
      −2

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −10 of −35
    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio is 51.7% (51.2 at year-end; target 50-55, cap 60), net debt/EBITDA 8.3× (8.1), and interest cover only 2.9× vs a target of at least 2. Average loan maturity fell to 2.7 years (3.0), 60% of loans fall due within 3 years, the fixed-interest period is 1.8 years, and only 51% is hedged (54.5). Available liquidity is only kr 925m, and 70m preference shares (market value kr 2.1bn) rank ahead of the ordinary shares.

      −7
    • Letting
      The economic occupancy rate is stuck at 92%, below e.g. Sagax and Catena (95%), and the vacancy value rose to kr 224m from 202m at the turn of the year. Net lettings were only kr 3m in Q3, and terminated contracts worth kr 111m have not yet been vacated (of which 56m in 2026) vs kr 118m in signed contracts not yet commenced. Remaining lease term is only 4.1 years.
