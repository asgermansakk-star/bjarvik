      The leverage covenant (net debt/adjusted EBITDA at most 3.0x) was breached in Q1, Q3 and Q4 2025, and a breach in Q1 2026 was expected; waivers have been obtained. The main facility of kr 300m expired 1/11-2026 and had to be refinanced. Net debt kr 268m incl. leasing (30/6), current ratio 0.95 and interest cover 2.9x.

      FCF was negative in 2022–2025 (−DKK 209m, −248m, −24m and −24m). The positive TTM FCF of 160m (approx. 135m after leasing) comes almost entirely from released working capital (lower receivables), while net income is negative; Q3 FCF fell to 27m from 36m.

      The Novo Nordisk group accounted for 14% of revenue in 2025 (12% in 2024) and is also a related party; tier-1 pharma customers' restraint is the main reason for the decline, and in Asia revenue from a large existing tier-1 customer fell markedly in Q3.

The status of the refinancing of the DKK 300m credit facility that expired 1/11-2026 has not been found in the 2026 reports and is therefore unknown. Web search was not available in this run, so Danish media and analyst commentary have not been investigated; the analysis is based on the company's announcements (Nasdaq), the annual report 2025 and stockanalysis. Only 3 analysts cover the stock (1 Strong Buy, 2 Hold), so price targets and upside are uncertain. The possible divestment of SCALES under investigation (Nordea Corporate Finance, very early stage) is not a special situation for the NNIT stock, but could change the company's profile significantly.
