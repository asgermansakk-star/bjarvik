    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share; ECC holders have 66.8% of equity, the rest is self-owned (grunnfond). It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful. The valuation is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (1.61); stockanalysis's P/B of 1.03 and book value count the self-owned capital and have therefore not been used. Intangible assets are only NOK 1.4bn, so P/TBV is only slightly above P/B (approx. 1.7). Stockanalysis's financial figures only go to 30/6, so Q3 2026 is taken from the bank's own report. Only 4 analysts cover the security, and there is only one EPS estimate for 2026 (3 analysts). Insider trades are only allocations in the employee savings programme; they have not been examined further. Large single exposures and concentration in the corporate book (e.g. fish farming and commercial property) have not been examined in detail. The sale of Kredinor (through SpareBank 1 Gruppen) is expected to complete around the turn of the year with a moderate accounting effect; it has not been scored.
