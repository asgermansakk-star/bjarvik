    In plain words

    A score of 39 shows a very small company making a loss. The auditor doubts it can continue, and cash was only 0.3m Norwegian kroner. The stock is expensive relative to sales, and there has been no chief executive since mid-2024. On the plus side, the main owners guaranteed the share issue, and salmon-industry sales are profitable. The price target is about 13% above the share price (two analysts).
