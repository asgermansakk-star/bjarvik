    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 3 analysts cover the stock, and daily turnover is approx. kr 3m. Q3 2026 is the company's best quarter in several years and is lifted by supplier price increases that have not yet come through in cost of goods; the TTM multiples are based on a quarter that may be a peak. The dividend of kr 5.00 per share (kr 103.9m) in 2026 was 166% of net profit for 2025 and above FCF after leases, but was paid from net cash; it has neither been deducted nor given a plus. Lease debt rose to approx. kr 108m with the new warehouse in 2025, so stockanalysis' net cash (58.8m) is incl. leases. Return rate, customer loyalty (repurchases) and the breakdown of suppliers by country have not been investigated (0 points). The insider register has only been reviewed from November 2025.
