    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    BW Energy reports in USD, while the stock trades in NOK on Oslo Børs; stockanalysis mixes the currencies (EPS NOK 2.87 TTM vs USD 0.29 in the 9M accounts). stockanalysis shows a 9.5% fall in share count, but the balance sheet shows 258 → 261m shares (LTIP issues) and no buybacks in the cash flow, so the figure has not been used. The Q3 figures are distorted by the timing of liftings (underlift of 1.0m barrels), so TTM EBITDA and EV/EBITDA are lower/higher than the underlying operations; revenue from the delayed Golfinho cargo comes in Q4. The consequences of the rig accident on 24/10 (five missing) for 2027 production have not yet been quantified by the company. Only 4 analysts cover the stock, and only 1 has an EPS estimate for 2026, so forward P/E and the estimate for 2027 (EPS +164%) are very thin. The entry into Angola is a framework agreement with economic exposure via Etu, not a direct licence interest, and awaits approvals. Customer concentration (the oil is sold to few offtakers/traders) and BW Group's exact ownership share have not been investigated separately. Q3 report 29/10-2026.
