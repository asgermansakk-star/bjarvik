    In plain words

    Quantinuum gets 41, almost solely because the share is extremely expensive: its market value is about $7.9bn for revenue of about $33m, more than any comparable quantum company. The loss is growing faster than revenue, and three customers account for about two thirds of sales, with the US government the largest. The government received about 0.9% of the shares at $42.20 each in connection with a grant of up to $100m, but nothing had been paid out by 30 September, and it can demand paid-out amounts back as debt. On the plus side, the company holds $1.9bn more in cash than debt. Watch 1 December, when the insider lock-up ends for about 233m shares.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated 24 Nov 2026 (government equity stakes). Since 29 Sep: the Q3 results (5 Nov) with revenue of $9.1m (+233%) and raised full-year guidance ($31–34m from $28–32m), but the adjusted EBITDA loss grew to $80.5m; the share price has fallen from $41.30 to $30 (23 Nov), and the lock-up expires on 1 Dec. The government stake is verified in the 8-K of 8 Sep and the 10-Q: the Commerce Department bought 2,369,528 A shares at $42.20 ($100m, about 0.9% of all 265.8m shares and about 5% of the A shares) on 9 Sep against a CHIPS agreement (signed on 4 Sep by the subsidiary Quantinuum LLC) for up to $100m in three tranches ($56m + $32m + $12m, the last two against milestones). The government does not vote its shares except on changes in the number of authorised A shares, may only sell in proportion to the funds paid out and not to competitors, and has the right to reclaim up to the full amount paid out as debt. At 30 Sep nothing had been drawn, and the shares are not counted as outstanding as long as the company's repurchase right applies. Up-C structure: 45.6m A shares + 220.3m B shares/units; the market cap ($7.9bn) is calculated on all shares. Honeywell's exact ownership and voting share after the blocker merger has not been checked in the 10-Q. No multiple history of its own: T2 rests on peers and absolute level. Insider trading after the IPO has not been investigated.
