    In plain words

    RTX scores 52 out of 100: a small technology supplier with weak earnings and shares that look expensive against them. The biggest drags are low profit, a return on invested money below the cost of financing, and three customers making up about 44% of sales. On the plus side, its outlook was raised in August 2026. The annual report is due on 25 November 2026.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
