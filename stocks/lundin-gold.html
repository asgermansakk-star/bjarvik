    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis has no Stockholm page for LUG (quote/sto/LUG gives 404), so the TSX listing in CAD is used as path and price source; the stock is traded as the same stock in Stockholm (SEK). The financial statements are in USD, the multiples on stockanalysis in CAD. P/B 28 is distorted by dividend in kind (LunR) and a new silver stream obligation of approx. USD 670m and is not used. Earnings are measured at record-high gold prices (realised USD 4,518/oz in 2026). The investment decision on FDNS and the mill expansion (capex unknown) is expected in Q4. Insider trades, mine life in reserves and tax rules in Ecuador have not been investigated in detail.
