    • Margin and ROIC
      Adjusted EBITA margin 11.1% in the nine months of the year (10.0%) and adjusted EBITDA excluding IFRS 16 only 9.4% of revenue R12; ROIC 6.6% vs a WACC of approx. 5.5%. That is clearly better than 4.2% in 2021, but still a low-margin, labour-intensive business area with a gross margin of 28%, where prices are set by public payers.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation: Humana acquisition
      Humana (revenue about SEK 10bn, adjusted EBITA margin only 4.8% in 2025) was bought for SEK 3,423m (932m in cash, 2,491m in shares). The combined group excluding personal assistance had a margin of 8.4% vs Ambea's own 9.6%, leverage rises to about 3.5x net debt/EBITDA excluding IFRS 16, and the share count rose to 89.0m. The synergies of SEK 120m only come in full in year two and cost SEK 150m in one-off costs plus about SEK 90m in transaction costs; personal assistance in Sweden is to be sold within 12 months.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Ambea took over 98% of Humana on 1/10-2026 (squeeze-out of the rest is under way); Humana is consolidated only from Q4, and there is as yet no preliminary acquisition balance sheet. stockanalysis' figures and history are therefore from before the merger, and the share count on stockanalysis (80.3m) is outdated: there are now 89.03m shares. The pro forma figures are the company's own aggregated 2025 figures from the offer announcement (unaudited, without synergies). The squeeze-out applies to Humana, not Ambea, so there is no special situation for the Ambea share. FCF is measured after lease payments (kr 1.3bn a year). The buyback of kr 1.47bn TTM (5.56m shares used as payment for Humana) is not covered by FCF after leasing and does not give a plus. The outcome of the Riksdag election in September 2026 and a new government's line on profit in welfare have not been verified. Only 2–3 analysts cover the stock. Insider trades, board independence and major shareholders' sales have not been examined separately (0 points).
