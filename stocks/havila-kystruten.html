    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −19 of −30
    • Margin
      EBITDA was kr 373m in 2025, but of that 103m was a retroactive adjustment of contract income for earlier years, and Q4 2025 produced 0 in EBITDA. H1 2026 produced only 128m (Q1 8%, Q2 21% margin). The net result was −kr 730m TTM, and H1 2026 was in the black (3m) only because of currency gains. An underlying EBITDA margin of around 15-17% is too low to carry debt of over kr 5bn.
      inderes.dk
      −10
    • Capital allocation
      Accumulated losses of kr 3.6bn have wiped out over kr 2.1bn in paid-in capital; the share count has been heavily diluted (+743% in 2023 and +104% in 2024), and the price has fallen from an adjusted kr 892 in 2021 to kr 45 after a 1:50 reverse split in November 2025.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Growth comes mainly from occupancy (83% vs 74% in Q2) and passenger nights (+17%), while price lags: ACR rose only 4% in Q1 vs a target of over 10% for 2026, and ticket revenue grew only 10% in Q2 due to the stronger NOK.

    • Debt
      Net interest-bearing debt of DKK 5.3bn, negative equity of −1.39bn, interest cover 0.49, current ratio 0.63 and Altman Z −0.26. The fixed hire on the lease rises from EUR 93,000/day in year 1 to EUR 125,000/day in year 4 (approx. DKK 0.4 to 0.53bn a year), vs an EBITDA of 373m in 2025.

      −10
    • Cash flow
      Levered FCF was −kr 247m TTM after kr 329m in interest paid, and the only analyst with an estimate expects FCF of −199m in 2026. The junior tranche's variable charter hire can be paid as PIK, so the debt can grow instead of being repaid.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Governance and related parties
      All debt (EUR 456m) is now a 15-year finance lease from a subsidiary of the main shareholder Havila Holding (the Sævik family), which thus both owns the majority and is the sole creditor. That creates a clear conflict of interest towards the minority, and free float is only about 3m of 17.1m shares.
      inderes.dk
      −6
    • Liquidity in the stock
      Average turnover of about 2,500 shares a day (about kr 0.1m) and only 2 analysts; the price is 15% below the 200-day average and −27% in a year.

      −2
    • Macro and currency
      The company hedges neither currency nor LNG; a stronger NOK lowers ticket revenue, and currency losses on the EUR/USD debt have driven equity negative. Higher energy prices in Q2 and Q3 are only covered approx. 70% via indexation of the state contract and with a 1–2 year lag.
