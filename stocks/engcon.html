    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 4 analysts cover the stock (price targets SEK 60–75, median 68.50), and stockanalysis' 2026 EPS estimate of SEK 1.92 requires Q4 EPS of about SEK 0.48 vs SEK 0.31 in Q4 2025, even though order intake fell 10% organically in Q3; the estimates are probably not fully adjusted after the Q3 report on 29 October. Q3's EBIT margin of 19.9% includes positive currency effects of SEK 15m in other operating income. FCF is measured after leasing and intangible investments (341 − 89 − 6 − 23 = SEK 223m TTM); capex is lifted by the property purchase in Poland (about EUR 5m) and the expansion in Strömsund. Insider ownership of 67.9% is the founding family (Stig and Monica Engström), not management purchases; the insider register shows no trades since July 2025 (only the latest 10 entries were read). Peers are Atlas Copco and Epiroc (forward P/E 24.3 and 23.8) and Alimak (14.0); the private competitors Rototilt and Steelwrist cannot be compared. Customer concentration has not been examined separately (sales via own sales companies and dealers as well as OEM collaborations with e.g. Komatsu and Volvo CE). No provision has been made for the Rototilt patent case; it is being heard in the Patent and Market Court of Appeal in H1 2027.
