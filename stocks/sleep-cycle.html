    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Special situation: Snark BidCo (Altor) owns 79.5% after the offer at SEK 24.50 in July 2026 and has announced that it will vote for the reverse merger with Audiowell at the extraordinary general meeting on 11/11-2026. If that happens, current shareholders are diluted by approx. 82%, and Audiowell Group AB must make a mandatory bid at SEK 24.50 per share; the price (24.20) therefore follows the deal terms. Altor is at the same time investigating whether a delisting can be avoided. Audiowell's figures (K3, 2025: revenue SEK 378m, EBIT SEK 216m) are unaudited and not comparable with IFRS; no pro forma findings have been made for the combined group. Forward P/E from stockanalysis is based on 1 analyst (the Fwd table shows 2026 EPS 0.38 and P/E 63.7), so analyst coverage is very thin. Free float approx. 3.8m shares and low turnover. Insider trades in 2026 have not been examined (0 points).
