    Transtema scores 34 out of 100. Operating profit is almost gone (0.4% of revenue), revenue fell 12.6% last quarter, and debt is 7.7 times annual earnings against a target of at most 2. Acquisitions in Norway have been written down twice. On the plus side, the Swedish business won contracts worth over 1.4bn kr.

The operating margin is −13.2% and the net margin −16% (TTM) including write-downs; even before them the company earns practically nothing, far from its own target of at least a 7% EBITA margin.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 8.7% in 9M 2026 and 12.6% in Q3 (organic −13.8%), with −0.3% in Sweden and −51.9% in Norway. Revenue (kr 2.44bn TTM) is lower than in 2022, and the decline is due to lower demand from telecom operators and price pressure, not only discarded low-margin business.

Equity has fallen from kr 557m in 2024 to kr 226m, and tangible equity is negative (−kr 135m).

      Rights issue of kr 40.3m at kr 2.50 (45% discount to TERP) in May 2026 to strengthen working capital during the restructuring; the share count rose 37.5% from 42.95m to 59.06m (27.3% dilution). With leverage of 7.7x and negative FCF after leases, a new capital raise is not unlikely.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Management and governance
      CEO Henning Sveder stepped down with immediate effect on 23/1 2026 (stock −21% on the day), and the company still has only an acting group CEO (Jonas Rigner) after more than nine months, while the founder and chair Magnus Johansson is executive chair. The Norwegian business got a new managing director (Thor-Egel Bråthen) in August.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Execution and guidance
      The savings programme of about kr 125m a year (about 250 positions) has not yet lifted the adjusted EBITA margin above 1% in Q3; the company itself writes that the savings cannot offset the volume decline in Norway. The company gives no specific guidance, and the financial targets (10% growth, 7% EBITA margin) are far off.
