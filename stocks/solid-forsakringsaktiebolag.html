    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 2 findings    −7 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Premium income net of reinsurance fell 3% in the first nine months of 2026 (kr 782m vs 802m) after −6.6% in revenue in 2025, and net profit has gone from kr 165m in 2023–2024 to 149 in 2025 and 147 TTM (EPS 8.86 → 8.31 → 8.27). The lost cooperation with Power gave −19% in the Product segment, and the underlying growth of 4% requires adjusting for Riddermark, Switzerland and a premium credit. The company's own target is 4–7% annual growth in premium income.

    • Moat and bargaining power
      Solid does not own the distribution: products are sold through banks, retail chains and car dealers, and acquisition costs (commissions) make up 61% of premium income in Trygghet (kr 75.5m of 124.5m in Q3) and an operating expense ratio of 62.7% for the group. The partners take most of the economics, and the Product segment (consumer electronics, bicycles) is shrinking with its customers.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −11 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      The largest partner in Trygghet accounted for SEK 93.1m of the segment's SEK 124.5m in Q3 (75%), corresponding to 35% of the group's premium income. The largest partner in each of the three segments together accounts for about 51%. According to Placera, the partner is Resurs Bank (96% of Trygghet at the IPO, about 78% in Q1 2026). The loss of Power and the renegotiation with Riddermark show that it hits directly when a partner disappears. A technical error at a partner also triggered a premium credit of SEK 8.2m in Q2.
      corporate.solidab.se
      −5
    • Governance and related parties
      Waldakt AB owns 32.4% and is, according to Placera, also co-owner of Resurs (via Ronneby, together with CVC), the company's largest distribution partner. That creates a conflict of interest around commissions and renewal of the agreement. Insiders own only 3.2%, and free float is about 51%.
      placera.se
      −2
    • Interest rates and asset management
      Asset management profit fell 14% to kr 47.9m in the first nine months and makes up about a third of profit before tax. Lower market interest rates also reduce the investment return transferred to the insurance operation (−kr 3.7m), so profit falls despite improved insurance operations.
      corporate.solidab.se
      −2
    • Macro
      Half of premium income (Trygghet, 47%) is payment protection tied to consumer credit at Resurs and other banks, so volume and claims follow credit growth and unemployment. Product follows retail sales of home electronics, which is already falling (−10% sales in Q3).

      Only ABG Sundal Collier covers the stock, and average trading is about 24,000 shares a day (about kr 2.7m). Estimates and price targets rest on one analyst.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Non-life insurance: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful. Stockanalysis' ROE of 28.7% is based on IFRS equity (SEK 515m) without the untaxed safety reserve; here the company's RoE (2) and RoNAV (2) on adjusted equity (SEK 902m) are used. Dividends and buybacks are measured against net income. Only one analyst (ABG Sundal Collier) covers the share, and stockanalysis has no forecast page, so forward P/E 12.5 rests on one estimate and cannot be compared with a forward history; Step 2 uses trailing against trailing. The largest partner in Trygghet is not named in the Q3 report; that it is Resurs Bank, and that Waldakt via Ronneby is co-owner of Resurs, comes from Placera (June 2026). Ownership by the individual major shareholders beyond the top 10 and the board's total holdings have not been examined; the insider ownership of 3.2% is from stockanalysis. Adequacy of reserves (run-off result) and any regulatory issues on payment protection insurance have not been examined and score 0.
