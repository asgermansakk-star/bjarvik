    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −16 of −30
    • Quality of growth: production and reserves
      Production is falling: W.I. production was 34,000 boepd in 2024 (pro forma), 30,800 in 2025, 27,100 in Q2 2026 (−12% in a year), and guidance for 2026 is 24,000–27,000. 2P reserves fell from 101.6 to 87.7m boe with no new discoveries or extensions (about 8 years of life), and 2P-NPV10 after tax fell from USD 2,128m to 1,499m. Revenue growth comes only from the consolidation of Prime and the high oil price.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Margin
      Loss despite high oil prices: net result −USD 31.6m in 2025 after a write-down of 105.3m on Agbami (higher costs for life extension of the FPSO) and −USD 96m TTM (net margin −13%). Tax on the Nigerian licences takes almost the whole pre-tax result (pre-tax margin 0.2% TTM), and the adjusted result was also negative in Q1 2026 (−13m).
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Moat
      All production comes from three deepwater fields off Nigeria with non-operated interests (8% of Agbami, operated by Chevron, and 16% of Akpo/Egina, operated by TotalEnergies). The company is a pure price taker and controls neither drilling timing nor costs.
      mfn.se
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −16 of −35
    • Price normalisation and dividend cover
      The raised guidance is mainly due to the expected Brent price being raised from about USD 63 to about USD 86/barrel (Q2: 103.8 owing to the conflict in the Middle East). With the original guidance (CFFO 185–255m, investments 100–140m, before interest) the dividend of about USD 100m a year is not covered when the 2027–2028 drilling programme starts.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Controlling shareholder and overhang
      BTG Pactual owns 35.5% (239.8m shares) with the right to board seats and has hedged price exposure with total return swaps on 21.5m shares (3.2%), expiring in July–August 2027. BTG writes that it can buy or sell at any time. Insiders own only 0.38%.
      smb.trailtimes.ca
      −3
    • Execution
      The drilling programme at Akpo/Egina was paused in 2025 and now only starts in Q4 2026; investment guidance has been lowered because drilling costs have been pushed to 2027. At Agbami, Meren is still working with the operator on the reliability of the gas compression.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Hedging and realised price
      In Q2 2026 the oil was sold at USD 92.8/barrel against a Brent price of 103.8, because an old trigger-price mechanism from 2025 was activated; derivatives gave a loss of USD 27.3m in H1 (of which 8.2m in cash). The hedging has cost a large part of the price rise.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Regulation and country risk
      The growth options are waiting for the authorities: FID on Venus in Namibia awaits negotiations on tax terms, and the environmental approval of Block 3B/4B in South Africa is still under appeal with no timetable. Production is 100% Nigerian.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Debt
      Net debt rose from USD 155.3m to 212.3m in H1 2026, and cash fell from 174.7 to 77.7m, even though the oil price was high. Leverage is still low (net debt/EBITDAX 0.5x).

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Traded in Stockholm (MER), but stockanalysis' Stockholm page (quote/sto/MER) has no statistics or financial pages, so the path is the TSX listing quote/tsx/MER with price in CAD. The accounts are in USD, the statistics page in CAD. The company was called Africa Oil until 2025 and owned only 50% of Prime (equity method), so multiples and returns before 2025 are hard to compare; the Prime merger closed in March 2025, more than 12 months before this analysis. stockanalysis' ROIC (−1,334%) and tax rate (6,100%) are meaningless and have not been used. The analysts' price targets on stockanalysis (CAD 1.78–1.80, last updated 17/7-2026) do not match Scotiabank's target of 2.50 and look outdated or wrongly converted. Only 5 analysts and only 2 with an EPS estimate for 2026. The Q3 report comes 10/11-2026, after this analysis. Insider trades beyond BTG have not been investigated.
