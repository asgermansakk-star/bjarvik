    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The equity certificate fraction is only 34.45%, so 65.5% of equity is self-owned; the security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.37 and book value of NOK 475.95 per share cover total equity including self-owned capital and have not been used; T2 is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (book value NOK 164.31 at 30/6). stockanalysis's +7.49% in share count has not been used: the number is 9,061,837 certificates, unchanged since 2021 according to the Q2 report, and the buybacks are small and go to the employee savings programme. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are Q2 2026; the Q3 report is due on 12/11-2026 and is not included. stockanalysis has no forecast page for the security, so analyst coverage is very thin, and forward P/E 9.77 has an uncertain basis. The Pillar 2 requirement before the SREP decision on 30/10 has not been examined, so the net effect of the new requirement (2.4%) against the lower expected margin (0.75% vs 1.5%) has not been assessed (0 points). Insider trades: only allocations in the employee savings programme found on Newsweb (0 points).
