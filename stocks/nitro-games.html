    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Finnish mobile game developer on First North Stockholm with a market cap of only approx. SEK 31m (approx. EUR 2.8m). The accounts are in EUR, the stock trades in SEK. TTM profit (EUR 1.15m) and P/E 2.4 are driven by Business Finland's waiver of a loan of EUR 1.61m in Q2 2026; without it, operations are loss-making. stockanalysis' FCF of EUR 1.25m TTM appears to include the waiver in operating cash flow and as repayment in financing, so the actual development of cash has been calculated instead. No analyst coverage and no forward estimate on stockanalysis (the forecast page does not exist). Launch data for Warhammer 40,000: Boltgun Boom (sales, conversion) have not been published beyond CEO comments. The name of the customer behind the orders of EUR 5.2m has not been disclosed.
