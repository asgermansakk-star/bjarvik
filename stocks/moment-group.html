    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    No analyst coverage: stockanalysis has no forecast page, so there is no forward P/E, price target or EPS estimates, and PEG becomes n/a. Stockanalysis' TTM figures (EPS 1.37, P/E 3.5) include the gain of approx. kr 49m from the sale of Tickster in December 2025; the findings use underlying figures calculated from the company's own reports (rolling 12 months: revenue approx. 1,121m, EBIT approx. 75m, of which 49m one-off gain). The business is highly seasonal, and Q4 carries the year's result, so a nine-month loss is normal; Q4 2026 is not yet known. The outcome of the written procedure on the bond is expected around 25 November 2026; more than 2/3 of the bondholders have committed to voting in favour. Peers have only been compared with Actic Group. It has not been examined whether the major shareholders Gelba Management and Robus Capital also own bonds, or who is behind Gelba. The insider register at Finansinspektionen shows no trades in 2026. Trading in the stock is very thin (approx. 6,000 shares a day).
