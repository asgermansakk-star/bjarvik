    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a Norwegian-Swedish media group where newspaper ads and printing are shrinking; the operating margin is about 3%. The share is pricier relative to profit than comparable media firms, which earn far more. The dividend exceeds cash flow and is paid from the cash pile. On the plus side, it holds over NOK 800m in cash and funds, almost no debt and 466,600 digital subscribers. Three owners hold 74%.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
