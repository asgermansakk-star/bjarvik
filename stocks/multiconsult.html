    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Special situation: The merger plan with Rejlers (Rejlers is the acquiring company; 0.9725 Rejlers B shares per Multiconsult share) was approved by both general meetings on 19/10-2026 and is expected to be completed around the turn of the year 2026/27, subject to competition approval. The price therefore largely follows the Rejlers price. With Rejlers at SEK 134.80, the consideration corresponds to approx. SEK 131 per share vs Multiconsult's price of NOK 130.80; the conversion depends on SEK/NOK and has not been calculated precisely. Engineering and architecture consultant; 'Construction & materials' was chosen as the closest industry in line with Sweco, AFRY and Norconsult. P/TBV is meaningless because goodwill (1.41bn) is larger than equity (1.23bn). stockanalysis' net debt (1.43bn) includes lease debt; the company's own interest-bearing net debt excluding leases was NOK 788m at the end of Q2. Forward P/E (10.4) is based on the estimates of only 2 analysts, which assume approx. 36% EPS growth in 2027, and the 2026 estimate (EPS 9.89) appears to be from before Q3, when EPS was −0.74. Insider trades from the Financial Supervisory Authority's register, single-customer concentration and contract terms (fixed price vs time and materials) have not been examined in depth; the Sotra Link case has not been penalised in the score, as the risk according to the company has been reduced after the ruling in Oslo District Court and the payments in April 2026. Only 3 analysts cover the stock.
