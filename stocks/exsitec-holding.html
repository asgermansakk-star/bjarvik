    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small company on First North with low liquidity (about 12,000 shares a day, free float about 6.9m shares) and only 2 analysts, who are probably paid coverage; price targets and estimates are therefore thin. Forward P/E on stockanalysis is based on adjusted EPS, which adds back amortisation of acquired intangibles and restructuring costs. Amesto Solutions is included only from June 2026, so TTM figures (ROIC, debt/EBITDA) are not pro forma; the company's own pro forma figures have not been found. There is no formal financial guidance beyond the synergy target of SEK 35m. The full notes of the Q3 report have only been skimmed; Q4's cash flow will carry the deferred restructuring payments.
