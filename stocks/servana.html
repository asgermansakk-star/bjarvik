    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −26 of −30
    • Margin
      Dynava (approx. 700 employees, contact centres, 118 118 and Samres booking centres) had an EBITDA margin of only 3.8% in 2025 (12 of DKK 318m) and 9.6% in H1 2026 (14 of 146m). Servana itself had revenue of DKK 26,000 in H1 2026 with negative EBITDA. These are thin margins in a labour-intensive business.

    • Future segment
      Outsourced customer service, switchboard and directory enquiries (118 118, 0100100) are exactly the tasks that AI chat and automation eat. Servana's own AI services (Userdesk, Spell, SaiMaa) still have almost no revenue.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      All growth is bought: Q3 revenue of kr 38.2m (0.0) comes from Dynava, consolidated for one and a half months. Dynava's own revenue fell from kr 159m per half-year in 2025 to 146m in H1 2026 (about −8%).
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat
      Contact centres compete on price in public tenders and have low switching costs. Dynava is composed of Eniro's divested customer service and Samres and has no documented pricing power.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The company has changed strategy several times: Medimi sold MedimiSMART, dropped the Medthings purchase, did a reverse takeover of Userdesk, bought the AI tool Spell and now Dynava for kr 102m. The purchase is almost entirely debt-financed, and it happened at a market cap of approx. kr 40m.

      There is no usable own history (new company after the acquisition) and no P/E, because the group has no pro forma net income.     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Servana (formerly Medimi) is, after the acquisition of Dynava Group (taken over 13/8-2026), in practice a new company; stockanalysis has no financial data, no analysts, and the price on the path quote/ngm/SERV has stood still since 30/9-2026 (SEK 0.0058), although the share continues to trade on Spotlight (MFN shows the list Spotlight and a price jump of 55% on 19/10). The trading venue is therefore uncertain. The valuation is based on Dynava's own figures (EBITDA SEK 14m in H1 2026, 12m in 2025) and an estimated net debt of approx. SEK 73m (Moank 40 + vendor note 52 + convertible 6 − cash 25.5); depreciation, FCF after leases, interest payments and a consolidated pro forma income statement have not been published and have not been assessed. The Q3 report comes on 20/11-2026. Insider trades and shareholder base beyond stockanalysis' 2.9% have not been examined. Industry set to 'Software, cloud & internet' because the list has no category for outsourced customer service; operations are really labour-intensive contact centres.
