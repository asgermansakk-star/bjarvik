    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Hotel and property company with one asset (Boho Club in Marbella, formerly Quartiers Properties). The holding is the common share (BOHO), not the preference share (BOHO.PREF). Intrinsic value is roughly calculated by me from the Q3 report: Savills' RICS value (SEK 511.7m for the hotel plus 18.4m for the apartments) against a book value of 307.8m, 25% deferred tax, and the preference shares deducted at market price (6.77m × SEK 11.45) plus unpaid dividend of 33.3m. The redemption price of the preference shares has not been found, so the common share's NAV is uncertain. The external valuation of EUR 130m (about SEK 19 per share) is not RICS, includes future development potential and has not been used as NAV. The expansion to 28,400 m² has no published budget, so the financing need is not quantified. No analyst coverage, no forward estimates and very low trading in the stock (about 12,000 shares a day). The ownership change at House of K (9.5 → 6.0m shares) and the insider trades in 2026 have not been examined in the insider register.
