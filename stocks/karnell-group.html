    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −8 of −30
    • ROIC after goodwill
      ROIC is 11.0% TTM (8.6–10.7% in FY2022–25) and ROCE on EBITA 16.4%. Goodwill and intangible assets (SEK 1.56bn) are larger than equity (SEK 1.46bn), so tangible equity is negative (−SEK 103m). The return is lower than at the big serial acquirers.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Earnings quality below EBIT
      EBIT rose 72% in Q3, but net profit fell 1% to SEK 45.9m and diluted EPS to 0.83 (0.85). This is due to financial items of −39.6m (−3.2), of which −16.1m in currency, −8.8m in revaluation of put/call options and earn-outs and −4.0m in dividend to minorities. The tax rate was 32% because the revaluations are not tax-deductible. The ownership structure with sellers as minority owners makes EPS volatile.

      −3
    • Cyclicality
      Most subsidiaries sell to other industrial companies, and according to the company this depends to a large extent on the industrial cycle. Lead times are short, and management has limited visibility ahead.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 3 findings    −10 of −35
    • Customer concentration and temporary effects
      One subsidiary in Niche Production has had demand from one customer throughout 2026 at levels the company does not consider sustainable. In Q3 the customer accounted for approx. SEK 10m of the EBITA growth, approx. a third of the group's organic profit growth, and a larger share in H1. In addition there are large one-off deliveries and price increases that came through before costs. The CEO writes that Q3 is not a new normal level.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Debt and acquisition financing
      Interest-bearing bank debt rose from SEK 370m to 726m in 2026 to finance OBA Tf and flex7 (349m in cash consideration). In addition there are put/call options and earn-outs of 185m and lease debt of 145m. Leverage is 1.4x net debt/EBITDA excl. leasing (target below 2x; 1.9x after Q2), but it rests on peak EBITDA. Equity ratio fell from 55.5% to 48.1%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Governance and dilution
      A shares with ten votes: the founders Patrik Rignell (chair) and Hans Karlander each have 6.1% of the capital but 24.6% of the votes, together about 49% of the votes for 12% of the capital. The CEO owns only about 0.4% of the shares. At year-end there were 3.39m outstanding warrants (about 6% potential dilution), and the share count has grown from 21.9m in 2021 to 53.4m (+0.8% in the latest year).
