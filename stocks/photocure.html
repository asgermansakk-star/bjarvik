    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a drug company whose profit looks better than it is. Reported figures are lifted by one-off payments; without them, operating profit is around zero. Nearly all sales come from one product, which grew only 3% in the latest quarter, and an expensive acquisition will not bring revenue until 2027. On the plus side, the company has more cash than debt, and its growth outlook was raised.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
