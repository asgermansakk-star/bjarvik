    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a business with high margins, but growth has stalled and the share is still expensive relative to earnings. Most development costs are capitalised on the balance sheet rather than hitting profit, so earnings look better than they are. The largest distributor has cut its purchases. The company has a small net cash position. Analysts' target is 51% above the price, but only two cover the share.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Only 2 analysts cover the stock (and the one forecast row on stockanalysis is based on 1 analyst), so price targets and estimates are thin. Earnings are strongly affected by capitalised development (71% of R&D expenses are capitalised), so P/E, EV/EBIT and ROIC overstate the economic earnings. It is not publicly disclosed who the largest distributor is or what share of sales it represents; distributor sales in total are approx. 28% (TTM). The status of the FDA and MolDx process in the US is only management's own account. Insider ownership of 56% at stockanalysis has not been checked against the owner list (Arnhult sphere approx. 24%). Insider trades after mid-August 2026 have not been investigated.
