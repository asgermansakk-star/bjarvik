    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −19 of −30
    • Margin
      The EBITDA margin fell to 4.0% in H1 2026 from 5.6% a year earlier, and the EBIT margin was only 1.2% (3.9%). The net result was −NOK 15.7m in H1 (EPS −0.19) vs +36m a year earlier. The company itself writes that capacity was filled with low-margin work when customers postponed investments.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −8
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose only 4.6% in Q2 (NOK 364.9m vs 348.7m), and the growth came, according to the company, from acquisitions. Without Austevoll Rørteknikk (approx. 16m per quarter) and one month of Einar Øgrey (approx. 13m), organic development was approx. −4%.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      Growth is bought: IMTAS, Austevoll Rørteknikk and Einar Øgrey in four years, partly paid for with shares. Goodwill is NOK 374m vs equity of NOK 413m, so tangible equity is negative. ROIC has fallen from 11.5% (2024) and 8.7% (2025) to 4.7% TTM, below the WACC of 6.7%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat
      Fragmented industrial services (pipes, steel, ventilation, electrical, maintenance) for smelters, fish farms, offshore and shipyards. 40% of the order book is fixed-price projects, and the company had to take low-margin orders in Q1 to keep its people employed. That suggests limited pricing power.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −3

      Bond loan of NOK 500m at 3M NIBOR + 7.75% (approx. 12%) from January 2026. NIBD is 360m (3.4x pro forma EBITDA), interest cover is 0.68, and financial items (−24.8m in H1) were almost three times EBIT (9.1m). Altman Z-score 1.75.

      −8
    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow was only NOK 7.4m in H1 2026 (before interest). After capex (12.7), leasing (10.7) and interest paid (23.2) FCF was approx. −39m. The cash of 245m comes from the bond, not from operations.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −4
    • Dilution
      The share count has risen from 74.9m (2024) to 83.0m (2025) and 89.2m after 6.0m consideration shares at NOK 3.86 for Einar Øgrey in June 2026. The earn-out of up to NOK 60m is paid half in shares, and there are 3.4m outstanding options.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −3
    • Governance
      The board gets options itself: the chairman received 100,000 in September and now has 2.1m options, and four other board members received 100,000 each. This is contrary to Norwegian corporate governance (NUES), where the board should be independent of share-based pay. Insiders own only 5.9%.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −2
    • Management change
      New CEO from October 2025 and new CFO from December 2025, and the COO retired on 31/8-2026 without the position being refilled (the CEO takes over). The entire top management has been replaced in under a year, in the middle of the integration of acquisitions.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −2
    • Customer behavior and macro
      Customers in smelters, fish farming and offshore postponed investment decisions in Q1 2026 (EBITDA 10.6m on revenue of 370m). The company writes that some customers are still hesitant, even though activity rose in Q2.
