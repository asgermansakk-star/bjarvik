    In plain words

    Clinical Laserthermia Syst. scores 49 out of 100. Revenue is 12.6m SEK against a loss of 39.5m, almost all of it comes from one partner, and cash was 3.9m against annual spending of about 30m. The share count rose 56% in the latest share issue. On the plus side, the largest order in the company's history arrived (about 23m SEK), and the partnership was extended to 2030.
