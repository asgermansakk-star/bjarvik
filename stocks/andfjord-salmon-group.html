      The flow-through facility (pools in the mountain at Kvalnes) has not yet been proven profitable at scale. Only the pilot cycle and a first harvest batch (about 367 t HOG, October 2026) have been completed, and the salmon is sold at spot price with no documented price premium.

    • Debt and liquidity
      Net debt was NOK 1.64bn at 30/6 with only NOK 36m in cash at 30/9. In October the NOK 750m bond was refinanced with a new one of NOK 1,250m at 3m NIBOR + 8.50%, so gross debt rises.       The share count rose 40.8% in a year. This is due to 19.5m new shares in H1 2026 and a directed issue at NOK 27.50 in April. The price is now NOK 24.60, below the issue price, and the build-out toward 17,000 t requires more capital.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Dispute with former main contractor
      AF Hæhre & Contur claims NOK 690.5m incl. VAT (recognised as disputed current debt, up from NOK 458.8m at the turn of the year), while Andfjord has raised a claim of over NOK 1bn. If the company loses the case, an amount of about 23% of market value must be paid.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 2026 report does not come until 17/11-2026, so the figures are based on the half-year report (30/6) and the preliminary figures as of 30/9, which the company published in connection with the bond issue. Realised prices and cost per kg from the first harvest (October) have not been published, so it is not documented that the flow-through facility is profitable. The lawsuit against AF Hæhre & Contur (the company's claim of over NOK 1bn vs disputed supplier claims of NOK 690m) is undecided, and the outcome could swing the value sharply either way. Only 3 analysts cover the stock. The full shareholder list and the covenants of the new bond have not been reviewed. Insider ownership (6.79%) is from stockanalysis. MFN lists Euronext Growth Oslo as the listing, while the company writes Euronext Oslo Børs; here the holding's trading venue has been used. Salmon is cyclical, and food production fits best in 'Consumer: food, beverages & restaurants'.
