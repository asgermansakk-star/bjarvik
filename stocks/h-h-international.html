    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Q3 2026 is reported on 10/11-2026, four days after this scoring; the figures are based on H1 2026 (11/8) and stockanalysis data last updated 11/8. The net loss in 2025 (DKK −665m) is mainly due to writedowns of DKK 612m in Germany/CWE and has not been used in the multiples; EBIT before special items has been used. The stock is marked non-cyclical according to the explicit list in Vaegte (building materials such as Rockwool and Heidelberg are not marked either), even though earnings in practice follow the housing construction cycle.
