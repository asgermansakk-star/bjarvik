    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −11 of −30
    • Profitability
      ROE (excl. AT1) was only 9.6% in Q3 and 9.7% in the first nine months of the year vs 10.5% in Q3 2025 and 8.8% for full-year 2025. It is below the owners' required return and below Instabank (12.4%), Norion (12.6%) and Morrow (10.7%); the 'adjusted' ROE of 13.3% is based on a theoretical equity without excess capital and fell from 16.8%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • Business model and moat
      Pure niche bank with unsecured consumer loans and credit cards in Norway, Finland, Sweden and Spain, largely distributed via loan brokers; management itself mentions elevated churn in broker-originated loans. Credit losses eat 50% of total income (46% the year before), so there is no real moat beyond low costs.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Gross lending grew 20% in a year to kr 10.9bn, but net interest income only 7%, because interest expenses rose 47% on more expensive deposits; credit losses rose 28%. Profit before tax fell 3% and net profit 6% in Q3 despite the growth.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −12 of −35
    • Credit quality
      Stage 3 loans make up kr 1,589m or 14.6% of gross lending (16.8% before the NPL sale in Finland) with a provisioning ratio of only 40.5%. In Sweden 22% of lending is in stage 3 and in Spain 18%, and the loss rate rose to 3.9% annualised.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital
      CET1 fell from 15.6% to 12.8% in a year (13.9% incl. the year's profit) vs a requirement of 9.0% and the bank's own target of 2.5 percentage points above the requirement (11.5%). The buffer is only about 1.3 percentage points (about NOK 110m) without the year's profit, so 20% lending growth must be financed with new AT1/Tier 2 capital (NOK 200m in September) and limits the dividend.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Execution and management
      Two CFOs have resigned in nine months: Gard Haugen in September 2025 and his successor Henrik Betsgren in June 2026, only four months after taking up the post. The bank now has an acting CFO in the middle of the move to Sweden.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Governance and related parties
      Competitor Multitude Bank (Malta) owns 29.7% – just below the mandatory bid threshold – and holds a board seat, and the bank is buying Multitude's Swedish and Finnish run-off portfolio (approx. kr 96m at 90% of book value) as a related-party transaction. Management owns little (CEO 0.4%), and a new option programme of 2m shares at kr 16.20 comes on top of remaining FPA warrants; diluted EPS (0.29) is 9% below undiluted (0.32) in Q3.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Macro and regulation
      The EU's new Consumer Credit Directive (CCD2) is to apply from 20 November 2026 with stricter creditworthiness assessment requirements and cost caps, which hits a bank that lives solely on unsecured consumer loans and credit cards; at the same time higher market rates press deposit costs.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful (negative operating cash flow is due to loan growth); valuation on P/TBV, P/B and P/E, and dividend is measured against net profit. Lea Bank AB is the former Norwegian Lea Bank ASA, which moved to Sweden and was listed on First North Premier in Stockholm 9/1-2025; stockanalysis only has multiples from 2025, and the P/B history from the Oslo period (in NOK) has not been examined. stockanalysis shows +12.3% in share count, but that is due to the merger with Lea Bank ASA; according to the balance sheet the share count rose only from 95.2 to 96.8m since 2023, so no dilution points have been given from this. stockanalysis has no forecast page (no or very thin analyst coverage), although a forward P/E of 8.65 is shown. Insider trades have not been examined (0 points). Affärsvärlden's analysis from June 2026 ('Nischbank med klen vallgrav') is behind a paywall and has not been read.
