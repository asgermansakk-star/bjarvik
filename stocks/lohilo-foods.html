    In plain words

    The score is low because profit is thin (operating margin about 3.5%), and cash flow from operations is negative despite the profit. The three largest customers account for about 65% of revenue. The shares cost about 39 times profit versus about 19 for the larger Cloetta. On the plus side, the CEO, who owns 13.5%, has bought shares, and exports grew 59.7% in the first half.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro-cap company: market cap about SEK 117m and 20 employees. No analysts, no forward estimate, no PEG and no price target on stockanalysis. The analysis is based on figures up to and including Q2 2026; the Q3 report is due 13/11-2026 (MFN calendar; stockanalysis says 20/11) and is not included. Net profit is burdened by an effective tax rate of 38%, which is mostly booked deferred tax (the tax asset is SEK 17.2m), so P/E (39) overstates the price of the underlying earnings; EV/EBIT has therefore been used as the main multiple. The share count has been fixed at 37.67m since 2023 (checked against the cash flow statement; no issue since 2023). Lease payments are not itemised, and FCF is therefore measured as operations minus investments. The board's composition and independence, the terms of the credit at Aros Kapital and any covenants have not been examined. Web search has not been used; the research is based on stockanalysis, the company's reports via MFN/Cision and the Financial Supervisory Authority's insider register.
