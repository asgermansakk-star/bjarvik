    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company reports in USD but trades in NOK (NDRs on Oslo Børs since 2026, before that Euronext Growth), and there is therefore no usable multiple history beyond the turn of 2025. Reported earnings for the last 12 months are negative due to a USD 127m impairment in 2025 and a non-cash warrant provision of USD 50m in Q2 2026, so T2 is based on forward P/E, EV/EBITDA on guidance and P/B. Lease debt (approx. USD 170m) and lease payments do not appear in stockanalysis' cash flow statement, so FCF after leases is not measured precisely. The share count has been checked against the announcements (87.76m shares after the warrant exercise), not against stockanalysis' +33.9%. Insider ownership and the largest shareholders' current stakes have not been found. Only 3–4 analysts cover the share, and the 2026 EPS estimates range from USD 0.57 to 2.82. The Q3 report comes on 18/11-2026, and an extraordinary general meeting on a buyback of up to USD 35m is held on 12/11-2026.
