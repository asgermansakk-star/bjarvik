    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −19 of −30
    • Margin
      The group's operating margin of 11% (TTM) is borrowed from the property: the prepress segment had an operating profit of DKK 5.7m on revenue of DKK 131.9m in 2025 (4.3%) and −DKK 0.2m in 2024, while Selandia Park contributed DKK 10.0m. Gross margin has improved to 27–28% from 22.2% in 2023, but the operating business itself earns almost nothing.
      filings.xbrl.org
      −6
    • ROIC and ROE
      ROIC 6.1% (TTM) and 3.9–8.4% in 2021/22–2025; ROE 7.1% after 2.8–2.9% in 2023–2024. That is close to the company's own borrowing rates (approx. 5%), so the capital creates little value.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from kr 147.0m (2021/22) to kr 136.1m (TTM). Offset, approx. 46% of prepress, is falling structurally (−2% in 2025) because of processless plates and digital printing. Flexo grew 23% in 2025, but revenue fell 10% in H1 2026 and 6.7% to kr 101.4m after nine months.
      filings.xbrl.org
      −4
    • Moat
      The company itself writes that most products are not patented, that prices of offset and flexo equipment are under pressure from competitors, and that cheap Chinese plates and developers are taking the market with quality that is 'good enough'. Most is sold as a development and production partner to large plate manufacturers, which own the customer relationship.
      filings.xbrl.org
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      No dividend since 2021/22, no buyback and an unchanged share count (1.82m). The sale of Selandia Park was attempted in 2020/21 and abandoned, was restarted after the general meeting in April 2025 and got a new broker on 1/8-2026. Capital is tied up in a property outside the core business.
      inderes.dk
      −2

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro-cap company (market cap about DKK 147m, 93 employees) with two segments: prepress equipment (offset and flexo) and the investment property Selandia Park in Ringsted, which accounted for DKK 6.7m of the DKK 11.3m profit before tax in 2025. The group's multiples are therefore a blend of a property at fair value (DKK 149m, level 3 estimate) and a low-margin operating business; T2 is also assessed on a sum-of-the-parts. No analysts, no forward estimate, no PEG and no price target on stockanalysis; P/E is also calculated on the company's guidance (profit before tax about DKK 12m for 2026). The company only publishes half-year reports with a short Q3 announcement without balance sheet and cash flow, so stockanalysis' TTM figures go to 30/6-2026. The sale of Selandia Park has been under way since 2025 (new broker 1/8-2026, expected 12–18 months) with no price or buyer; a sale could change the picture markedly. The shareholder register is dated March 2026; insider trades have only been searched in company announcements.
