    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company (Cyprus) listed on Euronext Growth Oslo; the accounts are in USD, and the ships' operations are not consolidated, so revenue, margin and ROIC on stockanalysis cover only fees, leasing income and administration. Net asset value is set equal to book equity (USD 23.2m at 30/6-2026, about USD 0.33 per share); all financial assets (USD 14.9m) are level 3 estimates made by management, and the company publishes no separate NAV. P/B 0.77 is stockanalysis' figure (implied about NOK 9.95/USD). The share count is unchanged at 71,009,348 since 2022; stockanalysis' −3.87% is an artefact and earns no points. Peers (other small Nordic shipping investment companies) have not been compared (0 points). Insider trades in 2026 have not been examined on Newsweb (0 points). No analyst coverage, no forward estimates and no guidance. The Q3 report is expected 20/11-2026. ADS Arendal's index-linked charter expired in mid-Q3 2026, and a new charter has not been announced.
