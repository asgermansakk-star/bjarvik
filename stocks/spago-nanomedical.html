    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Clinical biotech without sales: P/E, PEG, ROIC and FCF multiples are not meaningful, so T2 is assessed on price vs cash and P/B. No analyst coverage and no price targets. The board has decided to seek delisting from First North (EGM 19/11-2026); the decision requires 9/10 of the votes cast and the shares represented, and only approx. 65% have indicated yes, so it may fail. If it fails, the special situation lapses, but the financing need remains. Insider trades after the issue in June and the effect of Lindell's exemption from the mandatory bid have not been examined in detail. Customer concentration is not relevant (no customers).
