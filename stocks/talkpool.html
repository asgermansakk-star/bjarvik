    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Micro company (about SEK 120m) with no analyst coverage: no forward estimates, price targets or PEG on stockanalysis. The accounts follow Swiss OR (Swiss GAAP), where goodwill is offset against equity; stockanalysis' ROIC (21.7%) and ROE are therefore overstated and have not been used as a plus. Customer names and shares are not disclosed; the concentration has been derived from revenue by country (Haiti, Pakistan, Germany about 98%). Lease debt is small (about EUR 0.1m), so FCF after leases ≈ FCF. The chair's sale of 141,666 shares in February 2026 is probably linked to the option programme (he supplies the shares himself), but this has not been verified. The Q3 report comes on 25/11-2026 and is not included. The peer comparison (Eltel, Netel) is approximate, as TalkPool is far smaller and operates in frontier markets. Industry set to the telecom group (Software, cloud & internet) like Telia and Elisa; the business is network services.
