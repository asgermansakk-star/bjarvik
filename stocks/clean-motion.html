    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    stockanalysis uses an outdated share count (198.92m) and therefore shows market cap SEK 11.1m and P/S 3.72. After the rights issue in July and the consideration issue to the guarantor in August 2026 there are 346.6m shares, so the market cap at SEK 0.0556 is approx. SEK 19.3m; the multiples in the findings have been recalculated with the correct share count. stockanalysis has no cash flow data, so the cash flow is taken from the company's own half-year report (not reviewed by the auditors). The Q3 report (13/11-2026) had not been published on the analysis date, so cash after the issue is an estimate. No analyst coverage, no estimates and no price targets. Insiders' total ownership after the issue has not been determined (the owner list is as of 30/6, before the issue). The decisions on the EIC Accelerator (EUR 2.5m grant + EUR 5m equity) and the application to the Swedish Energy Agency are expected in Q4 and could change the picture markedly in both directions. The company produces light electric vehicles (L5e/L2e) for cities and municipalities; it is not flagged as cyclical because the loss is due to lack of scale and not the car cycle. No special situation.
