    In plain words

    The score is 59 out of 100: a small software company for digital rehabilitation, where profit margins are falling and free cash flow nearly vanishes once development spending is counted. The shares have grown pricier relative to earnings, and past acquisitions were written down heavily. On the plus side, the founder owns 25.6% and has bought shares, and two US hospital systems chose the product.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Small company (market cap about kr 171m) with accounts in EUR and thin analyst coverage; stockanalysis has no forecast page, so forward P/E 21.3 rests on very few estimates. stockanalysis' FCF (kr 41.8m) is before capitalised development of about EUR 3.1m a year; here FCF is measured after capitalised development. Net income is affected by write-downs and discontinued operations (Champion Health Plus clinics), so P/E and ROE are not usable. The competitive situation in the US (e.g. MedBridge, WebPT) and the dependence on US RTM reimbursement codes have only been examined superficially. The new EMR will only have paying customers from January 2027.
