    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −20 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen five years in a row: USD 145.5m (2021) → 134.3 → 131.3 → 102.5 → 102.2 (2025) and 84.6m TTM (−12%). Q3 2026 fell 20% to 19.3m. Paying users (MUP) fell 24%, DAU 15% and MAU 14% y/y; Jewels of Rome −39%, Sherlock −23% and Hidden City −11%. The analyst expects −17% in 2026 and −3% in 2027.
      mfn.se
      −10
    • Margin
      EBIT margin 2.2% TTM (USD 1.9m) and 2.4% in 2025 vs 16.8% in 2021. Q3 showed 9.8% (adjusted 10.6%) after the layoff of about 180 employees, but in May the company itself wrote that the savings will mostly offset declining revenue and higher UA, not lift the margin.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • ROIC
      ROIC 4.5% and ROE 5.4% vs WACC of 7.9%. The return on capital is below the cost of capital, even though almost half of equity is cash.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Moat and future segment
      The core is ageing casual titles (hidden object and match-3), which management calls 'mostly complete' and now run for long tail and cash flow, i.e. a harvesting phase. New hits are missing; two new games are in development. Bright spots: G5 Store +15.7% y/y, third-party distribution +41% q/q and a record-high gross margin of 74.1%.

    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow incl. capitalised development was −USD 1.0m in Q3 (2.1) and −USD 8,000 TTM vs 4.9m in 2025; working capital absorbed 2.5m in Q3, and severance costs came on top. The dividend was cut 75% to SEK 2 per share.

    • Platform dependence
      Most of the revenue goes through Apple App Store, Google Play and Amazon with a 30% fee; only 21% of mobile revenue is processed directly. The company itself cites dependence on strategic partners as a main risk.

      −2

      Revenue rests on a few families of games (Sherlock, Hidden City, Jewels, Mahjong Journey), all of which are falling year on year. The Jewels of Rome family is the weakest title at −39%.

      Severance costs in Q3 (USD 0.8m) were higher than the company had guided. The team is shrinking to about 450, and the company has warned that further reductions depend on the new games.
