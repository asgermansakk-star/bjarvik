    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Margin
      Adjusted operating profit fell from kr 218m (8.3% margin) in 2024 to kr 35m (0.9%) in 2025 after the takeover of the Oslo–Copenhagen route (Go Nordic Cruiseline); TTM EBIT margin is 0.7%, and H1 2026 was worse than H1 2025 (−210 vs −195m).
      corporate.gotlandsbolaget.se
      −8
    • ROIC
      ROIC has fallen from 11.8% (2024) and 8.5% (2023) to 2.3% in 2025 and 0.5% TTM; ROE is 1.35% TTM, even though half of equity has been held in cash.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue growth (+43% in 2025) was bought through Go Nordic Cruiseline; organically, passengers in the Gotland traffic fell 5.2% in H1 2026, and the rise in Q2 revenue came mainly from sales of FuelEU Maritime credits rather than operations.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The cash is being spent on a passive minority stake: Gotlandsbolaget pays with its own funds 54% of about SEK 6bn for 30% of the EQT-controlled Nordic Ferry Infrastructure (about SEK 3.2bn, more than the entire cash at 30/6), at the same time as the newbuild Gotland Horizon X; the dividend has been set at SEK 0 (SEK 20.30 before the split).
      corporate.gotlandsbolaget.se
      −5

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −17 of −35
    • Cash flow
      FCF was −DKK 309m in 2025 and −150m TTM, because capex (559–743m) for Gotland Horizon X and the rebuild of Nordic Pearl/Crown far exceeds operating cash flow of approx. 410–430m.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Funding need
      Cash was kr 2,934m at 30/6 before the NFI payment of about kr 3.2bn in July and remaining payments on Horizon X; the company has therefore signed a credit facility of up to kr 1,200m and goes from a large net cash position to a thin or negative liquidity buffer.
      corporate.gotlandsbolaget.se
      −4
    • Customer behaviour and volumes
      Destination Gotland: passengers −5.9% in Q2 and −1.4% in peak season, and more are opting out of the cheap departures; Go Nordic Cruiseline: guests −4.8% in peak season, onboard sales disappoint, and management is being replaced in the autumn.
      corporate.gotlandsbolaget.se
      −3
    • Concentration
      The core business is Trafikverket's tendered Gotland contract (approx. SEK 2.1bn of SEK 3.7bn in revenue), where the state owns the ferries and sets the framework; earnings depend on one public customer and one tender.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2
    • Governance and liquidity
      The Nilsson family (Trojaborg AB et al.) has 61% of capital and 76% of votes via A shares, and CEO Björn Nilsson sits on the board; free float is small and the A share trades only approx. 96 units a day on First North.
      corporate.gotlandsbolaget.se
      −3
    • Tax case
      Förvaltningsrätten has ruled against Gotland Capital Management on the settlement of foreign tax; the company has appealed and refers to guarantees from the seller of Opfutrade AB.
      corporate.gotlandsbolaget.se
      −1
