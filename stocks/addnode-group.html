    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −16 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Organic growth was −4% in the first nine months of the year and −11% in Q2 (−5% adjusted for early Autodesk renewals); all growth comes from acquisitions. Q3's organic +3% (Design Management +8%) is compared with a quarter that lacked about kr 80m in revenue. Customers are moving from three- to one-year Autodesk agreements, and the PLM division fell 3% organically owing to the weak German car industry.
      storage.mfn.se
      −5
    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC has fallen from 17.6% (2022) and 12.8% (2024) to 8.4% (TTM), and the company's own ROCE has fallen from 16.6% to 12.6% in a year. Goodwill and intangible assets have grown to kr 6.1bn vs equity of kr 3.1bn after acquisitions of kr 1.4bn in 2025 and 0.95bn (TTM), financed with loans.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Moat and partner dependence
      Design Management (approx. 40% of revenue) is the world's largest Autodesk partner. Autodesk now invoices the end customer itself and has changed the partners' compensation model, which alone cost approx. kr 20m in revenue in Q2. It is the supplier, not Addnode, that sets the terms for a large part of the business.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Margin
      The EBITA margin is 16.2% (TTM), below its own target of at least 17%, and the operating margin (IFRS) is 10.3%. Design Management had an EBITA margin of only 5.9% in Q2 and got a savings programme of kr 100m. Q3's 17.4% rests partly on an easy comparison base.
      storage.mfn.se

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Addnode sells both its own software and partner software (Autodesk, Dassault Systèmes). Autodesk's agency model from 2024 makes the revenue history on stockanalysis non-comparable (−25% in 2025), and stockanalysis' gross margin (28%) does not match the company's own (77%). Q3 2025, which Q3 2026 is compared with, was about kr 70m weaker on EBITA because Autodesk renewals were moved forward to Q2 2025. FCF is calculated after leasing (about 103m) and purchases of intangible assets/capitalised development (about 261m, TTM) based on stockanalysis. Net debt/EBITDA depends on the definition: the company does not disclose the ratio itself. Only 4 analysts cover the stock. Insider trades have only been checked in Finansinspektionen's register under the issuer name. Affärsvärlden's analysis of the AI threat to the company has not been read (paywall), and the AI risk for licence sales and consulting services has not been given separate points. Customer concentration in the partner agreements (Autodesk's share of revenue) has not been quantified.
