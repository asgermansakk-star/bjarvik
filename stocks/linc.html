    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company in Nordic life science: assessed on price to net asset value (NAV of kr 80.8 per share at 31/10-2026, kr 79.7 at 30/9). Stockanalysis' 'revenue' is fair-value changes, so P/E (55.6), ROIC, margins and FCF (−kr 272m, which is net investments and moving cash into ETFs) are meaningless; there is no analyst coverage, no forward estimate and PEG is n/a. 27% of NAV is 'övriga tillgångar', of which kr 978m is in US index ETFs in a capital insurance, kr 169m in loans to portfolio companies and only kr 66m in cash. MedCap (35% of NAV) and Stille (11%) are valued at market price, so NAV depends on two fully priced medtech stocks. The main shareholder's exact ownership share (Bengt Julander, chairman) has not been verified; insiders own 60.5% according to stockanalysis. NAV return versus a benchmark index and Karolinska Development's discount have not been examined. Industry set to Medicine & medtech as the closest, as for Flerie.
