    In plain words

    The score is 79 out of 100: Bjarvik sees a discount retail chain with decent earnings, but little to set it apart from rivals. The share costs more than competitor Europris, sales in existing stores grow only about 2%, and foreign markets earn almost nothing. Several board members and managers have bought shares. Watch the new CEO and the summer season, on which earnings depend.

    A strong score: the model found few serious problems, but analysts see limited upside from here.
