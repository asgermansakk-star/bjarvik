The accounts are reported in USD while the stock trades in NOK; USDNOK of about 9.9 has been used. Stockanalysis shows trailing P/E 1.5 and FCF yield 94%, but this rests on an accounting gain of USD 25.7m on the sale of Argentina and on working-capital movements; neither is used as a basis. No analysts cover the stock, and there are no forward estimates. Stockanalysis' insider ownership of 53.5% could not be confirmed because the top-20 list is dominated by nominee accounts; it therefore gets 0 points.
