    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −26 of −30
    • Margin
      No revenue. The operating loss is SEK 42m (TTM) vs 36m in 2025, and ROE is −176%. The company has never had meaningful income.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      The evidence comes from the open-label phase 2a study NEXUS with only 12 patients (9 RVO, 3 DR) and no control group. The treatment has been changed from five to three days and to being given after an anti-VEGF injection, because some RVO patients still needed one or two anti-VEGF injections.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Moat
      Vanexin (Annexin A5) is first-in-class, but the RVO market is dominated by established anti-VEGF drugs. The company now positions vanexin as an add-on to anti-VEGF rather than an alternative, so the product becomes dependent on the standard of care.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The company was founded in 2014 and has an accumulated loss of SEK 405m with no licence agreement. In spring 2026 a bridge loan of SEK 4m from the main shareholders at 10% interest was needed to reach the issue.
      mfn.se
      −4

    • Funding
      Cash was SEK 14.4m at 30/9 with a burn of about 10m per quarter, and according to the company the money lasts only to the end of February 2027. That is before topline data and the FDA meeting, so a new issue will probably have to be raised from a weak negotiating position at a market cap of SEK 78m.

    • Dilution
      The share count (adjusted for the 1:100 consolidation in 2025) has risen from 1.37m in 2021 to 8.72m, i.e. more than six times. It has risen 20% in a year, of which 19% from the rights issue in July 2026. There are issues in the cash flow every year 2021–2026.

    • Clinical and regulatory risk
      The phase 2b design depends on a Type C meeting with the FDA in early 2027, and the DR track is built on only three patients. The company must itself choose the next step in DR after the combined data, so all the value rests on a few uncontrolled data points.
      mfn.se
      −4

      The business model is out-licensing, but there is still no agreement after several years of partner dialogues. According to the managing director, an agreement only becomes more likely when topline data and the FDA's feedback are available, i.e. in 2027 at the earliest.
