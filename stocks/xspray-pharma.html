      The products are reformulations (505(b)(2)) of Sprycel and Tasigna, where the company itself states that the target market includes the original drugs and their generic copies. Dasynoc and Nilopki are to be sold at a premium over cheap generic dasatinib and nilotinib on arguments of lower dose, fewer interactions and less food effect.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    A company with no product sales: the value depends on FDA approval of Dasynoc and Nilopki and on NerPharMa's cGMP status, where the FDA began a new inspection on 5 November 2026. The announced rights issue (approx. kr 90m + up to kr 72m at kr 9.50, subscription period 17/11–1/12 2026) has not yet been completed, so share count and cash will change markedly. Only one analyst (Redeye) follows the stock, and stockanalysis has no estimates or price targets, so PEG and upside are not a basis. Peers have not been compared on multiples, and payers' willingness to pay above generic dasatinib and nilotinib has not been examined. 89% of book equity consists of capitalised development costs.
