    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is in wind-down: after selling the projects in Guatemala, El Salvador and Colombia in July–August 2026 and a capital distribution of USD 1.32 (NOK 12.19) per share on 1/9-2026, it owns only the Los Santos solar park (15.8 MWp, Mexico) and approx. 7% of the US microgrid developer Enernet Global. The price fall of −83% over 52 weeks is mainly due to the distribution and not a collapse. The stock trades on Euronext Growth Oslo (not the main list). The board has called an EGM on 8/12-2026 on delisting, a move to Euronext NOTC and conversion to a Dutch BV; as the vote is undecided, saerlig_situation is not set, but if the proposal is adopted, the stock should be flagged as a special situation. stockanalysis' TTM figures (to 30/9-2026) include the sold projects and are therefore only indicative; P/E, forward P/E and PEG do not exist, and there is no forecast page and, according to the company, no analyst coverage (a shareholder mentioned in the webcast a price target of NOK 3.20 from Fearnley, which has not been verified), so upside and revision points are 0. The expected net value of Los Santos after project debt, tax and costs has not been disclosed, and management warns of likely write-downs at year-end; the full shareholder list (beyond MPC Capital's 28.4%) has not been reviewed, and the appendices to the EGM notice have not been read.
