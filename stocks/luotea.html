    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Luotea is the former Lassila & Tikanoja (L&T), where the circular economy business was spun off as the new L&T on 31 Dec 2025; Luotea kept facility services. Stockanalysis' price history (-83% in a year) and TTM figures (net income EUR 149.7m, FCF 43.3m, P/FCF 1.6) are distorted by the spin-off and must not be used; there is no comparable multiple history before 2026. EPS 2026e (EUR 0.15) is from stockanalysis' consensus (2 analysts) and may not have been updated after the profit warning on 11 Sep 2026. Industry: facility services (cleaning, property management, technical services) is not on the list; 'Construction & materials' is the closest.
