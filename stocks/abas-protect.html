    In plain words

    The model sees a small company with weak profit and a thin financial position. Revenue fell three years in a row, debt is large relative to operating profit, and cash is almost gone. The auditor has issued a remark, and the stock rarely trades. On the other hand, the second quarter turned around, with revenue up 24% and a small profit. Next report: 19 November.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.
