The newest and best-let asset (the NKT building in Nättraby) has been sold.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −19 of −35
    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio has fallen to 43.6%, but interest cover is only 1.6× over 12 months (2.3× in H1), and net debt/EBITDA is 9.7×. 54% of loans (kr 266.5m) fall due within a year, most have three-month fixed interest, and only about 28% is swapped. The auditor already pointed out the short maturity structure in the 2022 accounts.
      cdn.bequoted.com
      −5
    • Governance and controlling owner
      EHF Invest (100% owned by board member Erik Hemberg) owns 52.4% after a mandatory offer of SEK 10, which BDO judged not fair to shareholders. Three of four board members had a conflict of interest. The CEO, CFO and property manager are employed by EHF and are billed via a consultancy fee to EHF, and the company has had three CEOs in 14 months (Derk, Gustafsson, Blad).
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Tenant concentration
      The five largest tenants account for approx. 40% of income, and the company itself describes that the loss of a single tenant would have a large financial effect.
      cdn.bequoted.com
      −3

      Average daily trading is only about 470 shares (under kr 5,000), free float is about 7.5m shares, and the three largest owners (EHF, Svea Bank, Axel Lindgren) hold 85% together. The stock is effectively almost impossible to trade in larger amounts.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

      The economic occupancy rate fell to 85.2% (89.0), and the rental value per sqm fell to kr 693 (728). Several properties are only partly let: Torsås 28%, Smålandsstenar 38%, Anderstorp 48% and Värnamo 63%.
