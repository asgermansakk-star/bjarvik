    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Scored on the B share (SCA.B) in Stockholm. The forest and paper industry has no industry of its own on the list; placed under 'Commodities & uranium' like UPM and Stora Enso, and marked cyclical (pulp, wood products and kraftliner). Stockanalysis' revenue (23.3bn TTM) and operating result (1.1bn) do not match SCA's own figures (net sales of 15.1bn for 9 months; EBITDA incl. change in value of biological assets 4.0bn), because the change in value of the forest is treated differently; the findings use SCA's own figures where possible. Analysts' estimates and price targets on stockanalysis were last updated 9/8-2026, before the Q3 report. The value of the forest is based on transaction prices (3-year average) and is sensitive to the forest market. Holmen has not been compared directly (stockanalysis rejected the lookup), and customer concentration has not been examined separately (broad export to Europe, Asia and the US). Industrivärden is the main owner through A shares.
