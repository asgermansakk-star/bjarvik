    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −9 of −35
    • Quality of order intake
      Management estimates that about SEK 130m in Q1 and about SEK 70m in Q3 were orders pulled forward out of fear of longer delivery times and price increases. The organic order growth of 22% in Q3 is therefore overstated by about 8 percentage points, and a gap may come later.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Margin pressure and guidance
      Gross margin fell to 62.7% (64.1) on rising component prices (memory several hundred percent, now also circuit boards and passive components) and broader tariffs between the US, EU and Canada. The price increases only take effect from 1 January 2027, and management expects pressure in Q4 and possibly into 2027, while consensus expects 26% EPS growth in 2027. The stock fell 7.6% on the report day.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Macro and cycle
      Growth is driven by factory automation, the semiconductor industry and data centres; management itself cites rising component prices, new trade tensions and the Middle East as uncertainties. The 2024 downturn, with destocking at customers, shows how cyclical demand is.

      −2
