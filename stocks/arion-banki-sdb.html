    • Credit quality and concentration
      Impairments rose 51% to ISK 2.0bn in 9M 2026; the NPL ratio has risen with interest rates to 2.5%, and non-defaulted forbearance loans have risen to 2.5% of loans. Construction loans of ISK 127bn (approx. 9% of loans) are heavy in accommodation and tourism.

    • Regulation and tax
      The government wants to raise the bank levy from 0.145% to 0.254% of liabilities with retroactive effect for 2026; this costs approx. ISK 1.7bn a year (approx. 5–6% of net profit) on top of taxation that is already higher than in neighbouring countries.

      The bank assesses the inflation outlook as worsened and expects unchanged policy rates next year; persistently high real interest rates hit some customers, and lower rates later will squeeze the net interest margin (3.6%). The Icelandic economy is small and tourism-dependent.
