    • Capital allocation and strategy (offshore wind)

The target of at least 1.4bn in total operating profit 2025–2027 was withdrawn. In July 2026 the government rejected Västvind and Najaderna, where Västvind has 48m capitalised. Equity per share fell from SEK 64.67 to 51.54 in a year.

      FCF of 898m in the last 12 months comes almost solely from released inventory of 2.1bn (sold projects). Ongoing operations burn cash: operating cash flow was −222m in Q2 2026 and negative every year from 2021 to 2024 (−97 to −1,796m).

    • Bond terms restrict distributions
      The terms of the 2025/2029 green bond prevented dividends and buybacks in 2026 because 2025 produced a loss, despite net cash. The dividend for 2025 was therefore 0 (SEK 2.25 the year before). Buybacks require amended bond terms, and the adopted programme is only 21,900 shares.

    • Market and execution
      The company itself describes a hesitant market. Uncertainty after the conflict in the Persian Gulf may cause customers to postpone investment decisions, so sales take longer, and no projects were sold in Q2. New PPAs are often a prerequisite for selling ready-to-build projects.

      Each year rests on a few buyers and single transactions, e.g. Mirova (88 MW), Latvenergo (Pienava) and a US IPP (Roccasecca, SEK 605m). The order book is SEK 510m. A postponed sale can turn a year from profit to loss.
