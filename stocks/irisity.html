    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (approx. SEK 102m) with low trading (approx. 210,000 shares, approx. SEK 55,000 a day) and a free float of approx. 30%. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates (stockanalysis' forecast page returns 404), so PEG is n/a. Stockhorn Capital's mandatory bid of SEK 0.132 (December 2025) has been completed and was declared unconditional; there is no pending offer, so special situation is not set. The Q3 report is due 17/11-2026 and is not included. The company reports under K3 with goodwill amortisation, so stockanalysis' gross margin (−20%) and operating margin contain large amortisation; the company's own gross margin is 86%. Equity consists almost only of intangible assets, so P/B 0.44 and P/TBV 0.50 on stockanalysis overstate the asset value. Insider trades in 2026 (beyond Stockhorn's purchase) and customer concentration on individual customers have not been examined (0 points). The peer comparison is based only on Precise Biometrics.
