    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Helsinki listing (KEMPOWR). Forward P/E 95 is based on 2026 EPS close to zero, so the multiple is very sensitive. Inderes writes that revenue guidance has been lowered to 10–25% growth (from 10–30%); the company's own announcement words it differently, so the figure should be checked in the Q3 report 4 Nov. 2026. Free float is only about 20m of 55m shares (large main shareholder), but the ownership structure has not been investigated further. The price fall of about 30% since August is not tied to a single news item in September/October.
