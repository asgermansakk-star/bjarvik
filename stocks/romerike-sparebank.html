    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share, and is scored as a share. After the conversion of primary capital in August 2026 there are 7,942,034 certificates (previously 2,836,270), and the ownership fraction has risen from 16% to 45%; stockanalysis still uses 2.84m shares, so market cap, EPS 4.92, P/E 31.4, P/B 0.18 and FCF figures are wrong and have not been used. T2 is based on the bank's own figures per equity certificate (book value NOK 136.87, earnings NOK 7.73 for Q1–Q3). Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. Special situation: on 29/10-2026 the boards approved a merger plan in which Romerike is merged into Skue Sparebank at approx. 0.446 Skue certificates per ROMER certificate; the plan must be approved by the general meeting and representative council on 8/12-2026 and by Finanstilsynet and Konkurransetilsynet, and completion is expected approx. 1/9-2027. The price therefore follows Skue's price. Insider ownership has not been examined; there have been no reportable insider trades on Newsweb since June 2025. No analyst coverage (no forecast page on stockanalysis). Peers are Norwegian savings banks scored the same day.
