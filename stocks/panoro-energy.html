    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company was transformed in 2026 by two acquisitions: 40.375% of Block G (Equatorial Guinea) from Kosmos, completed 17/6, and 9.09% of CI-27 (Ivory Coast) from DNO, completed 14/9. Under the rule on pro forma for 12 months, the company's pro forma figures for production and EBITDA are used, and stockanalysis' forward history is background only. The IFRS figures are distorted by lifting timing (1.17m barrels of unsold inventory as of 30/9) and hedging losses. The Q3 accounts are due 18/11, and the consequences of the rig accident at Dussafu have not yet been quantified. stockanalysis shows 132.95m shares, but the registered figure is 140.44m after the shares to DNO. Only 5–6 analysts cover the stock. No open insider purchases or sales have been found in 2026; the criterion is therefore 0. Customer concentration (few buyers of the oil and one gas buyer in Abidjan) has not been examined further.
