    • Costs
      Operating costs were approx. kr 28.9m in 2025 (of which salaries kr 22.8m for 3 employees) and approx. kr 12m in H1 2026, equal to approx. 6–7% of NAV a year. That is very high for a holding company and eats a large part of the expected return.
      northenergy.no

    • Portfolio quality and concentration
      Approx. 69% of investments (kr 248.1m of 360.4m at 30/6) is 15.5% of Reach Subsea, an offshore service company with negative ROE (−6.3%) and ROIC of 0.5%. The rest is WCS (early-stage floating offshore wind), Petrofac bonds and a mixed basket of listed shares.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The company has drawn kr 96m on the credit facility (kr 46.6m a year earlier) and bought listed shares such as Fugro, Wolters Kluwer, Thomson Reuters and Stellantis to 'optimise the return on cash', while the dividend (kr 15.2m) and operations are paid for without recurring income. It is leveraged stock picking outside the core strategy as an industrial investment company.
