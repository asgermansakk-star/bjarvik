    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 5 findings    −11 of −30
    • Margin
      The car business, which accounts for about 80% of revenue, had a margin of only 1.2% in Q3. Its return on capital excluding IFRS 16 was 4.4% (R12) vs the company's own target of 8%. The service business's margin of 12.0% is also below the target of 14%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • ROIC
      ROIC is 7.0% and the operating margin 3.4% (TTM) vs 15.2% and 5.7% in FY2021 and 11.2% and 4.9% in FY2022. The return has risen from the low of 6.3% in FY2024 but is only slightly above WACC of 5.3%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Quality of growth
      Organic revenue fell 1% in the first nine months and rose 1% in Q3, while organic growth in service was only 2.9% in Q3. The lift in operating earnings (+17% in 9M) is mainly due to an efficiency programme with kr 150m in annual savings, not growth.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Future segment
      The dealer role is under pressure from new sales models: the annual report warns that manufacturers may move fully or partly to agency models or their own sales channels. Electric cars require a different type of service, and new players in fossil-free cars and car sharing want into the market. XPENG already accounted for 8% of new car deliveries in 2025.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The Kia agreement from May adds approx. kr 2bn in revenue in the first full year, but with an operating margin of only approx. 2.5%. Growth thus adds low-margin volume, while ROIC has fallen from 15% to 7% since 2021.
      storage.mfn.se
      −1

    • Debt
      Net debt excluding IFRS 16 is DKK 2.2bn (1.2x EBITDA). Including leasing it is DKK 7.6bn (2.7x EBITDA). The equity ratio is 24%, and stockanalysis shows interest cover of 3.5.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Cash flow
      Operating cash flow was kr 1,045m in the first nine months vs kr 1,433m (kr 1,130m excluding property sales last year). Purchases of leased cars rose to kr 1,702m from kr 940m. R12 operating cash flow (kr 1,720m) minus lease repayments (kr 799m) gives approx. kr 0.92bn, which only just covers dividends and buybacks (kr 650m) with a small margin.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Brand dependence
      BMW (30%), Volvo (23%) and Toyota (13%) accounted for two thirds of new car deliveries in 2025. A general agent can unilaterally withdraw the sales authorisation or change the terms, which the annual report itself calls a material risk.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Customer behaviour and prices
      Demand for used cars is still somewhat weak. In Norway there is intense price competition on new cars, and used-car prices are squeezed by a large supply. Earnings in Western Europe fell in Q3.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The TTM figures run through Q3 2026 (30 Sep 2026). stockanalysis' FCF after leasing (−kr 163m) deducts purchases of leasing cars without offsetting the sale of them (kr 1,076m); here FCF is calculated as Bilia's operating cash flow (R12 kr 1,720m) minus lease repayments (kr 799m), i.e. approx. kr 0.92bn, and that is an own calculation. The debt of kr 10.8bn at stockanalysis includes kr 7.6bn in lease liabilities (IFRS 16). Only 4 analysts cover the stock, and both price targets and EPS estimates were last updated on 6 May 2026, so forward P/E and PEG are based on outdated estimates. Peers are US and UK retailers with different accounting models (floor plan financing), so the comparison is rough. Not examined in detail: credit rating (not publicly available), short interest (n/a), and how much of the sales takes place under agency models at the individual brands. Industry: car dealer placed under 'Cars & car parts' (closest, like Sonic Automotive); not marked cyclical, as dealers are not on the list of cyclical industries, and the service business carries 73% of operating earnings. Q4 report expected in February 2027.
