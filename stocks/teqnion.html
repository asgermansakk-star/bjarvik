    In plain words

    Score 75 out of 100. Teqnion buys small niche companies. The share is cheaper than in recent years, but growth was bought: excluding acquisitions, sales fell 3% last quarter. Debt has risen and an Irish subsidiary is in temporary liquidation. On the plus side, cash flow exceeds profit and insiders own 18.9%. No analysts cover the share, and trading is thin.
