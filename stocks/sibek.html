    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −6 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 5.9% in Q1 2026/27 (May–July) to SEK 45.9m, and EBIT fell 14.8% to 9.0m. Customers have deferred projects that were to be put into operation, and activity in May was low. TTM growth has thus slowed to 4.5% from 14.1% in 2025/26, and EPS fell to SEK 0.71 (0.82).
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat and scale
      With about 80 employees, the company is a small niche consultancy in Swedish railway signalling. Trafikverket's authorisations are a real barrier to entry, but growth is limited by how many authorised senior consultants the company can recruit. The company competes with Sweco, AFRY, WSP, Systra, Norconsult and Rejlers.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Future segment
      The business is built on invoiced hours, and Sweden is the only market: expansion to neighbouring countries is considered only a long-term possibility, because the rules and authorisations differ. The ERTMS rollout and Trafikverket's maintenance backlog provide a tailwind, but the pace is set by state budgets.
      storage.mfn.se

    • Customer concentration
      Trafikverket is by far the largest customer, and Trafikförvaltningen (SL) in Region Stockholm is the second largest; the company itself describes its customer base as 'few end customers, but many orderers'. Demand depends on government budgets and political decisions, and Trafikverket decides the timing of commissioning, as Q1's postponed projects showed.

    • Execution and order backlog
      The whole fall in Q1 came from fewer subcontracted consultants bought in, and the CEO writes that several large design assignments are in the final phase, so 'visibility is shorter than usual'. There is no numerical guidance; the financial target is 10% growth a year over an economic cycle.

      FCF was SEK 16.5m in 2025/26 vs net income of 24.7m, because receivables tied up 10.3m. The dividend of SEK 2.20 (approx. 22.3m, of which SEK 0.50 extra) equals 116% of FCF (TTM 19.2m) and 86% of earnings, so it eats into cash.

      Four founders (Aner Teknik & Konsult, DalaInfrateknik, Angarn Signal & Ekonomi and ME Signalteknik) each own 21.5%, and the five largest together own 87.9%.     Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (market cap about SEK 461m) on First North with only about 12% free float and average turnover of about 2,600 shares a day, so the price can swing sharply on small trades. No analyst coverage and no forward estimate on stockanalysis, so PEG and upside cannot be calculated from estimates. The price history only goes back to the listing in November 2023, so the multiples are compared with less than three years of history. The company does not disclose Trafikverket's share of revenue in figures. The financial year runs from May to April, and commissioning is strongly seasonal (strong Q1-Q2, weak Q3), so individual quarters say little. The Q2 report (August-October) comes on 11/12-2026. Insider trades have been checked in Finansinspektionen's register: no trades since a minor sale in October 2024.
