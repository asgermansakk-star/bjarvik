    In plain words

    The model sees a football club that loses money and is expensive relative to revenue and to other clubs. It has run a loss two years in a row, even as champion, and expects another loss in 2026/27. Earnings depend on player sales, and new shares were issued. On the plus side, it has 105m DKK in cash after debt. Watch the move to a new stadium in 2027.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose 22% to kr 231m, but driven by the championship: TV and season tickets kr 37m → 54m and record merchandise sales kr 15m → 28m. At the same time, staff costs, external costs and depreciation rose, so the result is still negative – bigger, not stronger.

    • Earnings model
      Earnings stand and fall with player sales: 2023/24 gave kr 60m in net profit on big transfers, while 2024/25 and 2025/26 gave losses. Guidance for 2026/27 includes no significant player sales and therefore gives a loss.

      Danish champion in 2025/26, but number 8 of 12 in the Superliga after 13 rounds in 2026/27 (2 wins, 6 draws, 5 defeats) and without a win in the Conference League. TV money, UEFA prize money and sponsorships rest on staying near the top.
