    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    HAV Group is listed on Euronext Growth Oslo and reports under Norwegian GAAP (NGAAP), so leasing sits in operations, and EBITDA on stockanalysis (NOK 30m TTM) differs from the company's own (about NOK 46m TTM). Cash of NOK 237m is adjusted for customer prepayments of NOK 391m in T2 and T3; the unadjusted net cash therefore gives no plus. stockanalysis shows +28% in share count, but the balance sheet shows an unchanged 35.0m shares since 2024 and no issue in the cash flow, so no deduction has been given for dilution. The latest report is Q2 2026; Q3 comes 20/11-2026. Since 30/4-2026 the company has had an open strategic review with SB1 Markets as adviser; there is no bid, and it is not a special situation. No analysts and no forward estimates on stockanalysis, so PEG and upside are without basis. Customer concentration on individual customers has not been investigated (only the segment split of the order book). Cyclical is set to false: order intake follows the newbuild cycle in aquaculture, offshore and ferries, but not a price or commodity cycle as a rule. Analysis framework, not investment advice.
