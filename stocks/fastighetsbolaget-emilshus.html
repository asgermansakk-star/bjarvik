    • ROIC and returns
      ROIC is 4.6% vs WACC of 6.75% and has been 2.6-5.2% since 2021. Return on equity is 11% (LTM) and approx. 10% on average over five years vs the company's own target of 15%, and the direct return on the properties fell to 6.5% from 6.7% a year ago.

    • Quality of growth
      Rental income rose 29% in 9M, but only 1% in the comparable portfolio, and net operating income in the comparable portfolio was unchanged. Net letting was −SEK 10m in 9M (terminations 47m and bankruptcies 4m). Growth in förvaltningsresultat per common share (+15%) comes from acquisitions of SEK 1.9bn in 9M, and earnings capacity per common share rose only 9% in a year (3.16 to 3.44).

      The acquisitions are financed with more debt and preference shares: the number of preference shares has more than doubled from 20.6m at the end of 2024 to 42m, most recently 12m shares at kr 30 in June. The preference dividend of kr 2 costs approx. 6.7% of the issue price, above the properties' direct yield of 6.5%, and takes approx. kr 84m of the förvaltningsresultat a year. Ordinary shares grew 10.6% through a directed issue in 2025.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: the valuation is based on price vs net asset value (NAV SEK 38.74 per common share after preference capital) and earnings capacity per common share (SEK 3.44 at 1/10-2026). stockanalysis' market cap, P/B (1.17) and share count (+11.15%) mix in the preference shares and are not used; the common shares rose only from 126.15 to 126.24m over a year (after a directed issue of 12m common shares in 2025), while the preference shares went from 30 to 42m. stockanalysis' insider ownership of 0.07% is wrong: Aptare Holding (owned by CEO Jakob Fyrberg, deputy CEO Pierre Folkesson and Tomas Carlström) owns 12.7% of the capital. EPS and P/E contain value adjustments of properties and derivatives. Insider trades have only been reviewed from November 2025 in FI's register. Credit rating and the full property valuation (Newsec) have not been investigated. Only 4 analysts cover the stock, and liquidity in the B share is low (approx. 44,000 shares a day).
