    In plain words

    The score is middling: a Swedish aluminium producer with very low profit margins and falling volumes. Growth comes only from dearer metal. Three chief executives in eight months and a failed expansion in Poland weigh on it. The price looks low, but part of the profit is gains on metal prices. On the plus side, debt is low and no customer exceeds 10% of sales.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    ProfilGruppen (Åseda) extrudes aluminium profiles for construction, vehicles and industry. 'Construction & materials' has been chosen as the closest industry. The stock is not flagged as cyclical: the metal price is passed on to customers, and earnings have not followed the aluminium price. Metal price gains on inventory (kr 3.4 of EPS in 9M 2026) are, however, an inventory-cycle element. Since 2025 the company uses its own APM figures with depreciation at replacement value. They show far lower profitability than the IFRS figures at stockanalysis (ROE 18%, ROIC 16%), and the assessment is based on APM. The company itself writes that the split into operating and metal price results is based on estimates. stockanalysis has no analysts or forward estimates (forecast gives 404), and the company gives no forecast. Peers (Gränges, Hydro Extrusions/Hydro Building Systems, Alumeco) have not been compared on multiples. Short interest and share count change (n/a at stockanalysis) have not been examined; there is no known dilution. Liquidity is very low: about 2,300 shares a day, and free float is 2.2m shares.
