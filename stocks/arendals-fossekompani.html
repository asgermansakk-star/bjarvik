    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a Norwegian investment company with a loss after tax and low growth in its core businesses. There have been repeated write-downs, the dividend is not covered by free cash, and the price is up 41% in a year. In its favour, an outside investor valued Volue at about 1.5bn euros in July. Three owners control 79%, and earnings rest on hydropower and power prices.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Industrial investment company that does not publish a net asset value (NAV). The price is therefore assessed on P/B vs its own history and a rough sum-of-parts: the Volue stake is set at the transaction value from the TA Associates deal (about EUR 520m), and Tekna at the market value on 30/6. The value of hydropower, ENRX and NSSLGlobal is an estimate, not a statement. Volue is accounted for under the equity method, so P/B understates the real assets. Marked cyclical because AFK Vannkraft (spot-sold power in NO2) accounted for about 85% of the group's operating profit in H1 2026, even though most of the value lies in Volue. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404) and no P/E estimates, so upside and revision points are probably missing, and PEG shows as n/a. The share count decline of −18.4% at stockanalysis is a data error: the share count is unchanged at about 54.98m, and no treasury shares have been bought back according to the cash flow statement. Insider trades in 2026 have not been examined. The Q3 report comes on 10/11-2026 and is not included. Volue's bid for Energy One is indicative and non-binding.
