    In plain words

    Score 75 out of 100. Orders and operating profit are up, but the share is pricier than in its own history. The return on invested money only just covers the cost of capital, and cash flow is weaker than it looks. On the plus side, orders are up 26% in nine months and the dividend has risen three years running. Watch pricier components and a shrinking order book.
