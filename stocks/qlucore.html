    In plain words

    A score of 42 out of 100 means the model sees major challenges. Sales fell 34% last financial year, partly because free tools compete, and the company is far from profit. The share count has grown more than tenfold and cash is only 6.1m SEK. On the plus side, the board owns about 40% of the shares and the operating loss has almost halved. The CEO is leaving.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −28 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Net revenue fell 34% to SEK 6.7m in 2025/26 (Q4 −44%). The company itself explains it by competition from free open-source tools (R and Python), savings at customers and US research cuts. The core product Omics Explorer is thus losing ground.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Margin
      Adjusted EBITDA of −SEK 12.9m on 6.7m in revenue and net income of −36.5m in 2025/26. Q1 2026/27 was better, but still with adjusted EBITDA of −2.1m on 1.7m in sales. The company is far from break-even.
      mfn.se
      −10
    • Capital allocation
      After several years of capitalising development (SEK 22m in 2023/24), SEK 19.5m of intangible assets was written down in October 2025. The cloud initiative was closed, the lung cancer test was postponed, and 30% of the diagnostics platform was written down.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Future segment
      The diagnostics (four RNA-seq-based tests) are used by only two Nordic hospitals, and customers are reluctant about RNA sequencing. The new growth plan is built on reselling BioTuring's software in Europe. That is a reseller layer with lower margin, not a moat of its own.
      mfn.se
      −4

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −24 of −35
    • Dilution
      The share count rose from 4.03 to 42.9m (+965%) in the rights issue in 2025 at SEK 0.50 plus an underwriter issue of 15.5%. The price of 0.30 is 40% below the issue price. In addition there are warrants with 2.4% dilution.
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Funding need
      Cash was SEK 6.1m in July 2026, and Q1's positive cash flow was due to an EU instalment of approx. SEK 5m. The total EU grant is EUR 2.5m in four instalments, and three have been paid. The company itself says operations are financed by issues, grants and loans, and the general meeting in September 2026 authorised new issues.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Execution and management
      The long-serving CEO Carl-Johan Ivarsson is leaving, and a new one is expected in 3–6 months (announced 30/9-2026). This comes at the same time as repeated rounds of layoffs (12 FTEs vs 18), so strategy and management are in upheaval.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Customer behaviour
      The Q1 improvement is partly due to an existing industrial customer's renewal being pushed from the previous quarter. Customers are postponing licence renewals, and multi-year licences are recognised in one go, so revenue fluctuates strongly with individual customers.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Liquidity in the stock
      The liquidity guarantee was terminated in November 2025, and on average about 8,150 shares are traded per day (about SEK 2,500). Free float is only about 13m shares, because the board and major shareholders own most of them.
