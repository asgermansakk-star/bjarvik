    In plain words

    The score is 58 out of 100: an investment company made up mostly of cash and securities. The main drags are a value per share that has fallen in recent years, rising costs, and one family controlling 88% of the shares. The shares trade about 20% below the value of the holdings, and there is no debt. Watch what the cash is used for, as there is no dividend policy.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company (investment entity under IFRS) domiciled in Cyprus with accounts in USD; valued on price to NAV (NAV approx. kr 2.23 per share). No analyst coverage and no forward estimate, so there are no price targets or estimates to calculate upside or revision from. stockanalysis shows −25.8% in share count and a 'buyback yield' of 25.8%, but the share count has been fixed at 524.48m since 2021 and the company owns no treasury shares; the figure is a data error and has not been used. FCF on stockanalysis includes purchases and sales of the trading portfolio and is not a measure of operations. The portfolio's individual holdings (listed shares and bonds of USD 41.1m) are not disclosed, so concentration risk has not been examined in detail. With 88% held by one family-controlled group of owners, a redemption offer or delisting is possible, but none exists.
