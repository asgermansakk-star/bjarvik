    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share; ECC holders have only 46.36% of equity, the rest is self-owned (community-owned capital). It is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful; dividend is measured against net income. stockanalysis's P/B of 0.86 and P/TBV of 0.88 are calculated on total equity including self-owned capital and hybrid capital and have not been used; T2 is based on the bank's own P/B per equity certificate (1.94 at a price of 174.68 on 30/9, approx. 2.0 at today's price of 180.88). Intangible assets are only NOK 241m, so P/TBV is almost the same as P/B. stockanalysis's insider ownership of 11.67% has not been verified (may be savings bank foundations) and earns no points. Only 4 analysts cover the security, and there is only one EPS estimate for 2026. The employee savings programme (management received approx. 52 certificates each in September) has not been counted as insider purchases. The sale of Kredinor through SpareBank 1 Gruppen is expected to complete in January 2027 and has not been scored. The bank's permission to increase its stake in SpareBank 1 Helgeland to 29.99% of capital (approx. 37.5% of the certificates) may lead to further purchases; this has not been scored beyond the capital allocation finding.
