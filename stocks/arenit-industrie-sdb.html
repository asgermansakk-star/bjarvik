      Revenue rose 54%, but net profit only 19% to EUR 1.4m in H1. The effective tax rate was 65% (EUR 2.6m tax on EUR 4.05m before tax; 41% in 2025), and EUR 1.2m went to interest. The net margin is only approx. 5% vs a 17% adjusted EBITA margin.
      mb.cision.com
      −3

      LTM operating profit of EUR 11.3m gives about 13.5% pre-tax return on invested capital (about EUR 84m). Goodwill and intangibles amount to EUR 57m, i.e. 67% of equity, so the return depends on what is paid for the acquisitions.

      Organic growth of 18–19% is compared with a weak Q2 2025 ('liberation day'), and management itself expects organic growth to approach general economic growth. Jewellery, film equipment and construction continue to face headwinds. The portfolio is small: 11 companies and EUR 86m in revenue.
