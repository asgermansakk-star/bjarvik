    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are kept in USD, while the stock trades in NOK (price NOK 16.90 on 6/11); stockanalysis' financial figures go only to Q2 2026 (TTM), because Q3 is only a trading update without net profit and cash flow. Forward P/E 4.67 is based on an EPS estimate of USD 0.32 for 2026, which is far above the realised earnings (USD 0.04 in H1) and is presumably adjusted; it has not been used as a basis for Plus. Only 5 analysts, and the price targets (NOK 31–34) are in all likelihood from before the dividend pause on 21/10, so the upside of approx. 94% is probably outdated. The dividend yield of 14.7% is historical: the distribution has been put on pause. The share of revenue from the largest customers (Equinor, YPF) has not been quantified (only assessed qualitatively), and the outcome of the Culzean negotiations is unknown. The company is marked cyclical like the other oil service companies.
