    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −12 of −30
    • Margin and returns
      Operating return is very low: EBVAT relative to equity was 2.5% in 2025 and 1.1% in H1 2026, and ROIC is 2.4% vs a WACC of 3.8% (2.0–2.6% every year since 2021). Result from primary operations (kr 39.7m) gives only approx. 2.7% of the property value of kr 1,458m, about the same as the interest on the newest mortgage loans (2.74–3.04%), so gearing does not lift the return.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has only gone from kr 54.7m (2021) to 60.7m (TTM), approx. 2% a year, and fell 1.5% TTM after a property sale; in H1 2026 it rose 1.5% and EBVAT 3% (7.1 vs 6.9m). The EBVAT lift in 2025 (15.3 vs 12.0m) came primarily from lower interest rates and debt repayment, not from operations. Net asset value per share is kr 277 vs kr 321 in 2022 (−14%).
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Moat and scale
      A small portfolio of approx. 25 properties (65,010 m², 80% residential) in central districts of Berlin with no employees of its own. Vacancy is low (2.16% in residential), but residential rents can only be raised within German rent law, so pricing power is regulated, and growth depends on renovation on vacating and new roof apartments.
      storage.googleapis.com

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −20 of −35
    • Refinancing 31/12-2026
      Two F5 loans totalling EUR 19.8m (kr 147.6m, approx. 20% of the mortgage debt) at an interest rate of 0.12% expire 31/12-2026. If they are refinanced at the level of the loans converted in 2025 (2.74–3.04%), interest expense rises by approx. kr 3.9–4.3m a year, equal to 24–29% of EBVAT guidance of kr 15–17m. After that, approx. a quarter of the debt is refinanced every year.
      storage.googleapis.com
      −4
    • Debt and interest rate sensitivity
      Net debt is kr 741m (LTV approx. 50%, equity ratio 42.8%), and interest cover is only 1.6x. The company's own sensitivity analysis shows that an increase in the yield requirement of 0.25 percentage points lowers intrinsic value from kr 267 to 221 per share (−17%), and 1 percentage point to 147 (−45%). No interest hedging is used.
      storage.googleapis.com
      −5
    • Governance and external management
      The company has no employees and is run by EgnsINVEST Management A/S, which in 2025 received kr 10.5m in administration fees (17.5% of revenue and approx. 69% of EBVAT) plus kr 5.6m in construction and sales fees, which are capitalised on the properties. A further kr 9.0m has been provisioned for a future sales fee, which depends on the sale price. Managing director Maibritt Lindstrøm is also managing director of the management company and of the major shareholder EgnsINVEST Holding (16.40%), and deputy chairman Vagn Hansen is chairman of both.
      storage.googleapis.com
      −5

      Operating cash flow was DKK 13.4m TTM vs DKK 34.4m in improvements and investments, so FCF was −DKK 21m. Cash has fallen from DKK 17.7m to 2.5m in half a year, and the construction of rooftop apartments is financed by unused credit lines of DKK 54.8m. Interest paid (DKK 23.9m) is larger than operating cash flow.
