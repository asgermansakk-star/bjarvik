    • Margin
      Operating margin has fallen from 37.0% (2024) and 31.5% (2025) to 20.8% TTM, and the net result is −USD 243.6m (−USD 0.83 per share). Without the debt extinguishment of 176.3m, there is still a loss. Adjusted EBITDA in Q2 was only USD 43.8m on revenue of 232.3m (19%), a fall of 51% from Q1.

    • Return on capital
      ROIC is 6.5% TTM vs WACC of 8.2% and has only been 3.9–7.8% in 2023–2025. The company has not earned its cost of capital, not even at the top of the cycle.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat and future segment
      Borr rents out jack-up rigs for shallow-water drilling. It is a uniform service where the price is set by day rates, and demand follows the oil companies' investments. The modern fleet gives high utilisation (98.4% technical in Q2), but no pricing power: coverage for 2026 is 73% at about USD 134,000 per day.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Capital allocation and dilution
      The share count has risen 20.4% in a year, and dilution was 39% in 2023, 32% in 2022 and 79% in 2021. Issues of USD 182.5m TTM and 177.9m in 2025 have gone to keep a debt-heavy fleet running. The dividend from 2024 has stopped, and a new convertible of USD 300m (2033) may dilute again.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has stood still at approx. USD 1.0bn since 2024 (1,011, 1,021 and 1,016m TTM), and analysts expect −3% in 2026. Fleet growth comes from acquisitions (five rigs from Fontis in a 50/50 JV), not from higher rates.
      stockanalysis.com

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock is listed on Euronext Oslo Børs (since 21/5-2026) and NYSE, but stockanalysis has no Oslo page (quote/osl/BORR gives 404), so the path and market data are the NYSE line in USD. TTM figures run to 30/6-2026; the Q3 report comes 18/11-2026. The net loss for TTM includes a one-off cost of USD 176.3m on repayment of old debt. After it there is still a loss. The financial consequences of the sinking of Prospector 5 (insurance, write-down, lost contract with BW Energy, liability and environment) are unknown and not included. Customer concentration has not been compiled, because the annual report (20-F) has not been read. Peers are only Transocean, Valaris and Noble (mostly floaters); jack-up peers such as Shelf Drilling/ADES have not been compared. The insider purchases all predate the accident.
