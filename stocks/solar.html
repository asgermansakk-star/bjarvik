    In plain words

    The model finds a Nordic electrical wholesaler with low earnings and high debt. Profit is very thin (0.3% of sales), debt is 5.2 times operating earnings, above the company's own target, and cash flow is negative. The shares are expensive relative to earnings compared with their own history and a competitor. On the plus side are 10.3% growth without acquisitions in the third quarter and about 50,000 spread-out customers.

    The model found several serious problems. That does not mean the stock will fall.
