    In plain words

    Bjarvik gives Pierce Group 82 out of 100. It has more cash than debt, has cut costs, and board members have bought shares. But profit is small (about 2.5% of sales), growth has slowed from 20% to 2%, and an IT switch caused delays. Analysts' expectations for this year need a much better second half. Watch the report on 13 November.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −14 of −35
    • Execution (IT switch)
      The WMS switch in July lifted outbound freight to 11.4% of revenue against 9.7% (more parcels per order), and overhead costs rose to 15.5% against 14.2% on extra warehouse staff and double staffing during the move of the finance function from Poland to Sweden. Only 10 of 16 markets are on the new e-commerce platform; the six largest will move after November, and management itself expects 3–4 months of lower sales and a negative effect on revenue in Q1 2027. The share fell 6.4% on the Q3 report.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Estimate risk
      Analysts still expect EPS of SEK 0.79 in 2026 (low 0.71), but nine months gave 0.46, so Q4 must deliver 0.33 vs 0.10 in Q4 2025, while freight and overheads are still elevated and the platform switch for the largest markets is getting under way. Only 2–3 analysts cover the stock.

      Sales are a hobby-type consumer product and highly weather-sensitive: Q1 2026 had the coldest January in 16 years, and Q1 typically produces a loss (EBIT −SEK 3m in Q1 2026, −15m in Q1 2025). Management points to geopolitical uncertainty and a challenging market in several large European countries.

Inventory quality is better after the clearance of old stock in Q2 (obsolescence provision 23m vs 52), but a new build-up increases the risk of new write-downs.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Re-evaluated after the report of 2026-11-13. Q3 2026 is now included; only the finding on macro and weather is largely unchanged, and the insider finding has not been updated with new trades (none found after the report, but Finansinspektionen's register has not been reviewed). Only 2–3 analysts cover the stock, and forward P/E 9.4 rests on an EPS estimate of 0.79 for 2026, which has not yet been updated after Q3 and requires EPS of 0.33 in Q4 vs 0.10 a year earlier. Management now writes that the company will 'lead a consolidation' of the market and take an active role when opportunities arise: an acquisition funded with the net cash is a new risk to capital allocation, but is not scored until something happens. The cash of SEK 257m is measured before Q4, which usually brings positive cash flow. The largest owner is the private equity fund Verdane with 20.3% (company report), which may create a selling overhang; this is not scored. Customer concentration is not relevant (B2C online store with 1.19m active customers) and is not scored. Online retail does not exist in the industry list; 'Consumer' is used, as for Zalando.
