    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is a shell: no revenue, one employee, no producing asset and negative equity, so P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA, ROIC and FCF multiples are not meaningful. The price is assessed on price performance and EV vs assets. stockanalysis shows 3.70m shares, but after the conversion to London Oil and Gas (in administration) there are 4,493,572 shares (market cap approx. kr 23m at kr 5.16). The debt write-down of kr 98.3m is conditional on a payment to Betri Banki that has not yet been transferred and is not included in the accounts as of 30/6; if completed, equity is still negative (approx. −kr 21m). No analysts, no price target and no estimates (stockanalysis has no forecast page). The Q3 report is due 30/11-2026 and is not included. Customer concentration is not relevant (no customers). It has not been examined whether the EGM on 8/9 also gave an authorisation for a larger capital increase.
