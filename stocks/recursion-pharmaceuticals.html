    • Margin
      Operating margin about −1,000% (TTM). Net loss $519m on revenue of $54.9m (TTM). Q2 2026 brought a net loss of $131m against $7.7m in revenue. Costs are being cut (Q2 loss −24% y/y), but there is no path to break-even in sight without an approved product.

      −10
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue is pure milestone and collaboration income and very uneven. Q2 2026 was $7.7m against $19.2m a year earlier (−60%), because Roche project phases ended. 2026 consensus is $41.8m against about $75m in 2025, a drop of about 44%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Segment of the future (pipeline)
      No products are on the market, and the most advanced programme is in Phase 2. REC-4881 (FAP) shows a 47% median reduction in polyp burden at week 13; a registrational study is planned for 2H 2027 at the earliest, and 4 of 26 stopped due to side effects. REC-1245 shows 3 PRs and a DCR of 91.7% (n=12), but the data are early. REC-617, REC-3565 and REC-4539 only read out in 2027, and REC-7735 gets dose-escalation data in 1H 2028. In May 2025 three Phase 2 programmes were stopped: REC-994, REC-2282 and REC-3964.
      ir.recursion.com
      −6
    • Moat (AI platform)
      The platform is validated by partners. Roche/Genentech has paid over $216m, and Sanofi $144m after an option on the first development candidate programme ($10m, Oct 2026). But the platform has not yet delivered an approved drug, and the first in-house candidates (REC-994, REC-2282) failed or were shelved.
      ir.recursion.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The Exscientia merger (Nov 2024) was followed by cuts, several programmes halted and the sale of Exscientia GmbH (2025). Management has since cut cash opex guidance to below $375m and focused the pipeline, but the ongoing burn of about $350m a year is funded with dilution.
      genengnews.com
      −2
