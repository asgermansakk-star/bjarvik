    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −16 of −30
    • Returns and track record
      The company aims for 10–15% a year, but return on equity was 20.5%, 9.7%, 9.9%, −38.7% and 18.8% in 2021–2025, averaging approx. 4%. NAV per share has fallen from kr 1.07 (end-2021) to kr 0.71 (31/10-2026). Even with kr 0.14 in dividends along the way, that is approx. −21% in just under five years.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −7
    • Costs and margin
      Operating costs rose from kr 6.1m (2021 and 2022) to kr 9.4m in 2025, while equity is smaller than in 2023. With approx. kr 4m in interest, operations cost approx. 3.5–4% of equity a year (318.6m at 30/6-2026), and that for a team of 5 employees. The management board's bonus is discretionary and not tied to fixed KPIs.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Moat and quality of the portfolio
      The portfolio is illiquid Danish and Nordic small and micro caps (incl. GreenMobility, Nord Insuretech, Agat Ejendomme and Glunz & Jensen) and an unlisted fintech company. Management itself writes that several portfolio companies need more capital. The company has no structural advantage beyond active ownership in a market with weak investor interest.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The company paid out kr 8.4m in dividends in 2026 while it raised kr 11.4m in a directed issue without pre-emption rights (15m shares at kr 0.76 in February) and has increased bank debt from 26.1m (2024) to 50.2m (30/6-2026). It has not bought back own shares in 2024–2026 despite a discount of 17–23% to NAV and an authorisation of 10%, but has instead sold own shares.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −25 of −35
    • Governance and main shareholder
      CIO Kim Mikkelsen controls 49.9% via Strategic Capital ApS and was elected to the three-member board in April 2026. Chair Joo Runge also sits on the boards of Strategic Wealth Management, Strategic Administration and other Strategic companies, so independence is doubtful. The company has changed auditor three years in a row (KPMG 2023, PwC 2024, Deloitte elected at an extraordinary general meeting in August 2025). It is jointly taxed with Strategic Capital and is jointly liable for taxes in the group.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −6
    • Portfolio concentration
      The five largest investments made up 93.4% of equity at 30/6-2026: GreenMobility 29.1%, Omnione 21.7%, Nord Insuretech 21.5%, Agat 11.2% and Glunz & Jensen 9.9%. GreenMobility alone cost DKK 54.4m in H1, and management itself calls it 'a significant swing factor'.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −5
    • Leverage and financing
      Gross investments were 125% of equity at 30/6-2026, financed with kr 50.2m in short-term bank debt at Sparekassen Danmark (secured on the securities) and a convertible loan of kr 25.6m at 5% with a conversion price of kr 1.25–1.32 (far above the price, so in effect it is debt). The risk framework allows up to 3 × leverage in equity derivatives. When the assets are illiquid, a price fall can force sales.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −4
    • Guidance and credibility
      The expected return of 10–15% in 2026 was maintained in August after −16.6% in H1. At 31/10 NAV has fallen from DKK 0.92 to 0.71, approx. −21% incl. dividend, so guidance requires a return of over 40% in two months. The pattern is known from 2024, when the expectation of 10–15% was cut three times (to −10–0% in August, −30 to −20% in November and −40 to −30% in December), and the year ended at −38.7%.
      view.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −4

      Omnione (kr 69.2m, 21.7% of equity) is carried based on a post-money valuation of EUR 100m from a debt conversion, which the main shareholder Achilles Capital was behind, in a company with approx. EUR 6.5m in revenue and negative EBITDA in 2024. Management writes that Omnione's liquidity must be continuously secured, and that an exit is expected in 2027 at the earliest.
      attachment.news.eu.nasdaq.com
      −3
