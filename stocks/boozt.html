    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Online fashion retail does not fit the industrial list and has been placed under 'Consumer: food, beverages & restaurants' like Zalando. Boozt's adjusted EBIT and adjusted EPS (forward P/E 19.9 is based on adjusted EPS 8.51) exclude share-based pay, which is an ongoing cost. Net debt is kr 12m by the company's definition and 702m incl. leasing (stockanalysis). FCF 2026 is depressed by exit tax (net 140m, neutral over five years through Danish tax deduction) and inventory build-up; the FCF assessment is therefore also viewed over several years. Only 4 analysts cover the share, and the price targets (165–180) are close to the price after a rise of 67% in a year. Competition from Shein/Temu, short interest and the board's composition have not been examined in depth. Q4 (Black Friday and Christmas) will decide whether the record-high inventory is sold at full price. Capital markets day 9 March 2027. The buybacks (401m over 12 months, share count −7.2%) do not earn a plus because FCF after leasing (291m) did not cover them, and Q3 was partly debt-funded.
