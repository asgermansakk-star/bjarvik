    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −9 of −30
    • ROIC and returns
      ROIC is 3.0% vs WACC of 4.6% and has been 2.2–3.6% since 2022. Return on equity is 5.0% (R12) and 5.4% in 2025, half of the company's own target of over 10%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Rental income rose 10% in 9M, but only 3% in the comparable portfolio; growth comes from acquisitions such as Storsjön 3. In Q3 förvaltningsresultat rose only 2% (SEK 37m vs 37), the surplus ratio fell to 66% (69), and property costs rose 19%, partly because of a provision for tenant losses. Intjäningsförmåga per share fell from SEK 2.4 to 2.2 in a year, because the new bond costs interest before the money is invested.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The dividend policy promises at least 40% of förvaltningsresultat after tax, but no dividend has been paid for 2023, 2024 or 2025. Instead the money goes to debt-financed acquisitions (Delfinen 1 for kr 95m at an initial yield of about 7.1%) financed with a green bond at STIBOR + 300 bp.
      mb.cision.com
      −1
    • Future segment
      74% of property value is housing (3,105 apartments) in growth municipalities in Western Sweden, and the economic occupancy rate is 98%. Part of the portfolio is, however, in small towns such as Lilla Edet, Tibro, Götene and Falköping, where demand and liquidity in the property market are weaker.
      mb.cision.com
      −1
