    In plain words

    AKVA group scores 52 out of 100. The share price tracks a cash takeover offer of NOK 161 per share, which holders of about 92% of the shares have accepted, so it reflects the offer more than the company. The biggest drags are a high price relative to earnings and high debt. On the plus side, the order book is growing. The offer period ends on 20 November 2026.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
