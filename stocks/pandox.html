    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −16 of −35
    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio is 52.2% (policy 45-60%), net debt/EBITDA 10.2× and interest cover down to 2.4× (2.6). The average maturity of bank loans is only 1.9 years, short-term debt has risen to kr 15.1bn (3.8), and EUR 665m of the acquisition facility for Dalata must be refinanced. Liquidity and unused facilities are kr 3.5bn vs issued commercial paper of kr 3.8bn, and the average interest rate has risen to 4.08%.
      storage.mfn.se
      −7
    • Tenant concentration
      Scandic leases 51 hotel properties with 11,543 rooms (27% of rooms) and now also has 30-year leases on 31 of the Dalata properties with 6,626 rooms, so about 43% of rooms have the same operator.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Tenants' behaviour
      The tenant Revo Hospitality Group (9 German hotels, 1,859 rooms, about 4% of rooms) went bankrupt in 2026. Pandox has taken over operation of six hotels, three are run on by the trustee, and rent in Leases fell by SEK 37m in Q3. The bank guarantee covers only one year's rent.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Governance and related parties
      Eiendomsspar owns 25.0% of the capital and 35.8% of the votes, and Helene Sundt AB and Christian Sundt AB own 20.7% and 33.3%, mainly via unlisted A shares. Eiendomsspar owns 8.8% of the Dalata acquisition vehicle (EUR 120m, booked as interest-bearing debt, which Pandox must buy out), minorities in German property companies, and Pandox has a management agreement with a Sundt-owned resort in the Bahamas.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Macro and hotel cycle
      Rent is revenue-based, and own operations make up 38% of revenue, so earnings follow the hotel market's RevPAR (Brussels −4%, UK unchanged in Q3). Beta is 1.2, and management points to rising inflation and interest rates.
