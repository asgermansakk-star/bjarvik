    A score of 55 points to a small payments company that is growing but short of cash. Cash was only 0.6m Swedish kronor, and the board sees a need for more funding. Part of the profit comes from counting its own development work as income, and the share count is up 41%. On the plus side, recurring income is 77% of sales, and insiders have bought shares.
