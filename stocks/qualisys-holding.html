    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −17 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 32% in Q1, 2% in Q2 and approx. 15% in Q3 2026 (approx. kr 46m vs 54.3m), so the first nine months are approx. 17% lower than a year earlier. Order intake (TTM) fell to kr 238.5m vs 264.7m – far from the target of 15% annual growth.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Margin
      EBIT was only approx. DKK 0.6m in the first nine months of 2026 (EBIT margin approx. 0.4% in Q3 vs 14.7%), pressed by lower volume and commercialisation costs for markerless. Capitalised development (DKK 9.4m in 2025, approx. 3.5% of revenue) lifts EBIT; the operating margin has fallen from 22.8% in 2022 to 14.3% (TTM June) before Q3.
      storage.mfn.se
      −6
    • Moat and future segment
      The core is marker-based camera systems for research laboratories, while the market is moving towards markerless (AI video-based) motion capture with lower barriers to entry. Qualisys responds with OnTraq and Prisma Pose and a partner (Theia) for the advanced needs, but is late and is spending costs on it without it yet generating meaningful revenue.
      mfn.se
      −4

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −13 of −35
    • Execution and guidance
      In the Q2 report (26/8) management wrote of a growing order book and a positive outlook for the rest of the year; two months later a profit warning for Q3 came, and the stock fell 27% on the day. The financial targets (15% growth and 20% EBIT margin) are maintained despite three weak quarters in a row.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Customer behavior and macro
      Life Science accounts for about 65–70% of sales, with academic and institutional customers that depend on public research funding. The company itself points to limited research funding in the US and geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East as the reason for postponed orders.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Cash flow and dividend
      The dividend of kr 3 (kr 30m a year, 8.6% direct yield) is twice as large as FCF after leases (kr 14.9m in 2025 and 10.2m TTM), so cash fell from kr 62.4m to 46.9m in H1; the FCF payout ratio is 177%. With a zero result in Q3, a further kr 15m is paid out in November.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

      The main shareholder Vätterledens Invest and the other sellers sold about half of the shares at the IPO at kr 76 (now 35), without the company raising capital. Insiders own only 1.8%, and no insider purchases have been registered in 2026 despite the price fall.
