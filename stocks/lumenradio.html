    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has stood still for three years: SEK 257m (2023), 247 (2024), 249 (2025) and 246 TTM. Q3 2026 fell 7.1% organically (Lighting Control −8.9% on weak Chinese demand), and approx. 3.5m per quarter is deliberately disappearing with the product mix shift. The target of >25% annual organic growth from the 2022 listing has been abandoned and replaced by >500m in 2030, which requires approx. 15% a year from a flat level.

      −6

      CRMX is a widespread standard for wireless DMX in entertainment lighting, and W-BACnet has won the AHR Expo Innovation Award 2027, so the niche position is real. But with SEK 246m in revenue and approx. 71 employees the company is small, depends on OEM manufacturers of lighting equipment and competes in building automation against far bigger players.

Building Automation has grown, but the acquisition has not lifted the group's overall growth, and the cash of 133m sits unused, with no dividend or buyback.
