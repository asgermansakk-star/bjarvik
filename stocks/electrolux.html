Home appliances do not fit the industrial list; placed under 'Consumer: food, drink & restaurants' as the nearest consumer category (the same choice as Clas Ohlson and Pandora). Cyclical = no: demand follows consumer confidence, the housing market and interest rates, not primarily a price, commodity or inventory cycle, even though commodity and logistics costs affect the margin. stockanalysis shows the share count −22% in a year, but the cash flow statement shows an issue of SEK 8.9bn, and the number of shares almost tripled (283 → 824m) in the rights issue in June 2026; historical prices and EPS are adjusted for the issue. Trailing P/E is n/a due to a loss; analysts' 2026 EPS (1.93) appears to be adjusted for one-off items, as Q3 gave a net loss. Net debt/EBITDA: stockanalysis 5.0x (incl. leases, reported EBITDA) vs the company's own 2.2x (adjusted EBITDA). Peer multiples (Whirlpool, Arçelik/Beko, Midea, Haier) and credit rating have not been examined. Investor AB is the largest owner; insider trades and Investor's underwriting fee in the issue have not been examined in detail. The share price is 48% lower over 52 weeks and approx. 27% below the 200-day average, so the price-decline safeguards may kick in. No dividend since 2023.
