    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Serial acquirer of European niche companies (35 units, B2B and B2C); 'Machinery & automation' is the closest industry, as for Lifco, Addtech and Indutrade. Listed in March 2025, so stockanalysis only has multiple history for 2025 and now; older multiples are missing. Röko consolidates the subsidiaries 100% with put/call options for the minority owners, so net income and EPS include the minority interest; dividends to minorities and revaluation of the option liability go through equity. Only 2 analysts (both Hold), and forward P/E 21.1 on stockanalysis does not match the EPS estimate for 2026 (SEK 63.40), so the PEG and upside points rest on a thin basis. Insider trades have been reviewed but not scored: the chairman bought 1,782 B shares (approx. SEK 3.5m) on 26–27/10 and the CEO 956 shares in July, while another insider and a related party sold approx. 5,700 shares (approx. SEK 11.6m) on 2–3/11. Acquisition multiples per deal are not disclosed and have not been examined.
