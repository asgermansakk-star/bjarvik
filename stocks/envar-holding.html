    • Margin
      The EBIT margin has swung between 3% (2023), 18% (2024), 17% (2025) and 21% TTM. Under K3, capitalised game development (SEK 21.1m in 2025, 14.6m in H1 2026) is booked as income under 'Research and development expenses' within total income, so the reported EBIT is clearly above the cash earnings.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Business model and moat
      The core business is outsourcing of graphics, where 142 external consultants in over 20 countries deliver the work (40 employees). The barriers to entry are low, and price pressure is heavy in a games industry that is cutting back, so the moat rests on relationships rather than own IP or technology.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3

      The strategy moves capital from stable consulting operations into hit-driven game development and publishing. Management targets four games a year and plans five new titles in 2027–2028 (one in 2027 and four in 2028) plus Witchspire 1.0, while Envar Publishing invests in external developers' projects.
