    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are kept in EUR, while the stock trades in SEK. stockanalysis has no analyst page for Eltel (forecast gives 404), so the estimate basis is thin, and no consensus price target has been found. The multiple history from 2021–2025 is based on depressed or negative earnings, so the P/E history is only partly usable; P/B, EV/Sales and EV/EBIT are therefore weighted more. Trailing net profit is lifted by a tax income (net profit above profit before tax). Lease liabilities are included in net debt. The order book includes estimated, non-binding parts of framework agreements. Contract terms (fixed price vs time and materials) on the new large agreements have not been examined.
