    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Accounts in EUR, price in SEK. Gaming does not fit the industry list: placed under 'Software, cloud & internet' like Evolution (B2B game supplier with its own RGS platform). Listed only since 25/6-2025, so the multiple history covers only year-end 2025; T2 is therefore assessed mainly against peers. The price targets (median SEK 94) were last updated 22/7-2026, before the profit warning on 12/10 and the price fall from 81 to approx. SEK 58, and the forward P/E of 6.7 is based on estimates that look outdated (FY26 EPS EUR 0.66 requires approx. 0.19 in Q4 vs 0.158 in Q3); upside and PEG scores are therefore misleadingly high. The share of revenue from locally regulated markets is not disclosed after the listing (12% in Q1 2025 according to Affärsvärlden), and the largest customer (according to the prospectus a 'crypto casino' with approx. 20% of revenue in 2024) is not named; its share in 2026 has not been found. Whether the major shareholders (Lollipop Assets and four Australian companies/trusts with 4.2% each) have connections to customers has not been verified. Brazil's share of revenue is not disclosed (no jurisdiction above 10% in 2025). Short interest and lock-up expiry have not been examined. Stockanalysis' share count increase of 11.65% is due to the restructuring before the listing (minority), not a cash issue; outstanding warrants dilute approx. 1.1%. FCF after leasing and capitalised development is approx. EUR 176m (TTM).
