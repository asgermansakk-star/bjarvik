    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −22 of −30
    • Margin
      EBIT was −SEK 11.7m on sales of 34.7m in the first nine months of 2026, and the operating margin is −50% TTM. Q3's EBIT of +0.1m rests on 2.3m of capitalised development. Gross margin was 63% vs 64% a year earlier and only 60% in Q3 (66%), even though in February the company promised approx. 10 percentage points better gross margin in 2026.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −9
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from SEK 66m in 2023 to 56m in 2024, 43m in 2025 and 42m TTM. Sales go through tenders and are lumpy: one Korean order of 12.6m made up two thirds of Q3 revenue of 18.5m, and Q1 fell 67%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has tripled from 84m in 2021 to 254m in 2025 through repeated issues, most recently SEK 40m directed at SEK 0.84 (the price is now 0.51). The company capitalises approx. SEK 9m a year in development, while ROIC has been negative in four of the last five years.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Moat
      Serstech is the only developer and manufacturer of handheld Raman instruments in the EU, and the Swedish origin helps in security tenders. But with 25 employees the company is very small against global competitors, and it sells mostly through partners in 66 countries.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −21 of −35
    • Funding and dilution
      At 30/9 cash was SEK 5.4m, but that corresponds largely to customers' prepayments of SEK 5.1m. Debt is SEK 20m and all short-term: the Almi loan of SEK 10m falls due 31/12-2026 and is being renegotiated, and the overdraft facility of SEK 10m is fully drawn. FCF after capitalised development was about −SEK 33m TTM, so a new issue is likely.
      ml-eu.globenewswire.com
      −8
    • Guidance and execution
      In April management promised positive cash flow for the whole of 2026, but cash flow from operations was −SEK 18m after nine months. February's promise of 'strong and profitable growth' in 2026 and over 30% annual growth to 2030 compares with just +8% sales growth after nine months. 2025 also disappointed (tariffs and postponed tenders).
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Customer concentration and customer behaviour
      Almost all sales go to public authorities via tenders with multi-year sales cycles. Quarters depend on single orders of SEK 10–13m (South Korea in Q3 and US Army via WS Darley in Q4), and a Saudi order was postponed because the customer did not follow its prepayment plan.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Management and governance
      The CFO is stepping down on 10/11-2026, and no successor has been announced while the company must renegotiate loans. The chairman changed in April. The CEO and chairman have each given a personal guarantee of SEK 0.5m for the Almi loan.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Macro and supply
      Management reports a shortage of electronic components with price increases and delivery times to Q2 2027. It also warns that high oil prices may delay Asian customers' purchasing.
