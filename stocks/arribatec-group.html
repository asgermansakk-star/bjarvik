    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Q3 report comes on 18/11-2026, and management has warned of a weaker Q3 (hardware costs, summer and a customer bankruptcy), so the TTM figures may get worse. stockanalysis has no forecast page; forward P/E 7.1 probably rests on a single estimate and should be read with caution. Insider ownership of 47.95% at stockanalysis is too high; the company's shareholder list shows about 17% with the board (the chair's Company One 10.15% and Terje Mjøs' AWE Invest 7.2%). The share count rose about tenfold after the rights issue at NOK 0.10 (before the 1:10 consolidation) in January 2025 and subsequent warrant exercises; the figures have been checked against the company's note 7. The dividend yield of 20.75% is backward-looking: the dividend of NOK 1.00 (about NOK 67m) was the first in the company's history and partly financed by the divestment of Hospitality and Marine in 2025. Net profit for 2025 (54m) contains a one-off gain of NOK 29.9m from the divestment; multiples are based on TTM, which does not contain the gain. Customer concentration at revenue level is not stated (only the share of new contracts) and is set to 0.
