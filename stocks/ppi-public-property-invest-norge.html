    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Property company: valuation on price vs EPRA NRV and ongoing operating profit (net income from property management) instead of P/E, because the IFRS result fluctuates with currency and value adjustments. The SocialCo portfolio from SBB has been consolidated from 16/12-2025, and the company has moved its domicile to Sweden (primary listing Nasdaq Stockholm from May 2026, secondary in Oslo); stockanalysis history and PEG from the period have therefore not been used, and the accounts are now prepared in SEK, while the price here is in NOK. Stockanalysis has no forecast page (no visible price targets), so upside points are based on thin or missing data. EPRA NTA has not been found; NRV includes reversed deferred tax and overstates the net asset value. Peers have not been compared directly (only Cibus in own database). Pledging of SBB's PPI shares has not been investigated. The ongoing exchange offers for Preservium, Origa Care and One Publicus have PPI as the buyer, not a special situation for the PPI stock.
