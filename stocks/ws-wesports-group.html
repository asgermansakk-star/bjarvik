    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Listed 9 December 2025 at kr 80, so there is no multiple history and no 52-week price change. stockanalysis shows EPS 2.65 and a 7.1% fall in share count, but the report shows EPS to parent company shareholders of kr 1.93 (LTM) on an average of 23.8m shares, and the share count rose from 18.6 to 27.8m at the listing. Trailing P/E 27.2 and PEG 0.16 on stockanalysis are therefore unreliable. Forward P/E 11.6 is based on adjusted EPS (5.60), while analysts' net profit for 2026 (104.5m) gives only about kr 3.76 per share. Q3 is preliminary figures only; the full report comes 12 November 2026. Only 3 analysts (ABG, Carnegie, Danske Bank).
