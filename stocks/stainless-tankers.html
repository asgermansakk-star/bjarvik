    In plain words

    The score of 56 shows a small shipping company with six old vessels and earnings lifted by high freight rates. Most weighing it down: low profit relative to equity, a share expensive relative to earnings compared with other tanker firms, and rare trading. In its favour, the board plans to sell the ships and pay out, and about 65% of capital raised is already returned.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The stock actually trades on Euronext Growth Oslo, not the main list. stockanalysis only has the statistics page (no financial history, no P/B, ROIC or analyst pages), so ratios are calculated from the company's own quarterly figures: TTM net profit of about USD 2.4m (Q3 2025 to Q2 2026), equity of USD 52.4m and NAV at 30/6 of USD 5.22/share before the dividend of USD 0.135 in September. In August the board decided to sell the fleet within 18 months. It is a plan, not a binding sale, an offer or a delisting, so saerlig_situation is empty; the price will, however, increasingly follow the sale proceeds rather than operations. The Q3 report comes 12/11 and is not included. Insider trades and the valuation of the individual ships have not been examined (0 points), and analyst coverage is very thin.
