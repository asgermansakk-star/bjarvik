    In plain words

    The score of 48 shows a software firm with a large order book but weak operations and tight finances. Revenue fell 7%, and cash flow was minus SEK 36.9m in nine months. Cash is SEK 8.6m; the overdraft is nearly used up. On the plus side are an order book of SEK 946.6m, up from SEK 281.5m, and board share purchases. About 74% of the order book is one contract.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.
