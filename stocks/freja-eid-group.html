    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −15 of −30
    • Margin
      Operating margin is still negative: −21% TTM (EBIT −SEK 12.2m on revenue of 58m) and net result −SEK 5.5m in the first nine months of the year. The trend is clearly better (−127% in 2022, EBIT −0.1m in Q3 2026), but the company has not yet made money on the bottom line.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Moat
      BankID has approx. 8.7m users and almost 7,900 connected services vs Freja's 1.88m users, and from 1 December 2026 a state e-ID arrives from the Swedish Police Authority. The same law forces public bodies to accept several e-IDs, but Freja still stands as a small challenger between a dominant bank-owned player and the state.
      realtid.se
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Freja eID grew 62% in nine months, but SEK 5.0m (12% of the segment) is development revenue from Teracom outside the subscriptions, and Fulfilment is falling 51%. ARR rose only SEK 1.6m and 1.8m in Q2 and Q3 after a jump of 7m in Q1, and NRR fell from 131% to 122%. The group grew 26% in Q3.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Capital allocation
      The 2023/2026 incentive programme gave management and key people 1.1m shares at the quota value (SEK 0.05), and the programme cost SEK 5.6m in 2025 (about 11% of revenue). The social charges were covered by selling 507,327 warrants to external investors at a 5% discount.
      mfn.se
      −3

    • Dilution and financing
      The share count has risen from 18.9m (2021) to 30.2m (+60%), of which a directed issue of SEK 32m in 2025 and 5.3% dilution from options in September 2026; stockanalysis shows +20.5% in a year. Cash is SEK 18.6m vs current liabilities of 36.5m (current ratio 0.97), and the board guarantees liquidity for only 12 months.

      −6
    • Customer concentration and execution
      The defence project with Teracom is a large part of the story. The framework agreement of up to SEK 27.9m in ARR guarantees no offtake, the SEK 7.5m from the development environment is not yet in operation, and the NATO accreditation, which is a prerequisite for continuing, has been postponed to Q2–Q3 2027. NRR was also propped up by a single Migrationsverket contract.
