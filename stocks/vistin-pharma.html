    In plain words

    The score of 67 describes a small drug maker with one product, net cash and a dividend of about 8% that is covered by cash flow. The stock is cheaper than usual, but earnings fell 23% in the first nine months of the year, and four customers make up 81% of revenue. The CEO has bought shares. Watch a planned four-week production stop in the fourth quarter.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −15 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      EBITDA for the first nine months of the year fell to kr 68m from kr 88m (−23%), and the EBITDA margin in Q3 was 17.4%. The operating margin has fallen from 20.4% in 2025 to 16.5% (TTM) and ROIC from 23.5% to 17.7%. The company is thus getting weaker, not stronger.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Moat and pricing power
      Vistin makes one generic API product (metformin), and the selling price follows the spot price of metformin and global commodity prices. The company sells in EUR but buys raw materials in USD, and competes with low-cost Asian producers. Quality and green power give a premium position, but little pricing power of its own.
      cdn.prod.website-files.com
      −5
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 6% to kr 321m in the first nine months of the year and was roughly flat at constant exchange rates, even though volume rose 10%. Lower average price and a stronger NOK thus offset the volume growth, and EPS fell from kr 1.32 to 1.05.
      cdn.prod.website-files.com
      −2
    • Capacity ceiling and capital allocation
      Sales of 4,710 tonnes in nine months correspond to approx. 6,300 tonnes a year, close to the capacity target of approx. 7,000 tonnes. More growth requires new capex or acquisitions. The company is in a strategic process on M&A or a European multi-product CDMO outside metformin, where it has no documented experience (owns 15% of CF Pharma).
      cdn.prod.website-files.com
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −16 of −35
    • Customer concentration
      Four customers each account for at least 10% of revenue. The largest accounted for kr 182m of kr 452m in 2025 (40%), and the four together for 81%. The company has only 19 customers, and the five largest make up 81% of receivables.
      cdn.prod.website-files.com
      −6
    • Execution and operational risk
      The main distillation tank is worn out and must be replaced in an extended four-week maintenance shutdown in Q4, when both lines stand still. Production and sales are hit accordingly. There were also unplanned minor stoppages this summer, and all production takes place at one plant.
      cdn.prod.website-files.com
      −4
    • Macro: currency and commodities
      By far most of the sales are in EUR, while raw materials are bought in USD. The stronger NOK weighed on revenue in Q3, and raw materials bought during the Hormuz crisis in April–May increased cost of goods.
      cdn.prod.website-files.com
      −3
    • Governance and liquidity
      Intertrade Shipping (controlled by a board member) owns 32.7% and Pactum Vekst (also board-related) 6.7%, so minority shareholders have limited influence. The free float is about 20m shares, and only about 9,000 shares are traded a day.
      vistin.com
      −2
    • Substitution and regulation
      The company itself cites new diabetes drugs (e.g. GLP-1) and requirements in the factory's discharge permit as key risks. Metformin remains standard treatment, but a product with no alternatives is vulnerable.
      cdn.prod.website-files.com
      −1
