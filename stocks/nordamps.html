    In plain words

    The score is 50 out of 100. The newly listed company has never sold a product, lives on grants, and has no customers or agreements yet. The share price is down 52% since listing, and the offering was only 91% subscribed. On the plus side, there is about DKK 40m in cash, little debt, and patented technology. Watch for first customer agreements and new shares that dilute owners.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue on stockanalysis (1.68m TTM) is capitalised development and EU/Vinnova grants (the ARCTIC project gave about 3.0m in 2025), not sales. Capitalised development fell from 1.64m to 0.30m in H1, and the company carried out savings in 2026, so activity has gone down, not up.
      nordamps.com
      −4
    • Future segment and moat
      Patented vertical nanowire transistor (InGaAs on silicon) from Lund University aimed at LNAs for satellite, defence, radar and 5G/6G, an attractive segment. But production has not been industrialised (the collaboration with VTT is under way), there are no design wins, and established GaAs/InP/GaN suppliers with a qualification track record dominate the market.
      nordamps.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The share count has gone from approx. 5.2m (H1 2025 average) to 10.88m after the IPO (dilution of 42.9% in the issue itself). The convertible loans were written up by a factor of 1/0.7 (+43%) before the IPO and bear interest of 2% a month, so the early lenders got shares at effectively approx. DKK 10.85. Deloitte wrote about material uncertainty regarding going concern in the 2025 annual report, and the accumulated deficit is approx. 39m.

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −18 of −35
    • Funding and dilution
      The IPO was only 91.2% subscribed (68.6m gross, but 25.5m was set-off of loans), so net cash added is approx. 37m vs the 43.9m on which the plan for liquidity to September 2028 was based. The plan also increases spending on customer projects, test systems and VTT. The warrant programme TO 2026/2031:1 and the employee options give further dilution of approx. 6%, and the Almi loan of 4m is secured by a floating charge. A new issue is likely before positive cash flow.
      mfn.se
      −6
    • Commercial execution
      There are neither customers, letters of intent nor design wins, only 'dialogues and evaluations'. According to the company, the road to revenue goes through test circuits, customer evaluation, qualification and design win, after which the customer's own product must reach volume. That takes several years, and the company depends on grants and on VTT's industrialisation. With 9 employees there is also heavy dependence on key people.
      nordamps.com
      −6
    • Governance
      The chairman's company NYN Consulting receives kr 10,000 a day for about 2 days a week (equal to about kr 1m a year vs a cost base of about 12m), and the CFO is engaged as a consultant. The chairman exercised 1/10 private call options from LU Ventures and got 50,000 shares at kr 8 vs the IPO price of kr 15.50. In July 2026 the CEO and the board received more call options at kr 8.68 from major shareholders.
      marknadssok.fi.se
      −3
    • Ownership structure and price performance
      The price fell 22.6% on the first trading day and is now kr 7.40 (−52% from the IPO price) with an RSI of 19 and turnover of only approx. 8,500 shares a day. Venture funds (SVP/STOAF, Almi Invest, LU Ventures and Butterfly) own approx. 42%, and the lock-up for the smaller shareholders expires after 6 months (approx. 30/3-2027), so there is selling pressure ahead.
