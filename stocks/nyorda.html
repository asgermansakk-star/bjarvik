    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −16 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      The operating margin has fallen from 2.6% in 2022 to 0.3% over 12 months, net income is −kr 7.7m, and ROE and ROIC are −1.9% and −3.6%. Even adjusted EBITDA is only 4.4% of revenue for Jan–Sep (unchanged from the year before) and 22.4% of gross profit in Q3 vs the target of over 25%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue rose only 3% in Jan–Sep (5% at constant exchange rates). Partner Marketing, approx. 89% of revenue, grew 1% and lost adjusted EBITDA (kr 103m vs 107m), and DACH fell from kr 48m to 38m. Growth comes from Metapic and the new US market; Q3 with +9% is the first quarter with broad-based improvement.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat
      Nyorda is an intermediary in a competitive affiliate market against larger networks with low switching costs. The gross margin has been around 21–22% for five years (19.5% in 2021, 22.2% in 2025), so there are no signs of pricing power; approx. 78% of revenue is passed on to publishers.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      Goodwill of kr 379m and other intangible assets of kr 79m exceed equity of kr 402m, so tangible equity is negative. The earn-out to KAHA was written up by kr 24m and 44m in 2024–2025, and now capital is being spread over new, early bets (EMNA AI, 50% of Bridge Retail Media, Appiness), while the core stands still.
      borskollen.se
      −3

      Operating cash flow was −kr 7m in Jan–Sep vs kr 82m the year before. After kr 23m in capitalised development, kr 11m in leasing and kr 20m in earn-out, cash fell from 126 to kr 63m, and a further kr 42m of earn-out is to be paid. Cash is also closely tied to kr 417m of debt to publishers.

      Reworld Media owns 55%, and three of five board members, including the chairman and Reworld's co-founder Pascal Chevalier, are dependent on the main shareholder. Nyorda has loans from Reworld (kr 30m) and ongoing transactions with Reworld, which is also EMNA AI's first major network partner. The minority has little influence.

      Content sites, an important partner category in affiliate networks, are losing traffic to AI-generated answers. Nyorda itself responds with EMNA AI (AI visibility), which only got its first customer in Q3 2026, so the core is exposed before the new offering contributes.
