    In plain words

    The score is high (90 out of 100), but the company only listed in June 2026, so the track record is short. Pulling it down: most growth was bought through acquisitions, returns on invested money are mediocre, and acquisitions were paid for with new shares. In its favour, cash exceeds bank debt. A lock-up that stops some early owners selling ends around mid-December.
