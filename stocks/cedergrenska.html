    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −14 of −35
    • Politics and profit in schools
      All revenue is Swedish and depends on the rules for free schools. The inquiry into profit in schools proposes a dividend ban for five years for new schools and at larger ownership changes, which directly hits an acquisition-driven model. After the election in September 2026, C has according to Placera opened to accepting a ban on profit withdrawal in schools as a concession to V in order to form an S-led government.
      placera.se
      −8
    • Execution in Q4
      EBITA fell to kr 28.1m (33.1) in Q4 despite 14.5% higher revenue, and the goodwill-adjusted net earnings fell to 16.7m (25.2); the company explains this by a larger change in the holiday pay liability and costs for the listing change, IFRS and internal control.
      cedergrenska.se
      −2
    • Debt and fixed obligations
      From net cash of SEK 38m to net debt of SEK 61m (0.4x EBITDA) in a year, because buybacks and acquisitions were financed with RCF and bank loans (185.8m vs 90m); the RCF facility has been raised to SEK 250m. Long lease contracts on the schools are outside the K3 balance sheet and are fixed costs if pupil numbers fall.
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

      The co-founders and main owners Niklas Pålsson and Håkan Söderström each sold 50,000 shares on 21/11-2025 to 'broaden the ownership base'; a small sale (about 0.8% of the shares), but a sale.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Swedish independent-school group (preschool to upper secondary and adult education, 10,486 children and pupils). The industry list has no education category; placed under 'Health insurance & services' like AcadeMedia and the other Swedish welfare companies with public payers. The financial year runs July–June, and the accounts have so far been prepared under K3 with goodwill amortisation: stockanalysis' P/E 29 and net margin 1.4% are therefore not comparable with IFRS peers; T2 is based on the company's goodwill-adjusted EPS (SEK 6.91 TTM, forecast min. SEK 7.3). From Q1 2026/27 (19–20/11-2026) reporting is under IFRS, which changes EBITDA, net debt (IFRS 16 leasing of school buildings) and EPS; the K3 figures have rent in operations, so FCF is already after rent. No forward estimates on stockanalysis; the only coverage is paid analysis from Analyst Group. The formation of a government after the Riksdag election in September 2026 and a new government's line on profit in schools have not been verified. The Swedish Schools Inspectorate's supervision, quality data and board independence have not been examined separately (0 points). The planned listing change to Nasdaq Stockholm Main Market has no set date.
