    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 4 findings    −13 of −35
    • Governance and related parties
      Aker Capital owns about 51.8% and Solstad Offshore 27.3%; free float is only about 21%. CEO Lars Peder Solstad and board members are closely tied to Solstad Offshore, which is also the counterparty in the Brazil bareboat contracts and is now buying the flagship Normand Maximus for USD 125m.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Customer concentration
      The ships in Brazil mainly operate on bareboat contracts through Solstad Offshore (the Petrobras market), and Normand Maximus is chartered to Solstad Offshore. A significant part of earnings thus hangs on one group-affiliated intermediary and one end customer.
      mb.cision.com
      −2
    • Dividend vs cash flow
      The quarterly dividend has been raised to USD 0.12 (approx. USD 56m, 224m annually), while 2026 guidance gives FCF after interest of approx. USD 198m. The 'free cash flow to equity' of 298m includes a new loan of USD 100m, so the dividend is partly debt-financed. Net debt is USD 488m (1.3x EBITDA), and repayments are USD 90m a year.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Market supply and cycle
      Management expects more AHTS vessels in the North Sea via redeployments, which may press utilisation and day rates. The CSV market has spare capacity, including several newbuilds without contract, and Australia will have lower activity in the coming quarters.
      mb.cision.com
      −3

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are kept in USD, while the stock trades in NOK. The company has been listed only since November 2024, so there is only one full year of multiple history (2025). Trailing P/E 4.65 is lifted by a tax income (deferred tax asset) and a gain of USD 8m on the sale of Normand Clipper; profit before tax gives approx. P/E 4.9. Only 3 analysts cover the stock. The share of revenue from Petrobras/Brazil via Solstad Offshore has not been quantified precisely. The fleet's age and the contract terms for the Brazil ships have not been examined in detail. The stock traded ex-dividend (USD 0.12) on 6/11.
