    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are in EUR, the stock trades in SEK (price SEK 0.183, market cap approx. SEK 9m). Olivine Holdings' cash offer of SEK 0.30 per share was completed 25/5-2026 and gave approx. 77–80% of the shares; the acceptance period ended 8/6-2026, and no compulsory redemption or delisting has been found, so special situation has not been set. If Olivine goes above 90% or applies for delisting, this must be changed. stockanalysis' insider share (45%) does not match Olivine's approx. 77–80%; the figure has not been used. The share count has been 51.1m since 2024 according to the balance sheet, so +4.5% in share count gives no points. The Q3 report is discontinued (transition to half-year reporting), so the latest figures are from 30/6-2026 and from before the account statement in October. The outcome of the settlement negotiations between the company, Maximum Games' sellers and Olivine is unknown and could both wipe out and save the stock. The content of the auditor's report (going concern qualification) has not been read. No analyst coverage and no forward estimates.
