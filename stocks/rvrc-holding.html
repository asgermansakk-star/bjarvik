    In plain words

    Bjarvik sees a very profitable online business whose growth is slower than its target. Sales grew only about 5% against a 20% goal, almost half comes from Germany where the market is weak, and marketing eats 42% of revenue. In its favour, the return on invested money is about 35% and the CEO bought shares. Watch whether the ICANIWILL acquisition, at SEK 775m, works out.
