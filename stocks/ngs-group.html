    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −18 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      EBITA for the last 12 months is about SEK 8.7m (9M 2026 7.6m plus Q4 2025 1.1m), a margin of about 3.1% on revenue of about 279m. That is below the company's own target of 4% and on a par with 2021 (3.7%), and ROIC of 7.9% looks decent only because the capital has been written down. Subconsultants eat about 60% of revenue.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has more than halved from SEK 571m in 2022 to 283m in 2025 (−19% in 2025, partly through the sale of Vikariepoolen). Q3 2026 gave +9%, the first growth since Q1 2023, but 9M is still −2%, and Norway fell to 6.3m from 8.8m in the quarter.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Moat and future segment
      Recruitment, interim and care staffing have low entry barriers and no pricing power, and NGS is a very small player (106 employees). The gross margin was 7.9% in 2025, and AI is squeezing the recruitment industry, which the company itself uses AI to make more efficient.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisitions have been written down three years in a row: goodwill SEK 108m in 2023, 50m in 2024 and 30m in 2025, in total approx. 188m, and goodwill has fallen from 227 to 39m. The company itself warns that further write-downs may come, and goodwill is still 58% of equity.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5

      Cash is SEK 3.7m vs 10.0m drawn on the overdraft (10m unused), a short-term owner loan of 7.6m from major shareholder and board member Charlotte Pantzar Huth and 8.7m in invoice financing. Management writes only that liquidity lasts 'during the coming quarter'.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Dilution
      The rights issue and the directed issue in 2024 (approx. SEK 51m) increased the share count from 9.0 to 24.8m (+175%). In addition come 500,000 warrants to the CEO from October 2025 (approx. 2%).

      −4
    • Customer behavior and macro
      The company itself writes that the regions' markedly lower demand for care staffing and the recession in the recruitment and interim sector have hit NGS hard since 2023. The business is tied to public framework agreements and tenders (Kammarkollegiet, Migrationsverket, Norwegian hospitals), where price decides.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Management stability
      Third CEO in four years: Erika Rönnquist Hoh took office in March 2023 and left in June 2024, the CFO left the same year, and Jan Bengtsson (acting 2024, permanent from September 2025) has already asked the board to find his successor by the 2027 AGM at the latest.
