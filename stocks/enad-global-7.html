    In plain words

    The score of 48 shows a games company with falling sales and an unclear future. Revenue has fallen two years running, from SEK 2,077m to SEK 1,647m, and earlier takeovers left large losses. It spent SEK 356m on a game from a studio once owned by its chief executive and deputy chair. On the plus side are low net debt and a new main-owner chair. Strategy comes in 2027.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisition wave in 2020–2021 (Daybreak, Piranha, Big Blue Bubble etc.) was paid for with shares, so the share count rose approx. 115% in 2021. Since then there has been a net loss of SEK 1,239m in 2022 and a write-down of 2,052m in Q4 2025. Together that is approx. 2.6 times the current market cap of 1.26bn. Goodwill fell from 3,892m (2021) to 958m.
      mfn.se
      −7
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen two years in a row: SEK 2,077m (2023) → 1,918 → 1,647 (2025, −14%). Big Blue Bubble has more than halved from 585 to SEK 237m. Q2 2026's organic growth of 30% comes mainly from one published third-party title (Far Far West at Fireshine, +127%), where royalties to the developer are still to be paid; Q1 was −13% organic.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Margin
      Adjusted EBIT was only SEK 0.6m in Q4 2025, and the EBIT margin was −7.5% as early as 2024 before the big write-down. The EBITDA margin is 11% TTM, but the capitalised development (258m TTM) exceeds EBITDA (183m). Q2 2026 with an adjusted EBITDA margin of 20.9% is the first strong quarter in a long time.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Moat and future segment
      The core is ageing MMOs (EverQuest from 1999, EverQuest II, DC Universe Online, Lord of the Rings Online) and My Singing Monsters. Several titles are built on licensed IPs (DC, Tolkien, Aliens), which the company does not own. Palia (10m players in total) is the only new growth leg of any significance.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −18 of −35
    • Related party and execution (Cold Iron / Aliens: Fireteam Elite 2)
      EG7 has invested SEK 356m in AFE2 from Cold Iron, which was owned by CEO Ji Ham and vice chair Jason Epstein; the studio was bought for USD 3m after approval at the general meeting. The game (launched 25/8) has, according to Raijin, sold approx. 172,000 copies on Steam (approx. USD 5.2m gross), has 61% positive reviews, and the player count fell 75% in October. Staff have been cut after the launch, and the Switch 2 version has been postponed to 2027. There is a risk of a write-down in Q3.

      The financial targets for 2026 were withdrawn on 16/2-2026. A strategic review is under way, and the result will only be presented at a capital markets day in Q1 2027. Investors thus have no targets against which to measure management.
