In its favour, costs are down 23% and some projects were sold at a gain.

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company in early-stage life science (Lund) with 6 employees and a market cap of approx. SEK 50m. The extraordinary general meeting on 25/11-2026 on the TEQCool agreement and the targeted issue has not been held yet. stockanalysis states insider ownership of 7.38%, but the list of owners shows that the CEO alone owns 25.4%.
