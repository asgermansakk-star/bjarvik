    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −24 of −30
    • Margin
      No sales. Revenue is grants only (incl. CEPI), SEK 9.1m TTM, vs an operating loss of SEK 21.2m. The loss has grown from 1.3m in 2024 (a year with many grants) to 10.6m in 2025 and 21m TTM, as GMP production of study material took off.
      stockanalysis.com
      −10
    • Quality of growth
      There are no data from humans. The lead candidate Ab-01.12 (nasal pneumococcal vaccine) had its first Phase 1 study approved by the Dutch authority CCMO on 4/11-2026. The study has up to 25 healthy adults and is mainly to measure safety, and the local approval at Radboud UMC is still missing. The influenza vaccine has only animal data (ferrets).
      mfn.se
      −8
    • Moat
      The BERA platform (OMV) is patented and could in theory provide serotype-independent protection. However, the benefit has not been shown in humans, and the pneumococcal market is dominated by conjugate vaccines from Pfizer, Merck and other large companies with several 'universal' candidates underway.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      With about 6 employees, the effort is spread across pneumococcus, influenza, pandemic preparedness and early projects in chlamydia, ETEC, covid-19 and tuberculosis. Capital has been raised in two targeted issues without pre-emptive rights within seven months: kr 6.56 in July 2025 and kr 5.35 in January 2026 to one private investor.
      mfn.se
      −3

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −21 of −35
    • Funding
      Cash was SEK 17.7m at 30/6 plus 7.9m from warrants in July, while operating cash flow was −8.3m in Q2 alone. The CEPI project ends in December 2026, and most of the grant has already been paid out. Phase 1 is according to the company funded, but the planned human challenge study is not, so a new issue in 2027 is likely.
      mb.cision.com
      −7
    • Dilution
      The share count has risen from 11.6m (2021) to 21.46m (+85%), of which +40% since 2024 (15.35m). This is due to the unit issue in 2025, the directed issue in January 2026 and warrants in July 2026.
      mfn.se
      −5
    • Clinical risk
      This is the first human test of a nasal OMV vaccine. The study measures safety and tolerability with escalating doses and a booster 8–10 weeks later, so meaningful data will come in 2027 at the earliest. All the value rests on the platform being safe and immunogenic in the nose.
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Partner and commercialisation
      The business model is to out-license in the early clinical phase, but there is no industrial partner, only academic collaborations (Radboud UMC, VU Amsterdam) and grants. Without a licence agreement the large vaccine studies must be financed with share issues.
      mb.cision.com
      −3
    • Governance
      Insiders own only 4.66%. The largest owner (about 8.7%) is a private investor who came in via a directed issue he initiated himself. He writes himself that he has no expertise in the area and that the amount took most of his liquid funds.
      mfn.se
      −1
    • Liquidity in the stock
      The stock trades very little: on average about 8,400 shares a day (about kr 60,000), and several days have under 1,000 shares. Spreads and price swings on news are large (−12% and +10% in June).
