    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company was called Refine Group until May 2026 and has since February 2026 changed strategy to buying majority stakes in DTC brands (51% of Andy okay from 10/4-2026, 70% of Criss Bellini from 1/10-2026). stockanalysis' history before 2026 therefore covers a different business (agencies, PIM, furniture e-commerce), and there is no forecast page (404), no analysts and no forward P/E. stockanalysis' statistics page is partly outdated: the share count (58.1m) and TTM revenue (kr 155.8m) are from before Q3; according to the Q3 report there were 63.2m shares as of 30/9 and 63.6m after the debt-settlement issue on 6/10, i.e. a market cap of approx. kr 270m at a price of 4.25. Criss Bellini is only included from Q4, and net cash after the purchase (kr 25m in cash), the repayment of the loan from Buntel and the loan from Resurs Bank of kr 20m is estimated from the announcements, not from a balance sheet. Andy okay's and Criss Bellini's standalone margins in 2026 are not disclosed in the report. The significance of tariffs and the abolished de minimis rules for sales to the US has not been examined in depth. The customers are consumers, so customer concentration is not a problem; dependence on one subsidiary has been assessed instead. Analytical framework, not investment advice.
