    • Margin and ROIC
      The EBIT margin has fallen from 6.4% (2021/22) to 4.3% (2024/25) and 3.0% over the last 12 months. ROIC has fallen from 10.6% to 3.1% and is now below WACC of 5.1%. Before special items, the first half of 2025/26 produced a loss of kr 3.2m, and the gross margin fell from 32% to 30%. Even at the high end of guidance (adjusted EBIT kr 12–15m, expected at the low end), the margin will be only about 4–5%.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from kr 338.7m (2021/22) to kr 303.1m (2024/25) and kr 300.2m over the last 12 months, i.e. approx. 11% in three years. In the first half of 2025/26 the construction division fell 18% (kr 50.7m to 41.7m). The industrial division grew 7%.

    • Moat
      RIAS is a small wholesaler of plastic semi-finished products with an EBIT margin of 3–6% and no significant production of its own. Management itself writes that competition is high and that the focus is on price and delivery reliability. Rising raw material prices could not be passed on in the first half, and gross margin fell 2 percentage points.
