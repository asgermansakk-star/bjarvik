      The fee margin is under pressure: net commission relative to year-end AUM fell from about 0.94% in 2021 (69.2m on 7.4bn) to about 0.54% in 2025 (111.3m on 20.5bn). 67% of AUM is now in interest-rate funds, and the new ICA Banken funds compete on the market's lowest fees. ROE of 11.6% and ROIC of 13.3% are moderate for a capital-light business, because intangible assets make up SEK 291m.

    • Accounting practice and impairment risk
      The intangible assets (SEK 291m, 87% of equity) are rights to manage acquired funds. The company considers the useful life indefinite and therefore does not amortise, even though K3 normally requires a finite useful life. With falling AUM and fees the risk of write-down is real, and profit is higher than with ongoing amortisation.
