    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −7 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue grew only 1.0% in 2025 and 1.7% in 2024 (SEK 30.3bn TTM), and end customers' service revenue rose 2% organically in Q3 2026; analysts expect 1.6% in 2026 and 2.2% in 2027. Mature, low-growth telecom.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • Earnings lift from savings
      Underlying EBITDAaL growth has fallen from 11-13% (2025 and Q1 2026) to 4% in Q2 and 3% in Q3, after the savings (about 650 jobs cut by the end of 2025) have been harvested. ABG writes that further upside will depend on a consolidation of the Swedish market.
      placera.se
      −2
    • Moat and market
      Sweden accounts for SEK 23.1bn of 30.3bn (76%) in a market with four mobile networks and fierce competition; the rest is the small Baltic markets. Tele2 is the challenger behind Telia, with no geographic diversification.
      stockanalysis.com
      −1
    • Capital allocation
      The sale of approx. 2,700 Baltic towers and rooftop sites to GCI brought in SEK 4.7bn in cash, but costs approx. EUR 35m a year in underlying EBITDAaL under a 20-year lease; a lasting loss of earnings for a one-off cash inflow.
      mfn.se
      −1

      The dividend of SEK 10.50 × about 694m shares ≈ SEK 7.3bn corresponds to 118% of 2025 eFCF and is above the SEK 6.7bn eFCF over the last 12 months; the difference is covered by the tower proceeds, which are a one-off source.

      Underlying EBITDAaL in Q3 of SEK 3,146m was below consensus (3,180); Nordea calls the quarter weaker and marked by intense competition in Sweden and lowers its forecasts for 2026-2028. Guidance was only narrowed to 4-5%, where ABG had expected an upgrade.

      Net debt SEK 25bn incl. leasing, net debt/EBITDA 2.1, interest cover 7.4 and current ratio 0.77; Altman Z 2.21. Manageable, but no buffer to pay out more than the cash flow over time.
