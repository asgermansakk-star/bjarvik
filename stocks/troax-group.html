    In plain words

    The score is 57 out of 100: a Swedish maker of safety fencing with declining profitability. Profit margins and the return on invested money have fallen, and growth comes from acquisitions while its own sales fell 4%. Net debt rose from EUR 54m to 183m. Record order intake in the third quarter and insider purchases count in its favour. Moving production in North America has had start-up problems.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are in EUR, the stock trades in SEK; stockanalysis' multiples are converted. Trailing P/E (332) is distorted by non-cash write-ups of contingent consideration (EUR 16.3m in 9M) and has not been used in Step 2; the assessment is based on forward P/E, EV/EBITDA and FCF. Analysts' estimates and price targets are from 17/7-2026 (before Q3), and only 4 analysts cover the stock. The content of the correction of the Q1 report and customer concentration in the data centre and China business (Vichnet) have not been examined and give 0. Marked cyclical because machine guarding follows the investment cycle of factory automation and warehouse automation (weak car and construction demand), and the company itself names the economic cycle as the biggest risk.
