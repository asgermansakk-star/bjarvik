    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −13 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC over the cycle
      Despite an EBITDA margin of 62%, net income was only USD 15.4m on revenue of USD 1,220m (TTM), i.e. a net margin of 1.3%, because hedging, interest and Danish hydrocarbon tax eat into earnings. ROIC is 17% TTM, but only 7–13% in 2023–2025 and negative in 2021–2022.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat and operator role
      Pure price taker without its own operator role: BlueNord is a passive partner in the Danish Underground Consortium, where TotalEnergies operates Tyra, Halfdan, Dan and Gorm. The company has 57 employees and no influence on operations or investment pace.
      worldoil.com
      −3
    • Future segment
      100% oil and gas from mature fields in the Danish North Sea with about 195m boe in reserves and contingent resources. Without new developments, production falls after the Tyra plateau.
      worldoil.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The dividends of USD 514m TTM (341m in 2025 plus buybacks of 50m) equal 86–105% of FCF. The payout ratio is 1,000% of net profit, and book equity has shrunk to NOK 0.94bn, so debt/equity has risen from 1.4 to 11.8.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

    Today's multiples against the company's own history and its peers. · 3 findings    −7 of −35
    • Multiples vs own history
      Forward P/E 5.5 is slightly below its own forward history (5.9–7.7 in 2021–2025), and EV/EBITDA 3.3 is at the low end of 3.2–7.2. It is however measured on Tyra's peak production and high gas prices; trailing P/E is 93, because earnings are low.

    • Peers
      EV/EBITDA 3.3 vs Vår Energi 1.9, Equinor about 2.5 and Aker BP 2.8. BlueNord is more expensive than the larger Norwegian producers, even though it is a non-operator with one area and higher leverage.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3
    • The price follows Vår Energi
      With 9.7153 Vår Energi shares per BlueNord share, the stock is in effect a Vår Energi position. Vår Energi's forward P/E is at the top of its own history at the peak of the price cycle; the spread to the merger value is only about 3% (558 vs 540 NOK).
      stockanalysis.com
      −2

    • Price normalisation
      The only analyst with estimates expects 2026 EPS down 79% to USD 0.65 and revenue down 8% in 2027. Consensus is Sell (2 strong sell of 4), and the hedging effect pulled revenue down in Q3.

    • Concentration in one area
      All production comes from the Danish North Sea, and Tyra accounted for about 30 of 43.6 thousand boe/day in Q3 (about 70%). A stoppage at Tyra, as in the planned shutdown in June, hits the whole company.
      energy-pedia.com
      −3

      Net debt USD 1.03bn (net debt/EBITDA 1.36), current ratio 0.55, interest cover 3.0 and liquidity of USD 393m, of which only USD 131m in cash.
      stockanalysis.com
      −3

      Net cash flow from operations was only USD 69.7m in Q3 vs USD 212m before working capital and tax payments, so FCF TTM of USD 599m overstates what is left after tax.

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Special situation: binding statutory merger with Vår Energi, approved at BlueNord's extraordinary general meeting on 24/8-2026. Each share is exchanged for 9.7153 Vår Energi shares plus NOK 76.83 in cash (approx. NOK 558 at a Vår Energi price of 49.53 vs the BlueNord price of NOK 540 on 6/11), so the price follows the deal terms and Vår Energi, not BlueNord. Closing is expected around the turn of the year 2026/27 and awaits regulatory approvals; the share is then delisted. Accounts in USD, price in NOK. P/B 14.8 and trailing P/E 93 are meaningless because of book equity eroded by dividends and TTM earnings weighed down by hedging, interest and tax. Only 4 analysts (consensus Sell), and the forward estimates are based on one analyst. BlueNord's own 2026 guidance, hedging losses and insider trades have not been examined in the full Q3 report.
