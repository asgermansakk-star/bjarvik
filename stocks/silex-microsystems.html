    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 3 findings    −9 of −30
    • Quality of earnings
      EBIT is flattered by unrealised currency gains from revaluation of balance sheet items: net SEK 44m in 9M 2026 (vs −39m), approx. 11% of EBIT, plus 16m in financial currency gains. In addition, rental income from external tenants in Järfälla (40m in 9M) is included in operating profit.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Quality of growth
      Development projects accounted for 48.5% (42.0%) of revenue in 9M and for 161 of SEK 199m in growth, while production grew only 38m; Industrial and automotive fell 24.6% in Q3 and consumer electronics 9%. The target of SEK 2.5bn in 2030 corresponds to only about 10% annual growth from the R12 level.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Capital allocation (US factory)
      The purchase and conversion of onsemi's 200 mm fab in Pennsylvania costs approx. SEK 1.6bn, of which 1.0bn in 2026–27 – more than the entire net cash from the listing. The fab is expected to reach EBIT break-even only in 2029–30 and to reach the Järfälla level of 2025 only in 2034; closing requires CFIUS approval and is expected around the turn of 2027/28. That drags down ROIC and FCF for many years.
      mfn.se
      −5

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −14 of −35
    • Ownership overhang (lock-up)
      Chinese Sai MicroElectronics still owns 9.1%, and Bure is to sell a further 19.1% on behalf of SMEI after a 180-day lock-up from the listing (first tranche of 9.0% within 18 months, second tranche within three years). In total approx. 28% of the shares are to come onto the market, and free float is only approx. 33m shares.
      mfn.se
      −4
    • Dilution
      The number of ordinary shares has risen from 88.2m to 109.8m in a year: 12.3m new shares in the IPO (SEK 1.0bn gross according to the cash flow) and 9.3m shares as payment for the buyback of employee warrants (SEK 500m). In addition, 9.7m C shares from LTIP 2025, subscribed at SEK 4.95, can be converted when the share price is above SEK 71.85 – which it already is. That gives up to 8.1% further dilution.
      storage.mfn.se
      −4
    • Customer concentration
      The company itself describes the customer base as concentrated. Optical circuit switches (OCS) for AI data centres account for approx. 30% of revenue and are supplied to 'several leading players'; North America accounts for 60.4% of sales in 9M.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Management
      CFO Maria Engström is leaving the company five months after the stock market listing and will be replaced on 11/11-2026 by Karin Rosenthal from Mips, in the middle of the largest investment programme in the company's history.
      mfn.se
      −2
    • Currency
      60–70% of revenue is invoiced in USD, while costs are mainly in SEK; the stronger krona has given reported growth of 20.2% vs 28.0% in constant currencies in 9M.
