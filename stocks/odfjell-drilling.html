    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The accounts are kept in USD, while the stock trades in NOK on Oslo Børs. The trailing figures for 2026 include insurance income from the Deepsea Atlantic incident (USD 82m in Q2, net +USD 32m on EBITDA) and USD 5m from discontinued operations. The multiples are therefore only roughly adjusted. TTM FCF is negative because the purchase of Deepsea Bollsta/Bergen (USD 480m in Q4 2025) is booked as capex. stockanalysis shows 0.03% insider ownership, but that is wrong: Helene Odfjell owns 49.9% through Odfjell Partners Holding (S&P). The tax case on Odfjell Offshore has been appealed to the Supreme Court by the tax authorities, and the outcome has not been assessed. Peer multiples have not been calculated for harsh environment drillers in Norway other than the American Transocean, Valaris and Noble. Only 4 analysts cover the stock.
