    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Stockanalysis' TTM FCF (EUR 1.94bn, FCF yield 18%) and net cash (1.25bn) are distorted by prepayments from the Spanish special modernisation programmes (PEM) – customer funds, not own cash; management's own FCF guidance for 2026 is over EUR 375m. ROIC of 39.8% is lifted by the same prepayments (ROCE only 7.9%). Part of the growth was bought (Hispasat/Hisdesat in Space, +398%). Net profit rose only 2% in H1 despite +51% in EBIT; the reason is not stated in the sources I have seen. The share count (−0.4%) is treasury shares bought and reissued, not a real buyback programme. Price targets are from July 2026. Insider trading not investigated beyond the Escribano sale.
