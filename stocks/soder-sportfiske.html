    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (market cap approx. SEK 168m) with no analyst coverage, so there is no forward estimate, price target or PEG. The company reports under K3 with goodwill amortisation (SEK 4.9m a year); multiples are therefore shown on both reported and adjusted EBIT. Leasing (rent) is expensed in EBITDA under K3, so FCF is already after leases. stockanalysis shows insider ownership of 0.43% and a gross margin of approx. 27%, which does not match the company's own figures (Rite Ventures approx. 39% plus the founders; gross margin 39.7%) – the company's figures are used. Who sold the 500,000 shares the chairman bought in 2026 has not been examined. Customer behaviour (returns, repeat purchases) and market shares have not been examined and give 0. The multiples are the lowest in the company's history (P/E 15.3 vs 25.8–42.6 and EV/EBITDA 6.4 vs 11.6–16.8 in 2023–2025), but the history is based on years of low margin, so it gives neither deduction nor plus.
