    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The company is the former investment company Nosium AB, which through a reverse takeover (completed 29/6-2026) took over 89.9% of the Finnish Medicortex Finland Oyj. Under the pro forma rule, stockanalysis' history (P/B 0.28–1.21 in 2021–2025, revenue from Dr Sannas, ROE and ROIC) has not been used. The same applies to trailing P/E 1.73, ROIC 14.6% and 'profit margin' 83%, because they are based on negative goodwill of SEK 11.5m. It is a one-off accounting income with no cash flow, and without it the group's result was −SEK 2.2m in H1. Medicortex Finland's own accounts from before the acquisition have only been used via the information memorandum. There is no analyst coverage, no forecast page and no price target. It is unclear whether the debt-settlement issues of 7.65m shares (agreed 1/9) have been completed, because stockanalysis still shows 23.33m shares. The value of the legacy holdings Co-properties (SEK 8.2m carrying value) and Invest Riddarholmen (5.1m) has not been verified. No insider trades have been found since the acquisition, and customer concentration is not relevant (no sales). The next report is the annual report on 19/2-2027; the company has not published a Q3 report. Low liquidity in the stock.
