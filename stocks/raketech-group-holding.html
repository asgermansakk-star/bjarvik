    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −26 of −30
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from EUR 77.7m in 2023 to 51.3m in 2024, 27.0m in 2025 and EUR 21.7m TTM. In Q3 2026 continuing revenue fell to EUR 5.0m (6.2) because the Paid Publisher Network is being phased out and the non-core markets are weakening. Only Sweden and Denmark are growing.
      raketech.com
      −9
    • Margin and ROIC
      Adjusted EBITDA margin of 23.0% in Q3 is due to cost cuts, but the reported operating margin is only 3.1% TTM (24% in 2021). Net income is −EUR 0.7m TTM, ROE −2.2% and ROIC 2.4% vs a WACC of 6.5%.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5
    • Capital allocation
      The acquisition of Casumba in 2019 ended in an earn-out of approx. EUR 20.3m for assets that were sold on for EUR 12.0m in 2025. Write-downs of EUR 58.9m in 2024 and a net loss of EUR 13.1m in 2025 have more than halved equity (99 to EUR 32m). Market cap has fallen from SEK 987m in 2021 to approx. SEK 90m.
      raketech.com
      −8
    • Future segment and moat
      SEO-based affiliate and comparison sites for gaming and betting are exposed to Google's algorithms, AI search and the operators' own marketing. The new media-based model (Casinofeber Media, BetXpert Media, Corriere del Gioco) is unproven outside Sweden, and the USA remains difficult.
      raketech.com
      −4

    Price

    Today's multiples against the company's own history and its peers. · 1 finding    −2 of −35
    • Multiples adjusted for earn-out
      P/B 0.25 and P/S 0.36 (0.29 at the end of 2025) look very cheap. Adjusted for the earnout, EV was about EUR 26m at 30/9. After the settlement net debt is about EUR 5.6m, so EV becomes about EUR 14m (about 6m with the seller credit deducted). That equals about 1.3–3x adjusted EBITDA, but about 10x the reported EBIT of EUR 0.7m TTM, which is falling. The low multiple reflects a shrinking business.

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −12 of −35
    • Governance and related parties
      Board member Magnus Alebo is the beneficial owner of one of the Casumba sellers and is therefore the counterparty in the earn-out settlement. He did not take part in the decision, but such a large transaction with a board member on the other side weakens trust.

      −3
    • Customer behaviour and regulation
      The player databases in the Paid Publisher Network fall with natural attrition, also in Brazil after the new rules. Revenue depends on the payments from gaming operators (revenue share and CPA) and on regulation in each country.
      raketech.com
      −3
    • Guidance and execution
      There are no financial targets. The preliminary figures for October 2026 show that Affiliation Marketing is below the Q3 average, and management speaks only of 'gradual improvement' in 2027.
      raketech.com
      −2
    • Seller credit and counterparty risk
      A large part of the value is the vendor credit from the sale of the Casumba assets: EUR 12.0m is paid in monthly instalments until December 2029, but it is only booked at EUR 6.6m, and only EUR 1.2m has been received. The credit is pledged to the bank, so the risk depends on a single buyer.
      raketech.com
      −2
