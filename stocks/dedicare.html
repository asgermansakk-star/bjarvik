    • Politics: profit cap on Danish supported housing
      The acquired Danish residential facilities and social services accounted for approx. 22 of the group's SEK 30.9m EBITA in Q3 (Denmark 15.5% margin). Prime Minister Mette Frederiksen opened in September 2026 for a cap on profit on private social services, and a bill against fraud in social services is being considered in the autumn; a cap hits precisely the business that carries the profit improvement.

      Over 90% of the market (excluding life science) is public customers. Copenhagen Municipality makes up 13.7% of Denmark's revenue, Trondheim Municipality 9.0% (6.3) of Norway's and the City of Stockholm 8.0% of Sweden's; in the UK one customer accounts for 50%. Demand depends on regions' and municipalities' budgets and restrictions on insourcing.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Dedicare has bought Hyldgaard & Larsen and Sustina (15/7-2026, total consideration SEK 184.8m incl. present value of earn-out) and WeCare (1/10-2025). Under Method 1.3 (4), trailing multiples and stockanalysis history have therefore only been used with reservations and held up against the company's own pro forma figures (acquired revenue SEK 218.5m and profit after tax SEK 26.5m in Jan.–Sep. 2026). The acquisition analysis is preliminary: the entire excess value (SEK 166.8m) sits in goodwill, so future amortisation of customer relationships may lower EPS. Q3's acquired EBITA (approx. SEK 22m) is higher than the pro forma level for H1 and should not be annualised uncritically. No analysts and no forward estimate on stockanalysis, so PEG and upside lack data. The insider trades from FI's register are A shares, which are not listed, and were probably traded off-exchange. The political profit cap in Denmark has not yet been tabled as a bill. The stock is illiquid (approx. 22,600 shares a day).
