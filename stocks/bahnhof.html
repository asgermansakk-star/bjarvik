    In plain words

    The score of 65 is middling: a Swedish internet provider with satisfied customers, but a high price. The share is expensive relative to earnings, and its price is lifted by a bid: Telenor Sweden has taken 57.5% and offers SEK 62 per share (deadline 7 December). Remaining owners become a minority, with a risk of delisting. On the plus side: nearly 500,000 customer households.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    • Margin
      The operating margin (EBIT) has fallen from 14.4% in 2023 and 13.4% in 2024 to 12.7% in 2025 (adjusted for Elementica) and 13.0% in H1 2026; the gross margin has slipped from 21.1% to 19.5%. Guidance for 2026 is 'just over 12 percent'.

    • Quality of growth
      The private market (about 70% of revenue) grew only from 489,859 to 498,550 connected households (+1.8%) in a year, and the company calls the 'öppna stadsnät' segment challenging; the 7% growth in H1 comes from price increases, business customers and abroad (5% of revenue).
      mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat
      Bahnhof owns only a small part of the fibre network and typically delivers via open city networks and others' network owners; access prices are not regulated by PTS, so price increases from the network owners squeeze customers and margin. Brand and service (Broadband Operator of the Year 7 years in a row) are strong, but give limited pricing power.

      The Elementica data centre project in Norra Djurgårdsstaden was abandoned in 2025 and written down to zero with SEK 42.5m; at the same time investments have risen to a record SEK 160m (acquisition of Bunkeberget Park and Norwegian Brdy) with no documented return yet.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Special situation: Telenor Sweden bought on 6/11-2026 the shares of the founders (Jon Karlung, Andreas Norman/K.N. Telecom) and Investment AB Öresund, in total 57.5% of the capital and 87.9% of the votes, and on the same day made an unconditional mandatory cash offer of SEK 62 per share (acceptance period 9/11–7/12-2026). The price (SEK 62.00) therefore follows the offer, not the company; the independent committee's recommendation and fairness opinion (ABG Sundal Collier) are not yet available, and a later compulsory redemption/delisting is likely if Telenor reaches 90%. Financial figures are from Q2 2026 (stockanalysis updated 18/8); the Q3 report (17/11 according to MFN) is not included. Trailing EPS is weighed down by a one-off write-down of SEK 42.5m (Elementica) in 2025; P/E is assessed both with and without it. Cash (SEK 519m) contains customer prepayments (prepaid subscriptions, approx. SEK 260m at the turn of the year). No analyst coverage, no forward estimate and no insider data on stockanalysis. Customer churn, ARPU and leases with network owners have not been examined. Analytical framework, not investment advice.
