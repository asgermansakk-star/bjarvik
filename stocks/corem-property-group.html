    In plain words

    The score is 50 out of 100. The property company has lost money four years running and has high debt: DKK 9.3bn falls due within a year. Ericsson is also moving its head office away from Kista. On the plus side, the B share costs about a fifth of the company's own value estimate per share, and the CEO and board members have bought shares. Watch the refinancing.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −21 of −30
    • Profitability and ROE
      The company has made a net loss four years in a row (−kr 1.9bn in 2022, −kr 8.0bn in 2023, −kr 1.1bn in 2024 and −kr 3.3bn in 2025) and −kr 2.3bn over the last 12 months. ROE is −13.2%, and ROIC of 4.2% is below WACC of 5.3%. Earnings per ordinary A/B share were −kr 0.20 in 9M.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 10% to kr 2,382m and net operating income 12% to kr 1,511m in 9M. Förvaltningsresultatet was flat (693 vs kr 695m), but only because it includes kr 302m in dividends from bank shares; without them it fell about 44%. The economic occupancy rate fell to 82% from 85% a year ago. In the comparable portfolio, revenue rose only 1%.
      mb.cision.com
      −6
    • Future segment
      Office makes up 60% of lettable area, and Kista makes up 14% of property value. The properties in Kista were written down by most of SEK 543m in Q3 because of higher yield requirements and lower expected cash flows. The average remaining term of the leases is only 3.3 years, and the CEO himself writes that parts of the Stockholm office market have large supply and long decision processes.
      mb.cision.com
      −4
    • Capital allocation
      With a loan-to-value ratio of 56% and a junk rating, the company bought Nordic bank shares for kr 2.6bn as a "liquidity reserve" and bought back its own shares for about kr 550m (B shares at an average of kr 2.47, above the current price). Both were financed with property sales and loans, not with free cash flow. In 2024 and 2025, new shares were issued for about kr 1.0bn and 0.9bn at the same time, and in 2025 hybrid bonds of kr 1.2bn were redeemed.
      stockanalysis.com
      −5

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 6 findings    −25 of −35
    • Debt and refinancing
      The loan-to-value ratio is 56%, interest cover 1.7× and net debt/EBITDA 15.6. Average debt maturity is only 1.8 years, and kr 9.3bn of debt falls due within a year. Interest rate swaps of kr 21.8bn at an average of 2.2% expire in 1.8 years on average, so the fixed-interest period has fallen to 1.5 years (2.1). Unsecured bonds of kr 3.8bn at Stibor + 2.75–4.25% fall due in 2027–2028. Scope rates the company BB+ (junk). Cash was kr 163m, and only kr 1,243m of the credit facilities can be used immediately.
      mb.cision.com
      −9
    • Cash flow and dividend
      Operating cash flow was kr 878m over the last 12 months (of which kr 302m was a one-off bank dividend), while property investments were kr 1,299m. Free cash flow is thus negative, but the company still paid kr 530m in dividends and bought back shares for kr 557m, financed with property sales.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Governance and related parties
      Rutger Arnhult is both CEO and controlling owner with 57% of the capital and 68% of the votes. In 2022 he had to sell Castellum shares for kr 4.6bn after the banks judged him over-leveraged. The company buys services from Wästbygg (associated with Arnhult's M2-Gruppen) and from the law firm Walthon, of which the chair of the board is a co-owner.
      realtid.se
      −4
    • Tenants' behaviour (Ericsson and Kista)
      Ericsson terminated 33,000 m² in Kista effective from the end of 2025 and has since decided to move its headquarters to Hagastaden. One of the company's largest tenants thus leaves Kista from 2030. Net letting in 9M was only +SEK 4m, and vacancy rose in Q3 after a tenant dispute that also cost SEK 20m in a one-off settlement.
      nordicpropertynews.com
      −4
    • Valuation of properties
      All properties are valued internally every quarter, and only 9% of property value was valued externally in Q3. The average yield requirement is 6.2%. The stake in the unlisted Klövern (kr 751m) is valued at level 3 and was written down by kr 328m in 9M 2025. At a share price of 0.20 × NAV the market clearly doubts the book values.
      mb.cision.com
      −2

SEK 5.9bn) and a fixed dividend of SEK 20 per share per year. That is approx. SEK 390m a year that must be paid before the ordinary shareholders get anything, vs an underlying förvaltningsresultat of approx. SEK 520m over the last 12 months excluding the bank dividend.
