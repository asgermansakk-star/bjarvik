    In plain words

    New Bubbleroom Sweden scores 62 out of 100, held back mostly by the business. The women's fashion web shop had losses every year from 2021 to 2025, and this year's profit comes from a one-off gain on selling a warehouse. On the plus side, it has 74.8m kr. in cash and no interest-bearing debt, about half its market value. The share is up 122% in a year.

    A good business with a real question mark: the price, a crack, or both.

    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Microcap (approx. kr 150m) with no analyst coverage: there is no forward estimate and no price target, so PEG is n/a. The reported TTM result (kr 2.9m, P/E 52) and EBITDA are lifted by a one-off gain of net kr 11.3m on the sale of the automation warehouse in Q3 2026. Margin and multiples are therefore assessed on adjusted figures (adjusted EBIT approx. −kr 5.5m TTM). The cash of kr 74.8m at 30/9 includes the proceeds of approx. kr 32m from the sale and is seasonally affected. The company reports under K3, so rent is an operating cost and FCF corresponds to FCF after leases. The largest owner and the shareholder list have not been examined (insiders own 36.5% according to stockanalysis), and no financial targets or guidance were found (0 points). Both criteria make the grade a cap.
