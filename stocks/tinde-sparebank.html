    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The security is an equity certificate (ECC), not an ordinary share. The ownership share fraction is 34.85%, so approx. 65% of equity is self-owned (primary capital). The security is economically equivalent to a share and is scored as such. The bank was formed by the merger of Romsdal Sparebank and Sunndal Sparebank on 2/6-2025, so stockanalysis's history (P/B 0.29, EPS growth, share count +95%, ROE and FCF) mixes pre- and post-merger figures and is based on total equity including self-owned capital and perpetual bonds; it has not been used. T1 and T2 are based on the bank's own pro forma figures and figures per equity certificate (the bank's definition: ownership share capital ÷ number of certificates, NOK 115.4 at 30/6-2026; approx. NOK 127 including the certificate holders' share of the fund for unrealised gains and profit for the period). Bank: EV, ROIC and FCF are not meaningful, and the dividend is measured against net income. The latest accounts are Q2 2026; the Q3 report is expected on 13/11-2026 and is not included, so it has not been checked whether losses fell in H2 as the board expects. stockanalysis has no forecast page (404), so forward P/E 16.67 has a very thin basis. Large single exposures and concentration in the largest customers have not been examined (0 points). The trading venue is Euronext Growth Oslo according to the bank.
