      Almost all sales go abroad (USA approx. 47%), while costs are in NOK in Tromsø. A weaker USD and EUR cost approx. NOK 7.7m in EBITDA in 9M 2026, equal to just under 40% of currency-adjusted EBITDA.

      EBITDA in Q3 fell 12% to kr 3.1m despite 15% sales growth: operating costs rose 14%, and there was a loss of 0.7m on rework in production. The capitalisation of payroll costs rose at the same time from 0.3 to kr 1.5m, which flatters EBITDA.
