    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    Investment company with holdings in oil services (HMH 36%, NES Fircroft approx. 15%, AKOFS Offshore 66.7%, DDW Offshore 100%); assessed on price to net asset value (NAV) and placed under Finance like Investor AB, Aker and Latour, even though the exposure is oil services (flagged cyclical). The current NAV is our own estimate: Q2 NAV of kr 17.3 per share with HMH at USD 18.74, adjusted to the HMH price of USD 10.94 (6/11) with approx. 16m HMH shares and USDNOK approx. 9.9, minus the dividend of kr 0.50 – approx. kr 12–13 per share. The exchange rate and NES Fircroft's value after the recapitalisation have not been verified. The Q3 report is due 11/11 and will show the NAV decline. Earnings figures, P/E and ROE are distorted by sales gains (kr 233m from ship sales in H1) and the equity method. Only 4 analysts and one EPS estimate; the price targets (kr 18–23) are from before the HMH fall and are outdated. The Aker group's ownership share and any insider trades have not been examined (0 points).
