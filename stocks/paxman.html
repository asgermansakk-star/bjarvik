    Business

    Is the company getting stronger, not just bigger? · 4 findings    −13 of −30
    • Margin and ROIC
      Gross margin is high and stable (64–66%), but operating margin is only 0.4% TTM (13.2% in 2024), and ROIC is −1.9% vs 26% in 2024. Adjusted EBITDA margin was 18.5% in Q2, but that is before goodwill amortisation, CIPN costs and relocation.
      stockanalysis.com
      −6
    • Quality of growth
      Sales rose 40% to SEK 105m in Q2, but 19.9m came from the acquired Dignitana, whose revenue stood still (19.9 vs 20.7m). Paxman alone grew approx. 25%, driven by the shift to insurance-based billing (IBBM) in the US, while the rest of the world grew only 5%.
      investor.paxmangroup.com
      −3
    • Capital allocation
      The share count rose 23% in a year (19.0 to 23.3m): a directed issue of SEK 117m and approx. 2.4m new shares as payment for Dignitana (bid of approx. SEK 153m with an 81% premium). The acquisition removed the biggest competitor, but has so far given ROIC close to zero and 154m in goodwill.
      storage.mfn.se
      −3
    • Moat
      With DigniCap, Paxman is virtually alone in mechanical scalp cooling in hospitals, but the alternative is cheaper manual cold caps, and earnings depend on reimbursement rates, which Paxman does not control itself.
      investor.paxmangroup.com
      −1

    Cracks

    Cash flow, customers, debt, insiders, guidance and what sits behind the headline numbers. · 5 findings    −13 of −35
    • Cash flow and receivables
      FCF is −SEK 22.5m TTM (before capitalised development of 5.6m), and operating cash flow was −3.2m in Q2. Trade receivables have risen from 46 to SEK 96m in half a year, because insurance-based invoicing pays more slowly; that equals approx. 84 days of sales.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Reimbursement and payers
      CMS's proposal for 2027 gives only USD 35.50 for cooling (CPT 97008), and the rates under the physician fee schedule fall slightly. The company itself calls it inadequate, and the IBBM model depends on insurers and Medicare paying.
      investor.paxmangroup.com
      −3
    • Regulatory execution (CIPN)
      FDA rejected the 510(k) route for the neuropathy system and requires a De Novo application. The US launch has been postponed to April–August 2027, so the new product leg will only generate revenue in the UK and Europe from the end of 2026.
      investor.paxmangroup.com
      −3
    • US dependence and currency
      The US accounted for SEK 67.6m of 105m in Q2 (64%), and sales are in USD while the accounts are kept in SEK; a weaker USD pulls directly on sales and earnings.
      storage.mfn.se
      −2
    • Governance
      Family-owned: founder Glenn Paxman sits on the board, his son Richard is CEO and his daughter is in management; the chair is dependent through CIMON. In May 2026 the CEO sold 60,000 shares to his father at kr 50.
