    Caveats

    What the score does not capture, or where the data is weak.
    The Stockholm line (GSKR SDB on First North) has only a price on stockanalysis (quote/sto/GSKR.SDB, approx. SEK 19); financials, multiples and price targets exist only for the main listing on TSX Venture (TSXV:GSKR) in CAD, which has therefore been used as path and currency. A SEK investor also bears CAD currency risk. Exploration and development company with no revenue: P/E, PEG, FCF and ROIC are not meaningful, and valuation is measured on EV per ounce in the resource. The resource statement is roughly summed (Barsele 0.32 Moz indicated + 1.83 Moz inferred, Rajapalot 0.867 Moz inferred; other Finnish deposits not included), and Rajapalot's PEA is from December 2023 with a lower gold price. Book equity is inflated by the acquisitions of Mawson and Barsele at market price, so P/B vs history has not been used. Insider trades have not been investigated beyond the ownership share (0.53%), and the Q3 report (expected 1/12-2026) is not out. Only 4–5 analysts, and the upside is over 100%, so upside points are limited by the caps. The share count after the 1:4 reverse split in December 2025 has not been reconciled with the cash flow, beyond the fact that share issues of C$101m and C$109m (FY2025 and TTM) confirm the dilution.
