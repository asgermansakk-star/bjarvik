    • ROIC and capital allocation
      ROIC has fallen from 17% (2022) and 15% (2023) to −0.3% TTM. dne pharma was bought in July 2025 for up to kr 225m with a promised EBITDA contribution of approx. kr 25m a year, financed with kr 210m in new loans and an issue at kr 21.50 (+35% shares); the stock now stands at kr 10.25. The addiction portfolio had revenue of kr 42m in nine months (below the kr 62m in 2024), and the group's adjusted EBITDA rose only kr 4.8m year to date.

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue fell 13.2% in Q3 and 2.3% year to date despite bought growth from dne pharma. Consumer Health fell 15% year to date (Modifast is losing to competitors, GeloRevoice is being phased out), and Nitrolingual and Gepan have been discontinued. The organic business is shrinking.
      newsweb.oslobors.no
      −5
    • Moat and future segment
      The business is largely in-licensed products. The largest single item, the obesity drug Mysimba, fell kr 10.5m in the third quarter in a market dominated by GLP-1 drugs. The rights changed owner (Azurity) and were only extended against an upfront licence fee, so the partners decide the terms.
