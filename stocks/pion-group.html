    • Margin and ROIC

    • Quality of growth
      Revenue has fallen from SEK 2,086m (2022) to 1,426m (TTM), partly through divestments (Norway, ROI Rekrytering, Lisberg, Student Node). Comparable growth is only 5.2% in 9M 2026, recruitment's share of revenue (the high-margin part) has fallen to 4.3% (6.0%), and QRIOS fell 24% in Q3 to 30.9m. Growth comes from Uniflex's low-margin blue-collar staffing.
      stockanalysis.com
      −4
    • Moat and future segment
      Staffing and recruitment have low entry barriers and no pricing power, and PION is a small player against Randstad, Adecco and Academic Work. Management itself writes that AI automates many white-collar jobs, i.e. exactly Poolia's market for qualified expertise (33% of revenue).
      mfn.se
      −3

      Acquisitions for SEK 32.7m in 2023 were followed by write-downs and restructuring in 2024 (goodwill 57.3 → 38.4m, customer relationships 34.3 → 15.9m), and the businesses were then sold off. The dividend has been cut from SEK 1.00 (2021) to 0 in 2024–2025. Now a new acquisition strategy is being launched with cash of 7.9m.

      Cash is SEK 7.9m vs 20.7m drawn on an overdraft of 60m. The temporary expansion to 100m expired 15/4 2026. The acquisition plans and the dividend target (at least 50%) can therefore not be financed from current cash flow without more debt or new shares.
      mfn.se

      Danir Resources (Dan Olofsson's sphere with Sigma and Nexer) owns 45.1% of the capital and 71.1% of the votes through A shares with full voting rights vs 1/5 vote for B shares. The minority has in practice no influence, and trading in the B share is very thin (approx. 14,000 shares a day).

      −3
    • Dilution
      Two warrant programmes (2.4m for 2026/2029 and 1.7m for 2024/2027 at a price of 9.00) equal approx. 8% dilution if fully exercised. That is a lot for a company that pays no dividend.
