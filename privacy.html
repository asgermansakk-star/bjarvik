    Privacy

    Bjarvik has no accounts, no forms, no analytics, no ads and no cookies. This page explains the little data there is.

    Who is responsible

    Bjarvik is run by Asger Mansa, a private person in Denmark. Contact: contact@bjarvik.com

    Server logs

    The site is hosted by Simply.com in Denmark. Like any web host, Simply keeps standard server logs, including your IP address, the page you asked for and your browser type. The logs are used to run and protect the site and are kept as set out in Simply's terms. Bjarvik does not use them to track or profile visitors.

    Stored in your browser

    A few settings are saved in your browser's local storage, so the site remembers them. They stay on your device and are never sent to the site. You can delete them at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

    • vanta-portfolio (localStorage): the holdings and amount in your fictional portfolio.
    • vanta-portfolio-prev (localStorage): your previous portfolio, kept when you open a share link, so it can be restored.
    • vanta-portfolio-filters (localStorage): the filters you chose in the portfolio builder.
    • vanta-horizon (localStorage): the holding period you chose on the slider.
    • vanta-intro (sessionStorage): that the opening animation has been shown. It is deleted when you close the browser.

    A portfolio share link holds the portfolio after the # in the address. That part of a link is not sent to the server.

    Fonts and links

    The fonts are hosted on this site, so no font service sees your visit. Links to sources, Instagram and other sites take you to other sites, which have their own privacy policies.

    Your rights

    You have the right to see the personal data held about you, to have it deleted and to object to its use. Write to the address above. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet) at datatilsynet.dk.
